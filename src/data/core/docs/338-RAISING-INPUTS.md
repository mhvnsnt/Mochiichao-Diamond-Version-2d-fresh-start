# #338 — RAISING INPUTS → the closed list of facts the variation engine is allowed to read

**The gap this fills.** #337's engine reads "raising
facts" — a phrase that rots if left open: every
later system would quietly nominate its own log as
a raising input, and drift would end up reading the
whole save. This spec is the **read set**: the
closed, named list of fact families the engine may
consume, each with its owning system cited. The
discipline is the yield: **the engine founds no
tracking of its own.** Every input already exists
because its own system records it for its own
reasons. A fact nobody records is a fact drift
cannot see — it reads as absent (weight zero),
never estimated, never interpolated.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/raising-inputs/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The read set (closed — eight families):**
  1. **Feeding & care acts** — #28's care profile
     log (the log is truth, the profile derived —
     #28's own doctrine) with #293's effect
     records: what was fed, what was given, the
     axis leans, the palate answers (liked /
     disliked are facts about the life, not just
     the meal).
  2. **Alignment acts** — #28's keeper-steered
     alignment history as #293 reads it: who
     raised, treatment facts (consistency, praise,
     neglect events of the #245/#220 classes).
     One channel of #337, per #293's seat — listed
     here so the set is complete in one place.
  3. **Bond facts** — the bond the world already
     keeps: the fact family #180 declares its
     effects over (care history, time in keeping,
     the keeper beats on #80's record). #339 owns
     what bond does to drift; the facts are
     #180's family's, read not refounded.
  4. **Activities** — what the individual has
     *done*: battles (participation facts in the
     #17/#177 family — fought, won, fallen,
     carried), care sports (#294 participation and
     results), field life (time as follower #58,
     habitats lived in #66/#29, travel with the
     party). Doing is raising: a Mochi raised in
     battle and a Mochi raised in a garden have
     different facts and will read differently.
  5. **Moves practised** — the individual's known-
     move set (a record fact — battle legality
     already keeps it) plus usage where #187's
     replay input logs are kept. #340 owns this
     family's drift read; the input seat is here.
  6. **Keeper & place history** — who kept them
     and where they lived: #80's history beats
     (met / kept / transferred — keeper changes
     ride #168's and #217's records), Garden
     residence (#28/#65), shelter time (#220),
     lab time (#44's seats — a lab-raised life is
     a raising), stolen time (#273 — captivity is
     a raising fact too, and pretending otherwise
     would falsify the read; what it *does* to a
     heart is #287's, what it does to a look is
     read here like any other hard season).
  7. **Rest patterns** — #134's camp rests, #262's
     sunlight rest for plant-bodied species, sleep
     in the Garden's rhythm (#28 drives read at
     the act): a rested life and an exhausted life
     are different inputs, in the open.
  8. **Checkup facts** — #293's grades: dated,
     kept facts about care axes at a moment. The
     engine reads the *trajectory* (grades across
     checkups), never a single grade as a verdict.
- **The read is over history, not state.** Every
  family is read as a *pattern across the life*
  (recency-weighted — data — but never last-act-
  only): one feast is not a raising; a childhood of
  feasts is. The derivation's weighting shape is
  this spec's; the weights are balance data.
- **Absence is honest.** A family with no records
  contributes nothing — the engine does not treat
  an unrecorded childhood as a neglected one
  (neglect is a *fact* — family 2's recorded
  events — not a gap in the paperwork). Wild
  individuals' families are the encounter-generated
  equivalents (#243/#214): thinner, and the read
  says so by reading thinner, not by padding.
- **Adding an input is a proposal into this file.**
  A system that believes its facts should shape
  drift writes the proposal here, with its
  recording discipline cited — it does not start
  feeding #337 on its own authority. The set is
  closed the way #164's task kinds and #149's
  screen classes are closed: by a list, in a home.
