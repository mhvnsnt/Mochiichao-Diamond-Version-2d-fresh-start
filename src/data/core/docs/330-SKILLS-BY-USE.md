# #330 — SKILLS BY USE → the Ranger gets better at what the Ranger actually does

**The gap this fills.** Everything in the programme
grows the *Mochi* — levels (#178), training values
(#179), care (#28), bonds (#180). The Ranger grows
only by verdicts: trials passed (#133), Fragments
held (#98/#100), honours stamped (#104). Nothing
records the quiet truth every open-world RPG runs
on, which Skyrim states as a design law: *you get
better at what you repeatedly do.* This spec pulls
that grammar in for the Ranger — and fences it,
hard, as a **soft layer**: proficiency flavours the
Ranger's life in the world and gates nothing in it.

**What was studied.** Skyrim's skill growth as
publicly documented (skills rise by use, contribute
to level, unlock perks), read against this
programme's own derivation law: anything countable
is derived at read time, never stored (#14/#96).
Skyrim stores levels; we derive bands. The kit
taken is the *shape* (use → growth → recognition),
not the numbers.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/ranger-proficiency/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Proficiency is a read, never a record.** Per
  Ranger, per family, a band worked out at read
  time from use-facts the world already keeps —
  there is no proficiency field on #98's licence
  record (identity only, its standing law) and none
  anywhere else. Bands are words, not numbers
  (the #201 reputation discipline: classes a person
  can be described by, thresholds as data).
- **Families (closed at build; each is a family of
  facts that already exists):**
  - `tracking` — #89's tracking work: follows
    completed, individuals found by sign.
  - `angling` — #111's angling: sessions, catches
    by rod and patience.
  - `care` — #28/#65's care acts and #193's
    daycare trust: feeding, tending, boarding
    done well over time.
  - `fieldcraft` — #134's camps made, #150's kit
    used, #125's roads learned by walking them.
  - `handling` — #38's captures and #164's
    assists/#245's rescues: Mochi met, helped,
    and kept without incident.
  A family with no facts reads at the floor band.
  No family may be founded on facts a system
  doesn't already write (a proficiency that needs
  a new counter is a proposal into that system's
  home, not a row here).
- **What proficiency may touch (the whole list):**
  - **#301 dialogue flavour** — options offered in
    the voice of what you're known to do (a tracker
    is offered tracker talk; a known carer is asked
    about the Garden). Options are derived from
    facts in #301 already; proficiency is one more
    fact family it may read.
  - **#304 barter margins** — at the edges only: a
    known hand is quoted the known-hand price
    within #304's own bands. (Cited by commissioned
    subject — List 31 is mid-build in parallel; see
    STAGING's register.)
  - **#93 NPC respect** — the world's small change:
    greetings, trust with small tasks (#164's board
    may *show* work suited to your hands — display,
    never gating; the board's own rules decide who
    may take what).
  - **The licence card's rendering** — #238's card
    borrows standings live; a proficiency line is
    one more borrowed rendering (a card may *say*
    "angler" the way it says a Fragment count).
- **What proficiency may never touch (the fence):**
  story gates, capture math (#38), trial verdicts
  (#133 tests what it tests — proficiency is never
  a substitute and never a prerequisite), battle
  anything (#17 reads no Ranger facts at all), and
  #100's gauntlet. A proficiency that locked a door
  would make it a second licence; the world already
  has one, and it is #98's.

## Wiring (proposed)

A derivation spec in the licence family's home
(beside #98/#238): the read, the band thresholds as
data, the consumer list above. No schema gains a
field. Reads: #89, #111, #28/#65, #193, #134, #150,
#125, #38, #164, #245 facts. Writes: nothing.

## Open slots (nothing invented)

Band names and thresholds per family (data — the
words a card may print are #23 text content) ·
whether the show ever names a proficiency (show
casting colour, not a system fact).
