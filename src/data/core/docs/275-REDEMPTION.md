# #275 — REDEMPTION → the ladder is climbed one deed at a time, and it can always be climbed

**The doctrine, first.** In this system, redemption
is **always possible and never instant**. RDR2's
honor can be bought back with good deeds but the
story remembers; FNV's factions can be courted back
from hatred only so far, and never to innocence
(#270's hated floor). Both bases agree with the
owner's world: the past is permanent (#268's record
law) and the future is open. A thief can become,
deed by deed, a Ranger a town trusts with its
strays — while their oldest Mochi's record still
shows, in sequence, exactly where it came from.
Redemption here is not erasure. It is outliving.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/redemption/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The roads back (all of them are deeds).**
  Redemption has no menu and no payment window; it
  is #269's table read upward:
  - **Restitution — the strongest road.** Returning
    a stolen Mochi to its true keeper (+8, the
    table's largest good deed) plus any restitution
    payment (+3) — and, on #270's side, restitution
    is the **only act that directly repairs a
    group's infamy**: a witnessed return credits the
    victim's groups with a repair entry (read-time
    weighting against that group's infamy from that
    deed — the group saw the apology happen; it
    counts differently from a stranger's good deed).
  - **Service.** #164's service-class postings (+1
    each, under #269's daily credit cap — the cap is
    what makes the climb a climb: four good points
    a day is the fastest the soul moves, whatever
    the player's intentions).
  - **The quiet roads.** Rescues completed (#245,
    +2), strays reunited (#220, +3), gifts (#217,
    +1), donations (+1) — the ordinary good of the
    world, credited at its ordinary rates. There is
    no redemption *bonus*: the system refuses to pay
    extra for goodness performed by the guilty,
    because the cap and the table are the same for
    everyone — that sameness is what makes a climbed
    ladder mean anything.
  - **Answering the law.** Presenting to face a
    #230 finding, completing a supervision term
    cleanly, and clearing wanted by process (#272)
    are reputation-facing acts: they do not move
    morale (the soul is not the court) but they
    close the law's files, and closed files stop
    generating new infamy reports.
- **Decay — time helps, slowly, and only the
  reputation side.** Morale never decays (#272's
  law). Infamy *weight* decays: for each full
  in-game week in which a group witnesses no new bad
  deed from the Ranger, that group's infamy read is
  weighted down by a step (data rate; drafted: one
  tally point per clean week, floor at the group's
  **hated floor** — a group that has Hated you keeps
  a permanent residue, #270). Fame decays at half
  that rate (being forgotten is also a fate). Decay
  is a read-time weighting over the ledger — the
  deeds remain, auditable, forever; what fades is
  how loudly the town tells them.
- **What redemption can never do.** It cannot edit
  a record (#274's law). It cannot un-Hate a group
  to Unmarked (#270). It cannot restore a stolen
  Mochi's history to a clean page, buy back a
  wanted level *instead of* answering it (bounties
  are answered by restitution and process; paying a
  fee to vanish a finding is a fence service that
  does not exist), or shortcut the daily cap. And
  it cannot be finished: there is no state named
  *redeemed* in any record — only a Ranger whose
  recent deeds are good, standing wherever the
  ladder now reads. The show's version of that
  sentence is #276's whole point.
- **The Syndicate door closes behind you.** Leaving
  the dark is the mirror of entering it: fence gates
  (#274) read current bands, so a climbing thief
  loses the underworld's welcome exactly as they
  gain the towns' — and the Syndicate's own #270
  track accrues *infamy* (their word: betrayal's
  tally) for restitution deeds done against its
  interests. Redemption is not costless in both
  directions; choosing the light is also a choice
  about who now hates you. That symmetry is the
  system working as designed.

## Show lane

Redemption is the long arc the whole list exists
to make tellable — and the owner has already named
its biggest use: a later generation's thief anti-
hero lead, whose seasons-long climb (and slides)
run on this engine. #276 carries the mirror and
the long game.
