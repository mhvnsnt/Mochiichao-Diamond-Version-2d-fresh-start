# #341 — STAT-SHAPED EMPHASIS → the most-raised capability is the one the body advertises

**What was studied.** The documented Chao type
grammar: a Chao raised on run-drives becomes a
**Run-type** — its grown form leans into the
stat that raised it (sleeker lines, the runner's
build), a Power-raised one a Power-type (broader,
heavier), and the type is *read off the body* at
the growth moment, not off a menu. Combined with
SA's grade system (a born letter grade per stat —
talent kept separate from training), the record
keeps three things apart that lesser systems blur:
**what it was born to, what it was fed, and what
it became.** Mochiichao already keeps the first
two apart (#80 variance = born; #293 grades =
checkup facts about care axes). This spec is the
third: the emphasis the raised life resolves into.

**The gap this fills.** #337's proportion channel
needs a vocabulary — *emphasis toward what?* —
and #293's care axes need a visible sum that is
not a grade. This spec founds **emphasis**: a
derived class naming the capability a life has
been built around, spoken in presentation only.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/stat-emphasis/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The emphasis read.** Derived (#337's family of
  reads — stored nowhere) from two input patterns,
  kept separate in the derivation and never summed
  into one score:
  - **Care emphasis** — the dominant care axis in
    the individual's #28/#293 history (the axis
    its feeding, gifts, and lessons kept feeding):
    the Garden-raised shape of the life.
  - **Work emphasis** — the dominant capability in
    its activity history (#338 family 4: the
    battles it fought and how, the sports it ran,
    the field life it lived): the world-raised
    shape.
  Where the two agree, the emphasis is strong;
  where they conflict, the read reports the
  stronger pattern and the conflict itself is a
  casting fact (#346: a Garden-soft body on a
  battle-hard life is a *look*, and directors
  should know it is derived, not designed).
- **The class vocabulary (shape only).** A small
  closed set of emphasis classes — swift-built,
  sturdy-built, soaring, grounded, bright, gentle,
  fierce is the *shape* (a handful of readable
  builds, one per capability family the axes and
  activities actually distinguish). The final list
  and every per-family rendering (what "sturdy"
  means on a plant body vs a beast body) are
  **art-lane data beside the sheets**; the classes
  land as a vocabulary slot, the read lands
  complete. An individual below the pattern
  threshold reads **unemphasised** — the plain
  sheet — and unemphasised is the common case for
  young and lightly-raised lives.
- **Where it speaks.** #337's proportion-emphasis
  channel, and only there (with #152's behaviour
  selection as a secondary read: a swift-built
  individual idles like one). Emphasis settles at
  growth moments with the rest of drift (#337's
  settling), so a stage's look is the emphasis of
  the life that reached it.
- **THE LINE, restated as this spec's own law.**
  Emphasis is how a life **shows**, never what it
  **scores**. Battle stats remain species base +
  #80 variance + #39 effort + #178 growth, earned
  in battle and training, read by #17 — a
  sturdy-built Mochi is not one point harder to
  hurt, and a swift-built one is not one point
  faster, unless its battle facts say so through
  the battle systems. The enforcement is #293's:
  **the emphasis derivation's output schema has no
  field capable of naming a battle stat**, and the
  fragment beside this spec is built that way on
  purpose. Care-sport performance is #294's own
  business (care axes, honestly) — emphasis does
  not secretly enter it either: a race reads axes,
  not looks.
- **Born vs raised, kept apart on purpose.** #80's
  variance (born, numeric, invisible) and #293's
  grades (care talent, checkup facts) are NOT
  inputs to emphasis: a born-swift individual
  raised gentle reads **gentle-emphasised with its
  variance intact underneath**, and the two facts
  never merge into a "true nature" score. The
  Chao record's three-way separation is the kit;
  blurring it is the known failure mode.

## Show lane

Casting reads emphasis as the silhouette's
adjective (#346): "the sturdy one," "the sleek
one" — the way a returning audience member tells
two of a species apart in a crowd scene without
a line of dialogue spent on it.
