# #302 — LAW ESCALATION → the law answers in proportion, and it remembers where you were

**The floor this grows.** List 28 founded the
witness split: a deed moves morale whether or not
anyone saw (#269), and only the seen deed travels —
witness, report, wanted (#272 owns that flow, and it
is cited here by commissioned subject: List 28's
272–276 are still building in this workspace, the
List 29 ↔ 28 precedent). #230 founded the licence's
answer: graduated findings (warning / supervised /
suspended / cleared), append-only, and discipline
never confiscates a Mochi. What neither owns is the
*field behaviour between the crime and the court* —
the GTA / Red Dead escalation the owner's essence
statement points at: the law as something that
arrives in stages, looks for you where it last saw
you, and cools if you lie low. This spec is that
ladder, as world behaviour, on #272's flow.

**What was studied.** Wanted-system escalation in
the named kin, as publicly documented: GTA's star
ladder (response force scales with the level, and
the search is centred where you were seen), Red
Dead's fuller model (witnesses must *reach* the law
for a report to exist; a bounty prices the pursuit;
paying or serving ends it — and #272's own research
floor in List 28's RESEARCH-SOURCES.md backs the
witness/evidence split this ladder stands on). The
kit taken: escalation is a *ladder of response*, not
a bigger punishment number; the search has a
geography and a memory; and heat is a perishable
thing. No thresholds are copied — the ladder's shape
is the kit, its values are our data.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/law-escalation/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The ladder (closed rungs, per incident).** An
  active wanted incident (#272's record) carries a
  rung, derived from the witnessed deed's class and
  the Ranger's history (#269's ledger read as the
  law's memory of pattern — mitigation and aggravation,
  per #269):
  1. `reported` — a report exists; local people know.
     No pursuit: the cost is #270's (the group's
     tallies heard) and a record that waits.
  2. `sought` — the place's own responders (#93 seats
     them: the town's law-minded Rangers, a dojo's
     wardens) will challenge on sight — a #210-class
     meeting with the law's terms, not an ambush: the
     Ranger may answer, yield, or run, and each writes
     its own facts.
  3. `pursued` — responders actively search: they work
     from the **last-seen place** (#7 area + beat of
     the last witness/report fact) outward through
     #297's terrain, on #93-class movement — pursuit
     has a geography the player can learn, outrun
     honestly, or wait out.
  4. `hunt` — the region answers with its best: an
     elite response (authored Rangers of #37/#100
     class) joins, and the incident is heard of
     beyond its home group (#270's neighbouring groups
     receive the report). Reserved for the ladder's
     gravest witnessed deeds — a snag seen, a fence
     raid's violence — as data on the deed class, not
     on a points total.
  Which rung a fresh incident *starts* on is data:
  the deed class's base rung, tempered by the
  ledger's history (#271's band effects name wanted
  tempering, and this is the machinery that honours
  it). Rungs de-escalate only through the decay and
  resolution rules below — never by leaving the area
  (leaving moves the *search*, not the rung).
- **Regional ceilings are world facts.** The ladder
  is climbed only as high as the place can answer:
  escalation data lives per region/area beside #93's
  seats — a settled region seats the full ladder; a
  frontier town may cap at `sought` because there is
  nobody to pursue with; a place with no law presence
  at all caps at `reported` (the deed is *known*, and
  that is all the world there can do — it still costs
  #270 in full). Where a ceiling holds the ladder
  down, that is the place's authored truth, not a
  difficulty setting (#70's law: no hidden dials).
  The Dark Forest's depths seat no responders — the
  register says so as data and draws no consequence
  from it here.
- **Decay — lying low works, slowly, and only down
  to the record.** Unwitnessed time at liberty decays
  the *active* rung (clean days with no new report —
  the same clean-conduct clock #275's redemption
  keeps), one rung at a time, never below `reported`
  while the incident's facts stand unresolved. Decay
  cools the pursuit; it does not forgive the deed:
  resolution is #275's work (surrender, restitution,
  service) or #230's (the licence's findings), and a
  decayed-to-quiet incident can be re-raised by a
  fresh witness fact at any time — the world's memory
  is the ledger, and the ledger does not forget.
- **Yielding is a designed verb.** At any rung, the
  Ranger can stop running: answer a challenge, walk
  into the group's seat, surrender the disputed
  thing. Yielding ends the active incident on the
  spot (the search stands down — pursuit machinery
  has no target anymore) and routes the facts to
  #230's findings and #275's roads. What yielding
  costs is what those systems say it costs — never
  a Mochi (#230's law is quoted into this spec so it
  cannot drift: **discipline never confiscates a
  Mochi** — and neither does surrender; a *stolen*
  Mochi's return is restitution, which is #273/#275's
  transaction, not a confiscation).
