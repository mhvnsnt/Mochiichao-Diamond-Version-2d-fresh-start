## Distilled canon rules (these do not replace the verbatim words above)

1. REPRESENTATION IS APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE. Non-binary, androgynous,
   gay, lesbian, asexual, disabled, prosthetic, wheelchair, amputated,
   skin-disorder, mental-disorder, and other different situations and
   disabilities are all in scope. Nothing in this list is a single
   token character by itself.
2. COOL, NOT LAME GENERIC. Like JoJo, Bleach, and Naruto: cool first,
   trait second. A character is a fighter, a trainer, a person with a
   style, a team, and a history first. The trait is part of that, never
   the whole character, never a lecture.
3. TWO TRACKS ONLY. Either a really generic background character who
   just exists in the world (walks, battles, shops, no story about the
   trait), or a really cool named character where the trait is built
   into design and fighting style. No middle track of a named
   character whose only point is the trait.
4. NEVER DIAGNOSED IN DIALOGUE, ON SCREEN, EVER. No matter whether
   the connotation is good or bad, no matter how it is written into
   the story, the trait is never named, explained, or diagnosed
   straight up in spoken dialogue. This is the standing rule for
   every representation character.
5. WHERE REPRESENTATION LIVES INSTEAD. All in: visual identity and
   character design, team, dialogue style (how they talk, not what
   they are labelled as), and relationships. A team or a design may
   hint at symptoms or at a lived experience. That is it.
6. VITILIGO IS THE TEST CASE. A character just has vitiligo in the
   design. Nobody says the word on screen. It does not have to come
   up. It stands out visually, and it is easy to digest because the
   player just sees a person.
7. ALL LEVELS, ALL DIRECTIONS. This rule covers positive
   representation that brings people together, and representation
   that shows separation, isolation, or negative effects, and
   everything in between. The direction changes how it is shown, it
   never changes rule 4: still never diagnosed in dialogue.
8. NO SINGLE REPRESENTATIVE. Never one token per trait. If a trait
   appears, it appears in more than one character, in different jobs,
   with different teams, so no single person has to represent everyone.
9. NO STAT PENALTY, NO CURE AS REWARD. A prosthetic in the Mainframe
   is tech. A limitation becomes a fighting style, like Rock Lee in
   Naruto becoming a taijutsu fighter, not a pity case and not
   something fixed as a reward.
10. POKEMON WAY, IN OUR FILES. In FireRed, HeartGold, and Black 2,
    representation is implicit: trainer class, role, team, and
    strength, not a label speech. Skin tones only arrive later, in
    Gen 6 X/Y presets, and Sun/Moon asks which preset looks most
    like you instead of asking gender. Mochiichao follows that:
    Mom follows the player pick (A Black, B East Asian, C Latina,
    D White), no separate parents-race selector. Adding more races
    means adding more player presets, with Mom following the preset,
    not a new parents menu — and only presets Mars names.
11. GET IT WRONG AND IT GETS CALLED OUT. Mars's warning, kept as a
    working rule: if representation is done wrong, people will get
    heated and call it all out. That is why every character under
    this file is show-first to Mars, and why rules 3, 4, 8, and 9
    are not optional.

## What this file does NOT do

- It does not create a vitiligo character, a wheelchair character,
  a non-binary character, or any other representation character.
  Those are still to be picked, designed, shown, and approved one by one.
- It does not change Castagnoli, Hoshino, Cowboy, or Mom canon, except
  where those are already locked elsewhere. Castagnoli faction is
  locked separately: former Syndicate boss, now on his island.
- It does not invent a diagnosis, a team, a name, or a backstory for
  any future representation character. Those come from Mars, shown first.
