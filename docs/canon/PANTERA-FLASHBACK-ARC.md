## 0. The owner's ruling (t323u, summarised)

- Demonio and Diablo **physically appear only in Region 4 / Season 4**.
- Before that they're foreshadowed as **cameos or figures shrouded in darkness
  in the background**, never part of the foreground of the scene that's happening.
- **Season 2 (Region 2):** Pantera's **flashbacks** put them front and center:
  the Diablo vs Demonio rivalry, how Pantera saw it or was involved, and his
  fights with them.
- **Device:** Pantera speaks in **big-cat growls**, **subtitles** tell his
  story, and the scene **fades into the flashback**, which becomes the foreground.
- Related owner rulings (2026-10-29): Pantera appears in R2 and his story
  foreshadows R4. R4 feels very Western Heart, shows who has full control,
  and is where the Wizard Gang comes in. R5 is Gang-controlled with Bannon as
  champion. Everyone with a Mochiichao is a Ranger.

## 2. The device: growls, subtitles, fade (rules)

1. **OWNER:** Pantera only growls. The subtitles carry his story, then the image fades into the flashback, which becomes the foreground.
2. **CANON-consistent:** this is the "subtitles at important moments" exception. Every Pantera flashback *is* an important moment by definition. Other creatures keep cries-only.
3. **PROPOSAL, sequence:** (a) present scene, Pantera growls and the first 1–2 subtitle lines appear; (b) a slow dissolve starts while the subtitles keep running; (c) full flashback, Pantera's growl track drops out and the flashback's own sound takes over, with subtitles as narration; (d) return through the same dissolve and end on a present-day growl with no subtitle.
4. **PROPOSAL, look:** flashback scenes use Western Heart's palette and staging (the trial's arenas, the five lands), so they read as *another world* inside the Mochiichao show. That is the same palette R4 later turns into a place (§5).
5. **PROPOSAL, carry over the WH caption gag:** subtitles occasionally misspell names, as in the WH canon (Zui → "Zooey"). Use it sparingly. Possible running gag: the caption fumbles "Demonio" but never "Diablo", because Pantera is wary of him.
6. **Hard rule (OWNER):** in flashbacks Demonio and Diablo are the foreground. In present-day scenes before S4 they're **never** foreground: never in focus, never interacting, never named on screen except in Pantera's subtitles.
7. **PROPOSAL:** who hears it. Present-day Rangers (the player's cast) see the subtitles as the *audience's* access, not theirs. In-world they only hear growls. That keeps the cries-only law intact.
