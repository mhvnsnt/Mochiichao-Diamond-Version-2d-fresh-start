## What the class is

> **OWNER-GIVEN (2026-10-29):** "Pantera is a creature by canon AND
> anthropomorphic like a human — a human-type PERSON also seen as a
> Mochi; he cannot communicate in human words. This hybrid situation
> is in the canon with Diablo, Demonio and others: boundary figures,
> persons and creature-class at once, not feeble like Rangers."

A **Mochi Ranger** (the owner's recalled name for the class;
spelling as he gave it) is a **person who is also creature-class**:
a person in mind, agency, and standing — whose body (or second
form) is a Mochiichao's. The world files them as creatures. The
people who know them treat them as people. Both readings are true
at once; that doubleness is the class, and it is a story engine,
not a filing error.

- **DIABLO — Devil Soul vessel** (roster §2.1: "an intruder, not
  one of the nine"). His is the person-form of the class: he holds
  a man's shape and a man's speech, and his cruelty is a person's
  cruelty — which is exactly what makes it worse than any wild
  creature's.
- **DEMONIO — Demon Soul, vessel of RIN** (roster §2.1; role
  brief, owner-ruled 2026-10-27). A person possessed: in human form
  he is a man who speaks and bows; the demon form that surfaces is
  creature-shaped, and the world mistakes it for a Mochi and
  battles him as one (Season 4 dojo seat). One person, two filings.
- **The wider vessel set** (roster §2.1, The Seven / the Nine):
  the class's extended family. Their individual landings vary by

  the class describes what they *are*; the roster decides where
  each one *sits*. Do not flatten them into one game treatment.
- **The mirror shape — MYTHAR:** a creature who wears a human form
  (roster/canon). The class's reverse: where a Mochi Ranger is a
  person filed as a creature, Mythar is a creature filed as a
  person. Same boundary, opposite direction. (Structural kinship
  only — canon draws no plot link, and neither does this doc.)

## The rules of the class

1. **Communication — no human words in creature form.** A member in
   a creature body speaks the creature register only: cries,
   sounds, gesture, expression. Written basis: the Voice Law
   (creatures speak in cries and sounds only) and the flavour-law
   guardrail — *"no speech (cries only — Digimon talk, Mochi
   don't)"* (REGIONAL-FLAVOR-LAW.md). Pantera never speaks in any
   scene, in any medium; other people render his meaning aloud, and
   the rendering is marked as theirs. A member holding a true human
   form (Diablo; Demonio in human form) speaks in that form only.
2. **Strength — creature-class, by anatomy.** Their bodies are
   creature bodies: creature-class strength, durability, and
   senses. A Mochi Ranger can grapple a wild Strikechamp-class
   creature hand-to-hand and live (Pantera, beats M5) — not as a
   human exception, but because that is what his body is. **They
   are not feeble like Rangers.** The Ranger-weakness ruling
   (2026-10-29 ~21:21 CDT) binds humans; it never bound this class.
3. **How the world treats them — filed as creatures.** Strangers,
   crowds, and world systems read them as Mochi: they are battled
   as creatures where canon seats it (Demonio's S4 gimmick), filed
   as species in regional records (Pantera, Region 2), and their
   personhood is recognised by those who know them — keepers,
   colleagues, rivals. The misreading is never a mistake the canon
   corrects globally; it is corrected person by person, which is
   where the stories live.
4. **Violence doctrine.** As persons, class members carry the
   human-violence doctrine's exception shapes by nature (see
   `human-violence-beats/THE-RULE.md` as amended 2026-10-29):
   hybrid strength (shape 1) and vessel/possession (shape 2) are
   this class's two faces. Their restraint, when they show it, is
   a person's choice — Pantera's holds-never-strikes is character,
   not anatomy.

## Game treatment (for the wiring lanes)

- **Not Rangers.** No licence, no Ranger systems read them; the
  Ranger-weakness rules do not apply.
- **Not wild species rows.** They never appear in wild encounter
  tables and are never ordinary captures. They are **individual
  records** (gap-fill S7, the individual record) carrying a person
  flag — one being, one record, wherever they appear.
- **Battled as creatures where canon seats it** (Demonio S4):
  those battles run on the creature battle path; capture outcomes
  follow the closed-heart rule (game doc #287 CLOSED-HEARTS) — a
  person is not caught. *This clause is derived from canon pieces
  above for the executing lanes; it invents no new ruling — if the
  owner rules otherwise, this clause is the one that changes.*
- **Dialogue systems** must branch on form: creature-form members
  get cry/gesture lines only; any rendered meaning is spoken by a
  human present, attributed to the human.
