**OWNER:**

He occupies a party slot as both trainer and battler. Design notes (PROPOSAL): he counts as one of the six slots; while he is in, losing him ends the battle (he is the Ranger); ~~the mechanic is unique to him unless the owner extends it~~ [SUPERSEDED t330u: the Chao-Ranger mechanic]. ## 0d. Owner ruling, 2026-10-29 (t330u, renamed t331u): the "Chao-Ranger" mechanic

**OWNER:** Sun Rā's self-swap (t328u) is generalized into the **Chao-Ranger** mechanic.
**Name (OWNER t331u):** always "Chao-Ranger". The short form "Mon" is never used for it (Mochiichao is not Pokémon).
- **Pantera, Yukihyō, Sun Rā and Demonio** are each **both a Mochiichao and a Ranger**. Each can **swap himself into battle as one of his own party**.
- The mechanic **unlocks in Region 2**, in both the game world and the story. (Pantera's R2 arrival is the natural introduction: PROPOSAL.)
- **Diablo can do it too**, but it is **held back for his later boss matches, Elite Four and endgame narrative after Region 2 / Season 2**.

**Effects on earlier docs:**
- The t328u "self-swap unique to Sun Rā" note is **superseded**. It is the Chao-Ranger mechanic.
- **Demonio's "human or Mochiichao?" question is resolved: both** (a Mochiichao and a Ranger). Whether his "cursed human" backstory comes first is still open.
- Diablo was ruled a human Ranger (t325u) and is now also Chao-Ranger-capable (shown late). **To confirm:** does that make Diablo a Mochiichao too, or a human with a demon form that battles?
- "Elite Four" is the owner's word: Diablo appears in Elite Four battles. That reopens the Elite seat dropped in t325u, as a battle appearance alongside his demon-king antagonist role.

**Game-design notes (PROPOSAL, needs owner sign-off):**
- A Chao-Ranger takes one party slot.
- If the Chao-Ranger is knocked out while battling, the battle ends.
- Swapping uses a turn.
- Each Chao-Ranger has a species entry in the dex and a trainer card.
- The player cannot do it unless the owner later says so.

## 1. Region 4 = the Western Heart region

**CANON (Mochiichao):** R4 is the Rift Region, "the oldest story". The Syndicate
is at its most organised there, calling itself a research institute and trying
to open the Umbral Rift. The Celestic-shape lore beat says "the Mainframe is older
than any region, and the Rift is a scar in it", and the post-game Rift dungeon
opens the way to R5 (`docs/world-builder/mochiichao-five-region-canon.md` § Region 4).
The R4 flavour is the full mix plus yokai/oni, spirits and demons
(`show/production/REGIONAL-FLAVOR-LAW.md` §0).

**CANON (WH):** the Western Heart is the world of the trial inside Tong-Nou, the
Purifier: five lands (Wood/Fire/Earth/Metal/Water), the Phantom Marketplace under
the Eyeball Mountains, and the Octagonal Shrine. Its call is "Fight. Die. Return.
Claim the forces. Forge the katana. Become whole." (WH `CANON.md` §1, §5).

**PROPOSAL: how R4 feels Western Heart**
- **OWNER (t326u):** the **Rift is the scar Diablo tore** (WH: "Whatever tore the membrane the first time, he followed the tear"). R4's land sits over a buried Western Heart.
- **OWNER (t326u):** R4's dojo circuit echoes **the Ladder**: dojos follow the WH generating cycle Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water. The **Phantom Marketplace** is a hidden trading town (the Trader, nameless, CANON). The final ascent is a shrine.
- The cast is the **WH trial vessels living as Rangers and Mochiichao**, seen in the present day.

**Who controls R4 (owner: R4 "shows who has full control")**
- **PROPOSAL (lead):** **Diablo, the demon king**, holds R4 through fear. The Syndicate's "research institute" works the Rift for him, or tries to use him (Pokémon villain-team shape, with the legendary as their goal). Fits WH: "He believes the trial is a kingdom and kingdoms are taken."
- **PROPOSAL (alternate):** the Rift / Tong-Nou itself controls R4, and Diablo only believes he does. That mirrors the WH Purifier twist.
- **OPEN:** owner to choose. The Wizard Gang entering R4 (OWNER) must not contradict either. PROPOSAL: the Gang enters R4 as the force that will hold R5, so R4's end hands control from Diablo's fear to the Gang's turf.
