# METHODS — the narrative-flow method (standing, reusable by any lane)

*Set down by the narrative-flow lane, 2026-10-29, under the owner's
directive of the same evening. Short on purpose: this is the method,
not the findings (those are GAME-FLOW-MAP.md, SHOW-FLOW-MAP.md,
GAP-FILLS.md).*

## §0 — The origin ruling (owner correction, 2026-10-29 ~22:02 CDT — read first)

**The test is ORIGIN, not RESEMBLANCE.**

His canon — the Show Bible, the Game Bible, and the game's
development material — contains things that read Pokémon-shaped
because he wrote them out of a lifetime of playing Pokémon: his
named examples are the whole Luigi situation and the Mysterious
Figure, and there are others in the same class. Those are **his
originals**. Subconscious influence is authorship.

Therefore, binding on every lane that uses this method:

1. His material is **never** flagged, rewritten, swapped out, or
   "fixed" for resembling Pokémon. Ever. Resemblance is not
   evidence of anything.
2. When his material rhymes with the flow grammar, the correct
   record is **"converges with the grammar independently — his"**
   — an observation in the map, never a defect, never a claim
   that his idea derived from the study. His canon outranks the
   grammar **and predates the comparison**.
3. The rewrite test (§2c) applies **only** to material a lane
   authors as a gap-fill. The question for a fill is "did *we*
   lift this?" The question for his canon is settled by origin,
   and his word on origin is final.

## §1 — What transfers, what never does

From any donor story (Pokémon named; the shape is general):

- **Transfers:** FLOW — structure, cadence, beat grammar,
  escalation shapes, journey architecture, spacing rules, dose
  and restraint rules (how often a sign may fire, how many sizes
  an antagonist gets, when a teacher appears).
- **Never transfers:** plots, episodes, characters, places, text,
  twists — the literal material, in any costume. A donor beat
  with its nouns replaced is still the donor's beat and is a
  failure of the fill, full stop.
- **His layer is the load-bearing one.** A good fill stands on a
  fact of *his* canon that has no donor counterpart (a place only
  he has, a sentence only his characters say, a system only his
  game runs). If a fill's load-bearing fact could be anybody's,
  it isn't finished.

## §2 — The three checks (run on every fill, in order)

a. **Flow check:** name the grammar rule the fill satisfies
   ( GF-/SF- number, from the maps). A fill with no rule is
   decoration; a rule with no fill is a gap that stays on the
   list honestly.
b. **Canon check:** every name, place, species, and fact in the
   fill is either (i) verified in a canon source the fill cites,
   or (ii) flagged as an owner slot in one line, with the fill
   designed to work without it. Invented canon is the one
   unforgivable shortcut.
c. **Swap check (fills only — see §0):** could this fill be
   mistaken for a Pokémon plot with the names swapped? Read it
   as a stranger. If yes — rewrite it from the canon fact up,
   or kill it and leave the gap open. An open gap is a work
   item; a swapped plot is a defect.

## §3 — Working order for future lanes

1. Read the grammar out of *held material* (decomp scripts,
   teardown docs, production research) — cite files, and mark
   any claim the corpus can't ground as DESIGN-GRADE with its
   basis stated.
2. Lay our plan against it. Record matches as convergence
   (§0.2), deliberate differences as canon winning, and only
   true absences as gaps.
3. Fill gaps Region 1 first, season 1 first, in story order —
   the sequenced-build doctrine applies to story exactly as to
   systems: a fill is supply for a sequence being driven to
   playable, not a decoration for a plan.
4. Same-coin, always: a game fill and its show version are one
   beat read two ways. Write both or say plainly why the beat
   lives in one medium only.
5. Dose is grammar: signs fire once, threads escalate in three
   sizes, home-base returns do bookkeeping. A fill that spends
   a scarce beat cheaply fails check (a) even if it is pretty.

## §4 — The one fenced gap

What stopped moving in the Dark Forest is not a gap under this
method. It is the owner's standing open item, fenced in both
bibles, and no fill, hint, sign, or stinger dividend may be used
to answer, imply, or route around it. Lanes name it only to say
they are not filling it.
