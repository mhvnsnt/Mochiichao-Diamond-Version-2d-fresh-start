# Mochiichao — Five-Region Canon Progression

World Builder lane, 2026-10-25. This is the proprietary Mochiichao version.
It keeps the *shape* Mars confirmed — intro → title → menu → new game →
Professor intro → hometown → lab → starter → rival battle → Codex and
capture cubes → first route → first town, then town → route → dojo →
story event → Elite → Champion → Hall of Records → post-game — and it
writes every town, person, dojo, and story beat as original Mochiichao
canon. The scene beats are the template; the dialogue, names, places,
and creatures written for Mochiichao are new, written to hit those beats
in Mochiichao's world. Generic field words (cut, surf, fly, waterfall,
and so on) keep their plain function, under Mochiichao tech names below.

## The one rule for the whole game

One save, one roster, one Codex, five regions played in order. You do not
start over in each region. You finish a region, play its post-game, and a
story link — not a menu teleport — opens the next region. The look stays
the same all the way through: 2.5D, Gen-4-level presentation — following
creature, day and night, animated world, camera tilt and zoom in battle —
even in Region 1, which is built to *feel* like the first, simplest story
in that modern presentation.

Modes share the save throughout: Trainer Mode is the main story,
Mochi Garden is raising and breeding, Creature Mode is playing as the
creature in real time, Arena Mode is the big-presentation battles.
A region is not a separate game mode. It is the next chapter of the
same journey.

## The shared start — every player, once — SWAPPED 2026-10-25

Mars, 2026-10-25: swap the starts. Region 1 now opens with the
truck / move-in start (the Hoenn-shape start), and Region 3 now
opens with the bedroom / Professor / lab start (the LeafGreen-shape
start). The rest of each region's map order, dojos, and story does
not swap — only the opening template swaps. The old shared start
below is now Region 3's chapter opening, adapted in § Region 3.

### Region 1 opening — truck / move-in (new game, once)

1. Intro cinematic — the Mainframe, Mochiichao waking, the world booting up.
2. Title screen.
3. Main Menu — CONTINUE / NEW GAME / ROSTER / SAVE / SETTINGS.
4. New Game — Professor Aeon intro. Aeon introduces the world, the
   creatures, and the journey, in his own voice.
5. "Are you a boy or a girl?" — player character choice, with the varied
   player options from the Human Characters lane.
6. Your name.
7. Rival's name — default Hex. Hex is the rival through the whole game,
   not just Region 1.
8. Truck sequence — you arrive in Sector 0 in a moving truck, as a
   new family moving in. Step off the truck, Mom meets you at the
   truck and walks you inside your new house.
9. House 1F — movers, boxes, Mom tells you to go set the clock.
   House 2F — your new room, set the clock, PC, poster. Come back
   downstairs.
10. Go meet the rival — Hex's house next door, Hex's family, then
    head north out of town.
11. Route 1.0, first steps — Professor Aeon is out studying wild
    creatures and is chased / cornered. Choose a starter from
    Aeon's bag to help him — the Mochii line, the Razorgater line,
    or the Tyrage line. Battle the wild creature, Aeon is safe.
12. Aeon's Lab — Aeon takes you to the lab, gives you the Codex.
13. Back in Sector 0 — Mom is standing in front of your house
    door and gives you Sprint Boots (run toggle, B to run).
    Only then is the road truly open.
14. Leave town. First route in full, first wild battles, first
    captures, first town: Silicon Town.

That is Region 1's opening. It is not a second new game — it is
the one new game, and it replaces the old bedroom / lab opening
for Region 1.

### Region 3 opening — bedroom / Professor / lab (chapter opening)

Region 3's opening, adapted from the old Region 1 start, is in
§ Region 3 below: you wake in the chapter's outpost bedroom,
meet the region's researcher, go to the lab, choose a Region 3
starter from the lab table, fight Hex in a lab-exit battle,
receive a Codex upgrade + Capture Cubes, then leave. Your main
team is not wiped — the Region 3 starter is an additional
partner, and your main team rejoins under the chapter's badge
gate, the same gate used in Region 2. Running / Sprint Boots
are already earned in Region 1 and are not re-given in Region 3.

## Field techs — same functions, Mochiichao names

These are the gates that open the world, region by region. A tech is
taught, not just found, and it stays with you into later regions.

- Data-Cut — cut small data-vines and barriers (Cut function)
- Wave-Ride — cross water (Surf function)
- Sky-Link — fast travel to visited towns (Fly function)
- Heavy-Lift — push heavy blocks (Strength function)
- Light-Pulse — light dark caves (Flash function)
- Cascade-Climb — climb waterfalls (Waterfall function)
- Shatter — break cracked rock (Rock Smash function)
- Deep-Dive — dive under water (Dive function, Regions 3–5)

## REGION 1 — The Mainframe Region (the first story)

*Feel: the first, simplest story. Presentation: full 2.5D, modern.*
*Antagonists: the Hackers, then the Syndicate behind them.*
*Home base you keep returning to: Sector 0, Silicon Town, Neon City.*

1. Sector 0 — TRUCK START (swapped in 2026-10-25): moving truck,
   Mom at the truck, new house, set the clock upstairs, meet Hex,
   Aeon rescue on Route 1.0, starter from Aeon's bag, Aeon Lab,
   Codex, Mom gives Sprint Boots in front of the house door.
   No lab-table starter, no lab-exit Hex battle in Region 1 —
   those beats now live in Region 3's opening.
2. Route 1.0 — first wild creatures, first captures, first trainers,
   now fully open after the Sprint Boots beat.
3. Silicon Town — Dojo 1, Master Lin. Garden hook here: the Mochi Garden
   in MochiiPlex / Silicon Town opens after Dojo 1, so raising, Garden
   Stats, and breeding are part of the story from here on, not a side menu.
4. Route 2.0 — Binary Woods. Tall data-grass, bug and plant creatures,
   the first forest maze.
5. Stoneport City — Dojo 2. A port town, a working harbour, the first
   Hacker cell messing with the docks.
6. Route 3.0 — Cave of Echoes. The first real cave, Light-Pulse gate
   later, fossil and rock creatures.
7. Neon City — Dojo 3. The big bright city, the Data Ferry, the first
   Syndicate sighting in force. Earn Data-Cut here, on the ferry, after
   the ferry story beat.
8. Route 4.0 — Signal Tunnel. The dark tunnel that tests whether you
   actually explored, not just ran through.
9. Pale Town — the Tower. The Syndicate occupying a memorial tower,
   the first time the story stops being "a gang" and starts being
   "someone is trying to rewrite the Mainframe."
10. Market City — Dojo 4. The market, the arcade, the Syndicate
    hideout underneath it. Clear the hideout to open the road onward.
11. Central City — the Syndicate HQ tower. The takeover beat: the tower
    is locked down, you fight floor by floor, you free the city.
    Dojo 5 is in Central City, after the tower.
12. Harbor City — Dojo 6. The preserve / safari area. Earn Wave-Ride
    here, after the preserve story, and the water routes open.
13. Ember Island — Dojo 7. The island lab ruins, the mansion logs that
    explain what the Syndicate is actually after: the Mythic creature
    data at the heart of the Mainframe.
14. Back to Silicon Town — Dojo 8. The Syndicate boss as dojo leader,
    the hardest fight in the region, the badge that opens Victory Route.
15. Victory Route — the Gauntlet. High-level wild creatures, veteran
    trainers, the final test before the Plateau.
16. Mainframe Plateau — Elite Four, then Champion. In Region 1 the
    Champion is Hex, your rival, who got there first. Beat him.
17. Hall of Records — your team is recorded, credits roll. That is the
    Region 1 ending, not the game ending.

**Region 1 post-game — the extra work that unlocks Region 2:**
Seven Data Isles, one by one, Syndicate remnants, altered creatures,
a National Codex upgrade that records creatures from beyond Region 1.
On the last isle, Aeon and Hex find the signal: a second region, a
second Mainframe shard, reachable by the repaired Data Ferry. That
signal, and the ferry ticket you earn for clearing the isles, is the
story link to Region 2. You do not get Region 2 by beating the
Champion alone. You get it by finishing the post-game.

## REGION 2 — The Twin Region (the return story)

*Feel: a new region that remembers the old one.*
*Antagonists: the Syndicate again, deeper, plus the Liberation Front
starting to appear.*
*New hook: three legendary beasts wake in a burned tower.*

1. Arrival by Data Ferry — Dawnfield, the new hometown for this chapter.
   Not a new Professor intro. Aeon hands you over to this region's
   researcher by radio, and you start with the team you finished
   Region 1 with, scaled to the new region's opening levels by the
   story's own gate: the ferry route only lets registered Region 2
   creatures through at first, and your old team rejoins as you earn
   the region's badges. That keeps the challenge real without wiping
   your save.
2. Cherryport — Route 29-shape opening roads, first town, first new
   wild creatures.
3. Violet Town — Dojo 1, the Sprout Tower. The tower puzzle, the
   first legendary-beast sighting.
4. Azalea Town — Dojo 2, Slowpoke Well-shape: the Syndicate draining
   a well / data spring, the first rescue beat of the region.
5. Ilex Forest — the forest maze, the shrine, the second beast sighting.
6. Goldenrod City — Dojo 3, the Radio Tower. The big city, the market,
   the Garden branch, the tower story that will pay off later.
7. Ecruteak City — Dojo 4, the Burned Tower. The three beasts wake
   here. This is the region's mythic spine: you do not catch them
   all here, you start chasing them here.
8. Olivine City — Dojo 5, the Glitter Lighthouse, Wave-Ride gate.
   The lighthouse keeper's story, the sea route opening.
9. Cianwood City — Dojo 6, across the water. The pharmacy / medicine
   beat, the island dojo.
10. Mahogany Town — Dojo 7, the Lake of Rage-shape: a red, enraged
    legendary creature in the lake, the Syndicate hideout underneath
    the town, the hideout raid.
11. Blackthorn City — Dojo 8, the Dragon's Den. The den trial, the
    final badge, the elder's test.
12. Victory Route — the Gauntlet again, harder, with Region 1 veterans
    mixed in.
13. Mainframe Plateau, second ascent — Elite Four, then the Region 2
    Champion. A new champion, not Hex, not Mythar yet.
14. Hall of Records, second entry — credits for this chapter.

**Region 2 post-game — the extra work that unlocks Region 3:**
Region 1 opens again, fully. All 8 Region 1 dojos rematch, stronger,
with new teams. The Radio Tower story resolves. Then Frost Peak:
the final battle at the top, against the First Champion — the
legendary trainer who finished Region 1 before you, waiting in the
snow, silent, with the strongest team in the game so far. Beat that
battle and the expedition signal opens: a sea route, storm-locked,
to a third continent. That is the link to Region 3.

## REGION 3 — The Sea and Land Region (the split story)

*Feel: the widest map — sea routes, diving, a region split by water.*
*Antagonists: two Syndicate factions, Land and Sea, each trying to
wake a different legendary creature.*
*New hook: diving, weather, and a region where the map itself is the puzzle.*

1. Arrival by storm ship — Root Hamlet, then BEDROOM / LAB START
   (swapped in 2026-10-25, the old Region 1 start): you wake in
   the outpost bedroom in Root Hamlet, meet the region's
   researcher, go downstairs, talk to the outpost keeper,
   go to the region lab, choose a Region 3 starter from the
   lab table (an additional partner, your main team is not
   wiped), Hex lab-exit battle with the advantage pick,
   researcher gives you a Codex upgrade + Capture Cubes,
   then leave. Sprint Boots are already earned in Region 1
   and are not re-given here.
2. Oldale Road — opening road: first route, the region's
   starter wilds, now open after the lab beat.
3. Rustboro City — Dojo 1. The stone and rock dojo, the first faction
   sighting.
4. Dewford Town — Dojo 2, across the water. The island dojo, the
   cave, the fighting-type trial.
5. Mauville City — Dojo 3. The electric city, the arena hook:
   Mauveville Arena's sister venue opens here, Arena Mode's first
   full tournament bracket in the story.
6. Lavaridge Town — Dojo 4. The hot-spring town, the volcano road,
   the Land faction's base on the mountain.
7. Petalburg City — Dojo 5. The return to the opening town's dojo,
   now strong enough to test you, the "father figure" dojo beat
   in Mochiichao form: the dojo leader who knew Aeon.
8. Fortree City — Dojo 6. The tree city, the canopy routes, the
   weather institute, the Sea faction's move on the weather.
9. Lilycove City — the port, the department store, the hideout
   under the city, the two factions colliding.
10. Mossdeep City — Dojo 7. The island research city, the space
    centre-shape: the Mainframe satellite link, the double-battle
    dojo.
11. Sootopolis City — Dojo 8. The crater city, under water and
    inside a volcano at once, Deep-Dive gate to reach it.
12. The awakening — both legendaries wake, the weather breaks,
    the region floods and burns at once, and you have to calm
    both, not just beat one. This is the region's climax before
    the League, not after it.
13. Victory Route — sea and cave, the final Gauntlet.
14. League — Elite Four, then the Region 3 Champion.
15. Hall of Records, third entry.

**Region 3 post-game — the extra work that unlocks Region 4:**
The Frontier Arenas: a chain of post-game battle facilities, each
with its own rule set — singles, doubles, rental teams, streaks.
Clear the Frontier and the mountain pass opens: an airship route
north, over the crater range, to a colder, older region. The
Liberation Front, who were background in Region 2, are now openly
moving creatures "for their own good" on that route. That is the
link to Region 4.

## REGION 4 — The Rift Region (the oldest story)

*Feel: the deepest lore — an old region, a rift underneath it, a
story about what the Mainframe was before Mochiichao.*
*Antagonists: the Syndicate at its most organised, calling itself
a research institute, trying to open the Rift.*
*New hook: the Umbral Rift, and creatures that do not belong in
any Codex yet.*

1. Arrival by airship — Twinleaf Hamlet, the small starting town
   at the edge of the snow.
2. Sandgem Town — the researcher's town, the first route, the
   lake trio-shape: three lake guardians, three lakes, one mystery.
3. Jubilife City — the media city, the first city proper, the
   Syndicate institute's public face.
4. Oreburgh City — Dojo 1. The mining city, the coal and rock dojo.
5. Floaroma Town — the flower town, the valley, the first Rift
   crack in the ground.
6. Eterna City — Dojo 2. The forest city, the old statue, the
   institute's first theft.
7. Hearthome City — Dojo 3. The contest city, the Garden and
   contest hall branch, the ghost-type dojo.
8. Veilstone City — Dojo 4. The institute's city, the warehouses,
   the fighting dojo, the meteorite beat.
9. Pastoria City — Dojo 5. The marsh city, the water dojo,
   the Great Marsh preserve.
10. Celestic Town — the old town, the cave painting, the lore
    dump that reframes Regions 1–3: the Mainframe is older
    than any region, and the Rift is a scar in it.
11. Canalave City — Dojo 6. The port, the library, the steel dojo,

12. Snowpoint City — Dojo 7. The snow city, the temple, the ice dojo.

14. Victory Route — mountain, snow, and Rift cracks.
15. League — Elite Four, then the Region 4 Champion.
16. Hall of Records, fourth entry.

**Region 4 post-game — the extra work that unlocks Region 5:**
The Battle Zone island, Stark Mountain-shape: a volcano with a
legendary creature inside it, and the Umbral Rift itself, the
Distortion-shape post-game dungeon — a world that does not obey
normal map rules, gravity and exits shifting as you walk it.
Close the Rift, or at least seal it, and the seal's backlash
opens a portal: a train line, a ship line, and a signal all
pointing the same way, to a fifth continent that the Mainframe
has been hiding. The Liberation Front is already there. That
is the link to Region 5.

## REGION 5 — The New Continent (the newest story)

*Feel: the most recent story, told in the same 2.5D presentation,
with the most modern systems — tournaments, seasons, and a
region built around cities, not just routes.*
*Antagonists: the Liberation Front, in full, plus the researcher
who built their ideology — the Colress-shape figure, a scientist
who wants to "free" creatures by breaking the bond system itself.*
*New hook: the bond itself is the story. Garden, Creature Mode,
and Trainer Mode all feed the same question: what does a
creature want?*

1. Arrival by portal train — Start City, the new capital's edge town.
2. Floccesy Town — Dojo 1. The small town dojo, the first rival
   rematch in this region: Hex, now a dojo leader here, testing
   you before he lets you onward.
3. Virbank City — Dojo 2. The port and studio city, PokeStar-shape:
   the film studio, where you make creature films as a side
   system that feeds Garden fame.
4. Castelia City — Dojo 3. The biggest city in the game, the
   sewers, the market avenue, the Liberation Front's first
   public rally, the researcher appearing in person.
5. Nimbasa City — Dojo 4. The entertainment city, the stadium,
   the amusement park, the electric dojo, the sports-season system.
6. Driftveil City — Dojo 5. The mining port, the Cold Storage-shape
   warehouse, the Frozen legendary plot starting: a legendary
   creature frozen, and the Front trying to use it.
7. Mistralton City — Dojo 6. The airfield city, the cargo plane,
   the flying dojo, the tower where the frozen plot escalates.
8. Icirrus City — Dojo 7. The marsh and ice city, the Dragonspiral
   Tower-shape: the old tower, the dragon legend, the ice dojo.
9. Opelucid City — Dojo 8. The old-and-new city, two halves,
   the dragon dojo leader — the region's dragon master.
10. Victory Route — the mountain road, the final Gauntlet, with
    trainers from all five regions mixed in.
11. League — Elite Four, then the Region 5 Champion.
12. Hall of Records, fifth entry.

**Region 5 post-game — the true ending:**
The Grand Tournament: a world tournament with leaders and
champions from all five regions, bracketed, seasonal.
The Tower and the Treehollow: two post-game towers, one Umbral,
one Bright, each a 100-floor climb.
The Nature Preserve: a protected wild area, no battles for
capture, observation and Garden research only.
Hidden Grottos: small hidden areas on every route, rotating
rare creatures, the completionist's hunt.
Market Avenue: the player-run market street, shops that level
up as you trade.
Bond Link: the memory system that ties your whole journey
together — creatures and people from Regions 1–4 reappearing,
with new dialogue that remembers what you did there.
Then, after the Grand Tournament, the final gate opens:
Mainframe Plateau, final ascent, Champion Mythar.
Mythar is not a region champion. Mythar is the Mythic
champion of the whole Mainframe, the creature-human figure
the Garden lore has been pointing at since Region 1.
Beat Mythar, Hall of Records, final entry, true ending credits.
After that, free play across all five regions, Garden, Arena,
Creature Mode, and the tournament seasons continue.

## How the world builder builds this, region by region

For each region, in order, build in this order — do not skip ahead
to a later region's maps before the earlier region's gates work:

1. Map list, in play order, with exits and gates. One map file per
   town, route, cave, tower, and dojo. Exits carry the tech gate
   that locks them, not just a destination.
2. Wild encounter tables per map, by time of day where the region
   uses it, with the region's new creatures first and older
   creatures as rare returns.
3. Trainer tables per map, in play order, with teams that match
   the dojo badge count at that point — no trainer should be
   unbeatable with the creatures available up to that map.
4. Dojo leaders, in order, with team, type theme, badge name,
   and the tech or road their badge unlocks.
5. Story scripts, beat by beat, in Mochiichao dialogue — each
   beat is a short scene: who is there, what they want, what
   changes after you beat or help them, what gate opens next.
   A script is done when a player who has never seen the
   other games' scripts can follow it from the scene alone.
6. Antagonist arc per region, with a named cell, a hideout map,
   and a defeat scene that changes the world — a road opens,
   a town frees up, a gate tech is earned.
7. Elite Four and Champion teams, Hall of Records entry, and
   the post-game checklist that gates the next region.
8. Playtest gate: can a fresh save walk Region 1 start to
   Hall of Records, then post-game to Region 2 arrival, without
   a softlock, a missing exit, or a trainer that cannot be
   beaten with the available roster? That gate, not a file
   count, is what marks a region done.

## What is NOT in this document, on purpose

- No other game's town names, character names, creature names,
  or dialogue lines. If a builder needs a beat reference, they
  use the beat list above, and write the Mochiichao scene fresh.
- No starter, dojo leader, or champion team is final until its
  creatures exist in the Mochiichao roster with stats. A name
  in this document is a slot, not a stat block.
- No region is "done" because its map list is written. A region
  is done when its gate playtest passes, above.
