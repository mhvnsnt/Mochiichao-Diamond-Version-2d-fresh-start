# Mochiichao Chao Garden Conversion

Study-only conversion, 2026-10-25. Key decision: species evolution stays as-is (Mochii → Tiidebiite → AquariiOS, etc.). Garden evolution is a second layer: a Garden Form (palette + accessories + aura + small stat bonus) on top of the existing 47 species, so the roster never breaks.

## Conversion table

- Garden Stats: Dive, Glide, Sprint, Might, Endurance — 1–99, 10 boxes per level, stored separately from battle stats. Dive = Aqua/Deep, Glide = Aero/Wind/Silk, Sprint = Strike/Beast/Flora, Might = Chrome/Core/Earth, Endurance = all.
- Data Shards (Chaos Drives): Aqua Shard = Dive, Aero Shard = Glide, Sprint Shard = Sprint, Might Shard = Might. Blue Data Shard = small all-stats, Gold Mythic Shard = all-stats + luck, Umbral Shard = random stat + ghost tint. Clean stat training, no cosmetic change. Earned by defeating corrupted data / Hackers.
- Garden Critters (animals): original Mochiichao helpers, not Sega animals. E.g. Tide-Pup (Dive, fins/tail, backstroke), Glider-Moth (Glide, wings, flutter), Sprint-Hare (Sprint, ears/feet, hop), Forge-Bear (Might, arms, chest-pound). Parts in slots Head/Back/Feet/Tail/Ears, persist through Rebirth. Critters do not become roster species.
- Alignment: Bright / Neutral / Umbral, slider -100 to +100. Bright caretakers: Prof. Aeon and Mochiko. Umbral influence: rival Hex visits, Deep Sector garden, Umbral Berries, night training — player does not join the Syndicate. Palette: Bright = white/gold, Umbral = black/purple, Neutral = base colour.
- Data Cocoon → Garden Form I: Dive↔Glide slider, Sprint↔Might slider, ±0.5 threshold, balanced = Normal Form. 20 Critters or 40 Shards locks a type. Dominance: Dive > Sprint = Might > Glide. Form, not a new species.
- Garden Form II: gradual adult change, same system as Chao second evolution. Form I + Form II = palette + accessory + aura layers, not 15 full sprites per species (47 × 15 = 705 sprites is unbuildable). One base sprite per species + palette swap + accessory overlay + aura.
- Mythic Form (Chaos Chao): Data Rebirth ≥2×, Bond >50, all 21 Garden Critter types collected in current life, no Shards after 2nd Rebirth, alignment chosen in 3rd life. Success = glowing Mythic Form, immortal (no death timer), cannot breed, Garden Stats locked. Mythar is the template / first Mythic in canon, but any species can earn a Mythic Form — it does not overwrite Mythar's species entry.
- Data Rebirth: 1 Garden Year = 1 in-game week / ~60 min active garden time, life ≈ 5 years. Bond/Happiness 0–100, >50 = Rebirth Egg (same name, Lv. 1, points ÷10, parts/palette kept, grades kept). ≤50 = grey cocoon warning first: Last-Chance window to feed/pet over threshold. If it still fails, Memorial entry in Mochiioteca + Legacy Egg at base stats.
- Bonding Season / Heart Berry: adults only, Garden Form I+ required. Natural season at Garden Age 2, or Heart Berry (rare Market/farm reward, last bite triggers, one per parent). Flower ring + heart icon. Both in season = guaranteed egg; one in season = may walk away. 1 free egg per life, Heart Berries allow extra eggs.
- Grades & inheritance: grades E→S per Garden Stat, plus Bond, favourite Data-Fruit, personality sliders (Kind / Bold / Curious). Child inherits 50% genes from each parent, higher grade wins visible grade 70% when parents differ, recessive grades can reappear in grandchildren. Two-tone = primary palette from one parent, accent from other. Glitch-Shine variant = dominant gene. Pre-evolution training in dominant stat can bump that grade one step.
- Mainframe Gardens: Neutral = Sector 0 Plaza, Bright = Aeon Spire Garden, Umbral = Deep Sector Garden. 8 creatures per garden, overflow to MochiiPlex storage. Visiting a garden shifts alignment slightly toward it.
- Mochiko Academy, Silicon Town (Kindergarten): Aeon Clinic = Doctor (stats, grades, personality, favourite fruit, age, rebirths, race/spar records, cures sickness). Classroom = Data Lessons: Dance, Song, Sketch, Rhythm — unlock Garden idle animations and +Bond, not battle power.
- Silicon Town Market (Black Market): rotating stock every in-game day / after Trainer quests. Unlock tiers by Dojo Badges and quest emblems. Sells palette-variant eggs, Data-Fruit seeds, hats/accessories, Heart Berries, Strong Nuts. No roster species sold — only palette eggs of species already in Mochiioteca.
- Garden Races, MochiiPlex Circuit: Dive / Glide / Sprint / Might courses + Grand Circuit. Endurance = pauses/sleep. Medals unlock toys, market tiers, training points. Auto-run with cheering/boost timing.
- Dojo Sparring (Karate): auto-battler in MochiiPlex Dojo. Endurance = HP, Might = damage, Sprint = ring-out speed, Dive/Glide = dodge. Rewards training points and titles, not XP — must not replace Trainer/Creature battles.
- Data-Fruit Farming: 3 trees per garden. Shake → harvest → feed. Regrow 1 in-game day (or ~7 min active time). Garden fruit = Endurance only. Specials: Bright Berry / Umbral Berry (alignment), Strong Nut (2× Endurance), Smart Berry (small all-stats), element fruit (Aqua Melon, Flora Berry, Chrome Nut, etc.). Favourite fruit = 2× Bond. Plant seeds, water, cross-pollinate for rare seeds.

## Breeding rules for the 47 roster

1. Same evolution line → base form of that line (Mochii + Tiidebiite = Mochii egg). Different lines → base form of higher-Bond parent; other parent contributes grades, palette, and one inherited move (egg move). No hybrid species — roster stays 47.
2. Grades: per Garden Stat, picked 50/50 from a parent, higher grade wins visible 70% when parents differ. Hidden recessive grades can reappear in grandchildren.
3. Palette/form: primary from one parent, accent from other = Two-tone. Glitch-Shine dominant. Garden Form is not inherited — child earns its own.
4. Not inherited: battle level, Garden Stat levels, Mythic Form, Rebirth count. Mythic cannot breed.
5. Starter lines are the tutorial: Mochii (Aqua → Dive), Razorgater (Chrome/Strike → Might/Sprint), Tyrage (Earth → Might).

## Garden minute-to-minute (5–15 min session)

Enter garden → check need icons (! hungry, Z sleepy, flower ring = Bonding Season, cocoon timer) → shake 3 trees, pick fruit, feed (favourite = 2× Bond, Strong Nut = Endurance, Bright/Umbral Berries = alignment; don't wake sleepers, don't snatch food) → train with Shards (clean) or Critters (stats + parts) watching Dive↔Glide and Sprint↔Might sliders → play (pet +Bond, toys, training dummy, Data Lesson) → race / spar / breed → leave. Garden creatures live on in-game time only while save is loaded — no real-time death while away, deliberate change from Chao's clock.

## What carries over

- Garden Stat levels → capped battle bonus: +1% per 10 Garden levels per stat, max +9% at Lv. 99, applied to matching battle stat. Garden Form palette/accessories/aura visible in battle.
- Bond hearts → small crit/dodge bonus in Creature Mode, recruit chance in Dungeon recruitment.
- Grades → growth rate in Trainer Mode (S-grade gains Garden Stat points faster, not battle XP faster).
- Mythic Form → unlocks Burst move in Creature Mode, permanent aura.
- Races/Sparring wins, Market unlocks, farm produce (fruit → Trainer Mode healing/crafting items) feed back into main game.
- Does not carry over / does not replace: Garden Races and Dojo Sparring never replace Trainer/Creature battles, Garden Forms never change species ID or evolution line, no Garden item required to finish story.

## Save fields to add (one creature)

gardenStats {dive, glide, sprint, might, endurance} (level + points + grade each), alignment (-100…+100), influence {diveGlide, sprintMight}, bond/happiness (0–100), careMistakes, rebirths, gardenForm {alignment, type, stage}, palette {primary, accent, shine}, accessories [head, back, feet, tail, ears], personality {kind, bold, curious}, favouriteFruit, gardenAge, mythic (bool, locked once true).
