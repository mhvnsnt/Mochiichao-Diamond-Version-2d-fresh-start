# WRITING STANDARD — how Mochiichao scripts get written

Set by Jamar 2026-10-27: write like HIS shows are written, grounded
in the cartoon craft in his repos — not generic filler. This
standard is studied from his own cape-jones EP01 "Immortal Qombat"
shooting script (his approved format), the Wizard Gang voice-pipe
lines, and the CN/Nick production material in the donor repos.

## 1. Jamar's house system (from his scripts — this is the law)
1. **Rules first.** Every script opens with the rules it obeys.
   Every beat traces to the owner's brief or a cited canon doc.
   Anything unknown goes in an OPEN ITEMS list — **never filled in
   silently.**
2. **Canon sources table.** The script names what grounds it
   (game canon docs, model sheets, this bible) before scene one.
3. **Scene format.** Slugline + timing estimate, then blocks:
   **VISUAL:** (specific — named props, exact colours, hex where
   canon gives one) · **ACTION:** (present tense, physical, playable)
   · **DIALOGUE:** · **SUBTITLES:** · **SFX:** · **MUSIC:** (cue ID).
4. **Gags are mechanisms, not quips.** A joke is a thing that
   happens (a cape being competent, a net becoming a hat), described
   plainly and played straight. No banter-padding, no winking.
5. **Silence is a tool.** "DIALOGUE: None. Music only." is a
   complete, respectable scene block. His scripts use it often.
6. **Voice discipline is written down.** Who/what produces every
   sound is specified in the cast table — including what is NEVER
   done (see §3).
7. **Line economy.** His dialogue (Wizard Gang queue): short, dry,
   declarative. "We are adjourning nothing. We haven't started."
   If a line could come from any show, it comes from none — cut it.

## 2. The show's style stack (Jamar, 2026-10-27)
- **Base: Pokémon** — creature-showcase engine, keeper-and-creatures
  bond, garden as home base, species-of-the-week structure.
- **Digimon** — the partner bond has stakes; creatures have inner
  lives and histories; the digital/keeper tech matters.
- **JoJo's Bizarre Adventure** — art-forward presentation: dramatic
  posing, held tension, sound-effect typography as graphics,
  menace played completely straight.
- **Persona 5** — hyper-futuristic Japan: graphic-pop UI, one
  dominant colour per sequence, stylish title cards, menus as
  performance.
- **Japanese lore & culture** — woven into the garden's world
  (Wispkin is a wisp of the night; the Dark Forest is yokai-dark),
  researched and used respectfully and specifically, never as
  wallpaper.
- **Tamagotchi / digicore hyper-pop cute** — the care loop is the
  heart: feeding, checking, little devices, pixel-cute keeper tech.
  Cute is a design system, not a tone accident.

## 3. Voice & subtitle law (decided 2026-10-27)
- Creatures: **pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
  name-saying. Emotion is performance + sound design.
- **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles:** they appear ONLY at
  certain important moments to carry what a creature means, then
  disappear. Model: the Rule 12 subtitle over the Dark Forest.
  Rationing is what makes them land — if everything is subtitled,
  nothing is.
- Humans speak — sparsely, dry, in Jamar's line economy. Mochiko
  interprets for the audience by reaction, not by translating
  every sound ("Ducks don't roll uphill.").

## 4. CN / Nick craft (from the donor-repo material)
- Storyboard clarity: one clear idea per beat; a board artist must
  be able to draw the scene from the VISUAL/ACTION blocks alone.
- Model-sheet discipline: characters stay on-model (show/
  production/model-sheets/); descriptions cite the sheet.
- HB economy: reusable cycles, held poses with intent, levels —
  write scenes the pipeline can actually afford (see PIPELINE.md).

## 5. Anti-slop checklist (run before any script is shown to Jamar)
- [ ] Every beat traces to brief/canon — open items listed, none hidden
- [ ] Every colour/prop that canon specifies is named exactly
- [ ] No line that could belong to any other show
- [ ] At least one "None. Music only." beat where silence works harder
- [ ] Subtitles: only at moments that earn them (count them — few)
- [ ] Gags are mechanisms described plainly, not quips
- [ ] The Pokémon base is visible (bond, showcase, care) AND one
      stack element is doing real work (not decoration)
