# EP04 — "Dojo at Dusk" (PROPOSED title — owner to confirm) — BEAT SHEET v1

Format: ~6 minutes · Cast: Hex + Prof. Aeon (humans, first full
episode), Mochiko, pilot six · Featured species: **Dorowani**
(equal-shine rotation: EP01 Wispkin, EP03 Gabu, EP04 Dorowani) ·
Locations (from parts-catalog, reused): Garden Gate, Garden Green,
Garden Pond (dusk) · Perspective: **creature-led — Mochii is the
protagonist** (continuity: EP02's Choosing; Mochiko is Mochii's
person, Hex is Dorowani's).

Show convention (Jamar, 2026-10-27): creatures do NOT talk in words -
they vocalize - PURE CRIES AND SOUNDS ONLY (no name-saying either).
SUBTITLES appear silent-film style ONLY at important moments to
carry a creature's meaning - see bible/WRITING-STANDARD.md. Humans
(Mochiko, Hex, Prof. Aeon) speak word-dialogue. Meaning comes from
performance, context and reactions.

**Continuity in:** EP01 — Wispkin revealed as night keeper, named
on the board. EP02 — the Choosing: Mochiko chose Mochii; Prof. Aeon
presented the starter lines (Mochii / Razorgater / Tyrage); Hex
issued a challenge. EP03 — first outing; the ring-game ritual;
Gabu featured. EP04 pays off Hex's challenge: the garden's DOJO
SPARRING — its formal play-battle custom.

**6-beat engine (engine slot 1–6, garden-sized):**
1. **Arrival (0:00–1:00)** — The Gate. Hex arrives exactly on time,
   exactly prepared: kit laid out in a line, Dorowani at their side
   in a perfect stance. The mirror of Mochiko's EP01 arrival — same
   gate, opposite keeper. Hex states the challenge formally. Aeon
   accepts on the Garden's behalf and names the custom: dojo
   sparring, at dusk, at the Pond ring.
2. **The oddity (1:00–1:45)** — Hex's exactness meets the Garden's
   looseness. Hex measures the ring to the stride. The keeper board
   is brought out for the stakes — and it has no column for wins.
   Nobody here has ever kept score. Kittember's tail-tip flame
   (attached, canon) leans, curious, toward Dorowani's fin-streaks:
   the Garden's sensor noticing the visitor's grace.
3. **Preparation (1:45–3:00)** — Aeon reads the dojo rules from the
   Manual: stakes are honour and a keeper-board mark, never harm;
   the bout ends by the dojo's own rule (rule text: OPEN ITEM,
   §script §6 — announced by custom, not quoted from canon).
   Warm-ups, two styles side by side: Dorowani drilled and
   graceful, fin-streaks sweeping white on every turn; Mochii's
   warm-up is water play at the Pond's edge — splashing, bouncing,
   entirely unserious. Mochiko watches Mochii and worries quietly.
4. **The bout, first half (3:00–4:15)** — Dusk. The ring under the
   Pond "Stakeout" key, inverted warm. Creature-perspective
   staging: the camera is with Mochii. PUNCH-INs land on PRESENT
   poses (POSING-SYSTEM). Dorowani scores by form — every move a
   finished picture. Mochii scores nothing and loses nothing: it
   keeps slipping out of Dorowani's lines on pond water, playing.
5. **The turn (4:15–5:15)** — Mid-exchange, Dorowani's own
   fin-streak turn carries it onto the wet ring edge. Mochii stops
   — stops the bout — and bounces it back upright instead of
   taking the opening. The one hard grade shift of the episode
   lands here: the light goes warm as the bout stops being a
   contest. Dorowani asks, in the only way creatures ask, to see
   the water play again (SUBTITLE 1). In the crowd, Wispkin takes
   its place at the ring's edge as dusk settles — a sightline is
   made for it, respectfully, without comment. Hex sees that.
   Something in Hex loosens — shown, not said: the clenched hand
   opens. Aeon calls the bout under the dojo rule: A DRAW.
6. **Resolution + tag (5:15–6:00)** — The keeper board gets a new
   kind of mark: not a win — one mark, shared by two names,
   MOCHII and DOROWANI side by side. Then the unthinkable from
   beat 1: Hex stays for dinner. Final beat, held wide: across
   the empty ring, Dorowani and Mochii trade a PRESENT pose —
   Dorowani's form, answered by Mochii's joy — rivals, now
   measured friends (SUBTITLE 2). Tag (mechanism): Gabu, who has
   been refereeing from a too-tall stool all episode, finally
   falls off it — nobody saw it happen; the stool is just empty,
   then the bellow from the pond edge, affronted and fine.
