## 0. THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Word-voices this
   episode: MOCHIKO, HEX, PROF. AEON (all human).
2. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — only at earned
   moments. This episode has exactly **two** (ration max three):
   cuts 20, 27. Both are Dorowani's.
3. **Hex is not a villain.** They are the mirror: prepared, exact,
   and lonely under it. The loneliness is SHOWN (hands, posture,
   what they watch), never stated in dialogue.
4. **The bout is a custom, not a fight.** Stakes: honour and a
   keeper-board mark. Never harm. The draw is called under the
   dojo's own rule — **the rule's exact text is an OPEN ITEM
   (§6.1)** and is NOT written in this script; Aeon names the
   custom and the result, not invented doctrine.
5. **Rule 12 is not used this episode.** It appeared in EP01 at
   full weight; EP04 does not spend it. If the owner wants it
   added, it goes in exact wording only: "Everything in the
   garden belongs to somebody... And everybody belongs to the
   Garden."
6. **Kittember's flame is attached to the tail tip** (owner
   canon) in every description and board.
7. **The Dark Forest is background only** — treeline in wide
   shots, still. No entry, no poses, no stinger (bible §3, §9.1).
8. **One hard grade shift in the whole episode**, spent at
   Scene 5, cut 20 (LIGHTING-SYSTEM ration): the moment the bout
   stops being a contest, the grade goes warm. No other hard
   shifts are available to any other scene.

## 3. THE SCRIPT
### SCENE 1 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — DAY (cuts 01–05)
**VISUAL:** The Gate in brand-blue day. The keeper board: names
from EP01–EP03, WISPKIN — NIGHT KEEPER among them. The path
beyond the gate, sunlit; the far treeline still (background only).
**ACTION:** On the stroke of the hour — exactly on it — Hex
arrives. Kit laid out on the path in a perfect line before they
speak a word. Dorowani at their side in a finished stance, held
like a photograph. The whole garden goes a little quiet, the way
it did not for Mochiko in EP01. Dorowani's fin-streaks sweep
white as it turns its head to take the Garden in.
**DIALOGUE:** HEX: "Hex. We wrote ahead." / "The challenge
stands, if the Garden accepts it." AEON: (arriving, tablet under
one arm) "The Garden accepts. We have a custom for this."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** gate creak, kit set down piece by
piece, Dorowani's low steady croak. **MUSIC:** Cue T1 stems,
formal register (CUE-SHEET.md).

### SCENE 2 — EXT. GARDEN GREEN — DAY (cuts 06–09)
**VISUAL:** The Green. The keeper board carried out and set where
all can see it — the stakes made physical.
**ACTION:** Hex measures the ring-to-be with exact strides.
Sproutle, helping, lays the ring line in petals, approximately —
Hex looks at the approximation for a long moment and, deciding
something, leaves it. Mochiko turns the board: there are names,
jobs, titles of care — and no column anywhere for wins. Aeon
sees her see it. Kittember's tail-tip flame leans, unmistakably,
toward Dorowani each time it passes.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "What does the winner get?" AEON: "A
mark on the board. And the honour of the thing." MOCHIKO:
"There's no column for it." AEON: "There will be, by tonight.
That's rather the point of a custom — it makes room."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** petal line scuffed in grass, board
set down, tail-flame hum (Kittember near Dorowani only).
**MUSIC:** T1 stems thin under preparation.

### SCENE 3 — EXT. GARDEN POND — LATE DAY (cuts 10–13)
**VISUAL:** The Pond. The ring laid at its edge. Light beginning
to lean toward dusk; the Stakeout key warming up.
**ACTION:** Aeon reads the dojo rules from the Manual — the
custom announced plainly: play-battle, honour and a board mark
at stake, never harm; the bout ends by the dojo's own rule
(see §0.4 — rule text open). Warm-ups in split attention:
Dorowani drills — three movements, each ending in a completed
pose, fin-streaks sweeping. Across the ring, Mochii's warm-up is
the pond: in, out, bounce, splash, in again. Hex watches the
wrong warm-up for a moment too long. Mochiko watches Mochii and
says nothing, which for Mochiko is a speech.
**DIALOGUE:** AEON: (the rules, paraphrase of custom — NOT a
canon quotation) "Dojo sparring. The Garden's formal play. You
may win a mark and the honour of the day. You may not win an
injury — there aren't any. The bout ends when the dojo rule
says it ends, and the dojo is older than all of us." HEX:
"Understood." MOCHIKO: (quiet) "He's just playing." AEON:
"Yes. Watch what playing does."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** Manual pages, pond laps, splash
cycle, Dorowani's drill footfalls. **MUSIC:** stems down to a
single line under the rules; pond bed up.

### SCENE 4 — EXT. GARDEN POND RING — DUSK (cuts 14–19)
**VISUAL:** Dusk. The ring under the Pond "Stakeout" key
inverted warm — rim light on water, faces in soft shadow, the
far treeline a still silhouette (background only). Crowd at the
ring's edge: Gabu elevated on a referee's stool, far too tall.
**ACTION:** THE BOUT, first half — staged from Mochii's side of
the ring; the camera lives with the protagonist. Dorowani opens
by form: advance, turn, fin-streak sweep — PUNCH-IN, PRESENT
pose held (POSE: Reveal-form). Mochii answers by leaving: a
bounce off the wet edge, a slide through pond-shallow, gone from
the line Dorowani drew. Twice more Dorowani scores by form —
each PUNCH-IN lands on a completed pose, and each time the
target is somewhere wet, laughing in squeaks. Dorowani's croak
changes: not frustration — interest.
**DIALOGUE:** HEX: (coaching, exact) "Again. On the line."
(then, quieter, to no one) "...It's not on the line. It's never
on the line." AEON: "The dojo allows the pond. The pond is in
the ring. It always was."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** fin-streak whoosh (GAP — cue
sheet), water play, crowd cries low, stool creak (Gabu).
**MUSIC:** investigation pulse from stems, playful register.

### SCENE 5 — EXT. GARDEN POND RING — DUSK, CONTINUOUS (cuts 20–24)
**VISUAL:** Same key, deepening. Wispkin's side of the ring in
soft focus at the edge of frame.
**ACTION:** The exchange peaks: Dorowani's fastest fin-streak
turn carries it onto the wet ring edge — balance going. Mochii
stops. Full stop, mid-bout, in scoring position — and bounces
the other way, into Dorowani's side, and holds it upright with
its whole round body. **THE GRADE GOES WARM — the episode's one
hard shift, spent here (cut 20).** Held beat: two creatures
leaning on each other in warm light, both breathing hard,
Dorowani looking at the pond water on Mochii like it is a
technique it has never been taught. Dorowani dips its head —
once — toward the water, then back to Mochii: ask it again.
At the ring's edge, dusk settling, Wispkin arrives for the
close of the bout: Sproutle shifts, a sightline opens, a place
is simply there for the night keeper, the way a place is there
for family. Hex sees the place made. Hex's hand, clenched on
their own sleeve since Scene 1, opens. Aeon rises and calls
the bout under the dojo rule: A DRAW (rule text §6.1).
**DIALOGUE:** AEON: "The dojo is satisfied. A draw — and a good
one." HEX: "...It stopped. It could have scored, and it
stopped." MOCHIKO: "That's Mochii winning. That's what it looks
like here."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 20 — DOROWANI: *"...Again. With the water."*
**SFX:** the stop (all bout sound cuts for one beat), water
drips, Wispkin's soft wail at the edge, crowd settling.
**MUSIC:** all stems out for the stop; one warm stem back under
the held beat and the call.

### SCENE 6 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — NIGHT (cuts 25–28)
**VISUAL:** The keeper board under gate light. Dinner things
laid out on the Green behind, steam in the night air. The
treeline beyond, dark and still (background only).
**ACTION:** Aeon paints the new mark on the board: not a win
column — ONE mark, and under it two names together, MOCHII and
DOROWANI. Mochiko sets out dinner; there is a pause where Hex
should leave, and Hex — in full view of their own perfectly
packed kit — sits down instead. Dorowani takes the place
beside Mochii without being asked. Final beat, held wide on the
ring left empty behind them: Dorowani rises, crosses to the
ring, and offers a PRESENT pose — its finest form, given, not
scored. Mochii answers with its own: the full-body joy pose,
bounce frozen at the top. Rivals. Measured friends.
TAG: the referee's stool by the pond stands empty. A beat.
From the pond's edge, affronted and entirely unharmed, Gabu's
foghorn bellow.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Dinner's nothing fancy." HEX: "...We
can stay for nothing fancy." (a breath) "Dorowani. Eat." AEON:
"The board learns a new shape tonight. Good. Boards should."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 27 — DOROWANI: *"...Same time tomorrow."*
**SFX:** brush on wood, dinnerware, crickets, the bellow (tag).
**MUSIC:** T1 melody stem, solo, warm — out under the held pose.
