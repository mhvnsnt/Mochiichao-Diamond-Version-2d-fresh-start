## 0. THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words,
   no name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Mochiko is the
   only word-voice this episode.
2. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — only at
   earned moments. This episode has exactly **two**: cuts
   18 and 28 (ration: maximum three).
3. **Perspective is Mochii's** (EP02 §THE SWITCH, locked by
   that episode's choosing beat). Camera at Mochii height;
   Mochiko is its person. No re-switch, no drift back.
4. **The keeper device stays abstract.** Light + icons +
   chime only — no invented industrial design (bible §9.5,
   OPEN ITEM; §6.3).
5. **Kittember's flame is on the tail tip** — attached,
   always (owner canon). A detached flame is a design-gate
   failure, not a shot.
6. **The ring game is a play-ritual, never a capture.** The
   ring is carried and offered; nobody is caught, owned, or
   beaten by it. Bond/trust is the mechanic.
7. **The Forest rules hold in the final beat:** no PRESENT
   poses inside the Forest (this pose happens in the Garden,
   facing it), patterns stilled at the treeline, camera
   never enters, nothing is shown. No hard colour shift
   this episode (REPAINT-PROTOCOL: none filed).
8. **Every beat traces to canon** (§1) or is marked TBD (§6).
   Rule 12 is **not** quoted this episode — it is rationed
   with the subtitles, and EP03's thesis is the care loop,
   not the Rule. (Recorded as a choice, not an omission.)

## 3. THE SCRIPT
### SCENE 1 — EXT. GARDEN GATE / GREEN — MORNING (cuts 01–06)
**VISUAL:** GATE-MORNING "First Bell." The keeper board,
two names on it now. Camera at Mochii height from the first
frame — the Gate is tall, the morning is taller.
**ACTION:** The care loop, run as a duet: the keeper device
does its check-in — a glow, a row of icons, a chime (no
more than that; §0.4) — and Mochii answers each icon with
the thing itself: water carried, berries counted by
bouncing on them, the board read at bounce height.
Dorowani follows with the Manual, tapping each completed
care like a gavel, once each. Gabu presents himself for
inspection and passes at maximum chest. Kittember's
tail-tip flame burns steady and small: all is well, and
the flame is how we know.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Care first. Then the world." /
(to the device) "Icons. It only ever shows me icons."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** device chime (cue sheet),
gavel taps, berry bounces, water pour.
**MUSIC:** Cue T1 stems, morning build (CUE-SHEET.md).

### SCENE 2 — EXT. THE GATE / THE FIRST ROUTE — DAY (cuts 07–13)
**VISUAL:** The Gate held wide. Past it, the First Route:
a path that gives out into tall grass — grass at Mochii
height is a forest of its own, green-gold, moving in the
wind (LIVING-WORLD wind state BREEZE). Route day key:
open sky, no canopy (script §6.5).
**ACTION:** Departure on the held wide — nobody rushes the
threshold. Dorowani taps the Manual twice: the page says
go. Gabu takes escort position like a parade of one. Then
the grass. ACTION BEAT A: Mochii's first full-speed run,
camera running with it (CAMERA-THERE, 1 of 2) — pure joy,
grass whipping past at face height. The grass closes over
it: one held beat, small, the world's noise suddenly big.
Mochiko's voice, unhurried, from off-screen; Mochii's cry
answers; found. Over the grass line, a ZEPHLING crosses —
sky class, unhurried, its own errand — and Mochii does not
chase it. Watches it all the way out of frame. HOLD.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "First rule past the gate: the
path is the path, until it isn't." / (off-screen, calm)
"Mochii. Sound off." 
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** gate swing, grass rush,
run foley (GAP — cue sheet), Zephling cry (distant, once).
**MUSIC:** T1 stems open out on the run; thin to near-
nothing for the lost beat; one stem back for the crossing.

### SCENE 3 — EXT. THE CLEARING — DAY (cuts 14–20)
**VISUAL:** The route ends at a clearing. Woven grass
rings hang on a low branch — the garden's game, kept where
the game is played. STAGE-WIDE: everybody in frame, the
clearing a little theatre.
**ACTION:** The ritual, taught by demonstration, the only
way the garden teaches: Kittember carries a ring at a
walk, offers it up, and Sproutle — standing post — takes
it over one raised arm, slow as a ceremony. Gabu watches.
Gabu understands. Gabu VOLUNTEERS, foghorn bellow, chest
at full parade: he will be the post. Sproutle, solemn,
balances a berry on his head — a handicap he requested by
pointing at it twice. First attempts at speed: Mochii,
too fast, overshoots — a ring bounces off Gabu's shell
and lands in the grass crown-wise on a stone. Nobody
laughs AT. Mochii retrieves it, looks at it, looks at
Gabu. ACTION BEAT B — THE HOLD: Gabu sets. Arms up.
The game swirls around him at full speed — rings
offered, taken, the berry trembling on its perch — and
Gabu does the hardest small thing there is: nothing,
magnificently, at full body, for a held count the board
will show in seconds (HOLD-TENSION). Mochii walks its
ring in — step, step, step — and loops it over his arm.
The garden erupts in cries. Gabu finally moves: he tips
the ring down over his own head like a crown (the EP01
net-hat mechanism, grown fond).
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "He's not the post. He
volunteered to BE the post." / "Walk it in, Mochii."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 18 — GABU: *"...Hold still. Hold
still. Hold still."*
**SFX:** ring-on-branch ticks, shell boing (gag, straight),
berry wobble, eruption cries. **MUSIC:** stems playful
through the attempts; ALL stems out under the Hold —
silence is the score — one stem back at the loop-over.

### SCENE 4 — EXT. THE FIRST ROUTE — LATE DAY (cuts 21–24)
**VISUAL:** GREEN-DUSK "Long Grass" register on the
route home: raking low light through the grass, the
world gone gold at the edges.
**ACTION:** The walk home, unhurried — until Kittember
stops. Dead stop, ears first, and the tail-tip flame
leans — low, sideways, toward the grass at the path's
edge: the sensor, at the opposite register from EP01.
Attention, not dread. Mochiko counts the rings on their
cord: one short. The flame holds its lean. Mochii goes
into the grass at the lean and comes out carrying the
dropped ring high — the ring from cut 17's overshoot,
missed by everyone, found by a flame the size of a
candle-end. It rides home on Mochii's head, badly, and
has to be caught twice.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Count them again... One short.
We don't leave one short."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** grass rustle, count ticks,
flame hum (small), ring catch — twice. **MUSIC:** T1
melody stem, low and warm.

### SCENE 5 — EXT. GARDEN GATE / POND — DUSK (cuts 25–28)
**VISUAL:** GATE-DUSK "Last Errand" at the Gate; then the
Pond under POND-DUSK "Stakeout" — the same key that held
EP01's dread, the rim now employed by welcome: warm band
at the horizon, the water holding the day's last light.
The stakeout chairs are just chairs now.
**ACTION:** The found ring is hung on its hook beside the
keeper board — two names, a full set of rings, a day
accounted for. Mochii looks at the board the way EP02
taught it to. Then, at the Pond's edge: Mochii turns
toward the Dark Forest treeline — across the garden, at
distance, the patterns there stilled as they always are —
and takes one quiet PRESENT pose (present_reveal, held
low, no grade re-key, §0.7): not a challenge, not a
performance for anyone in the frame. An acknowledgment.
It lives here now; something out there does too. HOLD.
Crickets, Garden side. End on the Garden's side of the
dark.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: (at the Pond) "Same pond.
Different evening." / (final beat) — none. Silence is
the tool.
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 28 — MOCHII: *"...Goodnight."*
**SFX:** ring on hook, pond lap, crickets (Garden side
only). **MUSIC:** T1 melody stem solo, out under the hold
— nothing under the subtitle.
