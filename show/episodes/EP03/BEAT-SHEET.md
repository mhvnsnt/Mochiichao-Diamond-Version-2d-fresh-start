## PERSPECTIVE (locked by EP02's switch)
EP02 ended with the Choosing: Mochiko chose Mochii, and the
show changed hands. EP03 is staged from **Mochii's
perspective** throughout — camera at Mochii height, attention
at Mochii's attention. Mochiko is its person: present, loved,
and framed the way Mochii frames her — the keeper who makes
the day possible. There is no switch this episode; the switch
already happened. This is the first ordinary day of the new
arrangement, which is the point: the protagonist's first full
day as a chosen partner.

**6-beat engine (episode formula, first-outing shape):**
1. **The care loop (0:00–1:00)** — Morning at the Gate and
   Green, Mochii-height. The keeper device does its check-in:
   light and icons only — care stats as glyphs Mochii reads
   the way it reads weather (device design is an OPEN ITEM,
   bible §9.5; it stays abstract — a glow, a row of icons, a
   chime). Feeding rounds, water, the board with two names on
   it (EP02 continuity). Dorowani runs the loop from the
   Manual; Mochii runs it at bounce speed. The loop is the
   Tamagotchi/digicore layer of the show made visible: care
   is a rhythm, and today Mochii knows the rhythm.
2. **The outing (1:00–1:50)** — Dorowani opens the Manual to
   the day's page and the Gate is held wide: a first trip
   BEYOND the Gate, keeper and partner together (the game's
   first-route energy — the world starts where the path
   starts). Departures get the held wide (ENVIRONMENT-AND-
   CAMERA: the Gate's grammar). Gabu insists on coming as
   escort — all confidence, chest out — and nobody argues,
   because the day will need exactly what Gabu has.
3. **The route (1:50–3:00)** — Tall grass at Mochii height:
   the world is enormous and kind. ACTION BEAT A: Mochii's
   first full-speed run through the grass, camera with it
   (CAMERA-THERE, 1 of 2). The grass closes over it once —
   one held beat of small-and-lost, ended by Mochiko's voice
   and an answering cry. Then a crossing: **Zephling**
   (roster 019, verified src/data/core/species.json) crosses
   over the grass line, ambient class SKY (LIVING-WORLD) —
   a first-route species, seen, not chased. Mochii watches
   it go. Watching, not chasing, is the day's first lesson,
   and Mochii learns it by doing it.
4. **The ring game (3:00–4:30)** — The route ends at a
   clearing where the garden's grass rings hang on a low
   branch: the ring game, the creatures' own play-ritual.
   The game's ring-capture idea, translated by the garden
   into what the garden does with everything: not a capture
   — an offering. A ring is carried, never thrown; it is
   looped over the post's raised arm by a partner who walks
   it in. The post's whole job is trust: stand, hold, do
   not flinch. Gabu volunteers to be the post. ACTION BEAT
   B is inverted: a full-body set piece of STILLNESS —
   Gabu holding, arms trembling, the game swirling around
   him at speed, a berry balanced on his head by Sproutle
   (gag mechanism, played straight: the berry is a handicap
   he requested). Speed fails first — Mochii overshoots and
   a ring bounces off Gabu's shell. Then Mochii walks its
   ring in, slowly, and loops it over his arm. SUBTITLE
   (earned moment 1) is Gabu's. His bravery is big; his
   body is bigger; the comedy is the mechanism of a huge
   creature doing the hardest small thing — nothing —
   magnificently. Nobody mocks him. The garden adores him
   for it, out loud, in cries.
5. **The small thing (4:30–5:15)** — Light going; the walk
   home. Kittember stops. Her tail flame — ON THE TAIL TIP
   (canon) — leans low toward the grass, the sensor doing
   what it did in EP01, at the opposite emotional register:
   not dread, attention. One ring is missing from the
   count. The flame finds it: a ring dropped in the grass
   on the way out, half-hidden. Mochii carries it home held
   high. A small thing, and it matters — the garden's rings
   are counted, and the garden does not leave one short.
6. **Home by dusk (5:15–6:00)** — Gate at dusk (GATE-DUSK
   "Last Errand"). The Pond at dusk: the EP01 stakeout
   location, the POND-DUSK "Stakeout" key — but the rim has
   changed employer for good this time; the same chairs,
   no stakeout, the location gone warm. The found ring is
   hung on its hook by the keeper board. Then the end
   beat: Mochii at the Pond's edge, facing the Dark Forest
   treeline across the garden — one quiet PRESENT pose,
   held low and still (present_reveal, no grade re-key;
   no hard colour shift this episode). Not entering. Not
   menace. Acknowledgment: something is out there, and
   Mochii lives here now. SUBTITLE (earned moment 2) is
   Mochii's, and it is one word of goodnight. The Forest
   thread stays a thread (bible §9, open item 1) — the
   patterns at the treeline are still, the camera never
   enters, and the episode ends on the Garden's side.
