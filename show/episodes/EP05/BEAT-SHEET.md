# EP05 — "Leave the Light On" (PROPOSED title) — BEAT SHEET v1

**Title status: PROPOSED — not yet owner-approved (house rule:
titles lock at owner approval, like EP01's did).**

Format: ~6 minutes · Cast: Mochiko + Prof. Aeon (humans), pilot
six · Locations (parts-catalog): Garden Gate, Garden Pond, the
Treeline (NEW — garden's edge set, MAKE item; the Dark Forest is
seen ONLY from outside it, per the Forest rules) · Featured
species: **Wispkin** (lead — its episode at last) with **Sproutle**
featured in the dawn sequence (rotation: Sproutle has waited
longest since EP03/EP04 featured Gabu and Dorowani).

Show convention (Jamar, 2026-10-27): creatures do NOT talk in
words — PURE CRIES AND SOUNDS ONLY, no name-saying. SUBTITLES are
silent-film intertitles ONLY at important moments. Only humans
(Mochiko, Aeon) speak word-dialogue. This episode uses **two**
subtitles; the lantern line is the episode's emotional centre.

**Continuity (canon so far):** EP01 — Wispkin revealed as night
keeper; the Dark Forest stinger: something stopped moving between
the trunks, Rule 12 burned in, one leaf fell straight down. EP02 —
the Choosing: Mochii is the protagonist; Mochiko is its person;
Aeon and Hex enter the garden's life. EP03 — first outing beyond
the gate, Gabu featured. EP04 — Hex's dojo sparring, Dorowani
featured, a draw, Hex stays for dinner. **EP05 is the Forest
thread's first dividend** — the stinger starts paying out, slowly,
and pays in dread, not answers.

**6-beat engine (mystery formula, garden-sized, night register):**
1. **Three nights (0:00–0:55)** — Cold open. Three quick night
   vignettes, comic-then-not. NIGHT ONE: the gate bell rings once
   with no wind (picture-side — the bell is a known audio GAP).
   NIGHT TWO: the Pond's cold spot has spread a hand's width past
   yesterday's chalk line. NIGHT THREE: nobody is sleeping —
   Gabu snores standing guard in the net-hat, Kittember's tail-tip
   flame sputters in its sleep, Mochii is simply awake, watching
   the gate. Under all three: Wispkin, on shift, watching the
   treeline. It has seen all of this. It has been waiting to be
   asked properly.
2. **The oddity, named (0:55–1:15)** — Morning after the third
   night. The keeper board carries three chalk tallies and a row
   of sleepless faces. Mochiko does the rounds and adds it up out
   loud: the garden is not haunted — the garden is *tired*. The
   cold spot is the only thing in it that's growing.
3. **Investigation — ask the night keeper (1:15–3:00)** — Mochiko
   waits for dusk and asks Wispkin properly, keeper to keeper.
   SUBTITLE 1: *"...Three nights. I counted."* Wispkin leads the
   night rounds it has walked alone: the bell rope, unmoved,
   filmed with dust; the chalk lines at the Pond, a hand's width
   a night. Kittember at the cold spot's edge: the tail-tip flame
   leans away from it — the garden's own measuring instrument.
4. **False scare (3:00–3:50)** — The walk to the treeline:
   Mochiko, Mochii, Wispkin. The lantern is lit — and gutters,
   hard, in no wind. Full freeze. Held. It's Wispkin's own smoke,
   curling past the glass. Played straight; nobody laughs; Mochii
   does not bounce, and that is the scare.
5. **The brave small thing (3:50–4:50)** — At the treeline, on
   the garden side, camera never crossing: they set the keeper's
   lantern down, lit. Under the lighting system's takeover rules
   the lantern becomes the scene's active light while it lives.
   SUBTITLE 2 (the centre): *"...The garden doesn't bar its
   gate. It keeps a light on."* Mochii sets one berry beside the
   lantern. Then the hold — a long one. Lantern light, treeline,
   black. Nothing moves. And then, far in the dark, ONE thing
   changes: a single leaf, somewhere in the black, turns.
   Nothing is shown. Nothing is resolved. The Forest has
   answered in its own grammar — stillness, moved once.
6. **Dawn + button (4:50–6:00)** — Dawn at the Pond. Sproutle,
   cheek to the ground (its EP01 job, kept): the frost edge has
   STOPPED, a hand's width short of the reeds. Kittember walks
   the edge and the tail-tip flame burns upright and normal —
   the measurement, on screen. The garden sleeps properly at
   last, in a pile, mid-morning. At the keeper board Mochiko
   lifts the brush — and writes nothing. Wispkin floats home
   with the lantern and hangs it by the Gate itself. BUTTON:
   Aeon, quiet, to Mochiko: "The garden has kept a light on
   before. Long before you." Nothing further. End.

**Action beats (motion budget):** (A) Beat 3 — Wispkin leads the
night rounds: a full smoke-swirl glide circuit (bell rope → chalk
lines → cold-spot edge), the float rig's showcase. (B) Beat 6 —
Wispkin's dawn return: lantern carried through the morning garden
and hung at the Gate, one continuous float.

**Reusable assets created:** treeline BG (night + dawn grades,
MAKE), keeper's lantern (prop — takeover light source), chalk-line
cold-spot decal set (extends EP01's cold-spot trail FX), Wispkin
rounds glide cycle (BANK after EP05).

**Stinger:** none this episode, by design. The leaf turning IS
the Forest beat, placed inside the episode where the season
earns it; the episode ends on Aeon's button instead. Whether the
leaf turning is comfort or warning is the season's open question
— flagged as a deliberate open item in the shooting script (§6),
not an answer this episode gives.
