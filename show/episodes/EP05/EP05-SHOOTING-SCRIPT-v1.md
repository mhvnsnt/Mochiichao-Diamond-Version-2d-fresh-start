## 0. THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Mochiko and Aeon are
   the word-voices.
2. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — only at earned
   moments. This episode has exactly **two**: cuts 10 and 17.
   The cut 17 line is the episode's emotional centre.
3. **Rule 12 is not spoken this episode.** It was read in EP01
   and burned over the Forest in the stinger; EP05 lets it sit.
   If it is used anywhere in production, it is quoted in the
   owner's exact wording (bible §5): "Everything in the garden
   belongs to somebody... And everybody belongs to the Garden."
4. **The Forest rules are absolute.** The camera NEVER enters
   the Forest — every Forest frame is shot from the garden side
   of the treeline. No PRESENT poses in the Forest. Pattern-fills
   are absent there. No birdsong from inside. Dread by stillness.
   Nothing in the Forest is shown, explained, or resolved.
5. **The leaf turning means nothing this episode can prove.**
   Comfort or warning is the season's question; it is filed as a
   deliberate open item (§6.1), NOT an answer the script fills in.
6. **Kittember's flame is attached to the tail tip** (owner
   canon). It is never a separate orb. Here it works as the
   garden's instrument: leaning away from the cold spot while the
   spot grows, burning upright and normal when it stops.
7. **The lantern is a takeover light source** (LIGHTING-SYSTEM
   TK rules): while it is lit, it is the scene's active light.
   The cold spot is the garden's one NEGATIVE source — it takes
   light away; it does not give any.
8. **Every beat traces to canon** (§1) or is marked TBD (§6).

## 3. THE SCRIPT
### SCENE 1 — EXT. GARDEN GATE / POND — THREE NIGHTS (cuts 01–07)
**VISUAL:** Night register: deep brand blues, garden asleep.
NIGHT ONE — the Gate, still. NIGHT TWO — the Pond, a chalk line
on the bank. NIGHT THREE — the garden's sleepers, restless.
**ACTION:** Night one: the gate bell rings once. There is no
wind — the gate's ribbon hangs dead. Wispkin, mid-rounds, stops;
mask tilts toward the treeline. Night two: at the Pond, frost
has crept a hand's width past yesterday's chalk line; a fresh
chalk line is drawn beyond it — small black hands, careful work.
Night three: Gabu snores standing up, net-hat over his eyes,
"guarding." Kittember sleeps curled, tail-tip flame sputtering
in time with nothing. Mochii is awake. Just awake, watching the
gate, not bouncing. TITLE CARD (Persona pop). Morning after:
the keeper board, three chalk tallies; Mochiko counts the
sleepless faces around her and does the arithmetic out loud.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Three nights. Nobody's slept. Not even
Gabu." / (beat) "Especially not Gabu."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** bell — PICTURE-SIDE ONLY (audio is
a known GAP, cue sheet); night garden bed; snores. **MUSIC:**
none under the vignettes; title track for the card only.

### SCENE 2 — EXT. GARDEN GATE / POND — DUSK (cuts 08–12)
**VISUAL:** Dusk grade. Dorowani opens the Manual on the keeper
board, looks at it a long moment, closes it without reading.
Some questions are not for the book.
**ACTION:** Mochiko finds Wispkin at its day-shade and does NOT
wake it. She waits for dusk, keeper's hours, and asks properly.
Wispkin rises. On the rounds (ACTION BEAT A): the glide circuit
Wispkin has walked alone — the bell rope, filmed with dust,
unmoved; the chalk lines at the Pond, a hand's width apart,
three of them; the cold spot's edge, where Kittember stands
with its tail-tip flame leaning away from the cold like a
compass needle refusing north.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "I'm asking properly. Keeper to keeper."
/ "You were waiting for me to ask, weren't you." / "A hand's
width a night. Towards the reeds."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 10 — WISPKIN: *"...Three nights. I counted."*
**SFX:** smoke drift, chalk scratch, flame hum (thin, wrong).
**MUSIC:** title stems, night register (organ + bass), low.

### SCENE 3 — EXT. THE TREELINE — NIGHT (cuts 13–18)
**VISUAL:** The garden's edge. Garden light behind the camera;
ahead, the treeline and the black past it. The Forest register:
high value, ~0 saturation where the dark lets anything read at
all. No pattern-fills. The camera stays on the garden side for
every frame of this scene. It does not cross. It does not lean in.
**ACTION:** The walk out: Mochiko carrying the unlit lantern,
Mochii at her heel — bouncing at half-height, then not at all —
Wispkin ahead, smoke flat and low. Behind them, small in frame,
the gate; Gabu asleep on guard. Mochiko lights the lantern.
It gutters — hard, once, in no wind. Full freeze: keeper,
wisp, and the garden's heartbeat, all stopped. Held. Wispkin's
own smoke curls past the lantern glass; the flame steadies.
Nobody laughs. Mochii does not bounce, and that is the scare.
They set the lantern down at the treeline, lit, on the garden
side of the first trees. TAKEOVER: from this frame the lantern
is the scene's light — faces, mask, and grass lit by it; the
dark beyond it stays dark. Mochii, after a moment, sets one
berry down beside it. Carefully. Like it matters, because it does.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "We don't go in. We were never going in."
/ "This part is ours."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 17 — WISPKIN: *"...The garden doesn't bar its
gate. It keeps a light on."*
**SFX:** lantern glass tick, match strike (foley — cue sheet),
grass underfoot, then the Forest bed: engineered absence.
**MUSIC:** stems out as the lantern lights; none after.

### SCENE 4 — EXT. THE TREELINE — NIGHT, CONTINUOUS (cuts 19–22)
**VISUAL:** One composition, kept: lantern low in frame, lit
grass around it, treeline, black. The garden's light behind
camera is gone from the grade now — the lantern owns what light
there is, and it does not reach far.
**ACTION:** The hold. A long one — the longest of the season so
far. Nothing moves. Not the smoke. Not the flame. Not Mochii.
Then, far in the black, where no shape can be made out and none
is offered: a single leaf, somewhere in the dark, turns. Once.
Nothing else follows it. Wispkin's small black hands tighten on
the mask; its smoke goes perfectly, unnaturally still — the
wisp answering stillness in kind. They leave the lantern
burning and walk back without looking back. Mochii looks back
once. The light is still there. So is the dark around it.
**DIALOGUE:** None. **SUBTITLES:** None — the episode has spent
its two; what the leaf said, it said without words.
**SFX:** the Forest bed only (engineered absence; NO birdsong,
NO wind cycles — LIVING-WORLD Forest rule). **MUSIC:** None.

### SCENE 5 — EXT. GARDEN POND / GATE — DAWN (cuts 23–28)
**VISUAL:** Dawn grade warming the Pond BG. The garden's colour
comes back in the order it left: water first, then grass, then
the board.
**ACTION:** Sproutle, cheek to the grass at the cold spot's edge
(its EP01 job, kept), chirps the pattern it found in episode
one — and then a new note: the frost edge has STOPPED, a hand's
width short of the reeds, inside last night's chalk line.
Kittember walks the edge slowly; at the spot where the flame
leaned all week it stands a moment, and the tail-tip flame
burns upright, steady, normal — the measurement, on screen, no
narration needed. Wide: the garden asleep at last, properly,
in a pile in full morning light, Gabu at the bottom. At the
keeper board, Mochiko lifts the brush over the blank line —
and sets it down again. Some nights you don't write up.
Wispkin floats in through the dawn with the lantern (ACTION
BEAT B), smoke gold at the edges, and hangs it by the Gate
itself, where it will live now. Aeon is standing there. It is
not said how long he has been standing there.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Stopped. A hand's width short of the
reeds." / AEON: "The garden has kept a light on before."
/ (beat) "Long before you."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** dawn garden bed, chalk scratch
(one line, not crossed), lantern hook creak. **MUSIC:** title
theme melody stem, solo, under the button; out on black.
