# THE GARDEN MANUAL — PROPOSED RULE SET (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT — OWNER LOCKS. Written 2026-10-27 under the
owner's ruling that script writers may invent numbered Manual
rules as drafts for his lock. NOTHING in this document is canon
except where marked CANON or OWNER. Rule 1 is read in locked
EP01 cut 05. Rule 12 is the owner's exact wording and is not a
draft. Rules 2–11 are proposals, written in Rule 12's register,
each with the place in the world where it lives. The owner may
lock, edit, renumber, or strike any of them; EP01 script v3 uses
exactly one (Rule 3, at V3-020) and is boarded to survive its
removal.**

Rule 12's register (the standard every draft is aimed at): two
clauses, the second widening the first; plain words; an ellipsis
for the breath between them; a truth about belonging stated as a
truth about work.

---

**Rule 1 — CANON (locked script, cut 05).**
*"Mind the gate."*
Lives at: the Gate itself — the first thing a keeper is told,
and the gate is a trampoline anyway (Mochiko's gloss, cut 04).

**Rule 2 — DRAFT.**
*"Count what you carry twice... The Garden counts once, and
remembers."*
Lives at: the berry store ledger — every tally in the Garden is
kept against a world that keeps its own books better.

**Rule 3 — DRAFT — USED IN EP01 v3 (V3-020, the morning ritual).**
*"Feed the helper first... The keeper is fed by the feeding."*
Lives at: the feeding rounds, morning and dusk — the care loop's
first law, learned by Mochiko as a counting gag before it is
ever read to her.

**Rule 4 — DRAFT.**
*"Leave every place as you found it... unless you found it
wanting."*
Lives at: the pond tools rack — the night keeper's tidiness, and
the keeper who learns to copy a hand she has never seen.

**Rule 5 — DRAFT.**
*"A trail is a sentence... Walk it slowly, and it will finish
itself."*
Lives at: the Green, at cheek height — the frost path, and every
track the Garden writes for those who read at ground level.

**Rule 6 — DRAFT.**
*"When the flame goes small, stop... Small is the loudest it
speaks."*
Lives at: wherever Kittember's tail tip is — the Garden's sensor
law, binding on every stakeout, chase, and rescue.

**Rule 7 — DRAFT.**
*"Never chase what the Garden is showing you... Stand still,
and let it arrive."*
Lives at: the stakeout posts — the investigation rule, learned
the night the net plan failed honestly.

**Rule 8 — DRAFT.**
*"Every name on the board was earned in the dark first...
Paint it in the light anyway."*
Lives at: the keeper board — how a name joins the Garden: by
work nobody saw, witnessed by everybody after.

**Rule 9 — DRAFT.**
*"The night round is a round... What is kept in the dark is
kept for the morning."*
Lives at: the cold path from the Green — the night keeper's
whole charter, and the day keeper's promise to honour it.

**Rule 10 — DRAFT.**
*"If a thing is stacked with pride, guard it... If it falls,
stack it again, smaller."*
Lives at: the berry pyramid — pride, practice, and permission
to begin again at half scale.

**Rule 11 — DRAFT.**
*"Do not name what stops in the Forest... Some rounds are not
ours to walk."*
Lives at: the Garden side of the tree line — the Manual's only
rule about the Forest, and it is a rule about restraint. (The
Forest's open question stays open, bible §9.1; this draft
proposes the Manual's posture toward it, nothing more.)

**Rule 12 — OWNER (exact wording; not a draft; do not edit).**
*"Everything in the garden belongs to somebody... And everybody
belongs to the Garden."*
Lives at: everywhere — read aloud at the Gate on the night the
board gained a name (locked cut 21), and burned in over the
Dark Forest (locked cut 27).

## Drafting notes for the owner's lock pass
- The set is deliberately keeper-shaped: gates, stores, tools,
  trails, boards, rounds. No draft rule legislates feelings,
  villains, or the wider world — the Manual is a working book,
  and its authority in the show comes from being about work.
- Numbers were assigned so the morning material sits early
  (2–3), the investigation in the middle (5–7), the night at the
  back (8–9), and the Forest last before Rule 12 (11) — the
  order a keeper would meet them in a first week.
- If the owner strikes Rule 3, EP01 v3's V3-020 note names the
  fallback: the ritual beat plays with any morning rule, or with
  the reading carried as pure ritual and no rule cited.
