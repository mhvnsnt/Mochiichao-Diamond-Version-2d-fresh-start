# REPAINT PROTOCOL — per-scene repaints, the Lustre System, and the JoJo licence

Owner ruling (2026-10-27): per-scene NON-CANON repaints in the
JoJo manner are welcome in this show — a valued quality of JoJo's
design principles — and the Lustre System gives the show a second,
in-world engine for them. This protocol governs both so repaints
stay a designed event, never drift. It sits on COLOR-SYSTEM.md
(Lustre Law) and LIGHTING-SYSTEM.md (CS-1..CS-6); where they speak,
they govern.

## The two repaint engines
1. **LIGHT REPAINTS (JoJo engine).** A scene re-keys its light:
   grade family, rim colour, temperature, takeover source. Fills
   are NEVER repainted — the shift is a lighting overlay (Lighting
   L7: base = canon, shift = layer; switch it off, base returns
   exactly). Triggers are the closed CS list (reveal, menace,
   emotion turn, state change); hard shifts rationed (2/segment);
   every shift has a named release.
2. **LUSTRE REPAINTS (in-world engine).** A creature's lustre
   colourway is a real state of that creature in this world, so a
   scene may present a creature IN its lustre form — a repaint
   with a story reason (light conditions, garden state, growth,
   the digicore care layer). Lustre repaints obey the Lustre Law
   absolutely: re-palette wholesale is allowed; accent PLACEMENT,
   eye-colour family, ink, and species filing never move; a lustre
   form is fixed per species, never per individual or per whim.

## What a repaint may never touch (protected anchors)
Silhouette and its reading accents · eye colour family · ink
#101010 · Wispkin's mask-white · the #a03436 discipline (red only
for Dorowani/alerts/Rule burn-in) · Kittember's flame placement
(tail tip) · subtitle colour rules. If a proposed repaint needs to
move an anchor, it is not a repaint — it is a redesign, and it goes
through the Design Gate as one.

## Scene repaint sheet (per scene, filed with the lighting sheet)
- Engine: LIGHT / LUSTRE / BOTH · Trigger (from the closed list) ·
  Grade family or lustre form named · Protected anchors present in
  frame (checked) · Release (where it returns to base) · Ration
  count for the segment.
The build manifest carries these fields; a scene with an unfiled
repaint fails its gate like any other unfiled element.
