# 48 — MOCHIIOTECA ENTRY STYLE GUIDE (dex flavor-text conventions)

Teardown List 6, item 48. Native writing standard for Jamar's
Mochiichao project, written in his **house writing system**:
rules first, canon-sourced beats, TBDs never silently filled,
specific over generic, silence as a tool. No third-party dex text is
reproduced or paraphrased entry-by-entry; the grammar below is
derived from the *published conventions* of the form (length bands,
voice, structure) and rebuilt for the Mochiioteca.

Applies to the species-sheet fields `bio_short` / `bio_long`
(Sideline A element list; NEW-SPECIES-SHEET-TEMPLATE.md).

---

## 1. Teardown — the flavor-entry form, as documented

- **Two lengths exist because two jobs exist.** The short entry is a
  *field-guide card*: one to two sentences, ~25–45 words in the
  documented games — it must be readable at a glance, mid-game. The
  long entry is the *naturalist's note*: a short paragraph that earns
  its length with behaviour, habitat, or relationship — never with
  stat repetition.
- **Voice is third-person naturalist, present tense**, observing the
  creature as wildlife: what it *does*, where it *lives*, how it
  *relates* — to its habitat, its line, its keeper, its rivals.
- **One entry = one idea.** The documented form's discipline: each
  entry carries a single memorable fact or behaviour (the "one wrong
  note" principle applied to prose). Entries that list three traits
  read as manuals; entries with one behaviour read as animals.
- **Numbers are banned from the short entry.** Height/weight/category
  live in the data fields; the prose that repeats them wastes its one
  idea.

## 2. The Mochiichao rules (house system applied)

**R1 — Rules before writing.** An entry may only state what the
species' locked data sheet supports: types, habitat, discovery data,
body plan, cry, signature behaviour from canon. Anything else is a
TBD in the TBD-REGISTER, not an invention in the prose.

**R2 — Length bands (Mochiichao standard, set here):**
- `bio_short`: 1–2 sentences, **15–40 words**. One behaviour.
- `bio_long`: 3–5 sentences, **60–110 words**. Behaviour + habitat +
  one relationship (line, keeper, rival, or place). Still no stats.

**R3 — Voice.** Present tense, third person, naturalist warmth with
the show's dread register available for Dark Forest species. The
creature is an animal first and a game unit never.

**R4 — THE SECRET discipline.** In-world artifacts never betray the
hidden truth (production-layer law): no computer vocabulary in entry
prose — no *data, digital, program, code, glitch, system* as in-world
words. The display-sweep replacements govern (Mochi Fruit, Mochi
Cocoon, Iridescence Plateau…). A Mochiioteca entry is an in-world
artifact the inhabitants could read.

**R5 — Cries are sounds, not speech.** An entry may describe what a
cry *sounds like* and when the creature makes it. It may never quote
a creature "saying" anything (Voice Law: pure cries and sounds only).

**R6 — Discovery honesty.** If the sheet's discovery/habitat data is
seeded or TBD, the entry does not fake specificity: write to the
region level the data supports, and file the gap. (Gen 1 audit:
habitat seeded for only 13/49 — most Gen 1 entries are blocked at
region level until the lane fills habitat; that is a data gap, not a
writing failure.)

**R7 — Signature behaviour first.** Where canon locks a signature
(species behaviour, signature move in display name, protected flaw),
the entry leads with it or orbits it. Wispkin's entry is about the
mask and the frost; Kittember's is about the tail-tip flame —
attached, never an orb.

**R8 — One wrong note.** Per species, one sentence may carry the
memorable oddity (the Sugimori balance rule, COLOR-SYSTEM §1.3,
applied to prose). Everything else stays clean so the oddity lands.

**R9 — Display names only.** Entries use current display names
(rename law); internal ids never appear in prose. A renamed move
referenced in an entry uses its Mochiichao display name.

**R10 — Silence is a tool.** A species whose canon is mystery
(what stopped moving; the Forest's depths) gets an entry that
*withholds* cleanly — states what is seen, ends before explanation.
Never fill a canon silence with lore.

## 3. Entry skeleton (the format writers fill)

```
bio_short:  [behaviour sentence]. [one-wrong-note sentence, optional].
bio_long:   [behaviour, expanded]. [habitat, at data-supported level].
            [relationship: line / keeper / rival / place].
            [cry: what it sounds like, when]. [withholding close, if R10 applies].
```

Worked *structural* example (placeholder species, no canon claimed):

> short — "It sleeps curled around its own plated tail, and wakes
> before the gate bell finishes ringing. Nothing in the Garden has
> ever seen it dream."
> long — behaviour (guard sleep) → habitat (Garden, gate-adjacent) →
> relationship (keepers trust it as an alarm) → cry (a low plate-hum
> when startled) → no R10 close needed.

## 4. Gate (run per entry before it ships)

1. ☐ Word count inside R2 band.
2. ☐ Every factual claim traceable to the locked sheet or canon (§9
   of the design brief); untraceable = cut or TBD-filed.
3. ☐ No computer vocabulary (R4 sweep list applied to prose).
4. ☐ No quoted creature speech (R5).
5. ☐ Discovery specificity ≤ data specificity (R6).
6. ☐ Signature behaviour present or consciously orbited (R7).
7. ☐ Display names current; ids absent (R9).

## 5. Wiring points

- Species-sheet template (NEW-SPECIES-SHEET-TEMPLATE.md) gains this
  guide as the normative reference for its bio blocks; intake Gate 1
  (data review) runs the §4 gate.
- Game data: entries land in the dex registry / species records
  two-lane (game + show read the same text — one source, both lanes).
- Sideline A gap-fill: Gen 1 `bio_short` 12/49 present, `bio_long`
  0/49 — this guide is the standard those fills are written to;
  R6 blocks most until habitat data lands.

## 6. Coverage notes

The form's conventions (length bands, naturalist voice, one-idea
discipline) are documented across published dex-text scholarship and
the games' own visible corpus — described here as grammar, with zero
entries quoted or adapted. The Mochiichao bands in R2 are *set by
this guide* (marked as house standard, not discovered fact). All
species-specific example text is placeholder. No canon entries are
written by this teardown — entry *authorship* is lane work under
this standard.
