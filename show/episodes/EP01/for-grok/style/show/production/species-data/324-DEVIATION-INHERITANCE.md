# #324 — DEVIATION INHERITANCE → what a child gets, and what it never can

**The question this answers.** If the maskless
Verdilance has offspring, are they maskless? If a
generated standout breeds, does the distinction
pass? Inheritance is where a careless rule quietly
unmakes the register — one inherited absence and
"the only one they've seen without a mask" is a
bloodline, not a person. This spec closes the
question at the machinery that already owns it.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/deviation-inheritance/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Ruled deviations never inherit. (Restated as
  law, because #318 made the stakes higher.)** An
  offspring's #80 record opens with an **empty**
  `deviations[]`, always. Doro's child has the
  species' eyes unless the owner rules otherwise
  *for that child, as its own ruling*. The bipedal
  Kittember's offspring are quadrupedal. The
  maskless Verdilance's offspring **grow the shield**
  — his lack is his story, not their anatomy. No
  field, flag, or probability in any system may
  carry a ruled deviation across a hatch; the check
  is schema-level (#80's record requires ruled
  entries to carry `ruled_by`/`ruled_on`/`source`,
  and a hatch writes none).
- **Generated variation may echo — as variation,
  not as copy.** #80's existing bounded slice is
  the whole mechanism: up to 3 stat variances pass
  (2 from one parent, 1 from the other), and the
  offspring's seed is fresh-rolled before the slice
  lands. For #322's visible kinds, the echo rule is
  the same shape, read through the offspring's own
  derivation: a parent's distinction raises the
  *weight* of the corresponding table cell in the
  offspring's derivation — the child is likelier to
  carry a similar marking placement or proportion
  lean, **derived from its own seed**, never
  assigned the parent's snapshot. An echo is a
  family resemblance; a copy would be a clone fact,
  and no system here clones.
- **Evolution reasserts the species.** At evolution,
  the destination stage's **sheet anatomy governs**
  (#199's evolution machinery, #228's sequence): a
  generated variation survives the change only where
  the destination sheet carries the same variation
  kind in its table — marking vocabularies and
  proportion bounds are per-stage facts. Part
  anatomy follows the stage records absolutely
  (#259/#317 `stageFirst`): no variation, echo, or
  story makes a shield grow early or a grown shield
  fail to grow. And the register's rule across
  evolution is #80's consistency, unchanged: a ruled
  deviation on an individual **persists across that
  individual's own evolution** (Doro evolves with
  his whites) — persistence within a life is the
  opposite direction from inheritance across lives,
  and this spec keeps the two from ever being
  confused in a citation again.
