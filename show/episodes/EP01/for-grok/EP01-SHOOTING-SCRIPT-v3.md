## 0.1 THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Cry palettes are
   per-species designed assets (craft doc §4): Wispkin is the
   quietest thing in a sound-led episode.
2. **No human dialogue is written in any new cut.** EP01 is a
   low-verbal, Jack-shaped episode (owner, 2026-10-27): Mochiko
   acts, works, watches, and listens; she does not speak outside
   the locked spine. The only human voice in the episode lives
   inside the LOCKED-SPINE blocks, carried verbatim from v2 and
   not touched. The two cameos carry no lines at all (rule 7).
3. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles — exactly FIVE in this
   episode** (owner-approved 2026-10-27): the locked three
   (V3-071 / cut 18, V3-077 / cut 22, V3-106 / cut 27) plus two
   new, both Wispkin, both in Act 3 (V3-089, V3-100). No other
   cut in this script carries a subtitle. Full ledger: change
   note §2.
4. **Rule 12 is quoted in the owner's exact wording** (bible §5):
   "Everything in the garden belongs to somebody... And everybody
   belongs to the Garden." It appears only where the locked
   script puts it (cuts 21 and 27).
5. **One new numbered Manual rule is used, in N2, and it is a
   DRAFT.** Rule 3 (draft text in the N2 notes and in
   MANUAL-RULES-DRAFT.md) is marked DRAFT-RULE everywhere it
   appears in this script. It is performed as ritual — Dorowani's
   reading cadence, heads bowing at the familiar part — never
   spoken by a human voice in the new material.
6. **The frost is canon: it is Wispkin's trace** (owner,
   2026-10-27) — where the chill of its smoke tail settles as it
   walks its nightly round; the map Mochiko learns to read. The
   frost carries a dark spirit aura: black-purple smoke/flame
   living inside the frost and snow. It is shown, never explained
   in dialogue.
7. **Cameos, the Jack way** (owner-approved): HEX — one distant
   figure at the edge of a held frame (V3-002), no line, no
   approach, no name; gone when the frame is revisited (rhyme,
   V3-098). AEON — presence by signal only: one device ping
   inside the Mochiioteca sweep (V3-043), one sound, once, from
   outside the Garden's sound-world. Neither cameo adds a cut or
   a second; both ride inside existing cuts.
8. **Living World:** finished scenes live — cycles, wind state,
   population. **Forest exception: cycles OFF.** No cut in this
   script adds motion to any Forest image. Wind state is called
   per cluster (CALM through the episode; the stinger's stillness
   is the absence of the state, not a state).
9. **One dominant colour per sequence** (style stack): dawn
   brand-blue → day clear → dusk amber-thin → night indigo. The
   single scripted exception is the FROST CLOSE-UP COLOUR SHIFT
   (V3-039), a JoJo-register palette event with explicit
   shift/restore beats.
10. **No baked shadows.** All character shadow is composite-stage
    work, per the standing rule.
11. **Every beat traces to canon** (SHOW-BIBLE, SEASON-CANON-MAP,
    the locked v2, the beat map) or is marked TBD in §7. TBDs are
    never silently filled.

### N2 — INT./EXT. BERRY STORE & THE ROUNDS — MORNING (V3-012–021, 128s) [NEW]
*Mochiko's first keeper routine, taught by the creatures
themselves. Species are introduced through work, never by
presentation. The rounds are cut on a rhythm — task, task,
task… hold (craft doc §3). Mochiko does not speak in this
cluster; she counts on her fingers, on the ledger, and on
Mochii's head.*

**V3-012 — 1:55–2:07 (12s)**
VISUAL: The berry store: shelves, baskets, the keeper's ledger
on its stand. Morning light through the slats.
ACTION: Mochiko opens the ledger, squares it like a ceremony of
one, and takes up the counting position. Around her, without
being asked, the crew assembles at its stations — this round
existed before her and will outlast her; she is being fitted
into it.
DIALOGUE: None. (Crew: settling sounds, each species' work-noise
palette.)
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Ledger thump; basket weave creaks; berry rustle.
MUSIC: T1 work stems, light.

**V3-013 — 2:07–2:21 (14s)**
VISUAL: The ledger pile, close. Mochiko's hand, berries, tally
marks.
ACTION: The inventory gag, first movement: Mochiko counts
berries into the pile, tapping each one — one tap, one tally
mark. Mochii "helps": it sits inside the counting frame, solemn
as an auditor.
DIALOGUE: None. (Mochii: auditor's silence — the joke is that it
makes no sound at all while helping.)
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Berry taps in a counting rhythm; pencil scratches.
MUSIC: Work stems keep the count's tempo.

**V3-014 — 2:21–2:35 (14s)**
VISUAL: Same frame discipline. The pile is right. The count is
wrong.
ACTION: Second movement: one berry per count disappears into
Mochii at exactly the count it was meant to prove. Mochiko
recounts. The pile agrees with the pile. The tally disagrees
with both. She looks at the tally, at the pile, at the auditor.
The auditor looks at the ceiling.
DIALOGUE: None.
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Taps; a small swallow, precisely on a count; pencil
stopping.
MUSIC: One stem drops out on the swallow. It does not come back.

**V3-015 — 2:35–2:47 (12s)**
VISUAL: Two-shot: keeper and auditor, the pile between them.
ACTION: She works it out — the audience watches her work it out
one beat before they'd get tired of the gag — and laughs without
a word: one breath, shoulders only. She points at Mochii, then
at the pile, then, deciding, at Mochii's mouth.
DIALOGUE: None.
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: The breath-laugh; a berry rolled across wood like a verdict.
MUSIC: Stems hold, amused, unhurried.

**V3-016 — 2:47–2:57 (10s)**
VISUAL: The pile, the bowl, the auditor — recomposed.
ACTION: The care loop, taught as comedy and landed as law: she
feeds Mochii first. A small bowl, filled before the count
begins. Mochii eats with both hands and total focus. The count
that follows will be right. (The Manual gets its say on this in
V3-020.)
DIALOGUE: None.
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Bowl set down; eating sounds, unembarrassed.
MUSIC: Work stems, warmer by one instrument.

**V3-017 — 2:57–3:11 (14s)**
VISUAL: The Green's edge beds. Sproutle with the watering can
carried in both arms, nearly its own size.
ACTION: Sproutle's watering round. Its visor fogs from the
inside with each breath; at each bed it stops, takes the leaf it
keeps behind one ear, wipes the visor clear, waters, moves on.
A kept tool is a character. The leaf-wipe happens exactly three
times. It will happen all season.
DIALOGUE: None. (Sproutle: breath-fog rhythm; one chirp per bed,
a worker marking tasks done.)
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Can slosh; water on soil; leaf on visor, squeak.
MUSIC: Work stems thin to a single line under the round.

**V3-018 — 3:11–3:23 (12s)**
VISUAL: The path to the Pond. Gabu, a bucket, and the problem of
his arms.
ACTION: Gabu "helps" with the water run: his arms are too long
for the bucket handle, so the bucket orbits him once per stride
like a moon. He has clearly done this for years and considers it
correct. Not one drop is spilled. That is the gag, and it is
also the truth about him: the Garden's methods look wrong and
lose nothing.
DIALOGUE: None. (Gabu: a hum of total self-satisfaction, pitched
to the orbit.)
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Bucket swing whoosh on the stride; water staying in.
MUSIC: Work stems pick the orbit's tempo up, briefly.

**V3-019 — 3:23–3:35 (12s)**
VISUAL: The Gate post, close: a row of small notches cut along
its inner edge, years of them.
ACTION: The mornings question, played wordless. Mochiko looks
from the new ledger to Dorowani; gestures, small: the book, the

The Garden was kept long before it was hers.
DIALOGUE: None.
SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Fin on wood, notch by notch; morning bed under.
MUSIC: One stem, low.

**V3-020 — 3:35–3:51 (16s)**

ACTION: The ritual beat. Dorowani reads — gavel-tap to open

DIALOGUE: None. (Dorowani: reading cadence only — cries in the

SUBTITLES: None.
SFX: Gavel-tap, twice; page turn; the Garden's bed lowered in

MUSIC: OUT for the reading. Room tone only.

entirely: the ritual is the beat, the text is the note.

DIALOGUE: None.
