1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Mochiko is the only
   word-voice.
2. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — only at earned
   moments. This episode has exactly **three**: cuts 18, 22, 27.
3. **Rule 12 is quoted in the owner's exact wording** (bible §5):
   "Everything in the garden belongs to somebody... And everybody
   belongs to the Garden."
4. **The Dark Forest stinger shows nothing.** Something stops
   moving; it is never seen (bible §9, open item 1).
5. **Every beat traces to canon** (§1) or is marked TBD (§6).

## 3. THE SCRIPT
### SCENE 1 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — MORNING (cuts 01–05)
**VISUAL:** The Gate in brand-blue morning. Keeper board, one
blank line at the bottom. Sign: GARDEN KEEPER.
**ACTION:** Mochiko arrives under a too-big backpack. The gate
swings open by itself — Mochii is bouncing on it, squeak-cry going
like a metronome. Dorowani enters with the Manual and taps its
cover twice, like a gavel.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "First rule of the garden: the gate is a
trampoline. Got it." / (reading) "Rule one: mind the gate." —
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** gate creak, bounce springs, gavel tap.
**MUSIC:** Cue T1 (title track, CUE-SHEET.md).

### SCENE 2 — INT./EXT. GARDEN POND — CONTINUOUS (cuts 06–09)
**VISUAL:** Pond glassy. Berry pyramid, stacked with pride.
**ACTION:** Behind the tour patter, the pyramid un-stacks itself,
one berry at a time. A toy duck rolls uphill. Kittember's tail flame
flickers at empty air; ears flat.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "You stacked those. Who unstacked those?"
/ "Ducks don't roll uphill."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** berry plops, duck squeak, tail-flame hum.
**MUSIC:** T1 stems thin out under the oddity.

### SCENE 3 — EXT. GARDEN GREEN — DAY (cuts 10–12)
**VISUAL:** The Green (battle-bg-garden). Frost patches on warm
grass — wrong, and specific.
**ACTION:** Gabu stomps in wearing the net as a cape, beats his
chest once: foghorn bellow of total confidence. Sproutle, cheek to
the grass, chirps the discovery: the cold spots make a path,
pointing at the pond.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "You want to catch it. ...By supper. Sure.
Why not." / "It's giving us directions?"
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** stomp, bellow, frost crackle (GAP —
cue sheet). **MUSIC:** T1 out; investigation pulse from stems.

### SCENE 4 — EXT. GARDEN POND — DUSK (cuts 13–19)
**VISUAL:** Dusk grade on the pond BG. Mist at knee height.
**ACTION:** Stakeout. Gabu LUNGES (action beat A) — comes up
holding smoke that pours through his fingers. The tail flame FLARES: a
tall spiky shadow — which is Sproutle's sprout, backlit (gag
mechanism, played straight). Mist parts: Wispkin, small, shaking,
holding the duck up like an apology. The softest wail yet, up at
the end like a question.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Caught it. Not caught it." / "That's
Sproutle's shadow. Everybody breathe." / "...Hi. You kept the
garden all night, didn't you. Every night." / "Then let's do it
properly."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 18 — WISPKIN: *"...I keep the garden at night."*
**SFX:** splash, flare whoomph, duck squeak (small). **MUSIC:**
all stems out for the wail; one stem back under the reveal.

### SCENE 5 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — NIGHT (cuts 20–24)
**VISUAL:** The keeper board. Fresh paint: WISPKIN — NIGHT KEEPER.
**ACTION:** Mochiko reads Rule 12 from the Manual (exact wording).
Nobody says anything else; the crickets do it for them. Wispkin
floats UP for the first time (action beat B), smoke swirling,
and rings the gate bell with its tail of smoke. TAG: Gabu tips
the net, worn as a hat, grandly.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: (Rule 12, §0.3) / "It's a successful hat,
Gabu. It's a successful hat."
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 22 — WISPKIN: *"...not alone anymore."*
**SFX:** brush on wood, bell. **MUSIC:** T1 melody stem, solo.

### STINGER — EXT. THE DARK FOREST — DAY (cuts 25–28)
**VISUAL:** Full daylight. The light does not reach in. The
forest floor is in night anyway. Trees too still for the breeze
we can hear on the Garden side.
**ACTION:** None. That is the action. Something far in the dark
stops moving. We never see what it is. One leaf falls, straight
down, in no wind.
**DIALOGUE:** None. **SUBTITLES:** Cut 27 — Rule 12 (exact
wording), burns in low and holds. **SFX:** birdsong, Garden side
only; none from in there. **MUSIC:** None.
