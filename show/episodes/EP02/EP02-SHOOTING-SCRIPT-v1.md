## 0. THE RULES THIS SCRIPT OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Word-dialogue belongs
   to the humans only: Mochiko, Prof. Aeon, Hex.
2. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — only at earned
   moments. This episode has exactly **two**: cuts 16, 26. Both
   are Mochii's, and both fall **after** the switch — creature
   meaning becomes legible on screen only once the show is the
   creature's. The third slot is held, unspent.
3. **Rule 12 is not quoted in this episode.** It was spent
   three times in EP01; its wording is locked (bible §5) and it
   is not small change.
4. **THE SWITCH (owner design):** until the choosing, the
   episode is staged from Mochiko — her eye height, her
   attention, humans framed whole. At the choosing (cut 14),
   a title-level marker (§3, Scene 3) hands the episode to
   Mochii. After it: camera at Mochii height, attention is
   Mochii's attention, PRESENT poses belong to creatures, and
   Mochiko is staged as Mochii's person. There is no switch
   back within the episode.
5. **The unchosen are not jokes.** Razorgater and Tyrage are
   presented with full ceremony and full dignity. The choosing
   is about fit (game canon spirit), and the script never
   frames either candidate as a wrong answer.
6. **The Dark Forest is presence only.** Background plates,
   patterns stilled, no entry, no PRESENT poses inside it,
   nothing shown. The Wispkin thread stays background exactly
   as EP01 left it (bible §9, open item 1).
7. **Every beat traces to canon** (§1) or is marked TBD (§6).

## 3. THE SCRIPT
### SCENE 1 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — MORNING (cuts 01–05)
*Staged from MOCHIKO. Dominant colour: brand blue.*
**VISUAL:** ESTAB-PAN across the Gate in morning brand-blue.
The keeper board in frame, its painted names held a beat too
short to read (the read is saved for cut 25, and for different
eyes). Gate sign: GARDEN KEEPER.
**ACTION:** Mochiko arrives dressed for an occasion — twin
tails tied neat, capsule backpack squared like a ceremony. She
practises standing still. It does not take. PROF. AEON is
already there, data tablet under one arm, watching the garden
the way other people watch weather. He does not hurry her.
**DIALOGUE:** AEON: "You're early. The garden likes early." /
MOCHIKO: "Is it — do I just — stand somewhere?" / AEON: "You
stand where you are. It comes to you."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** gate creak, morning garden
ambience, tablet soft chime (GAP — cue sheet).
**MUSIC:** Cue T2 (title track, CUE-SHEET.md), warm entry.

### SCENE 2 — EXT. GARDEN GREEN — CONTINUOUS (cuts 06–13)
*Staged from MOCHIKO. Dominant colour: presentation green-gold.*
**VISUAL:** The Green dressed plain and formal — no festivity
invented, the formality is in the spacing. Aeon centre;
Mochiko at the presentation mark; the residents held at a
respectful distance (Dorowani with the Manual; the pilot six
in attendance, background life per LIVING-WORLD).
**ACTION:** The presentation, candidate by candidate, each
given the same full measure. RAZORGATER first: it settles
into its PRESENT pose (Tension — steady, guardian weight,
water-calm) and holds it without showing off. A long cry, low
and even. TYRAGE second: PRESENT pose (Joy — proud, bright,
all forward motion, a creature already halfway to tomorrow),
and a cry like a struck bell. Each candidate is looked at
properly by Mochiko, and by the camera, for exactly as long as
it needs. Third: MOCHII. It attempts its PRESENT pose (Reveal)
and bounces straight through it and out the other side,
recovers, tries again, worse. Mochiko laughs — the first
unrehearsed sound she has made all morning. Aeon's tablet
stays down at his side. HOLD-TENSION: nobody moves the moment
along.
**DIALOGUE:** AEON: "Three lines keep this garden. Any of
them would keep it well." / "You're not picking the best one.
There isn't one. You're picking yours." / MOCHIKO: "...That
one isn't even standing still." / AEON: "No. It isn't."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** presentation spacing — grass,
breath, three cries; presentation chime (GAP — cue sheet).
**MUSIC:** T2 stems, presentation register; stems thin to one
under the third candidate.

### SCENE 3 — EXT. GARDEN POND — CONTINUOUS (cuts 14–16)
*The Choosing. Dominant colour: pond teal. This scene contains
the episode's ONE allowed lighting moment.*
**VISUAL:** The Pond's edge. Candidates at the water; light
coming up through the pond onto faces and fur — the takeover
source is the water itself (LIGHTING-SYSTEM, TK-class).
**ACTION:** Mochiko walks the line once, slowly — Razorgater's
steadiness, Tyrage's brightness, both honoured with a real
look — and stops at Mochii. She kneels. Candidate height. Her
height and its height are the same height now. She does not
announce anything. She holds out one hand, flat, the way the
Manual's keeper holds a hand (care-loop grammar, bible §6).
Mochii looks at the hand. Mochii looks at her. Mochii bounces
once — small, deliberate, nothing like the ceremony bounce —
and lands in front of the hand. Cry: short, certain, up at
the end like nothing about it is a question. THE LIGHTING
TAKEOVER lands with the cry: light through water rises across
both of them, one beat, no more (the episode's one hard moment,
spent here and nowhere else). Mochii completes its Reveal
PRESENT pose — the same pose it wrecked in Scene 2, done
properly this time, for an audience of one. PUNCH-IN lands on
the completed pose.

> ## — THE SWITCH —
> **From this cut, the protagonist of this show is MOCHII.**
> Camera height: Mochii's. Attention: Mochii's. PRESENT poses:
> the creatures'. Mochiko is its person. There is no switch
> back.

**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: (quietly, still kneeling) "Okay. You."
— the last line of the episode spoken from her perspective;
after the switch her lines are heard the way Mochii hears
them: warm, above, and not the point.
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 16 — MOCHII: *"...you picked me."*
(earned moment 1 — the first creature meaning the show has
ever subtitled from inside the creature's own episode.)
**SFX:** pond lap, the one bounce, light-through-water
shimmer (GAP — cue sheet). **MUSIC:** all stems out for the
cry; one stem back under the pose.

### SCENE 4 — EXT. GARDEN GATE / POND / GREEN — DAY (cuts 17–22)
*Staged from MOCHII. Dominant colour: warm amber — the garden
as it looks to someone it belongs to.*
**VISUAL:** The same three locations, re-shot at Mochii
height. The Gate is a mountain with a bell on top. The Pond
is an ocean with light living in it. The Green is a plain
that takes all morning to cross at a bounce. Humans are
framed the way EP01 framed weather: large, kind, partial.
**ACTION:** Mochii's first run of its own garden (ACTION
BEAT B). The residents greet the chosen one in chain, each in
their own grammar, no words anywhere: Dorowani taps the
Manual's cover twice, like a gavel, at Mochii's height this
time. Gabu attempts a formal bow and converts it, mid-fall,
into a chest-beat — played straight. Kittember's tail-tip
flame leans toward Mochii before Kittember does (the sensor
greets first). Sproutle brings a leaf, larger than Sproutle,
delivered with both arms and total ceremony. At the shade's
edge: a drift of smoke that does not approach and is not
approached — Wispkin, background only, EP01's thread resting
exactly where it was left. Mochiko follows behind with the
backpack, carrying things, content.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: (from above, to Aeon, half-heard by
design) "Does it know it's — " / AEON: "It knows."
**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** bounce cycle, gavel tap, leaf
ceremony rustle, garden ambience at low height (GAP — cue
sheet: low-height ambience variant). **MUSIC:** T2 melody
stem, re-registered low and warm.

### SCENE 5 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — EVENING (cuts 23–26)
*Staged from MOCHII. Dominant colour: evening teal-blue.*
**VISUAL:** Last light. The Gate from below, enormous and
homely. The keeper board at the top of Mochii's climb.
**ACTION:** Mochii comes home the long way — past the Pond,
where the water still holds the morning's light a little —
and stops under the keeper board. It looks up. The board,
read properly for the first time all episode, from the height
of someone who lives here now: **two names painted on it**
(EP01 continuity — Mochiko's keeping and Wispkin's night
keeping, the board the whole episode has walked past). Mochii
studies it the way it studied the hand. One bounce, small,
deliberate — the choosing bounce, now a punctuation mark.
**DIALOGUE:** None. **SUBTITLES:** Cut 26 — MOCHII:
*"...ours."* (earned moment 2.) **SFX:** evening crickets,
one bounce, board-creak in the cooling air. **MUSIC:** T2
melody stem solo, out before the button.

### BUTTON — EXT. GARDEN GATE — LAST LIGHT (cuts 27–28)
*Staged from MOCHII. Dominant colour: dusk violet — the first
new colour of the episode arrives with the first rival.*
**VISUAL:** The Gate at last light. A new silhouette in it:
HEX — black hair with a purple streak, amber eyes catching
what light is left, black-and-purple high-collar jacket, still
the way Tyrage is never still.
**ACTION:** Hex looks past the humans entirely and addresses
the garden's new chosen one directly — the first human besides
Mochiko to treat Mochii as the one you talk to. The challenge
is issued politely, precisely, and entirely for TOMORROW.
Mochii, at the bottom of the gate it just learned was a
mountain, completes a PRESENT pose (Tension — its first) and
answers with a cry that is not small.
**DIALOGUE:** HEX: "So you're the one the garden picked." /
"Tomorrow, then. Bring your keeper." — (beat) — "I'll bring
mine."
**SUBTITLES:** None — the third slot stays unspent; a rival's
words are words, and words don't get intertitles.
**SFX:** gate in still air, one steady cry. **MUSIC:** None.
Cut on the pose. (Season note: the challenge is paid off at
EP04's dojo sparring. EP03 comes first.)
