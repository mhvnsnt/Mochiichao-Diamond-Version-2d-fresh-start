# EP02 REVISION NOTE — v1 → v2 ("Choose, and Be Chosen")

**What this is:** the record of the EP02 rebuild. v1
(`show/episodes/EP02/EP02-SHOOTING-SCRIPT-v1.md`, three-
candidate Garden ceremony) is superseded **in part** by v2
(`EP02-SHOOTING-SCRIPT-v2.md`). The rebuild was directed by
the season canon reconciliation already on main —
`SEASON-CANON-MAP.md` ("EP02 reconciliation — canon wins")
and `SEASON-ARC-NOTE-EP01-EP10.md` Note A — and executed
here. Every change below names its canon source. Kept items
are listed too, so the delta can be audited both ways.

## THE STRUCTURAL CHANGE
v1 told the game's choosing as a **Garden ceremony**: Aeon
presents three starter lines on the Green, Mochiko picks
Mochii at the Pond. Canon's opening is a different shape
entirely — an **arrival and a rescue**. v2 follows the
locked ink's own beat order: truck → house/clock →
neighbour → cornering → rescue-choosing → lab → boots.
The episode's spine (follow the human → the choosing →
THE SWITCH at cut 14 → the creature's episode) is
unchanged; everything the spine hangs on was rebuilt.
