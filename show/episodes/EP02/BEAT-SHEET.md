## PERSPECTIVE — the episode's spine (owner design, 2026-10-27)
The episode follows **Mochiko** as the apparent protagonist:
her morning, her nerves, her choice. The moment she chooses
Mochii, the perspective **switches** — camera height, attention,
PRESENT poses and staging all re-key to **Mochii**. From that
beat on, the show belongs to the creature; Mochiko becomes its
person. The switch is marked in the shooting script as a
title-level event (§THE SWITCH), not a subtle drift. This is
the series' true premise arriving: EP01 was Mochiko's first
morning; EP02 is where the show changes hands.

**6-beat engine (episode formula, choosing-day shape):**
1. **Arrival (0:00–0:50)** — Choosing day. Mochiko returns to
   the Gate dressed for an occasion (capsule backpack, twin
   tails tied neat) and finds Prof. Aeon already waiting —
   tablet in hand, unhurried, like the Garden itself asked him.
   He is the game's professor figure; the Garden's keepers are
   chosen with his witness. The keeper board already carries
   two names (EP01 continuity — see beat 6).
2. **The presentation (0:50–2:30)** — On the Green, Aeon
   presents the three starter lines, one at a time, each with
   full ceremony: Razorgater's line (004) — steady, water-calm,
   a keeper's guardian shape; Tyrage's line — proud, bright,
   all forward motion. Each candidate is given a real PRESENT
   pose and a real moment. They are not obstacles to the right
   answer; in the game's spirit, the choosing is about **fit**,
   and Aeon says so in as few words as possible.
3. **The third candidate (2:30–3:15)** — Mochii's line (001)
   is presented last — smallest, roundest, and incapable of
   standing still for its own ceremony: it bounces through its
   PRESENT pose and out the other side. Mochiko laughs before
   she decides anything. Aeon's tablet stays down. HOLD-TENSION:
   the garden, the candidates, and the keeper all wait.
4. **The choosing (3:15–3:45)** — At the Pond's edge, Mochiko
   kneels to candidate height — her height, their height, the
   same height the camera is about to live at — and chooses
   Mochii. THE SWITCH: the episode's one takeover-grade
   lighting beat (light through water, LIGHTING-SYSTEM) lands
   as Mochii answers with a cry and its completed Reveal
   PRESENT pose; the PUNCH-IN lands on the pose, and from this
   cut the camera is Mochii's.
5. **Mochii's garden (3:45–5:15)** — The Garden re-introduced
   at Mochii's height and at Mochii's attention: the Gate is a
   mountain with a bell on it, the Pond is an ocean with light
   in it, the residents are enormous and wonderful. Dorowani,
   Gabu, Kittember and Sproutle greet the chosen one their own
   ways (no words — performance only). Wispkin is present only
   as EP01 left it: a background thread, smoke at the shade's
   edge, untouched. Mochiko walks behind, carrying the
   backpack, demoted to luggage — and visibly content about it.
6. **Home + board (5:15–5:45)** — Evening. Mochii at its new
   home in the Garden looks back at the Gate — and notices the
   keeper board now has **two names** on it. CONTINUITY
   (EP01): the board it bounced past all episode, read properly
   for the first time, from the height of someone who lives
   here now. SUBTITLE (earned moment 2) lands here.

**Button (5:45–6:00) — HEX.** At the Gate, at last light, a
figure the episode has not introduced: Hex — black hair with a
purple streak, amber eyes, high collar, all angles where
Mochiko is all rounds. A challenge, issued politely and
precisely, for **tomorrow**: Mochii answers it (cry, PRESENT
pose, Mochii's camera). Note for the season grid: the challenge
is paid off at EP04's dojo sparring — EP03 comes first; the
button plants, it does not skip the queue.

**Action beats (motion budget):** (A) Beat 4 — the choosing:
Mochii's full Reveal PRESENT pose + bounce answered at
candidate height, the episode's one lighting takeover riding
it. (B) Beat 5 — Mochii's first run of its own garden at its
own height, full-body, residents reacting in chain.
