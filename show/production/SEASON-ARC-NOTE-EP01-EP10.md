## 2. THE ARCS ACROSS EP01–EP10

- **The Fragment run (the spine):** 0 → 1 (EP06, Core) → 2
  (EP07, Wind) → 3 (EP08) → 4–5 (EP09) → 6–7 (EP10) → **[1 door
  left]**. The eight-squares keeper board, introduced as
  furniture in EP06 Scene 1, is the season's visual ledger —
  the audience can always count the run at a glance.
- **Champion Hex arc:** neighbour/rival (EP02 button) →
  challenger answered in the Garden's custom (EP04, a draw, a
  shared mark, dinner) → score-keeper at a distance (EP07
  relay: "The garden learns fast") → a past presence in the
  wider world (EP08 wall mark) → *known* by the wider world's
  old houses (EP09: Phantom — "Tell Hex his Dorowani is well
  kept") → approached as weather (EP10: "the champion's road
  starts asking your names" — champion still unnamed on
  screen). The arc's shape: Hex gets *bigger* the further the
  Garden travels, without ever re-entering a room. The finale
  block inherits a rival the audience has been measuring all
  season — which is what canon needs, because canon's Hex is
  not a late reveal; he is the region's Champion from the
  first data file. *When the show names that on screen* is
  the owner's call (ledger §3.6).
- **Syndicate thread:** unseen (EP01–EP06; at most a §EP06.5
  background plant) → a *voice* claiming flows at the docks
  (EP07, canon line, undecoded) → *paperwork*: claims filed on
  Stoneport (EP08 radio) → paper nailed inside a market stall
  and "new paper" on a dojo's custom (EP09) → an *open door in
  the home town* (EP10 button) → **the boss behind Dojo 8's
  desk (finale block)**. Menace escalates by *medium* — voice,
  paper, architecture, person — and by proximity: docks →
  market → home town → the dojo system itself. The two-layer
  rule holds throughout: no ordinary character ever decodes
  the hacker layer.
- **The signature grammar (show-lane move doctrine):** each
  signature is *learned by observation* (Leaf Blast, EP06),
  *recognised as innate* (Tremor EP08, Dark Wave EP09), or
  *practised across episodes then spent* (Water Orb: EP08
  fountain drill → EP10; Fire Stream EP10 after a season of
  the flame-as-instrument). By EP10 the audience has been
  taught the doctrine three ways and expects the fourth —
  which is the exact expectation the finale block's battles
  should be built on. Rare Moves arrive known; General Moves
  are earned in the world; no machine appears in the show
  lane, ever (the game's General/Rare machine system is the
  *game* lane's business — two-lane doctrine).
- **The Forest / Secret thread:** stinger stillness (EP01) →
  presence only (EP02–EP04) → the lantern answer (EP05: the
  leaf turns; meaning unproven) → *surface* opened as family
  country (EP07: hum, wild Kittember, the dojo door) while the
  *depths* stay shut → not touched again in EP08–EP10 (the
  map spends no Forest capital during the dojo run — dread,
  like Rule 12, is rationed). Aeon's "long before you" (EP05)
  and Luigi's hint (EP10) are the Secret's two human threads;
  they are not connected on screen, by design.
- **Mochii's growth (the protagonist line):** chosen (EP02) →
  first outing (EP03) → wins by stopping (EP04) → brave by
  not bouncing (EP05) → finds its place is the whole floor
  (EP06) → learns patience from the wind (EP07) → reads its
  own habits in a mirror (EP08) → picks the moment, not the
  strength (EP09, as the picker) → watches its friends be
  great without eclipse (EP10). The finale block inherits a
  protagonist whose every lesson is a *battle* lesson in
  embryo — Dojo 8 and the Plateau should cash them in order.
