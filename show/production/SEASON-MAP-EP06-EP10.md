## 0. THE RULES THIS WHOLE MAP OBEYS
1. **Creatures make pure cries and sounds only.** No words, no
   name-saying (owner-decided 2026-10-27). Word-dialogue belongs
   to humans only. Battle humans are **RANGERS** (term locked):
   Mochiko and Hex command as Rangers; a Ranger's command is a
   decisive beat *inside* a continuous fight, never a turn order.
2. **Battles are autonomous-action.** Mochi fight with their own
   agency, continuously; the fight runs between and without
   commands, anime-style (battle doctrine, owner 2026-10-27).
   No turn-waiting, no idle sprites, in any dojo scene below.
3. **Subtitles are silent-film intertitles** — earned moments
   only, ration max three per episode. Every episode below
   spends exactly **two**; the third slot is held unspent
   (EP02–EP05 pattern). All intertitle texts below are PROPOSED.
4. **Two-lane move doctrine.** The SHOW learns moves the anime
   way: training, practice, observation, tutors. Rare Moves are
   innate/prestige. No machine discs, no TM/HM objects, anywhere
   below. Signature moves are per-species canon only: Mochii
   HYDRO RUSH, Sproutle LEAF BLAST, Gabu TREMOR, Wispkin DARK
   WAVE, Dorowani WATER ORB, Kittember FIRE STREAM (display
   names; internal ids unchanged).
5. **Names as displayed in-world:** THE DARK FOREST (never the
   old data name), IRIDESCENCE (the region; game data name
   Mainframe), MOCHIIOTECA (the device; design still TBD —
   abstract light + icons + chime only), ILLUMINATE (the
   technique; never the old data term), CHAMPIONS DOJO,
   MOCHI FRUIT, MOCHII ISLES, Silicon Town (keeps its name).
   Route display names are the gemstone names — ROUTE TOPAZ /
   ROUTE EMERALD — **which gemstone maps to which numbered
   route is not stated in the sources read for this map:
   flagged TBD, not assigned (§6 of EP06 and the arc note).**
6. **THE SECRET stays secret in-world.** The world is a computer
   that grew a wilderness — production docs may hold this;
   no inhabitant knows, no in-world artifact betrays it. Aeon
   may slip and correct himself (production note only, never
   staged as a reveal); Luigi may hint without knowing what he
   holds; Syndicate hacker-speak is double-layered and is never
   decoded by ordinary characters.
7. **GAPS ARE SACRED.** Canon gaps are carried as named TBD /
   NAME GAP items in §6 — never silently filled. Preserved
   throughout this map: the dojo-count contradiction (the data's
   count block says **6 dojos**; its own beats award badges
   **1–8** and flag `victory_gate_badges_8` — the beats are
   followed for plotting, the contradiction is NOT resolved);
   the Dojo 8 leader's name (canon gives only "Syndicate boss
   as dojo leader" — NAME GAP); what stopped moving in the Dark
   Forest; what EP05's turned leaf meant.
8. **Rule 12 is not quoted in EP06–EP10.** It was spent three
   times in EP01 and left to sit in EP02–EP05; the dojo run does
   not spend it. If the owner wants it in the finale block, it
   goes in exact wording only (bible §5).
9. **Kittember's flame is on the tail tip** — attached, always.
   **No baked shadows** in any character art described below —
   shadows are composite-stage work.
10. **Forest discipline evolves, honestly.** EP01–EP05: camera
    never enters. EP07 is the season's first entry — onto the
    Forest's *surface path only* (mist, the hum, the dojo door
    the game canon puts there). The **depths** stay exactly as
    EP01–EP05 held them: no entry, no PRESENT poses, nothing
    shown, cycles OFF. The surface/depth distinction is stated
    on screen by staging, never by exposition about the Secret.

## 0. THE RULES THIS EPISODE OBEYS
1. Map rules §0.1–§0.10 apply in full.
2. **This is the show's first real dojo battle** — and it must
   teach the audience the autonomous-action doctrine by showing
   it: the fight is continuous; Mochiko's one Ranger command
   lands as the decisive beat *inside* it, not as its start.

   exact, fair, and — under the exactness — testing fit, not

   Mochiko: "The Mochi Garden is open now. Raise them here,
   feed them, race them, breed them. A creature you raise is
   stronger than a creature you only catch." (R1-BEAT3.) It is
   the only long human line in the episode, by design.
5. **Sproutle's LEAF BLAST is learned the show way:** by
   watching Lin's partner work a leaf-line drill in Scene 3 and
   practising it wrong, then right, across Scenes 4–5. No disc,
   no download, no sudden mastery.
6. **The Mochiioteca stays abstract** (light + icons + chime).
   In Silicon Town it records the town's creatures in passing —
   it records; it does not solve, translate, or explain.

### SCENE 3 — INT. CHAMPIONS DOJO, CORE GATE — DAY (cuts 11–15)

**VISUAL:** A dojo like a machine housing: floor squares, light

**SUBTITLES:** None. **SFX:** drill footfalls, floor-square

metronome-adjacent line (MAKE note: rhythm is the scene's score).

### SCENE 4 — INT. CHAMPIONS DOJO — CONTINUOUS (cuts 16–20)
*The battle, first movement. Autonomous-action doctrine on
full display.*
**VISUAL:** The ring of squares. Camera lives with Mochii
(creature-led staging, EP04 pattern).
**ACTION:** THE BATTLE OPENS WITHOUT A COMMAND — Lin's partner
and Mochii are already moving when the custom's start-tone
finishes; the fight is a continuous living thing: feints,
repositioning, water-play vs. form (EP04's grammar at official
stakes). Lin's partner scores by form, twice, cleanly. Between
the exchanges, at the ring's edge, Sproutle practises the
leaf-line it saw — wrong (leaves scatter like confetti), wrong
again (one leaf, spinning home to stick in Gabu's net-hat),
and the third attempt *begins* to hold a line. Nobody stops
the battle to teach it. That is the show's move law, staged.
**DIALOGUE:** LIN: (coaching, exact) "Again. On the line."
— the EP04 Hex rhyme, deliberate: form-keepers speak alike.
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 18 — SPROUTLE: *"...Not scatter. Line."*
(earned moment 1 — the featured creature naming its own lesson.)
**SFX:** battle bed (continuous, no turn-gaps), leaf scatter,
net-hat *thwip*. **MUSIC:** investigation pulse, metronome stem
under it, tightening.

### SCENE 5 — INT. CHAMPIONS DOJO — CONTINUOUS (cuts 21–25)
*The decisive beat.*
**VISUAL:** Same key, deepening. The floor squares lit in lanes.
**ACTION:** Lin's partner drives Mochii toward the square-grid's
edge — the machine closing. **THE RANGER BEAT:** Mochiko,
who has not spoken since Scene 3, gives ONE command — not a
move name, a *trust*: she calls Mochii *off* the squares
entirely, onto the dojo's water channel (the machine's own
part, unused all battle). Mochii answers mid-motion — the
fight never stops — and HYDRO RUSH runs the channel's length:
the part that didn't fit the squares was a *water* part, and
the machine had a place for it all along. Lin calls the bout
before the final exchange lands — the dojo is satisfied; the
fit is proven, which was the test. Sproutle, at the edge,
completes its first true LEAF BLAST line — leaves in formation,
past Lin, who inclines her head to it once: the tutor
acknowledging the student the dojo never enrolled.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: (the command, quiet) "Mochii. The
water. *Your* way in." LIN: "You fit. Take the Core Fragment."
(canon, quoted.)
**SUBTITLES:** Cut 24 — MOCHII: *"...My square is the whole
floor."* (earned moment 2.)
**SFX:** channel water rising, Hydro Rush run, the call (all
battle sound cuts for one beat — EP04's stop, rhymed).
**MUSIC:** all stems out for the command; one warm stem back
under the award.

### SCENE 6 — EXT. GARDEN GATE — DUSK (cuts 26–28) + BUTTON
*Dominant colour: evening teal-blue.*
**VISUAL:** Home. The keeper board; the chalked row of eight

**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "The Mochi Garden is open now. Raise
them here, feed them, race them, breed them. A creature you
raise is stronger than a creature you only catch." (canon,
R1-BEAT3, quoted in full — §0.4.)
**SUBTITLES:** None — the third slot stays unspent; a canon

crickets, the one chime. **MUSIC:** T1 melody stem solo, out.

   wind, politely, and the episode is about what answers.
