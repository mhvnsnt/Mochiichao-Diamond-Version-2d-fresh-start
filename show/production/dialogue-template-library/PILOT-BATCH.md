## REFUSALS — DO NOT ADAPT (Rule 7)

Spotted in the banks while cataloguing; excluded from this batch and from
production use, with reasons. This list is the library's immune system —
extend it whenever a reviewer recognises another.

1. **The shorts boast** (Gen 1 route youngster: comfort-and-ease clothing
   manifesto). The beat *is* the fame — a young Ranger praising his outfit
   reads as quotation no matter the words. P-16 was deliberately built on a
   different claim (an undefeated streak that isn't).
2. **"Light years" pedantry** (Gen 1 gym underling's end-line). Iconic
   correction-as-punchline; any version is the same joke wearing our coat.
3. **The villain team motto** (Gen 1, rhymed prepare-for-trouble couplet).
   A rhymed villain motto in our mouths would be recognisable in two lines.
   Our Syndicate speaks in manifests and schedules instead — menace by
   paperwork, which is ours.
4. **The professor's opening questions** (Gen 1/3: the world-lecture that
   ends by asking the player's name and presenting the boy/girl choice as a
   joke beat). Our opening is already locked and written; the skeleton is
   retired, not recycled.
5. **The mansion journals** (Gen 1: dated lab-diary entries about a created
   creature). The *function* (writing as a lock, F12) may be templated
   fresh; these entries' cadence and content are signature story text and
   were not touched.
6. **The rival's farewell catchphrase** (Gen 1: smell-based goodbye). Any
   variant is a signature. Hex keeps score instead — that mechanism is
   already his in canon.
7. **Ghost-interrogation exchange** (Gen 1 tower: "do you believe in…").
   The yes/no haunting bit is a set piece audiences quote; our Deep Sector
   night register (P-03) was built from the F1 skeleton instead.
8. **Cry-as-name text blocks throughout the sources** (creature text written
   as the species name plus syllables). Refused as a *class* under Rule 4 —
   Mochi cries are sound-words, never names. Every adapted creature block in
   this batch (P-02, P-29) follows the refusal.
9. **Storage-PC / network system text** (all gens: accessing a computer to
   move creatures between boxes). Refused as a *wording class* under Rule 3;
   the underlying service (overflow storage at the MochiiPlex) is re-housed
   as shelves and a keeper's ledger before any adaptation — see F2/F7 notes
   in TAXONOMY.md. The one licensed home for machine vocabulary is F8.
