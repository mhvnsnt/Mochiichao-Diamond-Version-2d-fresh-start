## Native spec — `mochiichao/anti-hero-route/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Content parity is the law of the route.** A thief
  playthrough runs start to finish on the same world
  the hero route runs: regions reachable (#127 —
  discovery does not ask your morals), dojos and story
  beats present, an ending reachable. What differs is
  **which doors are open**: List 28's two economies —
  the respectable world's (its prices, its trust, its
  licences) and the underworld's (#274's fences and
  contacts) — trade places as the ladder moves the
  Ranger. A door closed by reputation is replaced by a
  door reputation opens; a route with only closures is
  a punishment demo, and this spec forbids the shape.
  (Sonic Adventure 2's hero/dark structure is the
  owner's named shape: two full campaigns through one
  world, neither a tutorial for the other.)
- **The route's beats are ladder beats.** The thief
  route's story structure is written against List 28
  state, in both lanes: befriend (#280) → steal (#268)
  → be seen or not be seen (#272's split) → fence or
  keep (#274/#284) → fall further or turn (#275). An
  authored beat names the ladder positions it is
  written for (a beat that only makes sense while the
  world still trusts him is labelled so, in the beat's
  own record) — the SA2/JoJo lesson applied at beat
  scale: the same world, honestly different stories
  depending on where on the ladder the lead stands.
- **Consequences are systems, not taxes.** Nothing in
  the route adds difficulty for its own sake: no stat
  penalty, no encounter tax, no "evil" surcharge beyond
  what #269/#270's documented effects already do
  (prices and reactions are reputation facts, in both
  directions — the underworld's discounts are as real
  as the respectable world's). If a consequence cannot
  be cited to a List 28 system, it is not in the route.
- **The ending forks on #275, honestly.** The route
  can end in the fall (the ladder's bottom, reached by
  deeds) or in redemption (the long climb, at #275's
  documented cost — never instant, never a cutscene
  pardon). Both endings are endings: full content,
  recorded in the world's history the same way (#126's
  append-only law does not grade its records). Which
  ending a given playthrough or season reaches is
  play/writing; that both exist at full size is this
  spec.
