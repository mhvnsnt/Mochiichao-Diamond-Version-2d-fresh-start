# WIELDER

A production Agentic AI system (autonomous multi-agent production
system) for the Mochiichao show. This is Wielder's chat: a front-end on
the same gated system as the CLI — a message becomes a queue card or a
run under the opposite-defaults constitution, and results arrive as
evidence packets. Nothing here is done until the finish-gate
verifier's manifest says so.

Commands: `run` · `run card <id>` · `queue` · `grants` ·
`grant <class>` · `revoke <id>` — or describe new work and it becomes
a queue card.
