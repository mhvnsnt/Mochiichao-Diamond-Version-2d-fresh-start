# #349 — THE RESCUE CONFIRMATION → the same throw, the other moral shape

**One mechanism, two shapes — and the device
knows which one you're standing in.** #268's
addendum founded this beside the theft prompt: the
same physical throw, aimed at a Mochi whose heart
is closed, prompts as a **rescue**. This spec
builds that shape out: what the device reads, what
the prompt says, what a confirmed rescue writes
(and never writes), and the dark mirror — what the
Syndicate's stripped devices do instead, and why
that absence is itself information.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/rescue-confirmation/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The heart read decides the shape.** A snag-class
  throw's prompt class is chosen by the target's
  state, read before any prompt is composed:
  - Target **owned, heart open** → the theft shape
    (#347's founding member): "Are you sure you
    want to try and steal this person's Mochi?"
  - Target **heart closed** (#287's heart record —
    whatever the keeper seat says) → the rescue
    shape: the device states what it sees first —
    the heart is closed — and the confirmation
    frames the act as rescue.
  - Target **wild, heart open** → no prompt at all:
    ordinary capture (#347's never-list).
  The read is the device's own (#287's Aura-Reader
  function — identify class), and it is the *only*
  input to the choice: the player's intent is never
  consulted, because the record judges acts by what
  they were done to. A rescue throw at a closed
  heart that happens to be stolen property is a
  rescue in the ledger; the custody question is
  settled afterwards, by #288/#273, not at the
  throw.
- **The rescue prompt's shape.** Same family shape
  as #347: one question, two answers, NO is free
  and writes nothing. The prompt's content differs
  in kind, not just wording: the theft prompt asks
  about *your* act; the rescue prompt first **tells
  you a fact** (the device states the closure it
  sees — a gauge band, never a fake-precise number,
  per #287's read discipline) and then asks about
  your act. The information is not a reward for
  pressing YES — it is shown as part of the asking,
  because a Ranger who backs out still deserves to
  know what they saw. (Copy is #23 string data; the
  fact-statement slot is structural — the fragment
  carries the shape, content-lane writing fills the
  words in the owner's plain register.)
- **What a confirmed rescue writes — and never
  writes.** A confirmed rescue snag runs #268's
  math (the device is the same device) with the
  moral vocabulary switched whole:
  - **No morale entry.** #269's addendum already
    rules it: confirmed rescue attempts write no
    morale row — not a cost, and not a credit
    either. The credit, if the story earns one,
    lives downstream (#288's opening, #273's
    recovery, #275's restitution rows) where the
    *completed* good is scored; the throw itself is
    morally silent.
  - **No theft vocabulary.** Success appends no
    `theft` entry; the keeping-state it opens is
    #288's road (a held-for-purification state in
    the rescuer's keeping, custody per #245/#220
    discipline where those apply), and the true
    keeper citation stays on the record untouched.
  - **Failure is free of everything but the item.**
    A failed rescue throw consumes the item like any
    capture attempt and writes nothing moral, legal,
    or reputational — there is no attempted-rescue
    deed (#348's class boundary: rescue attempts are
    outside the attempt class, because there is no
    completed dark deed for them to be the attempt
    *of*).
- **The stripped device — the absence as a tell.**
  The Syndicate's snag gear (#268's NPC tiers)
  carries no heart read: that is what "stripped"
  means in mechanism. Consequences, stated as
  system facts:
  - A Syndicate throw at a closed heart **prompts
    as theft or not at all** — never as rescue,
    because the device cannot see what would make
    it one. In player terms this matters when the
    player holds captured Syndicate gear (item data
    carries the reader's presence or absence — the
    gear's class is on its #31 record, not in a
    menu): a throw made with a stripped device is
    scored by the record as the throw it was —
    against a closed heart, an unseeing taking is
    still a taking, and #269 reads it as theft,
    because the ledger judges the act, not the
    tool's marketing.
  - **The absence is staged.** In the show lane,
    the missing beat is a tell the audience learns
    to read: the hero's device *pauses and speaks*
    before a hard throw; the Syndicate's never
    does. #276 carries the grammar; the system fact
    it rests on is founded here — a device that
    cannot see hearts cannot hesitate.

## Show lane

Covered above and in #276: the rescue prompt is
the game's version of the show's oldest staging
rule for this material — the audience must always
know, before the throw, whether they are watching
a theft or a rescue. The device read is how the
game tells its player the same thing the camera
tells the audience.

## Wiring

#245/#220 discipline where the rescue is a service
act. Device capability (reader present/absent) is
#31 item data — proposed as a record field beside
the snag class flag (STAGING step 3).
