## Wiring points (what "wired" means here, concretely)

1. **Animscripts ↔ moves.json / registry:** `move_id` + `display_name`
   are the join keys; `registry_anim_id` is read from moves.json at
   build time. New performances append to the move's one file —
   the variance registry the doctrine requires is the collection of
   `variance_notes` across those files.
2. **Animscripts ↔ battle UI:** the BATTLE-UI-FLOW queue's
   `animscript` item references these files by move_id + species
   performance block; the beat map is the sync contract for the
   damage item that follows it.
3. **Animscripts ↔ cries:** `cry` cues resolve through the species
   sheet's cry spec (demonstrated by the Flickfin override) — no
   audio is stored in animscripts.
4. **Lustre ↔ avatar-stack:** adopting the proposal = adding the
   `palette_variants` block emission to `generate_bundle.py` and a
   palette-map field to the species-sheet apparatus. Both are
   one-spot changes in existing files; they are **proposed, not
   made** — the avatar-stack contract is the design lane's to bump,
   and the bump should land with the first real lustre (authored from
   canon art, per the style law: batch-001 art is rejected-style and
   must not become the exemplar).
5. **Palette tool performance:** before lustre *clips* are produced,
   `palette_variant.py` needs a LUT/numpy fast path (per-pixel Python
   measured ~18 s on one 1920×1280 still). Poster-grade use is fine
   as shipped.
