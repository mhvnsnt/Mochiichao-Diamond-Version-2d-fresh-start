# #180 — BOND EFFECTS IN BATTLE → every edge declared, or it does not exist

**Doctrine seat:** bond is already a quantity in this
programme — it accrues in #39's effort ledger (the
`bond` family: check-ins, shared outings) and is
already an operand elsewhere (`{bondCount: n}` in #8's
bond-class growth edges). What no package defines is
what bond *does* inside a battle. This spec defines
it under the programme's hardest honesty law, verified
at its source: **#70-DIFFICULTY refuses hidden
rubber-banding in writing** — "outcome math is fixed
and auditable… no hidden modifiers, no loss-streak
mercy math." (The commissioning brief cited this
refusal as #76's; #76 is the web-export spec. The
refusal lives in #70, and #70 is what binds here —
the correction is registered in STAGING.) So: bond's
battle effects are presentation-first, and any
mechanical edge must be a **declared data row with a
visible cue** — or it does not exist.

**What was studied.** Bond/friendship effects in the
publicly documented record: at high bond, creatures in
the reference games gain presentation warmth (closer
field behaviour, responsive cries) and, in later
documented forms, mechanical edges — enduring a hit
that would faint them, shedding conditions, elevated
critical rates — applied as **invisible probability
bends** the player cannot audit. Mochiichao takes the
presentation half wholesale and refuses the invisible
half: the same edges are only ever admissible here in
declared, visible form.

## Native spec — the bond-effects table (shape in prose; no fragment)

- **The operand (one bond, one read).** Battle reads
  bond as #39's accrued `bond` family value — the
  auditable quantity whose trail is #28's `careLog`.
  #28's profile also carries an affection-class drive
  slot, but that is a *need gauge*, not the bond
  operand; two systems reading two different "bonds"
  is forbidden by this sentence: **mechanics read
  #39's; presentation may read either, and says which.**
- **The declaration law (the system's whole law).** A
  bond effect exists **iff** a row exists in the
  bond-effects table (data, beside the battle systems):

  ```
  row { minBond: <value | OPEN>, hook: <target>,
        cue: <presentation beat id> }
  ```

  `hook` names exactly one target: a **stage of #17's
  pipeline** (the #107 discipline — bond composes in
  the pipeline; it carries no math of its own), a
  **#10 queue/presentation behaviour**, or a **#155
  cry priority class**. `cue` names the #9 animscript /
  presentation beat that makes the effect *visible
  when it fires*. A hook with no cue is a data error:
  an effect the audience cannot see happen is the
  invisible bend #70 refuses, whatever its numbers.
- **Rows instanced: zero.** Thresholds (`minBond`) are
  owner/balance data; this spec instances no edge —
  not even the documented survival-class edge. If an
  owner pass ever wants one, it lands as a row whose
  cue stages the endurance visibly (the creature is
  *seen* to hold on — presentation carries the
  explanation, the value sits in data), and only then
  does it exist.
- **Presentation effects (the sanctioned yield, still
  row-gated).** The table's natural first citizens,
  when the owner populates it: #10 `LIVING_FIELD`
  tempo reads (a bonded partner fights closer, checks
  back), #155 `answer`-class cry priority for the
  bonded individual, #152-profile field reads (a
  bonded individual stays near its Ranger — #58
  follower texture). Each is a row; unrowed, none of
  them fire either. Presentation effects write nothing
  (#10's architectural guardrail: the living field has
  no write path to battle state).
- **Battle never moves bond.** No accrual in battle
  (#179's law: battle is not a care activity), and no
  loss either — a faint is not a bond injury in data.
  Bond enters the battle as a read and leaves
  unchanged. What changes bond is care, in the Garden,
  on #28's log.

## Show lane

Bond in the show is character, not system: the
depicted care (#39's montage vocabulary — feeding,
check-ins, outings, sparring) and its visible warmth
in fights under the autonomous-action doctrine. No
table, no thresholds, no numbers on screen — and no
show fight may be *decided* by a bond mechanic,
because in the show lane there are none.

## Wiring

Reads #39's ledger (bond family) via the individual
record; the table sits beside #107's registry in the
battle-systems home; hooks resolve into #17 stages,
#10 behaviours, #155 classes; cues are #9 animscript
beats. Landing demonstrates the negative: with an
empty table, a maximum-bond and a zero-bond individual
resolve identical battles under identical commands
(#70's auditability, made a test).

## Coverage — honest

- **Architecture: fully documented**, including the
  invisible-edge pattern this spec deliberately
  refuses.
- **Contents: an empty table, on purpose.** The yield
  is the declaration law and the single-read rule —
  the guarantee that whatever bond ever does in a
  battle, Jamar can read it in data and see it on
