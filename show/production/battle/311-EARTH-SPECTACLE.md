# #311 — ROCK, EARTH & GROUND SPECTACLE → the ground itself performs

**The gap this fills.** The earth family's grammar:
how power that comes from *the planet* is staged.
Where strength (#310) is a body's mass, this family
is the ground's own mass answering — strata splitting,
dust veils, the horizon itself moving. The family
holds a seed performance: Gabu's **Tremor** — the
ground-shaking signature, owner canon (#131's
register: Tremor is Gabu's alone).

**How the reference record fills the same gap:** the
documented earth-attack staging in the record is
always *architectural*: fissures with a direction,
pillars with a grain, dust that behaves like weather.
Earth is never an effect *on* the ground; the ground
is the performer. The games agree in their own idiom
— the type is literally named for the ground, and its
greatest moves are staged as the field itself turning
hostile. The kit taken: the plate is an actor; the
performer is the one who *asks* it.

## Staging grammar — `mochiichao/spectacle-earth/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The build (the deep before).** Earth telegraphs
  through the feet first: a low sound under the bed
  (#215's low family, felt more than heard), grit
  dancing on stone, small stones hopping in place —
  then stillness of the wrong kind. The build is
  longer than other families' tells because the mass
  is the largest: the audience is being told the
  *planet* is loading.
- **The performance (strata, direction, horizon).**
  Fissures run *somewhere* — a crack has a direction
  and a destination, and staging it wandering is a
  defect. Pillars and slabs rise with their grain
  showing (layered stone reads as strata, never as
  grey boxes). At signature class and above, the
  family's exclusive register opens: **the horizon
  moves** — the far line of the world tips, dust
  veils roll across it, the sky's edge is briefly
  wrong. Nothing else in the list is allowed the
  horizon; it is how the audience knows this family,
  at full stretch, out-scales the frame.
- **Dust veils (the aftermath with a memory).** Earth
  leaves the air full: dust veils hang, drift, and
  settle over following beats — visibility itself is
  scene state while they last (the mist family's
  cousin: #252's `MIST` hides by weather; dust hides
  by consequence). Settled dust coats the plate —
  like #309's wetness, the place stays marked.
- **The underground is present.** The family's grammar
  always implies the below: sounds arrive through the
  ground before through the air; a burrower's approach
  is staged as the surface's small betrayals (a line
  of rising dust, grass shivering in sequence); a
  performance from below begins with the ground
  *bulging* — the world pregnant with the performer.
  Even unseen, what's underground is in the scene.
  (Depths fence: in the Dark Forest's depths, the
  surface grammar applies only where canon stocks the
  place — the #128 depths plate ships empty and this
  family does not fill it.)

## Rock vs earth vs ground — one family, three tempers

The brief names them together and the grammar keeps
them distinct *inside* the family: **rock** is earth's
hard register (edges, strata, things that shatter per
#310's debris honesty), **earth** is the mass register
(slabs, rises, burial-weight), **ground** is the
*surface* register (tremor, dust, the field as a
membrane over the deep). A performance declares its
temper in the script; Tremor is ground-temper, and
#316 owns how Gabu's differs from any other ground
performance.

## The discipline

- **The ground never hurries.** Earth staged at
  lightning tempo is a defect (#308's grammar is a
  different family's); even a fast fissure *arrives*
  with weight — cracks race, but the slabs they free
  fall like slabs.
- **Direction or nothing.** A fissure, a slide, a
  dust-line without a readable direction reads as
  noise; every ground cue names where it is going.
- **The horizon is rationed.** Horizon movement is
  signature-class and above only, and never twice in
  one battle (#307's climax economy in family form).
