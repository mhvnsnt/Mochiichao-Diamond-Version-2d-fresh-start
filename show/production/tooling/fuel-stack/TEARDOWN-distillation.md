# TEARDOWN — Distillation (fuel-stack item 3)

*What it is, in plain language: you run one big, smart model (the
**teacher**) and train a small model (the **student**) to copy how
the teacher answers — not just its final words, but, in the strong
form, the teacher's whole probability distribution over next words.
The student ends up behaving like a model several times its size on
the tasks it was distilled on, at a fraction of the memory and cost.
It is how the industry turns one expensive brain into many cheap
ones, and it is the purest "make your own fuel" move in this stack:
a weekend of teacher time becomes a permanent, owned, small model.*

## How it works (the mechanics that matter)

1. **Sequence-level (offline) distillation** — the simple, robust
   form. The teacher generates answers to a large prompt set; the
   student is fine-tuned on those question→answer pairs like normal
   SFT. What the student learns is the teacher's *behaviour*.
   Weakness: the student only ever sees teacher-perfect text, so at
   run time its own small mistakes can compound (exposure bias).
2. **Distribution-level (logit) distillation** — the student is
   trained to match the teacher's next-token probability
   distribution (soft targets), usually with KL divergence, often at
   a temperature that softens both distributions so the "dark
   knowledge" — the teacher's ranking of plausible alternatives —
   transfers. This is the original Hinton form and what GKD
   generalises.
3. **On-policy distillation (GKD)** — the student generates its OWN
   answers during training and the teacher scores/ corrects those,
   so the student learns on the distribution it will actually
   produce. TRL's GKDTrainer implements this with the generalised
   Jensen-Shannon divergence: `beta` interpolates forward KL
   (mode-covering) and reverse KL (mode-seeking); `lmbda` mixes
   student-generated sequences into the batch. This kit ships GKD as
   the main event, with Phase A offline generations as the data
   foundation (they also serve plain SFT through the programme's
   existing LLaMA-Factory kits if GKD is ever the wrong tool).

## Our teacher / student chain (open models only — hard rule)

- **Teacher (24 GB seat):** Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507,
  **Apache-2.0** — the grok-local primary seat, served by its vLLM
  kit. The student learns Grok-pattern behaviour FROM AN OPEN MODEL;
  per the owner's rule, no xAI (or any closed) model's outputs are
  training data anywhere in this programme.
- **Teacher (Kaggle 2×T4 variant):** Qwen/Qwen3-14B, Apache-2.0, on
  GPU 1 — the 30B does not fit one 16 GB T4. The Kaggle job says so
  in its header and its records name the smaller teacher honestly.
- **Student:** Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Apache-2.0 — big enough
  to hold the behaviour, small enough for every seat class (24 GB
  training, T4 serving, and — quantized — the 8 GB phone class).
  Named alternates: Qwen3-1.7B (phone-class student), OLMoE-1B-7B
  (Apache-2.0 sparse student — see item 9).

## Data (the licence firewall)

Full audit in `distill/dataset_sources.json`. Short version:
- **UltraChat 200k (MIT)** supplies PROMPTS ONLY. Its bundled
  responses are discarded unread past the first user turn —
  `prepare_distill_data.py` enforces this in code, and the fixture
  test in the smoke record proves a bundled response cannot leak.
- **Our own seeds** (grok-local's `grok_pattern_seeds.jsonl` +
  owner-written prompts) supply the on-distribution prompts.
- **Every response** in the training set is generated by our teacher
  on our seat and carries a `provenance` block (teacher id, prompt
  source). `gkd_train.py --check` refuses a dataset with
  unprovenanced rows.
- **Refused, with reasons recorded:** Tulu-3 mixture as-is
  (non-commercial subsets + GPT-4-generated subsets), OpenHermes
  (response side traces to GPT-4), any closed-model output dataset.

## The kit (this folder)

- `distill_config.yaml` — teacher/student/licences, GKD knobs, PEFT.
- `prepare_distill_data.py` — prompt pool builder (stdlib).
- `teacher_generate.py` — Phase A generations against the seat
  (stdlib, resumable, provenance-stamped).
- `gkd_train.py` — Phase B TRL GKD wrapper; `--check` validates
  config + licences + dataset provenance with no GPU.
- `eval_scorecard.py` + `eval_prompts.jsonl` — teacher-vs-student
  scorecard in the grok-local validation style (programmatic checks
  first; a record, never a verdict). `--self-test` proves the
  harness itself against stub endpoints.
- `kaggle/` — the job spec (kernel + metadata) in the programme's
  kernel pattern.
