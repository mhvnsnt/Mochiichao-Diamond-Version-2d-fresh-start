# Dorowani canon notes

Dorowani is a blue bipedal creature: a crocodile with a turtle shell.
He has red spiky fin cheeks, red eyes, a light belly, a dorsal fin,
and a tan shell on his back. The orange-finned long-eared version is
the wrong version and its files were deleted from the repo.

Dorowani's signature move is Water Orb. His walk views come from the
official rebuilt sheet: right, down and up views, two frames each.
