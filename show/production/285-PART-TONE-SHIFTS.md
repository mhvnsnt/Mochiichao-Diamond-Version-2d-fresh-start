# #285 — PART TONE SHIFTS → each part may feel like a different show, in the same world and the same hand

**The owner's observation, made law (2026-10-27).**
JoJo's parts do not merely continue — they *feel*
different: Steel Ball Run against Part 1 is nearly a
different work in temperature, country, and weight,
and it is unmistakably the same world in the same
hand. Jamar wants that freedom for Mochiichao's parts
(#277): a later part may run hotter or colder, rougher
or stranger, in different sectors under different
light. This spec draws the exact line the freedom
needs — **what a part may retune, and the one thing it
may never touch.**

## The invariant, stated first

**The render style of record never varies.** Species
art is governed by MOCHIICHAO-STYLE-SPEC.md (the
canon cutouts are the style of record: flat saturated
fields, two-tone-max cel shading, one decisive
contour, the family eye construction) — for every
species, in every region, in every part, forever. A
part that "feels gritty" by rendering its Mochi in a
new finish has not shifted tone; it has broken the
franchise's face, and the anti-preschool/fight-read
gates would be the least of its problems. Tone lives
in **lighting, sector, staging, pacing, and sound** —
the world rendered *around* an unchanging cast, never
in the cast's own render. (This is the species-art
law's per-part reading, recorded here so no future
part's director has to re-derive it under deadline.)

## Native spec — `mochiichao/part-tone/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The tone declaration.** Each part record (#277)
  carries a `tone` block — the part's declared dials,
  written once in its canon docs before production,
  changeable only by the owner's hand on the record:
  - `paletteWeight` — the *staging* palette's centre
    of gravity (which environment palettes dominate
    the part's boards and plates: a part staged mostly
    in dusk harbours and underworld rooms is a heavier
    part than one staged in noon meadows — the species
    on those plates render identically).
  - `sectorFocus` — the sectors the part's geography
    leans on (cross-cited to #277's span; the Dark
    Forest's register, the isles' register, a new
    region's register — place as temperature).
  - `storyTemperature` — the declared heat of the
    writing: threat weight, consequence permanence,
    how dark a beat may run (the thief's part is the
    briefed hot case — its ladder falls are real
    falls). A dial, stated so a part's darkness is a
    *choice on the record*, never drift.
  - `stagedLight` — the part's lighting habit (time-of-
    day weighting in boards, weather lean within #61/
    #142's tables — a part may favour the world's
    existing weathers; it may not invent a climate,
    #142's regional facts govern).
  - `soundRegister` — the part's lean in the existing
    sound ecology (#84's tables, #215's beds): which
    beds carry the part, how much silence it is allowed.
- **Tone is production grammar on #82.** Arc plans
  inside a part read the part's tone block the way
  they read entry state: an arc that stages against
  the declared temperature (a farce arc in the fall
  stretch of the thief's part) must say so in its plan
  and own it as a deliberate counter-beat — relief is
  a tool; unrecorded drift is a defect. #82's
  conservation checklist gains the tone line in
  landing (proposed into #82's home, cited).
- **What tone may never reach:** species render (above);
  the voice law (a darker part does not get creature
  dialogue — darkness is in human choices and their
  prices); the subtitle law; and List 28's facts (a
  part's temperature never softens what the ladder
  recorded — the world remembers a hot part's deeds at
  full weight in the next one, #286).

## Game mirror

A part-keyed campaign (#277) reads the same dials as
presentation defaults: plate and bed selection weights
(#128/#215 families), encounter staging light — all
reads over existing tables, zero new mechanics. The
game's Mochi render under the same invariant, for the
same reason.

## Wiring

Governed by: MOCHIICHAO-STYLE-SPEC.md (the invariant's
home — verified: the canon cutouts as style of record,
restyle = render-only, design untouched). Reads: #277
(the part record carrying the dials), #82 (arc grammar
the dials bind), #61/#142 (weather facts), #84/#215
(sound beds). Writes: the tone block in each part
record; a checklist-line proposal into #82's home.

## Open slots

**Every part's dial settings — the owner's production
call, per part, entire.** (The Gen 1 part's settings
are implicit in the locked season and are not
retro-declared by this package.) No mechanic is open:
the dials, the invariant, and the drift rule are
specified; the values are art direction, labelled as
such rather than dressed as engineering.
