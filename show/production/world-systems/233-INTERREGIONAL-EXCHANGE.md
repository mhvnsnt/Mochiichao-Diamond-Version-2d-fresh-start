# #233 — INTERREGIONAL RANGER EXCHANGE → Rangers visiting other regions on programme

**What was studied.** Exchange programmes in the
publicly documented record — school and civic
exchanges where a participant is *hosted* in
another place under a programme's umbrella while
remaining a citizen of home — and their fiction
form, the guest arc: a traveller whose foreignness
is the story's engine precisely because the place
does not rearrange itself for them. The kit taken:
the programme moves the Ranger; it does not move
the world's knowledge into the Ranger.

## Canon anchors (verified in the packages)

- **#127 owns travel and discovery.** The
  connected-world doctrine: regions are reached by
  connections, and discovery (`discoveryKinds[]` —
  `progression / exploration / side-story /
  map-connection`) is **derived per Ranger per
  connection**, never granted. An exchange is a
  *reason to be at* a connection — in #127's
  vocabulary it is closest to a side-story-class
  occasion for the crossing — and the crossing
  itself is derived exactly as for anyone else.
- **#207 owns what discovery looks like** (List 22,
  building in parallel — forward citation; see
  STAGING): locked-but-undiscovered species stand
  in the Mochiioteca as silhouettes until the
  Ranger discovers them. **An exchanged Ranger's
  Mochiioteca fills with silhouettes like anyone's
  — the programme grants travel, never knowledge.**
  Their home-region discoveries stay discovered
  (discovery is per-Ranger and permanent, #127/#137);
  the host region starts as silhouette pages.
- **#145 owns travellers.** Travelling NPCs are
  one record with cross-region rows and `transit`
  as a place. An exchange cohort is that pattern
  with paperwork: named Rangers, a host region, a
  span — travellers with arcs, continuous across
  their appearances, per #145/#82 continuity.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/interregional-exchange/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The exchange record**: `{rangerId, homeRegion,
  hostRegion, cohort, span{fromBeat, toBeat},
  status}`. The record is the programme's whole
  footprint. It grants: presence (the Ranger may
  be in the host region without the crossing being
  treated as an anomaly by civic systems — #93
  schedules may seat them, #232 towns may greet
  them), and nothing else.
- **What an exchange never grants** (the negative
  space is the spec):
  - No discovery: no connection state changes, no
    Mochiioteca entry pre-fills, no silhouette is
    lifted by arrival. First-arrival staging (#127)
    fires for the exchanged Ranger exactly once,
    exactly as for a self-made traveller.
  - No league standing: the host region's circuit
    (#143) is entered on its own terms, from its
    own start, or not at all — exchange records are
    not read by #143's machinery.
  - No licence transfer: the home licence (#98)
    remains the licence; host-region permissions
    that require local standing follow #230/#133's
    ordinary rules for any visitor.
- **Cohorts.** A cohort is a named set of exchange
  records sharing span and host — the social unit
  the show lane casts from and #145's continuity
  tracks. Cohort membership writes nothing to any
  member's records beyond the exchange record
  itself.

## Show lane

An exchange Ranger is a ready-made guest arc under
#82: they arrive knowing the *customs* wrong and
the *species* not at all (silhouette knowledge —
they've seen the host dex's shapes, not its
answers), which is the honest dramatic position
the system's rules create for free. Home-region
regulars guesting in a later season are cast from
the same records — the programme is how the show
reuses its own cast across region seasons without
inventing teleportation.
