# #262 — SUNLIGHT & REST (PLANT CARE) → for a plant body, sunshine is food and rest at once

**The gap this fills.** The care systems (#28's axes,
#65's loop) feed, play with, and rest a Mochi the way
the genre feeds animals. But the plant-bodied sheets
already say, in their own words, that their species
eat differently: Seedquire "feeds on sunlight and
rich soil like any seedling"; Budguard's diet is
"sunlight, water, and soil feeding as a growing
plant," and it "rotates slowly through the day to
keep its leaf shield to the light"; Verdilance takes
"sunlight through the lance-flower and leaves" as
breakfast. Canon has the behaviour. No system has the
*input* — the care loop has no way to give a plant
sunshine, or to notice it went without. This spec
adds the input, in canon's own terms.

**How the gap is filled** (canon first, per the
gap-filling rule): the sheets are the authority —
sun-feeding is declared per species in Diet &
behaviour prose. Pokémon has no care-sunlight to
copy; the Chao half of the world's ancestry does
have environment-shaped care (a garden's conditions
steer growth), which is the shape taken: an
environmental input the keeper arranges, not a
bottle they administer.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/sunlight-care/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The input.** `sunlight` is a care input in #28's
  system, available to — and needed by — species
  whose sheets declare sun-feeding (the declaration
  is a sheet fact, read from Diet & behaviour /
  body plan in the distillation pass; Flora-typed
  plant bodies are the standing population, but the
  *sheet* rules, not the type label). It accrues the
  way a plant actually takes it: standing in day
  phases (#19) in a sunlit place (outdoors in CLEAR/
  OVERCAST reads, the Garden's sun spots below,
  a camp pitched in the open per #134) — slowly,
  capped by the day, zero at night and zero in the
  Forest depths (weather NONE has no sun to give;
  a consequence, stated once). It is not an item,
  not purchasable, not stockpilable: you cannot save
  sunshine for later, and no #31 record exists for
  it (the non-join is the point — this is the care
  system's one input that is a *place and a time*,
  not a thing).
- **Rest is the same act, for a plant.** #134's camp
  verb set is closed (rest / cook / play) and stays
  closed: a plant Mochi at `rest` in sunlight is
  doing this — roots easy, face to the light — and
  the camp's rest yield for a sun-feeding species
  reads its sunlight accrual as part of the rest,
  the way the centre's rest is a service read. No
  fourth verb is proposed. What changes is only
  that #28 can now *see* the input the rest was
  already giving.
- **Presentation is behaviour, and the sheets wrote
  it:** turning to the light through the day
  (Budguard's slow rotation is the type example) is
  an idle/read behaviour under #152's Garden and
  follower views — a sun-feeding species in a sunlit
  scene orients to the sun the way other species
  orient to their Ranger. The keeper's skill is
  placement: putting the plant where the light is.
- **The axis seat** — one honest proposal: the input
  joins #28's care families as a species-conditional
  input; its exact seat inside #28's authored axis
  set is proposed into #28's home (GARDEN-RAISING-
  SYSTEM) with citation, since #28's file is the
  axis authority. What this package fixes is the
  input's existence, sourcing, and reads — not a
  rewrite of #28's axes from outside.

## The Garden's part

The Garden (#28/#65) gains a feature class: **sun
spots** — authored places in the Garden where the
light falls longest, where plant residents turn up
in idle behaviour and where #264's planting spots
prefer to be. Sun spots are Garden content (the
Garden-complex authoring owns them; zero are placed
by this package), recorded in the Garden's own
tables like its other features.

## Show lane

Sun-care is the show's quietest care beat: a
Verdilance tilting its flower to the morning is
care, breakfast, and prayer in one held shot (the
sheet's own words). Episodes show plant partners
being *placed* well by people who know them — the
input gives that beat its name in production, and
#202's mood read may cite a sun-fed plant's
contentment as presentation colour (mood's inputs
are #202's to accept; the citation is offered, not
taken).

## Wiring

Input joins #28 (seat proposed into #28's home);
accrual reads #19 (phase) × #61 (sky) × place
authoring; rest join is #134's existing `rest` verb
(no change to #134); Garden feature class joins
the Garden-complex tables beside #65's features;
presentation via #152/#58. Writes: no item, no
currency, no stored sunshine — accrual lives
inside #28's care state like every other input.

## Open slots

None that are mechanics. Which species declare
sun-feeding is sheet content (distillation pass);
where the Garden's sun spots are is Garden content.
