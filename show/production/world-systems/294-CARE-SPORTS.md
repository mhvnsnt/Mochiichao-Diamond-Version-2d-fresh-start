## Native spec — `mochiichao/care-sport/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Two forms (closed at founding):**
  - **Races** — a course, a field, a finish order:
    #236's record shape verbatim (`class`, `course`,
    `field`, `finishOrder`), with `class` extending
    by this package's annex: the documented race
    family (foot / swim / flight / course) already
    seats in #236's vocabulary — no new class is
    founded; what this package adds is the arithmetic
    below.
  - **The duel-sport** — a formal one-to-one contest
    of bearing and nerve (the karate-class form):
    rounds of judged exchanges, **no damage, no
    faint, no #17 pipeline anywhere in its
    resolution** — a bout record of rounds and a
    decision, in #236's results family. Its etiquette
    is #265's family (formal challenge, bowed
    beginnings): the duel-sport is what duel
    etiquette looks like with an institution around
    it.
- **The performance arithmetic (the filled slot).**
  Finish order and bout decisions derive from the
  entrants' **care-axis profile** (#28/#293): each
  course class reads a declared axis weighting (a
  swim race reads the swim-class axis first —
  course data, authored per course class), plus
  anatomy facts (eligibility stays #236's derivation
  from the sheets — care stats never make an
  anatomy eligible that the body isn't), plus the
  day's conditions (#61 weather as #236 requires),
  plus a seeded variance draw (#94's reproducibility
  discipline: a disputed result is replayable).
  **Battle stats are never read** — not as a proxy,
  not as a tiebreak (#293's line, enforced here as
  a negative rule landing can test). A champion
  battler raised carelessly loses races to a
  beloved weakling, and that is the system working.
- **Entry and keeping.** Entrants are #80 individuals
  entered by their keepers (or wild individuals
  present as themselves, #236's agency rule). A
  closed-heart individual (#287) **cannot be
  entered**: competition is a joy-class activity,
  and entering a wounded Mochi in a sport is a deed
  the system refuses at the gate (a shown refusal,
  #132 legibility) — its keeper's trying is not
  itself recorded as a crime; the gate simply shuts.
- **Prizes are honours, never power** (#236/#56 law,
  inherited whole): #104 stamps and occasions,
  ribbons as #31 material items, chronicle standing
  (#224). No care-sport result writes a battle fact,
  a care axis, or a grade — winning a race does not
  train the racer (training is #39's honest labour
  and #293's honest feeding; the sport is where the
  results *show*).
- **Grades and matchmaking.** Fields are grouped by
  checkup grade bands (#293) where a venue offers
  graded classes (venue data) — a beginner's race is
  a real institution, not a tutorial fiction; ungraded
  open classes stand beside them. Records keep class
  and grade band so a result means what it meant.
