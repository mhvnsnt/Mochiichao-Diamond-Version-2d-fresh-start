# #339 — BOND-SHAPED VARIATION → friendship leaves marks, and so does its absence

**The gap this fills.** #180 founded bond effects
under a hard law: a bond effect exists only as a
**declared row with a visible cue** — no invisible
luck. #337's engine reads bond facts (#338 family
3) like any other input. What neither says is the
Chao truth the owner ordered pulled in: **bond is
the strongest single shaping force in a raised
life, and its marks are the ones an audience reads
first.** A beloved Mochi and a neglected one do
not just behave differently in a declared battle
row — they *carry* differently, in every scene,
at rest, at a glance. This spec owns the bond
channel of #337's drift: how friendship, and its
absence, write themselves on an individual — and
what the same facts decide at the end of a life.

**What was studied.** The documented Chao record's
happiness grammar: a Chao's lifetime treatment is
summed in how it is held, fed, and answered; its
drift (Hero/Dark, #293's seat) is steered by *who*
raises it, but its **end** is decided by how it was
*treated* — a life that ends loved resolves one
way (the pink cocoon: continuation), a life that
ends neglected the other (the grey cocoon: an
ending). The kit taken: treatment sums, visibly,
across a life; the sum is legible on the body
before it is ever totalled; and the end reads the
sum. All contents native; the end's *contents*
stay canon's (below).

## Native spec — `mochiichao/bond-variation/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The bond marks (drift channel, declared).**
  Bond's contribution to #337's read speaks in the
  posture/bearing and surface channels, in three
  broad classes (the class vocabulary's shape —
  per-family rendering is art-lane data):
  - **Ease** (sustained high bond): open bearing,
    approach without hesitation in #152's stocked
    behaviours, rest taken *near* the keeper
    (proximity is a posture fact), a finished,
    groomed surface — the look of a life that
    expects kindness.
  - **Ordinariness** (the middle most lives live):
    no mark. The engine's zero is a real class —
    an unremarkable bond writes nothing, and the
    species sheet shows plain.
  - **Want** (sustained low bond / recorded
    neglect): guarded bearing, startle in the idle
    selection, distance kept, surface dulled
    within the palette fence — the Chao honesty
    translated: **a neglected Mochi's presentation
    says so.** It says so quietly (margins, never
    wounds — damage is #287's), and it says so to
    everyone, including the keeper who caused it.
- **The declaration join.** These marks are
  presentation, and presentation is where they
  stay: any *battle* consequence of bond remains
  an #180 row or it does not exist (#180's law,
  untouched). The marks satisfy #180's visible-cue
  demand from the other side — where #180 requires
  a cue for an effect, #339 makes the cue
  continuous: the bond a declared row reads is the
  bond the audience has been *seeing* all along.
- **Marks are reads, not scars.** Because drift is
  derived (#337), sustained care re-reads Want
  toward Ease across a life — no forgiveness
  event, no reset: the pattern changes, the read
  follows. What growth-moment settling (#337/#293)
  preserves is the life *so far*, and the next
  settling re-reads the whole. A rescued Mochi is
  allowed to end up looking loved. (The closed-
  heart extreme — where the marks are not drift
  but damage — is #287/#288's and this spec does
  not reach into it.)
- **The keeper is in the picture.** Bond facts are
  per-keeper (#338 family 6): a transfer (#168/
  #217) does not zero the read — the individual's
  marks carry its history with *people*, and a new
  keeper's care accrues alongside. #273's stolen
  time reads here too: what captivity does to a
  look is drift; what it does to a heart is #287.
- **The end of a life reads bond (the future
  read).** At an individual's end-of-life moment,
  an **outcome class** is read from the lifetime
  bond pattern (the #338 history read, whole-life
  window): the Chao shape — loved resolves toward
  continuation, neglected toward ending. This spec
  builds the read and its seat only:
  - the read's **inputs** are lifetime bond facts
    (no other family votes — not level, not wins,
    not price: how it was *treated*);
  - the read fires **once**, at the end, and its
    class is a fact other systems cite (#225's
    memorials are the place-side neighbour for
    endings that leave a place behind);
  - **what the classes ARE — what continuation
    means in this world, what an ending leaves,
    whether the show ever stages one — is canon,
    entire, and OPEN here.** The machinery is
    bond-shaped so that when canon speaks, it
    speaks into a read that already honours the
    owner's source. Nothing in this spec instances
    an end-of-life event for any individual.

## Show lane

The show's quietest continuity: a partner's ease
is cast from the same read (#346), so an audience
that has watched a neglectful arc has *seen* the
Want marks gather before any script names them —
and a redemption arc can be played in posture
across a season without a word of dialogue.
Creatures stay cries-only; bond is drawn.
