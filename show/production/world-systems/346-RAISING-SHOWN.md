# #346 — RAISING SHOWN → the audience can read a life off a Mochi at sight

**The gap this fills.** #337–#342 built the game's
drift engine: raising writes itself on individuals
as derived presentation. The show has no save file
to derive from in the moment — it has **casting**:
named Mochi are drawn, boarded, and rigged by
artists who need the drift *as a decision on the
sheet*, not a derivation at runtime. This spec is
the mirror discipline — how raising becomes
casting — and it closes List 35's loop with List
33's #326 (record to cast, cited by subject — List
33 is mid-build): a cast individual's raising read
is part of its record-to-cast packet, quoted from
the same derivation the game runs, so the two
lanes never raise the same individual differently.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/raising-shown/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The raising read (casting form).** For every
  cast Mochi, the casting packet carries a
  **raising read**: the #337 drift values, #339's
  bond class, #340's dominant leaning, #341's
  emphasis class, and #342's lean where lineage is
  known — **quoted at casting time from the
  individual's record** (the #326 discipline:
  citations, not inventions). The read is a
  snapshot with a date and a season scope; it is
  re-quoted when the packet is (a long arc's
  midpoint re-casting reads the life as it then
  stands — a partner visibly *becoming* across a
  season is #339's re-readability staged).
- **The legibility standard (the envelope test,
  show form).** #337's two-lives half becomes a
  board QA: **in a lineup still, a viewer who
  knows the species can sort the cast by how they
  were raised** — lab-even, beloved, hasty,
  neglected, wild-weathered — without a caption.
  A board where two differently-raised cast
  members of one species are interchangeable
  fails the gate the same way an unregistered
  deviation fails #80's style gate: the *absence*
  of a ruled read is a defect.
- **Worked readings (the derivation applied, not
  new canon).** The spec's examples are readings
  of records canon already holds, stated so
  casting can imitate the method:
  - **The thief's partners** (#284's record
    family): theft-and-haste raising — bond marks
    split (Want from captivity seasons, Ease
    where #284's hard road genuinely settled),
    emphasis toward swift/fierce, wear high.
    Their bodies carry how they were *got* and
    how they were *kept* as two separate facts,
    because #338 keeps the families separate.
  - **Lab-raised** (Aeon's seats, #44): even care
    across axes, low wear, ordinariness bond
    class tending to Ease, unemphasised or
    bright — the measured look of a life where
    nothing was ever scarce.
  - **A wild-raised elder:** no keeper facts at
    all — the read runs on #214-family wild facts
    (territory, weather, seasons survived):
    weathered surface, grounded emphasis, Want/
    Ease classes *inapplicable* (bond classes are
    keeper-shaped; a wild life is not a neglected
    one — the #338 absence rule, staged). This
    inapplicability is a designed output, not a
    gap: casting must be able to draw "no human
    has ever raised this one" as its own look.
- **The joins.**
  - **#320 (faces & expression):** the raising
    read includes the face's ease — #339's bond
    classes read at the face first (approach and
    rest are facial before they are postural),
    and #320's expression grammar stages them.
  - **#326 (record to cast):** the raising read
    rides the same packet as ruled deviations
    (#318) — with the fence kept bright: the
    packet lists deviations under RULED and the
    raising read under DERIVED, in separate
    sections, and a board may never promote a
    derived read into a deviation or draw a
    deviation as if it were drift (the two errors
    are mirror images; both are continuity
    defects of the #53 class).
  - **#316 (signature dialect):** a cast
    individual's dialect is cast from #340's
    dominant leaning quoted in the same packet —
    the spectacle an audience remembers them by
    grows from the life the packet describes.
- **What the show never shows.** No meters, no
  grades on screen, no before/after captions, no
  flashback-makeover grammar (#293's show law,
  extended): raising is visible **biography** —
  present in every frame the individual is in,
  remarked by the story only when a character
  who knows them remarks it, in behaviour
  (#320/#152), never in narration.

## Show lane

This spec IS the show lane's half of List 35; its
game mirror is the derivation itself (#337–#342),
which the game renders live from the same facts —
one life, two readings, no third truth.

## Wiring

Casting packets seat with #326's packet home
(proposal into its file when List 33 lands it);
the derivation is #337's, quoted — landing writes
no show-side drift store. QA gate joins #80's
style-gate family (lineup legibility check beside
the deviation checks).
