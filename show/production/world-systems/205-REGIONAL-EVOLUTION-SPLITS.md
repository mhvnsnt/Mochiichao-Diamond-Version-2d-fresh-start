# #205 — REGIONAL EVOLUTION SPLITS → one destination, geography-resolved

**Seat in the doctrine:** a split line's later
stage takes a different form depending on **where**
the evolution happens. Three form axes exist and
stay separate (#130's separation law): regional
variants (#40), formes (#130), lustre (#15). A
split lives **entirely on #40's axis** — #40
already defines a regional variant as a form *of*
the species: one species, one slot family, the
form following geography. A split is #40 applied
at an evolution's result. It is not a new axis, not
a branch in the roster, and not a second species
wearing the first one's number.

**What was studied.** Region-split evolutions in
the publicly documented record: one pre-evolution,
two regional outcomes, the outcome decided by
where the evolution occurs and stable thereafter.
The kit taken: geography resolves the result **at
the event**, and the result is then simply who the
creature is. No split line, form design, or
regional mapping is copied — canon instances below:
zero.

## Native rules — the split mechanism

- **One edge, one destination.** The line's edge
  (#8) points at the stage species, singular. There
  are not two destination species and not two
  competing edges. The split is declared on the
  line's sheet as **variant-stage data**: the stage
  species' #40 variant entry names the stage it
  applies at (declaration is sheet data; per #8's
  fields-only rule, the edge index derives from
  the prose, and the prose is the record).
- **Resolution at the event.** When the evolution
  fires, the result's regional form resolves from
  #40 by where the individual **is** — its
  location/area at that moment (#106/#7 facts). If
  that region carries a variant form of the stage
  species, the individual evolves into that form;
  otherwise the base form. The individual's own
  history, home region, or keeper's origin are
  irrelevant inputs — it is where *it* is, per the
  brief's law, and the record (next) says so.
- **The outcome is stored — as biography.** Which
  form resulted is written onto the individual's
  record (#80) as part of its evolution history: an
  evolution is an event in a life, and #80 stores
  the facts of a life (the seed at birth; the form
  taken, here). **Later travel does not re-form the
  individual.** A form taken is taken; the creature
  who evolved in the mountains carries the mountain
  form to the sea, unchanged by the journey.
- **The contrast that keeps the axes clean:**

  | | Weather forme (#175, on #130) | Regional split (#205, on #40) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Resolves | at read time, continuously | once, at the evolution event |
  | Stored | nowhere — reversion is automatic | on #80, as evolution history |
  | On travel/weather change | follows the current state | never changes |

  A system that makes a split revert on travel has
  confused #205 with #175; a system that makes a
  weather forme persist has confused #175 with
  #205. Both confusions are named defect classes,
  recorded here so landing tests can check for
  them by name.
- **Show-lane mirror.** A split is geography made
  visible (#127/#137 continuity — the connected
  world's seams, written in bodies). A character
  who evolves abroad carries that region's form
  home, permanently, as biography; the show treats
  the form as who they now are — never a costume
  that changes at a border crossing, and never a
  transformation a Ranger schedules (the #199
  biography law applies with full force: the place
  is the moment's meaning, not its lever).

## Wiring (proposed)

A reading rule over #8's edges + #40's variant
data; lands as a citation layer in #40's home.
Reads: #8 edges, sheet declarations, #40 variant
entries, #106/#7 location at the event. Writes:
the evolution-history entry on #80, via the
evolution consumer — nothing else.
