# #288 — PURIFICATION → opening a closed heart (Colosseum/XD native)

**Doctrine seat:** #287 founded the wound; this is
the healing — and it is deliberately the largest
care system in the programme, because in the source
design healing is not a menu item. It is walking,
fighting beside them, tending them, and time. This
package is the missing engine List 28 promised:
#273's stolen Mochi and #275's redemption both run
through here. A heart opens by *relationship*, and
the system refuses every shortcut that skips it.

**What was studied.** The documented Colosseum/XD
record, two roads:
- **The walking road (Colosseum):** the gauge falls
  as the creature is carried and used — steps walked
  together, battles fought, being called back from
  frenzy (#289), scent/massage care sessions. When
  the gauge empties, a **rite at a shrine** opens the
  heart; at opening, all experience held during
  closure is granted at once, and shadow moves are
  forgotten.
- **The chamber road (XD):** a facility where sets of
  creatures are placed together and *flow* between
  them does the work — a set arranged so each member's
  nature presses on the next builds **tempo**, and at
  maximum tempo even deep closures drain fast. One
  documented flagship: a heart closed by the deepest
  process could not be opened by the walking road at
  all — only by the chamber at full tempo.
The kit taken: both roads, the completion payout, and
the flagship exception class. Every name and number
is native.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/purification/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Road one — the walking road (anywhere).** Gauge
  reductions, all data-valued, all events on records
  that already exist:
  - **Steps / travel together** — the follower and
    field systems (#58/#160) already count shared
    ground; shared travel is the largest slow drip.
  - **Battles fought beside the keeper** — each
    completed battle the closed Mochi took part in
    (win or lose; *presence and trust under pressure*
    is the mechanism, not victory).
  - **The call-back** — being called out of frenzy
    by the keeper (#289) is the single largest
    walking-road event: being *reached* is what
    opens hearts.
  - **Care sessions** — #28's care loop acts (the
    documented scent/massage class): grooming (#162),
    feeding, rest at camp (#134). Care from the
    *keeper* counts; the same act from a stranger
    counts less — the ratio is data.
  - **Temperament weighting** — the rate is read
    through #108's temperament (documented: natures
    purify at different speeds). The seed finally
    gets a say in healing, without ever having been
    changed by the wound (#287's law).
- **Road two — the chamber (a Garden facility).**
  The Mochi Garden (#28) gains a **purification
  chamber**: a facility that seats **sets** of
  residents — one closed Mochi per set, the other
  seats open-hearted residents chosen so their
  natures *flow*: a set flows when each member's
  type/temperament presses supportively on the
  next in the ring (the flow relation is data authored
  over the type chart (#249) + temperament (#108)).
  A set's **tempo** (0 up to a data maximum) is
  derived from how completely the ring flows.
  - Chamber drain rate scales with tempo; at
    **maximum tempo** the chamber is the fastest
    road in the game — and the only road for the
    flagship class (below).
  - The chamber seats are Garden residents — which
    means **purification is social**: the Mochi who
    heal a stolen stranger are the player's own
    Garden community, and a set only exists while
    its members are resident (#264/#212 Garden
    facts read; nobody is consumed, moved, or
    written by being in a set — tempo is derived,
    membership is a placement fact).
  - This gives the Garden real teeth: building a
    Garden whose residents flow well is a long-term
    raising goal (#293), not decoration.
- **Completion — the opening.** When the gauge
  reaches 0 the heart is *ready*, not yet open
  (documented shape, kept deliberately): opening
  takes a **rite** — a quiet ceremony at a shrine-
  class place (a #154/#225-class site; which sites
  qualify is world content) performed with the
  keeper present. At the rite, atomically:
  1. **The bank pays out** — all #177 gain held
     during closure applies at once (#287); levels
     and #129 learning resolve in order, in the
     rite's presentation (the catch-up is *shown* —
     it is the emotional peak of the whole system).
  2. **Shadow moves are forgotten** — #290's slots
     empty and normal move learning re-opens.
  3. **`heart` clears** — the field leaves the record
     (absent = open, #287). The *history* keeps the
     closure and the opening as beats forever: the
     record never pretends it didn't happen (#274's
     law — history does not launder), but no live
     system reads a former closure as a present fact
     except #275's redemption accounting, which reads
     history by design.
  4. An **opening honour** may seat in #104's
     vocabulary (proposed there, not founded here).
- **The flagship exception class.** A closure whose
  cause is the deepest Syndicate process (class name
  is a canon slot — Syndicate vocabulary is the
  owner's) **cannot be opened by the walking road**:
  its gauge may drain to the last segment and hold.
  Only the **chamber at maximum tempo** finishes it,
  and its opening rite is a story event, not a
  service. The class exists so the game's worst act
  has a weight no routine can lift; its contents —
  which Mochi, what process — are owner canon, and
  this package instances none.
- **Who can purify whom.** Any keeper can walk the
  road with a closed Mochi in their keeping —
  including a thief with a Mochi they stole (#284's
  hard road: the bond that forms is real, and so is
  the theft on the history; purification does not
  adjudicate keepers, it heals Mochi). Shelters
  (#220) run the chamber road for strays as a
  service under #132 discipline.

## Show lane

Purification is the show's strongest care grammar:
it is never a montage of machinery — it is episodes
of walking, fighting beside, and one rite. The
chamber on screen is a Garden scene of residents
arranged in a ring, played quietly. The payout at
opening is staged as the Mochi *becoming* — cries
full-length again (#155), colour back — under #296's
staging rules.
