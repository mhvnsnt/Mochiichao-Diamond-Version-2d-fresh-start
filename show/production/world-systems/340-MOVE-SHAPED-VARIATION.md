# #340 — MOVE-SHAPED VARIATION → what a Mochi practises is written on how it moves through the world

**The gap this fills.** A species' learnset is a
menu; an individual's life is what it actually
ordered. Two Mochi of one species can know disjoint
working sets — one raised on water work, one on
flight, one a brawler — and until now nothing in
the programme let that difference *show* outside
the battle screen. #147 founded move-animation
coverage (which species × move performances exist);
#316 (List 32, mid-build) founds the signature
spectacle dialect (how a named individual's
signature performance differs from the family's
generic). Neither covers the ordinary, lifelong
fact this spec owns: **the moves an individual
actually knows and uses tilt its everyday
presentation** — its ease in elements, its carriage,
its habits of motion — and that tilt is part of
what makes two of a species two individuals (#337).

## Native spec — `mochiichao/move-variation/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Inputs (and only these).** #338 family 5: the
  individual's **known-move set** (a record fact)
  and, where #187's replay input logs are kept,
  its **usage pattern** (which known moves it
  actually throws, across its battles). Where no
  usage log exists, the read runs on the known set
  alone and says so by reading flatter — the
  #338 absence rule, inherited.
- **The tilt (what the read produces).** A small
  set of **practice leanings**, one per move family
  the individual's working set is actually built
  from (families are the move data's own classes —
  element and delivery — read from the moves
  themselves, no new taxonomy):
  - a water-built individual carries water's ease:
    comfort in rain and shallows in #152's
    behaviours, a swimmer's carriage where the
    species has one within its posture class;
  - a flight-built flyer is *built by flying*:
    wing/levitation carriage emphasis in #337's
    proportion channel, skyward idle selection;
  - a brawler's set thickens the bearing: planted
    stance, impact-ready posture in the posture
    channel.
  The leanings are **presentation tilts in #337's
  channels** — posture, proportion emphasis,
  behaviour selection. The vocabulary's shape is
  this spec's; per-family renderings are art-lane
  data. A leaning needs a *pattern* (the #338
  history rule): one tutor move learned last week
  is a fact, not a leaning.
- **The spectacle join.** #316's signature dialect
  is cast from what a named individual *does most* —
  this read is its evidence: the individual's
  dominant leaning names the family inside which
  its signature dialect is authored (#316 owns the
  dialect; #147 owns whether the performance
  exists). An individual whose life changes its
  working set can, across a growth moment, change
  dominant leaning — and a show dialect re-cast on
  that fact is continuity, not drift error (#346
  carries the casting side).
- **The fences (all hard).**
  - Knowing never grants: a leaning is not a move,
    not a type change, not a resistance — it has
    **no battle effect of any kind** (the #337
    no-battle-number law, applied to knowledge).
  - The species learnset is untouched: this read
    consumes the individual's record, never edits
    the species' menu, and a leaning can never
    cite a move the individual does not know.
  - Signature moves stay signatures (#131's
    register owns their identity): an individual's
    leaning *toward* its species' signature family
    flavours its presentation of that signature;
    it never makes the signature transferable,
    inheritable (#204's absolute bar stands), or
    anyone else's.
  - Rare/General machine facts are game-lane data
    the read ignores: how a move was *acquired*
    (tutor, machine, level) is #338 family 5's
    record detail, not a leaning input — the read
    is over what is known and used, not how it
    was bought. (Show-lane acquisition — skill and
    training — shows in the same read: the show's
    individuals also have known sets, kept by the
    show's records, and the read is lane-blind.)

## Show lane

Training arcs become visible: a Mochi that has
spent a season learning the water carries the
season in its carriage before it ever performs —
the audience reads the training off the body
(#346). A tutor's students share a leaning family
and differ in everything else, which is what a
class looks like.

## Wiring

Reads #338 family 5; writes leanings into #337's
posture/proportion channels and #152's behaviour
selection; cited by #316 (dialect evidence) and
#346 (casting). Move families read from the move
registry's own classes (PR #366's machine registry
shape — element/delivery fields), never a parallel
taxonomy.

## Open slots

Story/canon facts only: none. Which move families
a given species' working sets can lean toward is
derived from move data, not authored per species.
Per-family leaning renderings are art-lane data.
