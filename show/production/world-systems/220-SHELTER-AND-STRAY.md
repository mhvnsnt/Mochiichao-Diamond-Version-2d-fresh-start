# #220 — SHELTER & STRAY SYSTEM → custody for the lost and the surrendered

**Seat in the doctrine:** every transfer system so
far (#68/#168/#217/#219) moves an individual between
keepers. None founded the institution that holds a
Mochi who currently has **no** keeper — the surrendered,
the separated, the stray. This spec founds it, on the
boundary the care systems already drew: **custody is
not keeping.** A shelter is a centre-class seat
(#132's family by shape, #193's custody discipline by
precedent): it holds individuals, records what
happened, and writes nothing about who they are.

**What was studied.** Shelter and lost-animal
institutions in the publicly documented record:
intake records that preserve identity and history;
the holding interval as a legal/custodial state, not
an ownership; reunion as the primary exit (the animal
goes home), adoption as the second exit (a new home,
with the record intact); identification as the
mechanism of reunion — the record is how a lost
animal gets home. The kit taken: custody-without-
keeping, intake legibility, reunion-first exits, and
the record as the way home.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/shelter/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The shelter seat.** A shelter holds individuals
  under a **custodial record**: the individual's #80
  record is *not* rewritten — no keeper seat change
  occurs at intake. Custody rides as a service record
  beside the individual (the #193 daycare pattern:
  custody relocations + the service record are the
  only writes), with `location` reading the shelter
  under #106's discipline (a shelter is a place an
  individual can *be*, proposed into #106's location
  vocabulary in its home — custody is not a fourth
  location *kind* of keeping, and the proposal lands
  there or the seat stays spec-level).
- **The centre boundary, exhaustive.** A shelter
  writes: intake records, custody relocations,
  reunion/adoption transfer stamps (via #217), and
  its own service record. A shelter never writes:
  care axes (#28), bond, effort (#39), gain (#177),
  honours (#104), dex state (#14), or story flags
  (#12) — #132's non-join list, inherited whole. A
  sheltered Mochi's vitality is centre business
  (#132's one write); their *life* is not.
- **Intake classes.** **Surrender** (a keeper gives
  an individual into custody — the intake entry names
  the surrendering keeper; the record's history gains
  a custody entry, not a keeper change) and **stray
  intake** (an individual found without their keeper;
  the intake entry records where and when found, in
  #12 beat terms). Both classes preserve everything:
  honours, deviations, care history — the individual
  who leaves a shelter is the individual who entered,
  plus custody facts.
- **Reunion — the primary exit.** A stray's original
  keeper is found **via the record**: the #80 record's
  keeper history (`previous_keepers[]`, the current
  keeper seat, #168) *is* the identification — there
  is no separate lost-and-found database to invent.
  The seeking state (a keeper recorded as seeking a
  separated individual) is a flag on the custody
  record; while it stands open, the adoption exit is
  closed for that individual (#217's validity rule).
  Reunion completes as a custody release — no keeper
  change, because the keeper never changed.
- **Adoption — the second exit.** When no keeper
  stands (surrender completed, or a stray's seeking
  window closed by shelter procedure — the window is
  policy, OPEN), adoption proceeds as #217's adoption
  class: shelter as `from`, history fully legible,
  the new keeper receiving the individual's whole
  past with them.
- **The Dark Forest clause.** Strays from the Forest
  are found **at its edge** — a Ranger brings out what
  wandered out, or takes in what waits at the
  boundary. No shelter procedure reaches into the
  depths: the depths table is empty (#163's law), no
  intake class is a retrieval from the depths, and a
  "rescue expedition into the depths" is not a system
  this package will ever express. What the Forest
  keeps, the shelter system does not go and get.

## Show lane

A shelter is ready-made show texture (the quiet
institution at the town's edge), and a stray's reunion
is a complete small story under #218's agency centre
and #82's conservation — the Mochi's recognition of
their keeper is the climax, staged in cries and
behaviour (voice law). Surrenders on screen are
handled as the serious act they are: a surrender
depicted as casual or comic is a grammar failure.

## Coverage — honest

- **Architecture: fully documented** — custody,
  intake, reunion-by-record, adoption exit, all on
  verified boundaries (#132/#193/#168/#106).
- **Contents: zero shelters, zero strays.** The yield
  is the institution's shape: a place where a Mochi
  without a keeper is held safely, identified by
  their own history, and routed home or onward —
  never erased, never sold, never fetched from the
  depths.
