## The scope tie-break (this package's charter, stated for landing)

The owner directed (essence statement, 2026-10-27)
that Mochiichao is an open-world RPG built on the
Pokémon base. Landing rule for this package, and the
reading this package was built under: **the Pokémon
base fills mechanics; the open-world reading wins
ties on scope.** Where a List 31 spec and an earlier
package could be quoted against each other on *how
big the world is allowed to be* — how far a Ranger
may wander (#297 vs corridor-shaped readings of #11),
how much a side story may cost (#300 vs a toothless
reading of #138), how free the league's order is
(#305 vs a machinery reading of #143) — the open
reading governs. On mechanics, nothing in this
package outranks the base: every arithmetic, record
shape, and derivation below runs on machinery the
earlier packages already built. If the link-check
finds a genuine mechanical conflict (not a scope
reading), it is a defect to send back, in either
direction.

   open-world reading losing a tie it was directed to

   that is equally a defect: the base fills mechanics
   and this package never outranks it there. The
   check is directional, and both directions are
   named in the charter section above.

   List 25.** Every OPEN in this package is a
   story/canon fact or authored content (the inventory
   above). If the link check finds a List 31 sentence
   that reads as an unfilled mechanic wearing an OPEN
   label — a price nobody decided, a rung nobody
   defined — that is a spec defect to send back, not
   a slot to land. (The one deliberate non-fill is
   named as such in its spec: #293-style battle-stat
   questions belong to List 30's line, not this one,
