# #192 — WATER TRAVERSAL & DIVE SYSTEM → the water half of #123

**What was studied.** Water traversal in the publicly
documented record, in the same two eras #123 found on
land: the move-gated era (a technique opens the water) and
the capability era (a partner whose body swims, ferries, and
dives). The documented failure of the first era is the
HM-slave problem — a party slot taxed by a traversal move.
The kit taken is the capability era's, which is this
programme's doctrine already: bodies, not move lists. No
maps, speeds, or dive sites are copied.

## Canon anchors (verified in the packages)

- **#123 owns traversal and its derivation.** Eligibility
  is derived, never hand-listed: size class (#43's ladder —
  Palm / Knee / Waist / Tower) plus the species sheet's
  recorded anatomy for its locomotion class, in the format
  `mochiichao/traversal/v1`. #192 is that system's water
  annex — **the same format family, not a rival system.**
- **#117 owns the held/performed split.** A technique is
  *held* (a #12 `tech.*` fact) and *performed* (a live
  check: a party member knows it and carries the anatomy)
  — and its canon roster has exactly one row, Illuminate;
  "the rest of the roster is an OPEN owner ruling per
  move". Water traversal borrows the split and instances
  no row.
- **#119 owns interiors.** An interior record hangs off a
  #11 host node (`hostNode`) with rooms and closed
  connection kinds — the shape a dive area needs: a place
  below a place.
- **#66 leaves the Seven Isles' contents OPEN** — "seven
  named slots, zero invented" — and the isles are Region
  1's water geography.

## Native spec — water annex to `mochiichao/traversal/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Modes.** The traversal record gains water modes
  `surface` and `dive`, proposed into #123's home beside
  its land modes. `surface` = crossing and travelling on
  water with a partner; `dive` = descending at a dive
  point into the area below.
- **Eligibility is anatomy, derived.** A partner is
  surface-eligible iff its sheet anatomy records aquatic
  locomotion and its size class (#43) can bear a rider
  (the #123 rider test, unchanged); dive-eligible iff the
  same anatomy supports descent (pressure-tolerant,
  breath-holding or water-breathing — sheet fields, read
  as recorded). **There is no species list in this
  system**: a species is traversable exactly when its
  sheet says its body can do it, so new species arrive
  pre-integrated or honestly excluded by their own data.
  Whether a Ranger may swim *unaided* is a canon question
  this package does not answer (OPEN); the specified path
  is the partnered one.
- **Dive points are gates, tested #117's way.** A dive
  point is a gate on the water area's graph seat (a #11
  edge / #59 lock — kind `dive` proposed into the gate
  vocabulary, landing in its owners' files). The test has
  two independent halves, either of which can be the
  binding one in an authored place: *permission* — if an
  owner ruling ever names a dive technique, its `tech.*`
  fact is required, #117's discipline verbatim, roster
  OPEN with zero rows asserted here; *performance* — a
  live party member must be dive-eligible by anatomy.
  A gate authored with no technique row tests anatomy
  alone. Neither half is ever faked by the other.
- **Dive areas are #119 interiors with #61 state.** A dive
  area is an interior record whose `hostNode` is the
  surface water node; its rooms are depth strata. It
  carries world state in #61's terms — the surface's
  `weather_state` is inherited at the threshold; below,
  the interior's own state fields (light, pressure class)
  are **presentation data, values OPEN**. Encounters
  below read the host area's #7 table through a proposed
  `dive` method slot in #7's schema — zero rows; the
  table's own stock is the only stock (a dive invents no
  fauna).
- **The Region 1 instance, named and bounded.** The waters
  around the Seven Mochii Isles are this system's first
  geography — and their contents are #66's OPEN slots.
  This package instances **no** isle water: no dive sites,
  no strata, no stock. It fixes only that when the isles
  are authored, their waters are authored in this format.
- **The Forest.** Nothing here adds water content to the
  Dark Forest; its surface waters, if any are authored,
  read #61's Forest rules (MIST at the surface), and the
  depths — weather NONE, table empty — are not a dive
  area and cannot become one through this system.

## Show lane

A swimming or diving partner is staged partnership, in
#123's show reading: the body carries the scene — the
crossing, the descent — because the body is the system.
No traversal UI exists in the show; a dive in an episode
is a place the story goes, shot as a place (#119's
grammar), under the water's own light.

## Wiring

Format: annex proposed into #123's traversal home.
Gates: #117 pattern; `dive` kind proposed into #11/#59
vocabularies. Areas: #119 records on #11 nodes. State:
#61. Encounters: proposed method slot into #7's schema.
Geography: #66's open slots, untouched.
