# GARDEN CARE LOOP — the Mochi Garden service system (List 7, #65)

Teardown: the healing-centre loop in creature games — the most-
repeated service ritual in the genre: arrive hurt, hand over your
partners, a short treated interval, receive them back whole, and
a small warmth beat that makes the loop a place, not a menu.
Public structure: the loop's power is its *invariance* — same
steps, same cadence, every town — so the player reads safety
before a single line of text.

## Canon frame

The Mochi Garden is opened by **Assistant Mochiko** at **R1-BEAT3**
(game canon — "The Mochi Garden is open now…"; EP01's keeper
framing anticipates that beat). The care loop lives behind that
flag-gate (gate record: `flag` class, #59). Mochiko is the loop's
keeper in canon; other Gardens in other regions take the same
loop with their own keepers as canon names them — none named here.

## The loop (five steps, fixed order)

| Step | Game behaviour | Show register |
|---|---|---|
| 1. ARRIVE | Player enters with partners in any state; follower (#58) transitions to PARKED at the Garden | The Garden as the world's exhale — cycles at full life, SCORE-STATE SETTLE |
| 2. ASSESS | States read at a glance: health, status conditions (#27 system), fatigue/bond readouts — the Mochiioteca shows what the keeper sees | One look does it: the keeper reads a Mochi the way the audience has learned to (the show teaches the read by repeating it) |
| 3. TREAT | The treated interval: partners pass to the Garden's care; time cost is the ritual's, not a resource's — the genre rule: healing is free, the *trip* is the cost | A held, quiet beat — care shown as handling, never as machinery or a montage of tech |
| 4. BOND | The return: each partner comes back with a small individual beat (the loop's only variable step — species reaction, follower REACT set) | The character-bonding beat (prosody type) — this is where the loop earns its place in episodes |
| 5. RETURN | Partners restored; follower resumes FOLLOW; loop flags updated (below) | Release back into the world's motion — cycles, wind, and score resume with them |

**Invariance rule:** steps 1–3 and 5 never vary in structure
between Gardens or episodes; step 4 is the sanctioned variation
point. A care scene that skips ASSESS has skipped the teaching;
a loop that varies its ritual reads as a different service.

## Interfaces (what this system does NOT define)

- **Care axes** (how feeding/play/rest steer a Mochi's growth —
  the Chao half of the world) are the care/raising system (List 4
  #28). The care loop *consumes* a rest/treatment tick from that
  system at step 3; the axes, rates, and growth effects are
  defined there, not here.
- **Status conditions** cured at step 3 are the status system
  (List 4 #27)'s vocabulary; the loop cures by class reference,
  it does not keep its own condition list.
- **Flags:** the loop writes `progress.*` only for first-visit/
  story beats (R1-BEAT3 class); routine visits set no persistent
  flags — a service you use a hundred times must not bloat the
  flag space (#12 namespace discipline). Visit *counts*, if the
  completion system ever wants them, are derived vars (`vars.*`),
  never flags.

## Failure/edge rules

- A partner at full health still gets steps 4–5 in full — the
  loop is care, not repair; skipping the bond beat for a healthy
  partner teaches the wrong lesson.
- The loop never refuses: no currency gate, no capacity gate
  (economy hooks, if ever, live in the economy system — List 3
  #20 — not in the ritual).
- In the show, the loop is never the place a threat lands. The
  Garden's dread grammar is *absence* (the world outside it), per
  the Forest inversion: safety is the Garden's invariant.
