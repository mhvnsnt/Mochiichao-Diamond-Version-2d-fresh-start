## Interface-check note for landing

Three things for the one-pass link check, beyond the
usual two-way link confirmation:

1. **The register is a view — check no PR makes it
   a store.** #318's whole discipline is that #80's
   records own the facts. If the link check finds a
   register-shaped fact (a deviation's text, a
   ruling's date) stored anywhere but an #80 record,
   that is a spec defect to send back, not a slot to
   land.
2. **The absence direction is the fragile one.**
   #317's vocabulary cannot express "unmasked";
   #318's third entry is an *absence*; #325 must
   render a *lack*. Any landing step that models the
   maskless individual as carrying a part (even a
   null-valued one that a renderer might "helpfully"
   fill) breaks the chain — the check is: his record
   shows the deviation, the species record shows the
   part, and no join between them invents a state.
3. **The two bloom/face confusions to keep apart:**
   #317's `face-cover` is anatomy; it is not a
   #130 forme, not a #257 bloom state, and not
   equipment in #31's item layer — a grown weapon
   already may not be an item (#258's non-join), and
   a grown face shield inherits the same non-join.
   A citation that routes a face-cover through any
   of those three is a landing defect.
