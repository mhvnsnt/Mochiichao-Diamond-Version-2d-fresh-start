# #256 — CENTRE SERVICES FLOW → the full centre visit, one designed walk

**Doctrine seat.** #132 fixed what a centre *is*: a
service that restores the party and writes nothing
else — no care axes, no bond, no honours, no dex, no
story flags, and it stores nobody. What no package
stated is the visit itself: the order a Ranger moves
through a centre and what each step offers. This spec
states the documented flow over #132's boundary. The
flow adds no writes; it sequences the ones that exist.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/centre-flow/v1`

### The flow (identical in every centre)

1. **Arrive.** The centre is a #119 interior; entering
   is the whole trigger — no appointment, no cost, no
   gate of any kind. A blackout arrival (#253) enters
   at step 2 directly.
2. **Heal.** The party is restored to full vitality and
   cured of persistent conditions (#132's `restore` —
   its only write, free at the point of use, as #132
   already rules). Volatile conditions clear by their
   own definition (#182). This step is never skippable
   by the centre and never refused: a centre heals who
   is brought to it.
3. **Storage access.** The centre's terminal opens
   #106 (Mochi storage) and #190 (item vault) — access,
   not custody: the centre holds the door, the systems
   hold the records, and #132's "stores nobody" is
   untouched because the terminal is a window onto
   #106, not a box in the building.
4. **Services.** Whatever services this centre's data
   seats are offered from the counter: a shop counter
   where one is seated (#118's stock, #20's prices), a
   move service where one is seated (#129's relearn /
   #101's tutoring are their own institutions — a
   centre hosts one only when its data says so), the
   daycare counter is **never** here (custody lives in
   #77's Garden housing — List 20's correction, kept).
   The flow is fixed; the seating is data. A centre
   with nothing seated still offers steps 2–3 and is a
   complete centre.
5. **Depart.** Leaving is walking out. Nothing is
   recorded about the visit — no visit counter, no
   loyalty, no log (#132's non-writes extend to the
   visit itself; #146's visit record is about *regions
   and places discovered*, not about errands).

### Regional skin, same skeleton

The flow never varies; the room does. A centre's
presentation — its palette family, its counter
materials, its ambient bed (#215) — is regional flavour
data (#142's climate identity, #116-class presentation),
so a Region 3 centre *feels* like Region 3 while doing
exactly what a Region 1 centre does, in the same order,
for free. Familiarity is the service: a Ranger who
learns one centre has learned every centre, the way the
base game's players do — that recognition is a feature
this spec protects by refusing per-centre flow
variants.

### Show lane

A centre in the show is a place of rest and repair,
staged under #82: the counter, the heal light, the
quiet. The flow above is the staging order the show
borrows (arrive → heal → business → leave) because
audiences read it instantly — but no system runs on
screen, no storage terminal opens a menu, and a centre
visit is a breathing beat, not an errand montage.

## Wiring

Sequences #132 (heal) + #106/#190 (access) + seated
services (#118 et al.); entered from #253 (blackout)
and ordinary #119 interiors; presentation skinned by
#142/#215 data. No new schema: the centre record's
service seats are the data #132 already implies, and
landing confirms their home in #132's file rather than
founding one here.

## Coverage — honest

- **Flow: complete and asserted** — five steps, one
  order, every centre, with #132's boundary carried
  verbatim rather than renegotiated.
- **Contents:** which services seat in which centre is
  centre data, authored with the towns. The flow is
  finished even where a given centre's counter is
  still empty.
