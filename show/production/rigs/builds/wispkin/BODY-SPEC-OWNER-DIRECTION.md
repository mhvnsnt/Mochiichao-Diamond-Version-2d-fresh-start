# Wispkin — body construction direction (owner, 2026-10-27)

Owner's read of the first Wan test: the generated skull + smoke
are CLOSE. What the generation dropped is the BODY. Build/add the
body to the character sheet's likeness:

- BODY: one fat, round, black ghost body under the mask — the
  sheet's round black body. No legs, no feet, no tail.
- BOTTOM: the body tapers into a little floating ghost tail
  (Casper-style) that dissolves into the smoke trail beneath him.
  The smoke comes off the bottom of the body.
- ARMS + HANDS: small black arms and hands, up at the sides of
  the mask, TOUCHING / HOLDING the mask onto his face — the hands
  on the mask are the character's expression channel (with mask
  tilt + smoke shape), per the corrected model sheet.
- TOP: the sheet's small black flame tip above the mask stays.
- NOT THIS: no hair tuft, no bill/beak, no legs-and-tail body
  plan (the owner used Psyduck's body as the shape to strip
  those parts FROM, not as a part to keep).

Gate use: a Wispkin generation passes likeness only if ALL of
the above read in frame — mask held by visible hands, round black
body, ghost-tail-to-smoke bottom. A floating skull alone FAILS,
however good the skull is.

Production route this implies: parts assembly, not one-shot
generation — the TextRig puppet already carries the exact body /
hands / mask (canon pixels, no redraw). Generative passes supply
smoke, atmosphere and motion reference; the puppet (or a
composite over the canon cutout's body) supplies the body.
