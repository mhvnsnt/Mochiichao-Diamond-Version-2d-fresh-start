## FRONT MATTER — THE TRANSPLANT NAMING LAW (owner, 2026-10-27)

His **OWN creations** — Western Heart, Brutal Fist, BANNON, Off the Top
Rope, and his other original shows/games — transplant into Mochiichao
**LITERALLY BY NAME**, straight over. No renaming, no identity
adaptation: the person keeps their name and who they are; only the
landing (species form / Ranger / dojo / Elite role) is new.

The **ONE check** run per character: if a character's name is somebody
**ELSE's** proprietary name (a real wrestler's ring name carried as-is,
another production's character name), it is **FLAGGED** in this roster
for the owner's call — never renamed by the drafter, never dropped.
Flags are collected in §5. Donor bases in his home canons (a Tekken /
VF / other fighter used as a body or moveset donor) are **not** names
and are **not** flagged — his construction-kit rule already governs
those: the body is a kit piece, the name and soul are his.

### Tier key

- **OWNER-DIRECTED** — the owner ordered this transplant today.
- **OWNER-LOCKED** — already canon by the owner's earlier lock.
- **CONSIDERED** — drafter's casting proposal; ideas tier only.
- **FLAG** — proprietary-name or real-person-basis note for the owner's
  call (see §5). A flag changes nothing by itself.
