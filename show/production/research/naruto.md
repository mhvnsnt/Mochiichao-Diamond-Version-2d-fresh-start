# Production research — Naruto / Naruto Shippuden (Studio Pierrot)

Study layer for the Mochiichao show build. Sources: the five-series
production-research report compiled 2026-10-27. Prices and dates are
**as reported 2026-10-27**; Japanese settei books are Japanese-
language and often go out of print — second-hand availability varies.

## Production method (the headline)
**No rigs.** Classic Japanese TV-anime production: storyboard
(e-konte) → layout → **genga** (key animation, pencil + coloured
pencil on animation paper) → **douga** (in-betweens/clean-up) → scan
→ digital paint/composite (with digital blur/speed effects pioneered
on the show). No cut-out rig system exists for the series and **no
official rig/template files exist publicly**. Learning = studying
genga and settei, not downloading files.

## Free / public study sources
- **Sakugabooru + Sakuga Blog** — the best free technique archive
  for Naruto: cuts tagged by animator (Norio Matsumoto, Hiroyuki
  Yamashita, Masayuki Kouda, Chengxi Huang, Shingo Yamashita) plus
  `genga` / `production_materials` posts of raw key drawings.
  https://www.sakugabooru.com/ · https://blog.sakugabooru.com/
  (2016 year-end piece analyses Shippuden #476–478.)
- **Studio Pierrot official YouTube — "ROAD OF NARUTO"** (3 Oct
  2022): the studio's own 10-minute retrospective with **newly
  animated re-creations** of famous scenes — a sanctioned masterclass
  in re-boarding/re-animating the same scenes.
- **Naruto Official Site** — https://naruto-official.com (news,
  gallery, events).
- ***Naruto the Gallery*** (Anime 20th Anniversary exhibition, Akihabara
  UDX, 10 Dec 2022 – 31 Jan 2023, then touring) — anime production
  material and never-before-seen concept art in person; **no official
  catalogue reproducing the settei was identified**.
- **Narutopedia "Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets" citations** — a
  finding aid confirming settei details (heights/proportions) are
  publicly documented; not a source to copy art from.
- **Original genga/douga via galleries/auctions** (e.g. Galerie de la
  Bande Dessinée, Brussels) — viewable study images of hand-drawn
  Pierrot keys/in-betweens.

## Study route (from the report)
1. Import the two Movic 2026 Setting Materials Collections —
   characters/props + background art.
2. Viz *Anime Profiles* 1–2 in English for production interviews
   and designs.
3. Sakugabooru / Sakuga Blog by animator name for fight-animation
   technique.
4. Pierrot's ROAD OF NARUTO for studio-sanctioned re-animation
   comparison.

**Bottom line (report): HIGH legitimate access (in Japanese).** With
the 2026 Movic sets, a researcher can legitimately own most core
character/prop/background settei in print — rare for a long-running
TV anime.
