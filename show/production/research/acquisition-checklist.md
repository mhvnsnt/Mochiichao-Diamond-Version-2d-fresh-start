# Acquisition checklist — building the study shelf

The books that give the Mochiichao show build legitimate, ownable
production material for the five researched series (plus the two
bonus ecosystems from the report). Titles/ISBNs exactly as the
2026-10-27 research report states them; **prices/dates are "as
reported 2026-10-27"** — Japanese settei books are Japanese-language
and frequently go out of print, so check current availability and
second-hand prices before ordering.
