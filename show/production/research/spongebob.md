# Production research — SpongeBob SquarePants (Nickelodeon)

Study layer for the Mochiichao show build. Sources: the five-series
production-research report compiled 2026-10-27 (publisher/retailer/
museum/staff pages). Prices and dates are **as reported 2026-10-27**
and will drift — recheck before buying (see acquisition-checklist.md).

## Production method (the headline)
**Hand-drawn, storyboard-driven. No rigs exist for the main series
and none have ever been publicly shared.** ~50 people per episode:
storyboarded at Nickelodeon Animation Studio (Burbank), animated by
hand at Rough Draft Studios (South Korea), backgrounds painted,
finishing/edit/music in California. Season 1 was cel animation;
Season 2 onward digital ink-and-paint; from Season 5 (2008) boards
and drawing moved to Wacom Cintiqs — still hand-drawn, not puppet
animation. The CG spin-off *Kamp Koral* uses CG models, also never
shared. There is no official Toon Boom/Flash rig pack — anything
offered as one online is fan-made or unauthorised.

## Free / public study sources
- **Vincent Waller (creative director), own YouTube channel** — he
  posted **rough AND final storyboard animatic videos** himself
  ("Chatterbox Gary", "Don't Feed the Clowns", "Bottle Burglars",
  "Larry the Floor Manager"), with rough/final/plussing/timing
  credits. The best free boarding study material for any show here.
  Coverage: https://www.nickalive.net/2018/02/chatterbox-gary-and-dont-feed-clowns.html
- **Board artists' own blogs/Tumblr** (e.g. Benjamin Arcand's "Drive
  Happy" boards) — follow artists directly, not re-uploads.
- ***Square Roots: The Story of SpongeBob SquarePants*** (2009
  documentary, VH1; on *The First 100 Episodes* DVD) — primary
  interview source.
- ***The Oral History of SpongeBob SquarePants*** — Hogan's Alley:
  how Hillenburg's bible was fully realised pre-pitch; Rocko and
  SpongeBob were outline→board shows.
  https://www.hoganmag.com/blog/the-oral-history-of-spongebob-squarepants
- **Traditional Animation model-sheet library** (SpongeBob section)
  and **Character Design References** — educational study archives;
  grey provenance: study, don't republish.
  http://www.traditionalanimation.com/animation-library/model-sheets/
  · https://characterdesignreferences.com/art-of-animation-gallery-2
- **Sakugabooru** — SpongeBob cuts tagged by artist (Aaron Springer,
  Chris Mitchell) + at least one production_materials/settei post.
  https://www.sakugabooru.com/post/show/61312
- **Heritage Auctions** — original style-guide/publicity model
  drawings (c.1999–2000s) freely viewable with descriptions.

## Study route (from the report)
1. Buy *Art of an Undersea World* (2026) — concepts/boards/
   backgrounds across the franchise.
2. Work through Waller's rough-vs-final videos shot by shot.
3. *Exposed!* + *Experience* + Season 1 DVD extras for early
   sketches and Model Pack excerpts.
4. Hogan's Alley oral history for the storyboard-driven workflow.

**Bottom line (report): MEDIUM-HIGH legitimate access.** No complete
bible/model pack or files — but unusually good storyboard access
because the creative director published boards himself.
