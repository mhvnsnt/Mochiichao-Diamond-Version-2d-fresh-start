# Production research — JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (anime, David Production)

Study layer for the Mochiichao show build. Sources: the five-series
production-research report compiled 2026-10-27. Prices and dates are
**as reported 2026-10-27**.

## Production method (the headline)
**No rigs for the TV series.** David Production's JoJo is hand-drawn
2D TV animation (e-konte → layout → genga → digital paint/composite),
with graphic manga effects (onomatopoeia, palette shifts) translated
into motion. **CG exception:** about half the openings (notably Parts
1, 2, 3 and 6) were made by CGI-specialist studio **Kamikaze Douga**;
their models and David Production's files are **not publicly
shared**. (The 1993–2000 A.P.P.P. OVA is a separate cel-era
production — don't confuse its designs with David Production's.)

## Free / public study sources
- **Sakuga Blog — exclusive interview with Terumi Nishii** (Part 4
  character designer), 31 July 2019: adapting Araki's detailed,
  non-animation-optimised designs, using manga-based figures checked
  by Araki as movement reference, slimming Part 4's bodies.
  https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2019/07/31/exclusive-interview-with-terumi-nishii-jojos-bizarre-adventure-as-a-fan-and-as-a-designer/
- **Sakugabooru** — JoJo `genga / production_materials` posts (e.g.
  Toru Iwazawa, *Vento Aureo*).
- ***Hirohiko Araki JoJo Exhibition: Ripples of Adventure*** —
  National Art Center, Tokyo, 24 Aug – 1 Oct 2018 (then Osaka):
  ~280 original drawings / ~200 exhibits, a "JOJO's Design" section
  and a section on how story ideas and JoJo poses are generated.
  https://www.nact.jp/english/exhibition_special/2018/jojoex-2018/
- **Shueisha Manga-Art Heritage** — Shueisha's own programme of
  museum-grade lithograph/lenticular reproductions of Araki
  originals (San Francisco Sept 2025, then Kyoto) — a legal,
  high-fidelity way to study his line work.
  https://mangaart.jp/lotteries/jojo-lithograph-1
- ***JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Anime 10th Anniversary Exhibition*** —
  Sunshine City, Tokyo, 10 Aug – 4 Sept 2022, then nationwide:
  storyboards, scripts, manuscripts and setting data used in
  production (in-person only).

## Study route (from the report)
1. Araki, *Manga in Theory and Practice* (English) for the
   character/world/story system.
2. AAA + AAA2 for anime genga and designer commentary across all
   six Parts.
3. Nishii Sakuga Blog interview for the manga→animation design
   translation.
4. *JoJo 6251* (English) for Araki's colour/Stand design.

**Bottom line (report): HIGH legitimate access** — split evenly
between manga method (in English) and anime genga (in Japanese). No
other property here has its creator's own textbook in English.
