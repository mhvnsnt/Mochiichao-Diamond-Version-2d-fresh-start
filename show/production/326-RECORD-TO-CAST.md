# #326 — FROM RECORD TO CAST → how a standout individual becomes a member of the show

**The gap this fills.** The maskless Verdilance
exists three ways at once: as a design (the
maskless art, now a special unique variant file),
as a game-side possibility (#323's placed standout,
default show-only), and as what the owner actually
ruled him to be — **a standout individual in the
story, the only Verdilance they've seen with no
face mask**. Nobody had written the road between
"individual on a record" and "character in the
cast." This spec is the road, written once so every
future standout walks the same one.

**The doctrine under it** is #218's, applied to
people: the show and the game keep **one canon of
records** — a depicted trade writes the same history
record the game's system would. A cast standout is
the same doctrine at individual scale: **one
individual, two lanes**, never a game version and a
show version who can drift apart.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/record-to-cast/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The pipeline (four seats, in order):**
  1. **The record.** The individual exists as an
     #80 record — species, seed, and its distinction
     in the distinction's own home: a ruled deviation
     in #318's register (the maskless Verdilance),
     or an authored distinction on the record for a
     story-placed standout. No record, no pipeline:
     a standout invented in a script with no record
     behind it is a casting defect of the same class
     as #283's fresh-sheet return.
  2. **The story individual.** Canon docs name the
     individual as a story person — who he is, where
     he lives in the world, what the story knows of
     him (#319's fame facts live here, as story
     facts). This seat is **lane-owned writing**
     (owner + show lane); the pipeline specifies its
     inputs and its fences, never its contents.
  3. **The cast seat.** The individual enters cast
     planning through #82's arc machinery like any
     cast member — with two additions the pipeline
     owns: his **register entry travels with the
     casting** (every board, rig, and portrait order
     for him cites #318, per #318's binding list),
     and his entrances are staged under #319's show
     law (reputation arrives first; he has no
     anonymous entrance after his first).
  4. **The continuing cast rules.** Across parts, a
     cast standout is carried by #283's returning-
     cast machinery — built from the recorded
     history, never redesigned — and his expression
     and story staging run on #320/#321 (his face is
     an open face; the species' covered faces around
     him are staged as faces, and his uncovered one
     is never staged as their revelation, #321's
     register exception).
- **The fences (closed):**
  - **Casting never back-writes the species sheet.**
    The Verdilance sheet keeps the species standard
    — grown face shield and all (#317). The maskless
    one is an entry in the register, not an edit to
    the species. A sheet PR that "fixes" the species
    to match a cast standout is out of bounds on
    this citation.
  - **The game mirror is the same record.** If the
    content lane places the individual in the game
    (#323's placement), the placement cites the
    **same** #80 record and the same register entry
    — one individual, two lanes. A game-side copy
    with its own seed or its own deviation text is
    a second individual wearing the first one's
    story, and is a defect.
  - **The register is the casting's source of
    truth for the distinction** — a board that draws
    the distinction from memory, from the variant
    *file* alone, or from another artist's last
    episode, instead of from #318's entry, fails
    #53-class continuity review.

## The worked path — the maskless Verdilance

Record: #80 individual, species Verdilance,
deviation `face-cover` absence, RULED 2026-10-27
(#318 entry 3; the maskless art file is the
variant of record the entry points at). Story
individual: **open canon seat** — his name, his
place in the story, and when the cast first meets
him are the owner's and the show lane's to write,
and this package instances none of it. Cast seat:
taken when the show lane casts him, under the
additions above. Game mirror: unplaced — #323's
default stands (show-only until the content lane
places him on this same record).

story, and his first meeting** — owner's/show-lane
canon, entire; likewise the story-individual seat
of any future standout. The pipeline lands complete
with those seats empty.
