## Native spec — `mochiichao/fossil-revival/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Intake.** A Ranger presents a remnant (an item
  in keeping, #31 class). The service reads
  `pointsTo`. **If `pointsTo` is null, the service
  cannot process it — and says so, in-world.** A
  remnant whose species has not been authored is a
  mystery in a drawer (#44's honest state), and the
  service never resolves a null pointer by guessing,
  generating, or substituting a species. Intake
  refusal is a normal outcome of this system, not
  an error state and not a teaser with a timer.
- **Process.** Cost and wait are consumed from
  #44's method record — both OPEN data; the service
  sets neither and quotes neither in its own text.
  During the wait, the remnant's status reads as
  in-process: the status field is #44's, and the
  in-process value is **proposed into #44's home**
  (proposal discipline — it lands there, citing
  this package, or it does not land). The wait runs
  on #19's clock.
- **Delivery — the write set, exhaustive:**
  1. The remnant record's status transition —
     held → processed. The remnant is **consumed**
     by the process; it does not survive as an
     item. (A surviving remnant would make revival
     a duplication engine; the consumption is the
     system's honesty about matter.)
  2. **One new individual record (#80).** A *new*
     individual of an old species — #44's H4 shape
     as system rather than story: variance and seed
     rolls fire fresh under #80's rules, because a
     revival is a **birth-class event** for record
     purposes. The revived individual is nobody's
     continuation, copy, or return.
  3. **Nothing else.** The species-level
     consequences are *derived*, not written: #14's
     kept state follows from the new record
     existing, and completion counts the species
     under the named-only rule (#44/#41) — only
     once its species record exists and is named
     into the registry, which is authoring, not
     service.
- **Non-writes (the #132-style boundary, stated as
  a table so landing can test it):** no care axes ·
  no bond — the new individual's bond begins where
  every newborn's begins; the service does not
  pre-love it · no honours · no story flags · no
  licence facts. And above all: **no write path to
  species records exists in this service.** It
  *reads* `pointsTo`. Species authoring is the
  data-first intake (#44's Gate 0 → sheet →
  design), a different room entirely.
- **`extinctInWild` is permanent — stated flatly.**
  Revival never touches it. A revived species is
  **still extinct in the wild**: #7's spawn tables
  gain no rows, #102's range maps gain no range,
  #163's sealing discipline is undisturbed. Process
  every remnant of a species and the species exists
  only in keeping — living, kept, and wild-extinct,
  all three true at once. Any consumer that reads
  "revived" as "restored to the wild" has misread
  this system; the misreading is named here so
  landing tests can check for it by name.
