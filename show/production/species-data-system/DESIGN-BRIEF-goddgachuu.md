## The Diablo pull (the owner's own work — cited)

DIABLO is the owner's own character in his Western Heart work —
the Devil Soul, one of its core fighters, whose tradition layer
is written in his own canon as **"Shinto Raijin + Hagoromo — the
thunder god and the feather robe"** (`mhvnsnt/western-heart`,
`roster/diablo/CANON.md`).

Traits this brief takes from Diablo, each as written there:

- **The Hagoromo.** Diablo's "Devil God" attire (Attire 2) is
  the Hagoromo feather robe — "the divine drapery"
  (`roster/diablo/CANON.md`; `roster/diablo/ATTIRE-SLOTS.md`).
  Goddgachuu takes it in the form the owner directed: a flowing
  heavenly **sash**, not a robe.
- **Raijin's Taiko Drum Ring.** The ring behind Diablo in the
  same attire: "the ring behind him is Raijin's Taiko Drum Ring
  — the Mitsudomoe swirl, the thunder god's halo of drums"
  (`roster/diablo/CANON.md`). ATTIRE-SLOTS.md adds the
  mythology: Raijin rides the clouds surrounded by the wheel of
  taiko drums and beats them to make thunder; each drum is
  painted with the Mitsudomoe (three-comma) swirl. Goddgachuu
  carries the ring the same way — behind him, a thunder god's
  halo.
- **Long flowing lines.** Diablo's written design carries "long
  flowing silver locks" (his wing hair grown into long flowing
  locks) and a rope-tied sash "with flowing cloth ends"
  (`roster/diablo/CANON.md`; `roster/diablo/ATTIRE-SLOTS.md`).
  Honest note: the owner's brief names **long dragon whiskers**
  from the Diablo mix; whiskers as such are not in Diablo's
  written description — the long-flowing-line trait is. The
  brief carries the whiskers as owner-directed (trait 2) and
  records the difference here rather than papering over it.

Also on Diablo's record, for the artist's context — **not**
directed into this mix by the owner: two short horns; a snarling
demon face; irezumi tattoo work across chest, shoulders and
arms; purple hakama with **gold dragon embroidery** on the legs
(same sources). If the owner wants any of these on Goddgachuu,
that is his call to add.

## Variant programme (owner plan, 2026-10-28 ~17:57 CT; matrix structure corrected ~18:00 CT — planned, not yet made)

Goddgachuu gets the most variations of any species. The
locked concept-5 version is the main/default — the most
common form. The structure is **one matrix** (owner
correction, 2026-10-28 ~18:00 CT — an earlier two-batch
reading of this programme was wrong and is struck):

- **Five variants (V1–V5),** each set apart by its jewel
  and sash treatment — including the **two-colour sash**
  variant (V3: a main colour + a secondary colourway
  colour).
- **× all five colourways EACH:** every variant exists in
  every colourway. CW1 is the main colourway (concept 5's
  white-and-blue); CW2–CW5 are full different colourways
  whose palettes the owner sets as each is made.
- **Jewel colours run across the matrix:** a pool of 10
  (sapphire blue the default), 5–10 in play across the
  versions; each version can carry a different jewel
  colour from the pool.
- **The build count:** 5 variants × 5 colourways = **25
  core versions**, × the jewel colours across them. This
  is a **BUILD LIST — all of them get made**, not a list
  to pick from.
- **Occurrence:** each variant sits at roughly **10%**
  for now; the percentages rebalance as more versions
  land. The locked main version stays the default — the
  one encountered most.

The matrix is written out in full in
`VARIANT-GRID-DRAFT-goddgachuu.md` beside this brief. Its
candidate colourway names for CW2–CW5 (Tempest Black,
Crimson Storm, Jade Heaven, Royal Violet, Golden Sun) are
**starting suggestions only — not decisions**; the
colourway palettes are the owner's to set as each is made.

Status: planned only. No variant art exists yet; the
versions land through the normal design gates as they are
made.
