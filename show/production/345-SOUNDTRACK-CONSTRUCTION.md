# #345 — SOUNDTRACK CONSTRUCTION → our OST, built the SA2 way, from our themes

**OWNER'S DIRECTION (Jamar, 2026-10-27), with the
standing honesty law applied:** SA2's soundtrack
*construction* is the model — per-character themes
stating identity, a finale theme stating the
season's point — and the soundtrack is built on
**our theme and our identity**. What transfers is
the *method* (construction-kit rule): how the
songs are assigned, shaped, and reprised. Every
note, word, and performance is Mochiichao's own,
produced on the pipelines named below. #344
founded the theme records; this spec is the
**build order** that turns records into an OST.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/soundtrack-construction/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **Per-character vocal themes (major humans).**
  Each major human (#344's casting call) gets a
  **vocal theme**: a song whose lyric is the
  identity brief set to the motif — who they are
  in the verse, what they want in the chorus, what
  they're hiding in the bridge (the brief's three
  facts are the song's three movements; a song
  that skips the bridge has skipped the character).
  - **Voice law:** vocals are human performances
    of human characters — legal and required to
    be *the character's* voice where the character
    sings in-world; where the theme is non-diegetic
    (the SA2 norm — the song is *about* them, over
    their scenes), it is the production's voice
    stating identity, and the record says which.
  - **Pipeline (named, per the honesty law):** the
    voice kit (Chatterbox TTS, MIT-licensed open
    weights, per the kit's own manifest at
    `teardowns/voice-kit/`) renders sung/spoken
    human vocal material from Mochiichao's own
    cast references; instrumental realisation
    draws on #72's instrument bank and the
    adaptive-music machinery. No commercial OST
    material, sample, or performance is used at
    any point — the donor is a *method*, named in
    the credits as method, never as source audio.
- **The season thesis theme.** One song per season
  states the season's point — the SA2 finale
  structure: the thesis theme is introduced as
  the season's flagship (the OP seat, below),
  arranged under the season's places and battles,
  and **reprised at the finale as itself** — the
  #343 shared finale is its home stage, where the
  song the season opened with says what the
  season was about, now with both campaigns'
  facts behind it. The thesis theme's record is a
  #344 place-class record whose subject is the
  season (seated in the season's bible entry);
  its brief's three facts are the season's own
  (what this season is / wants / is hiding).
  **What any season's point IS is canon — the
  owner's, entire.** The machinery is built so a
  season cannot ship without the slot being
  answered in the bible or explicitly deferred
  there; the deferral is a bible fact, not a
  pipeline gap.
- **Motif economy (one identity, many rooms).**
  Battle, place, and event arrangements are
  **versions inside #344's records**, composed
  from the subject's motif — a dojo's battle
  arrangement grows from the place motif and the
  leader's motif together (two records, one cue;
  the cue cites both, and #156's promo and #215's
  beds read the same versions under the
  current-version law). No arrangement introduces
  a new motif for an existing subject: new
  musical identity for a known subject is a
  #344 event (a new version with supersession
  named), never arrangement drift.
- **The OP is the identity flagship.** The opening
  titles (the List 3 opening-titles format owns
  the visual record) carry the season thesis
  theme's flagship arrangement. **Length-agnostic
  machinery:** the construction takes the OP slot
  as a parameter — the owner's standing 60s/90s
  slot question stays open and this spec asserts
  neither; arrangement versions are cut to the
  slot the bible rules, and a re-cut is a version,
  not a new theme.
- **Production honesty (the licence law, applied
  to music).** Every production input is named
  with its class: the voice kit's weights (open,
  MIT), #72's bank (in-house), any future sample
  or font library (named at adoption, class
  stated before use — open / free proprietary /
  paid, never flattened). The OST's credits roll
  (the List 3 credits format) lists method debts
  (SA2's construction) as *method* and contains
  no borrowed audio to credit — that sentence is
  the audit this spec demands of every season's
  delivery, checked at the #75 house finish.
