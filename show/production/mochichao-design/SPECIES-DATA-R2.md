## A1 — The guard-dog line

### Pupparmor — A1 · stage 1 of Pupparmor → Shieldane → Bastihound
- **Line / concept:** A1 — a round-armoured puppy whose soft plates harden each stage; the final stands upright as a tower-shield hound.
- **Types:** Beast / Chrome
- **Category:** the Puppy Plate Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — about the size of a plump household puppy, low to the ground.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Pupparmor is born with soft, overlapping plates that flex when it tumbles and stiffen when it is startled. It practises guarding by sitting very still in doorways, usually asleep within moments. A sleeping Pupparmor in a doorway is treated by everyone who lives there as a door that has earned its rest.
- **Temperament:** Loyal, earnest, and sleepy; brave in intention long before it is brave in fact.
- **Habitat:** Region 2 settlements, farmyards, and garden paths — wherever there is a threshold it can appoint itself to.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Eats soft grains, roots, and whatever it is given for good guarding. Rolls rather than runs when excited; its plates clink faintly as it goes.
- **Evolution:** Becomes Shieldane as it grows and its soft plates harden into true armour through everyday guarding and play (growth class — no special trigger).
- **Discovery:** Region 2 — early-route grassland edges, village outskirts, and garden gates. Rarity: common. Method: grass walk, daytime. Level band: early-route. A Ranger first meets it close to home, often sitting in the middle of a path refusing to be a hazard.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: a round puppy yip with a faint metallic clink from its plates underneath. Pitch contour: short upward chirp, then a drop. Duration: very short. Rhythm: single yip, or two quick yips when excited. Variations — idle: soft sleepy huff-yips; call: repeated bright yips toward its Ranger; hurt: a sharp yelp and plate-clatter; faint: one low whine fading out; affection: muffled contented yips against a leg; evolution moment: yips deepening mid-sequence as the plates ring harder.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Quadruped puppy; short legs, no manipulators; small jaw with puppy teeth, mouth opens wide for yips but bite reads playful, not dangerous. Locomotion is a waddling trot and a sideways roll. Plates are segmented over the back and shoulders and must flex, not move as one shell.
- **Palette seed:** warm cream belly, soft slate-grey plates, tan ears, dull steel-blue plate rims.
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: pad print. Four toes plus a main pad per foot, claws usually not showing (soft puppy claws). Gait: alternating steps that wander and double back, with occasional round roll-marks where it has tumbled. Print size class: small. Distinctive marker: a faint crescent scuff behind each pad print where a plate edge brushes the ground as it sits down mid-walk.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: round quadruped, almost a ball on legs. Proportions: head large against the body, legs very short. Key masses: the domed plated back is the main mass, head second. Reads at sprite size as a round armoured ball with ears. Identifying negative space: the small notches between ear and plate dome, and the low belly arc barely clearing the ground between the short legs.
- **Stat profile (words only):** sturdy for its size, slow, defence-leaning even this young. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A round puppy wearing its armour as a soft domed blanket of plates — the silhouette must read "puppy first, armour second." Key features: overlapping back plates, floppy ears, cream belly. Must never change: it stays quadruped and unmistakably a puppy; the plates are grown, rounded, and soft-edged, never spiked or bladed; no held items.
- **Flags:** Line flag (source §5.5): the final stage's name was coined as Bastihound after an earlier draft name was dropped for echoing an existing source name — Bastihound is the settled name. No other ⚠ sharing recorded for this line in source §5.

Identifying negative space: the slim gap between shield edge and free foreleg, and the arch between its planted hind feet under the body's hem of plates.

Key features: shield-foreleg planted like a gate, plated shoulders, kind hound head above the shield rim. Must never change: still unmistakably the same dog as Pupparmor (source evolution note); the shield is grown from the foreleg and can never be removed, held in a hand, or sold; the read is guardian, never knight-with-equipment or menace.

## A2 — The lance-fish line

### Flickfin — A2 · stage 1 of Flickfin → Pikeperil → Halberdrake
- **Line / concept:** A2 — a minnow with a needle nose; the needle becomes a pike, then the whole fish becomes a halberd-headed drake that duels by fencing.
- **Types:** Aqua / Chrome
- **Category:** the Needle Minnow Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — a minnow that fits the idea of a hand, all flicker and dart.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Flickfin is a minnow with a needle for a nose, and it has not yet learned what the needle is for. It jabs at bubbles, falling leaves, and its own reflection, then darts away delighted. Whole schools practise on the same reed until the reed gives up and falls over.
- **Temperament:** Daring, excitable, and easily distracted; brave at the size where bravery is cheap.
- **Habitat:** Region 2 shallow rivers, reed beds, and clear pond margins.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Snaps up water insects and tiny larvae, stabbing first and checking afterwards. Moves in flickering schools that turn as one fish.
- **Evolution:** Becomes Pikeperil as it grows and the needle nose lengthens into a true pike (growth class).
- **Discovery:** Region 2 — early-route rivers and ponds. Rarity: common. Method: water (shallows / fishing-class encounter), daytime. Level band: early-route. A Ranger first meets it in the first river they wade, needling their boot.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: underwater, a tiny ticking click made with the needle against the water; above water, a small wet pip. Pitch contour: flat and very high. Duration: extremely short. Rhythm: single clicks, or a rapid rattle when schooling. Variations — idle: occasional single clicks; call: a quick click-chorus with the school; hurt: one sharp tick and silence; faint: clicks slowing to a stop; affection: soft rapid ticking near a hand; evolution moment: clicks spacing out as the pike lengthens the sound.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** True fish body; no limbs, no manipulators. The needle nose is the line's one idea and must be in every action — jabs, probes, and fencing beats all come from the snout. Mouth is tiny and behind the needle's base. Locomotion is darting swimming only; out of water it flops.
- **Palette seed:** silver-blue back, pale belly, bright steel needle, dark bead eye stripe.
- **Footprint:** Leaves no footprints. Trace spec: in shallow margins, fine needle-prick pits in soft mud where it has jabbed at things, in loose clusters; in water, a brief V-wake far too pointed for its size. Distinctive marker: the prick-pit clusters.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: slim teardrop fish. Proportions: needle about as long again as the head, tail fin small. Key masses: the body is one small mass, the needle a single leading line. Reads at sprite size as a fish with a pin. Identifying negative space: the thin triangle between needle and lower jaw line, and the notch between tail-fin lobes.
- **Stat profile (words only):** very fast, very frail; a needle, not yet a weapon. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A minnow whose whole design is one needle nose on a small silver fish. Must never change: the weapon grows from the snout and is part of the face at every stage (the line's one idea, per the source); no fins shaped as hands, no held items; the read stays quick and bright, not predatory-menacing.
- **Flags:** none

### Pikeperil — A2 · stage 2 of Flickfin → Pikeperil → Halberdrake

- **Temperament:** Proud, disciplined, and touchy about honour; a duellist's manners in a fish.
- **Habitat:** Region 2 slow rivers, lake edges, and flooded meadows with open water for duelling.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Hunts smaller fish and large water insects with a single pike-thrust. Spends its still hours holding a fencing guard, nose up-current.
- **Evolution:** Becomes Halberdrake at full maturity, when the pike broadens into a halberd head and the body takes its drake form (growth class into the line's single final-stage break).
- **Discovery:** Region 2 — mid-region rivers and lakes. Rarity: uncommon. Method: water, dawn and daytime. Level band: mid-region. A Ranger first plausibly meets one by disturbing a dawn duel — both fish will turn and consider the interruption together.

- **Footprint:** Leaves no footprints. Trace spec: long single score-lines in mud at the water's edge where a duellist has beached its pike testing the bank, and paired parallel wakes on still water at dawn. Distinctive marker: the bank score-lines, always in duelling pairs.

- **Stat profile (words only):** fast, precise, physical-leaning; still frail for its length. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A pike-fish that reads as a fencer mid-guard: lean body, formal posture, the snout grown into a true pike. Must never change: weapon stays grown from the snout; silhouette stays a fish — the drake break is reserved for the final stage only; no armour plating beyond the Chrome sheen of the pike itself.
- **Flags:** none
