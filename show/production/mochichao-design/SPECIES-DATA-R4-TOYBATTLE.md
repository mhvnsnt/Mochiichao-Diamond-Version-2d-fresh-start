# Species Data — Region 4 Toy-Battle / Mecha Register (Bucket C1)

**Source:** `~/workspace/species-brainstorm/NEW-SPECIES-IDEAS.md` Bucket C1 (§ lines 188–251) and §5 collision/flag record (lines 534–638); `~/workspace/species-brainstorm/REGIONAL-FLAVOR-LAW.md` §1 and §4, especially §4.2

**Count:** 7 concepts / 16 stage sheets

**Register note — Secret-law care:** Every creature in this bucket is a living creature of the Rift whose body happens to read as machine-like — grown shell, grown armour, grown masks, spinning bodies, ball forms, and carrier frames are all anatomy, born and grown, never built. Nobody in-world builds, operates, pilots as technology, or explains these creatures as machines; a "rider" here is always a creature-bond between two species who choose each other, a "frame" is always a living body, and restraint armour is always a shell the creature grew itself. In-world they are strange beasts of the Rift, full stop.

### Marblit — C1-1 · first stage, ball-form chick

- **Line / concept:** C1-1 Marblit → Popriffin — a chick that sleeps, travels, and hides as a perfect carved ball, springing open into a griffin when startled or called; donor mechanic: the pop-open battle toy, translated into a ball resting form and a spring-open anatomy event.
- **Types:** Gale/Beast
- **Category:** the Pop-Ball Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — about the size of a curled-up hedgehog or a large grapefruit held in two hands, easily cupped and carried.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Marblit is most often found as a smooth, feather-carved ball sitting perfectly still among Rift stones. If a trusted voice calls or a sudden noise startles it, the ball pops open with a clear, ringing pop — wings first — and a small griffin chick tumbles out, blinking. It folds itself away again just as neatly to sleep, roll downhill, or hide from anything larger than itself.
- **Temperament:** Shy, startle-prone, and deeply comforted by routine; bold for a heartbeat after popping open, then quick to fold away again.
- **Habitat:** Rift scrub, stony crossings, and the quieter edges of the institute grounds in Region 4, where round stones give its ball form good company.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Pecks seeds, soft berries, and small insects in its open form; spends much of the day balled, rolling short distances rather than walking them, and naps in shallow stone hollows it has worn smooth.
- **Evolution / form change:** The pop-open is a form change, not an evolution — seams along the feather-shell part, the wings unfold first, then head, talons, and tail spring free, and the whole body can refold along the same seams. It evolves into Popriffin after a life of startles survived and flights attempted, when its wings are strong enough to carry the ball form rather than merely burst out of it; the ball form itself is never outgrown.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 Rift crossings and stony institute outskirts; rarity class uncommon, because most are passed over as stones. Method: wild encounter — listen for a soft pop at dawn, or call gently near suspiciously perfect round stones. Conditions: calmest and most likely to open in morning light; stays balled through storms. Level band in words: low band. First-meeting note: first meetings almost always begin with the pop — Rangers are advised not to flinch, as flinching sends it straight back into the ball.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character — a small hollow pop followed by a bright chick chirp, the pop made by the feather-shell seams, the chirp from a small hooked beak. Pitch contour in words: a flat percussive pop, then a chirp that rises quickly and settles. Duration class: very short. Rhythm pattern: single pop-chirp, sometimes doubled when excited. Variations per state — idle: soft muffled chirps from inside the closed ball; call: pop first, then a loud repeated chirp to answer a trusted voice; hurt: a sharp squeak and an instant refold click; faint: a dwindling chirp as the ball settles loosely; affection: quiet peeping with the head tucked out of a half-open ball; evolution / form-change moment: the pop itself is the signature sound, louder and rounder at evolution into a full ringing crack of feathers.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Four limbs in open form — two winged fore-limbs with feathered wings that emerge first, and two hind talon legs for hopping and perching; no hands, manipulation is by beak and talon grip. Mouth is a small hooked griffin beak that opens wide for chirps. Locomotion is triple-mode: rolling in ball form, hopping and scrambling open, and short fluttering bursts — it cannot yet sustain flight. Mode-change anatomy is the whole brief: the feather-shell is continuous body surface with visible seam lines, the wings fold outermost, and the pop must read as a flower opening, fast and organic, never as panels on a hinge.
- **Palette seed:** warm cream, dusty gold, soft sky blue, stone grey
- **Footprint:** Two traces, form-dependent. Ball-form travel leaves a rolling track: a single continuous smooth groove, narrow and shallow, with faint feather-seam ridges pressed at regular turns and no footfalls at all. Open form leaves tiny griffin prints — three narrow forward talon toes and one short back toe, in a hopping pattern of paired prints with frequent gaps where it fluttered. Print size class: tiny. Distinctive marker: a rolling groove that abruptly becomes hopping talon prints where it popped open.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class per form — resting/ball: a perfect circle, unbroken except for a faint seam arc; open: a round chick body dominated by two oversized unfolding wings, small hooked head at the top, short tail fan at the back. Proportions in words: head large for the body, wings larger still when spread, legs short. Key visual masses: the ball itself, then the wings. What reads at sprite size: circle, seam line, wing burst. Identifying negative space: the clean crescent gaps between each spread wing and the round body.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Modest all-round with surprising burst speed on the pop and in short flights; fragile when caught open, hard to hurt when balled; low staying power. — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is a perfect ball that becomes a griffin chick, wings first. Key features are the carved-feather seam lines on the ball, the hooked beak, and wings visibly too grand for the chick. Must-never-change list: the ball is the creature's own feathered body, never a shell it carries or a toy it lives in; the pop is anatomy, never mechanism; the griffin read — beak, wings, talons — stays at both stages.
- **Flags:** ⚠ Name proximity noted in §5.5 of the source: Marblit was a closest surviving call (vs. Litwick — different word, different creature, kept).

### Popriffin — C1-1 · final stage, pop-open griffin

- **Line / concept:** C1-1 Marblit → Popriffin — the adult griffin that still folds back to a perfect ball to sleep, old ones becoming perfectly round; donor mechanic: the pop-open battle toy, translated into a lifelong ball resting form retained at full size.
- **Types:** Gale/Beast
- **Category:** the Pop-Ball Mochi
- **Size class:** Medium — about the size of a large dog or a small pony when open, and a strikingly large, smooth ball when folded.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Popriffin keeps Marblit's trick for life: however grand its wings and talons become, it still folds into a seamless ball to sleep, and the oldest individuals are so perfectly round that Rift travellers mistake them for carved monuments. Its pop is famous across the region — a deep, resonant crack of feathers that locals use as a doorbell, a greeting, and a warning. In flight it is a true griffin, eagle-fronted and lion-bodied, proud of every feather.
- **Temperament:** Proud, territorial over favourite perches and sleeping hollows, and theatrical — it enjoys the pop, and times it for an audience when it can.
- **Habitat:** High Rift crags, crossing towers, and old institute rooftops and gateposts in Region 4, anywhere a ball can sit in plain sight and a griffin can launch.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Hunts small Rift gamebirds and mountain rodents, scavenges generously, and carries food back to its perch in its talons; sleeps balled through the hottest and coldest hours, and answers other Popriffin's pops across valleys in rolling conversation.
- **Evolution / form change:** Final stage; no further evolution. The form change is unchanged in kind from Marblit and grander in scale — the ball's seams part, wings burst out first with the full pop, then the eagle head, fore-talons, lion hindquarters, and tail unfold in sequence. Elders fold more slowly and open more deliberately, and their roundness in ball form is a mark of age and honour in the species.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 high Rift crossings, crag paths, and institute rooftops; rarity class rare — more often heard (the pop, carrying far) than seen open. Method: wild encounter at perch sites; approach a resting ball openly and call, never sneak, as a startled pop at this size is a physical event. Conditions: opens most readily at dawn and at familiar voices. Level band in words: mid-to-upper band for wild Rift adults. First-meeting note: the first pop is routinely described as a door slamming and a banner unfurling at once — stand clear of the wing arc.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character — a deep seam-pop from the feather-shell under a powerful eagle-griffin screech. Pitch contour in words: percussive pop low, then a screech that climbs, holds, and cuts off clean. Duration class: short but carrying. Rhythm pattern: pop-screech as a single unit; greeting exchanges are answering pairs across distance. Variations per state — idle: low feathered huffs and occasional soft pops from shifting in its sleep; call: full pop and screech together, unmistakable; hurt: a harsh bark-screech and a defensive half-open wing flare; faint: a weakening screech as it folds, ending in a dull final pop; affection: a surprisingly gentle churring screech with the ball rocking slightly; evolution / form-change moment: no evolution remains, but every pop-open carries the same signature crack, deepest in old, round individuals.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Four limbs — eagle forelegs ending in true talons that are its main manipulators, lion hindlegs for launching and landing, and very large feathered wings rooted at the shoulders that always deploy first in the pop. Mouth is a strong hooked beak; jaw opens on a clear hinge for screech and prey-carrying, never for speech. Locomotion: rolling in ball form (slow, deliberate, downhill-assisted), powerful sustained flight open, and a bounding quadruped run. Mode-change anatomy: seam lines run visibly through the adult feathering and flank fur; the fold order — tail, hindquarters, head, talons, wings last — and the reverse on opening must stay consistent for animators.
- **Palette seed:** rich gold, warm cream, deep bronze, sky blue, stone grey
- **Footprint:** Two traces, form-dependent. Ball-form travel leaves a rolling track: one broad, deep, continuous groove, wide as the folded body, pressed smooth with faint seam ridges, often running in long straight downhill lines. Open form leaves a mixed griffin print — large three-toed talon marks in front and broad padded lion-paw prints behind, in a bounding gait that breaks into long printless stretches where it took wing. Print size class: large for its size class. Distinctive marker: a monument-smooth groove that ends in an explosion of talon and paw prints at the pop-open point.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class per form — resting/ball: a perfect, monumental circle; open: a classic griffin — eagle head and wings over a lion body, tail curving out behind. Proportions in words: wings the dominant mass, head held high, hindquarters heavy and grounded. Key visual masses: wing pair, feathered chest, ball form's pure roundness. What reads at sprite size: circle versus winged griffin, nothing between. Identifying negative space: the tall arch between the raised wings and the back when it flares.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Strong and fast in the air with a powerful opening burst; well-defended balled, exposed in the half-second of opening; proud stamina over long flights. — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A true griffin that happens to fold into a perfect ball, at both ends of its life. Key features are the resonant pop, the seam-lined feathering and fur that allow the fold, and the elder's perfect roundness. Must-never-change list: the ball never goes away and is never a separate object; wings always emerge first; no harness, saddle, or artefact read — this is folklore griffin anatomy, start to finish.
- **Flags:** none

### Whirligig — C1-2 · first stage, spinning beetle

- **Line / concept:** C1-2 Whirligig → Cyclossus — a top-bodied beetle that battles by spinning, its small bright creature-core showing its face at full spin; donor mechanic: the spirit-core housed in a spinning toy body, translated into a dense spinning beetle body with a living core-face.
- **Types:** Chrome/Beast
- **Category:** the Spirit-Core Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — about the size of a household spinning top crossed with a plump beetle, easily held in one hand when still.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Whirligig's body is a dense, polished top of a beetle, and its way of settling anything is to spin. At rest it waddles on short legs, mild and a little dizzy-looking; wound up by its own effort, it spins faster and faster until the shell blurs — and through the blur, small and bright, the creature's true face shows, calm at the centre of the storm. Duels between Whirligig are contests of endurance, balance, and nerve, and can outlast their audience.
- **Temperament:** Patient, stubborn, and serenely competitive; rarely starts a quarrel, almost never concedes one.
- **Habitat:** Hard flat ground across the Region 4 Rift — crossing plazas, dried lakebeds, institute courtyards — anywhere smooth enough for a long spin.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Grazes algae, lichen, and fallen fruit, storing energy in its dense body for long spins; spends still hours sunning its shell to a polish, and settles disputes with neighbours by formal spin-duel rather than by biting or fleeing.
- **Evolution / form change:** No second form; the spin is a state, not a form — legs tuck, the body drops onto its spinning point, and the shell blurs as speed builds until the core-face shows. It evolves into Cyclossus when a lifetime of duels has grown the body massively around the core, the face at the centre remaining exactly itself.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 institute courtyards, Rift crossing plazas, and dried flats; rarity class common in the right places, where the ground is scored with old duel circles. Method: wild encounter — follow the humming of a duel in progress, or set down a smooth stone and wait. Conditions: most active and most willing to spin in dry, still weather. Level band in words: low band. First-meeting note: a first meeting is usually mid-spin; wait for it to wobble to a stop rather than reaching in, as a grabbed Whirligig spins harder on principle.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character — a small bright chirring voice from the core-face, layered under the rising hum of the spinning shell. Pitch contour in words: at rest, low soft chirrs; spinning up, the hum climbs steadily while the voice stays level at the centre. Duration class: short calls, but the spin hum itself can run long. Rhythm pattern: even chirrs in a steady beat, quickening slightly as balance is challenged. Variations per state — idle: drowsy chirrs between long silences; call: a clear level chirr held through a full spin; hurt: a wobbling, pitch-dipping chirr as the spin falters; faint: the hum runs down in a long descending slide, voice last; affection: gentle chirrs at a slow, rocking spin; evolution / form-change moment: entering full spin is marked by the shell's hum swallowing the voice, then the voice re-emerging, unchanged, at the centre.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Six short beetle legs that tuck completely away at spin; no hands — manipulation is by two small foreleg claws and by nudging with the shell. Mouth is the core-face's small mouth, set in the bright inner creature that only becomes visible at full spin — the face is the creature, and animators must keep it identical at every speed. Locomotion: a waddling six-leg walk at rest, and true gyroscopic spinning on a single body point in duel, travelling in slow drifting arcs while spinning. The shell is grown and polished, with ridge rings that catch light at speed; the blur of the shell and the clarity of the core-face must read simultaneously.
- **Palette seed:** polished bronze, bright teal, warm cream, beetle black
- **Footprint:** Two traces, state-dependent. Walking leaves a double row of tiny six-leg beetle ticks, small and neat. Spinning leaves its signature trace instead of footprints: a spiral scuff — concentric scored circles with an inward spiral tail where it drifted, polished bright at the centre point. Print size class: tiny for the steps, small-to-medium for a finished duel scuff. Distinctive marker: duel grounds keep overlapping spiral scuffs like signatures, newest brightest.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class per form — resting: a squat beetle-top, domed shell over a pointed base, legs splayed; spinning: a clean spinning-top cone-and-dome with blurred edges and a small bright face at the centre. Proportions in words: body almost entirely shell, legs minimal, core-face small. Key visual masses: the polished dome and the bright core. What reads at sprite size: top shape and central face. Identifying negative space: the thin clear ring between the blurred shell edge and the still centre where the face sits.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Defence and endurance carry the species — it wins by still spinning when the other stops; modest offence that is mostly momentum, low walking speed, remarkable balance. — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A beetle that is also a top, with a small bright face at its heart. Key features are the dense polished shell with its ridge rings, the tuck-away legs, and the core-face that appears at full spin. Must-never-change list: the core face is the same individual at both stages and is the creature itself — the body is never a toy it operates; duels are endurance and balance, never blades or blows; the shell is grown beetle chitin with a chrome sheen, never metal plating.
- **Flags:** none

### Cyclossus — C1-2 · final stage, spin-duel colossus

- **Line / concept:** C1-2 Whirligig → Cyclossus — a walking colossus that still settles disputes by spin-duel and has never once been seen to stop on its own; donor mechanic: the spirit-core in a spinning toy body, translated at colossal scale — the body grew, the core-face did not change.
- **Types:** Chrome/Beast
- **Category:** the Spirit-Core Mochi
- **Size class:** Huge — a walking colossus, taller than the institute's gatehouses, whose shadow settles arguments before it arrives.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Cyclossus walks the Rift like weather coming, a mountain of a beetle-bodied giant — until a dispute is put to it, when it lowers itself, tucks its great legs, and spins. Nothing that big should spin that long, and nothing that spins that long should have so small and calm a face at its centre, yet there it is: the same bright core-face Whirligig had, unchanged. No one has ever seen a Cyclossus stop spinning of its own accord; duels end when the other party, the audience, or the day gives out.
- **Temperament:** Imperturbable, formal, and almost ceremonially fair; treats every duel as a court session and every result as final.
- **Habitat:** The open Region 4 Rift flats and the great crossing grounds, where there is room to spin; it walks established duel-circuits between them like a judge on rounds.
- **Diet & behaviour:** A slow, vast grazer of Rift scrub, lichen beds, and windfall fruit, eating in the long still hours between walks; its social life is its circuit — arriving, hearing disputes simply by being present, spinning, and walking on.
- **Evolution / form change:** Final stage; no further evolution. The form change is the duel stance: walking body settles onto its spinning point, legs tuck in tiers, the colossal shell builds speed until it blurs to a shining wall, and the small core-face shows, calm and identical, at the centre. Returning to walking is equally deliberate — a long, controlled run-down that it permits rather than suffers.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 great Rift flats and named-by-use duel grounds near crossings; rarity class very rare, and effectively announced — humming and dust announce one long before it is seen. Method: wild encounter by following a duel-circuit; wait at a marked duel ground. Conditions: walks in clear weather, spins in any weather a dispute demands. Level band in words: upper band. First-meeting note: first meetings are formal — stand at the edge of the duel ground, state nothing, want nothing loudly, and let it regard you with the small calm face.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character — the same small bright voice as Whirligig, improbably unchanged, carried at the centre of a shell-hum like distant thunder rolling in a circle. Pitch contour in words: voice level and clear; the hum rises in vast slow steps as speed builds and falls just as slowly. Duration class: long — calls and hums outlast ordinary encounters. Rhythm pattern: slow even chirrs, widely spaced, metronome-steady through a duel. Variations per state — idle: rare, soft chirrs from a body at monumental rest; call: one level call that carries absurdly far on the spinning air; hurt: the voice dips once, steadying as balance is recovered; faint: a run-down lasting a long while, hum descending storey by storey, voice calm to the end; affection: a low rocking spin and warm slow chirrs, usually for a lifelong keeper; evolution / form-change moment: no evolution remains; entering the duel spin, the thunder arrives first and the familiar small voice emerges inside it.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Six colossal legs in tucking tiers for walking and stance changes; no hands — it acts on the world by presence, spin, and delicate nudges of the shell rim. The mouth belongs to the core-face only; the great body has no other face, and animators must resist adding one. Locomotion: slow mountainous walking, and gyroscopic spinning on the body point with a drifting, arena-filling travel arc. Anatomy emphasis for move animation: attacks are momentum, balance, and endurance made visible — rams are glancing, duels are won by outlasting; the core-face's expression carries the emotion of every scene while the body carries the scale.
- **Palette seed:** weathered bronze, bright teal, warm cream, deep slate
- **Footprint:** Two traces, mode-dependent. Walking leaves huge round beetle-foot prints in two heavy rows, each print a broad polished oval, with a furrowed drag line where the spinning point trails between strides. Spinning leaves its true signature: a vast spiral scuff, arena-sized — concentric scored rings running inward to a bright polished centre, cut deeper with every hour of the duel. Print size class: huge in both modes. Distinctive marker: a finished Cyclossus duel ground can be read like a record — rings for the duel's length, centre for its calm.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class per form — walking: a domed mountain on six legs, core-face a small bright point at the front centre; spinning: a colossal top, a shining blurred dome-and-cone with a still, tiny, bright face at its exact centre. Proportions in words: body overwhelmingly shell, legs long but visually minor, face deliberately, startlingly small. Key visual masses: the dome, the blur wall at spin, the core point of light. What reads at sprite size: giant top, tiny face — the contrast is the species. Identifying negative space: the calm circular eye of stillness at the centre of the spinning blur.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Enormous defence and endurance, respectable force delivered as momentum rather than strikes; slow to start, slower to stop, and nearly impossible to unbalance. — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A colossus built around an unchanged small face. Key features are the tiered tucking legs, the polished monumental shell, and the core-face that is Whirligig's own, exactly. Must-never-change list: the face never scales up, ages into menace, or moves off-centre; it has never been seen to stop on its own — frame its stopping as permission, never defeat-by-exhaustion played cheaply; duels stay contests of endurance and nerve, never brawls.
- **Flags:** none

### Packpup — C1-3 · first stage, carrying pup

- **Line / concept:** C1-3 Packpup → Rigrex — a pup that carries things on its back by instinct; donor mechanic: the alt-mode idea in seed form — a back already shaped by the carrying it will one day do at full scale, translated wholly into biology.
- **Types:** Beast/Terra
- **Category:** the Carrier Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a sturdy pup about the size of a young farm dog, broad-backed well beyond its height.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Packpup cannot be persuaded to walk anywhere empty-backed. It collects — sticks, stones, lost tools, smaller friends — and arranges its load with a pup's total seriousness, trotting the Rift paths with its spine already dipping into the shallow saddle shape it was born with. Dropped objects along the crossings are understood locally to be Packpup deliveries in progress, and are left exactly where they lie.
- **Temperament:** Helpful to a fault, earnest, and mildly offended by uncarried things; happiest mid-errand.
- **Habitat:** Region 4 Rift paths, crossing camps, and institute service yards — anywhere with traffic, loads, and an audience for carrying.
- **Diet & behaviour:** An omnivore of camp scraps, tubers, and tough Rift grasses; behaviour is defined by the carrying instinct — gathering, balancing, delivering, and returning for more, with play-fights conducted entirely under load and points awarded by onlookers for nothing dropped.
- **Evolution / form change:** No alt-mode yet at this stage — the saddle-dip back is a promise, not a second form. It evolves into Rigrex as the carrying life grows it into a great saurus, when the load it can take, and the riders it can welcome, outgrow a pup's back entirely.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 Rift paths and institute service yards; rarity class common, and usually discovered already at work. Method: wild encounter along travelled routes — the reliable way is to set down something carryable and step back. Conditions: active all day, busiest at crossing rush hours. Level band in words: low band. First-meeting note: first meetings typically involve being relieved of a bag, a hat, or a smaller companion, carried a short and honourable distance, and returned with ceremony.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character — a bright pup bark and whine set, all dog, no words. Pitch contour in words: barks short and level, whines rising at the end like a question. Duration class: very short barks; longer pleading whines when a load is refused. Rhythm pattern: bark pairs when setting off, single checking yips at each rest stop. Variations per state — idle: contented huffs and the odd experimental bark at its own load; call: an eager triple bark, tail going; hurt: a sharp yelp, load protected before itself; faint: a trailing whine as it sits down carefully so nothing spills; affection: squeaky whines with the whole rear end wagging; evolution / form-change moment: no form change at this stage — the carrying settle, a little grunt as the load seats on the saddle-dip, is its signature sound.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Four sturdy legs, a broad flat back with a visible shallow saddle dip grown into the spine, and a short balancing tail. No hands — manipulation is mouth-carry and back-carry only, and loads rest on the saddle-dip, held by body posture rather than straps. Mouth is a pup's jaw, good for sticks and handles, gentle by instinct with living cargo. Locomotion: an earnest trot with a built-in load-compensating sway; animators should bounce the cargo, never the spine. Its signature move animation is the proud set-off — dip, settle, tail up, trot.
- **Palette seed:** sandy tan, warm brown, cream belly, dusty red
- **Footprint:** A single trace at this stage: four-toed pup paw prints with clear pad marks and short claw ticks, in a steady trotting gait of evenly spaced alternating prints, often running in purposeful straight lines between crossing points. Print size class: small. Distinctive marker: prints frequently pause in neat clusters beside a shallow resting-scuff where a load was set down and taken up again — a delivery, recorded in dust.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class — one form only: a low, broad-backed pup, saddle dip readable even in outline, ears up, tail a short flag. Proportions in words: back long and wide for its height, head mid-sized, legs sturdy and short. Key visual masses: the back and whatever it is carrying. What reads at sprite size: dip-backed pup plus load. Identifying negative space: the shallow concave arc of the saddle dip against the sky — no other Rift pup has a hollow in its outline.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Balanced and hardy for a first stage, leaning to defence and stamina; speed is a working trot, and its best stat in practice is not dropping things. — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A pup whose back is already a saddle and whose character is already a porter. Key features are the grown saddle dip, the earnest expression, and the ever-present load. Must-never-change list: the saddle is grown anatomy, never tack or equipment; the carrying is instinct and joy, never labour imposed on it; nothing about the pup reads as a vehicle — it is a dog doing a dog's chosen job at larger meaning.
- **Flags:** none

### Rigrex — C1-3 · final stage, beast mode and carrier mode

- **Line / concept:** C1-3 Packpup → Rigrex — a great saurus with two complete ways of being: beast mode, and carrier mode, a walking cargo frame smaller species ride like a bus with opinions; donor mechanic: alt-modes, one being in two complete forms, translated as grown anatomy — nobody built the frame, it grew saddle-shaped.
- **Types:** Beast/Terra
- **Category:** the Carrier Mochi
- **Size class:** Large — a great saurus, long as a wagon train and tall enough at the shoulder for a standing Ranger to look it levelly in the eye in beast mode, lower and broader in carrier mode.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Rigrex is the Rift's living road. In beast mode it is a great saurus entire — browsing, bellowing, shaking the ground at a run. In carrier mode the same body settles, levels, and opens along grown rails of rib and saddle into a walking cargo frame, and smaller species climb aboard and ride like passengers on a bus with strong opinions about the route. Both modes are wholly the creature; it chooses between them the way other beasts choose between walking and swimming.
- **Temperament:** Patient, route-proud, and stubborn about schedules it has invented entirely by itself; gentle with riders, immovable with anyone who treats it as furniture.
- **Habitat:** The long Region 4 Rift routes between crossings, institute depots, and Rift settlements — a wide-ranging species that treats the region's paths as its own territory.
- **Diet & behaviour:** A bulk browser of Rift canopy, tough shrubs, and depot-provided fodder, eating hugely in beast mode; its behaviour runs in services — regular routes at regular hours of its own choosing, rest stops where riders expect them, and a famous refusal to depart until a familiar passenger arrives.
- **Evolution / form change:** Final stage; evolution from Packpup arrives with the second way of being. The job is done with a coordination witnesses describe as three arguments conducted at the speed of one thought. Then, always, they separate — and argue about whose fault the posture was.
- **Temperament:** A negotiated temperament — Ratchetta's precision, Coilcoon's cheer, and Dingbat's calm in continuous, audible committee; heroic in effect, domestic in process, and funnier than any titan has a right to be.
- **Habitat:** Wherever the trio's Region 4 home range presents a job of its size — Rift crossings, institute construction and rescue sites, riverbanks in flood season; Gestaltitan has no separate habitat, because Gestaltitan is a visit the trio pays to a problem.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Gestaltitan does not eat — a combine lasts for exactly one job, and meals belong to the separated lives before negotiation and after separation, where the trio characteristically eats the job's thank-you feast together and re-opens the posture argument over it. Behaviour in form is single-minded: arrive, align, do the one job with enormous care for bystanders, finish, and come apart.
- **Evolution / form change:** No evolution; the form change is the merge, and this sheet carries its full description. The merge: on unanimous agreement to one named job, the three align — Coilcoon plants and compresses as the base, hips, legs, and spring-tail; Dingbat rises and seats as the chest, bell-core forward, wings spreading to the back; Ratchetta crowns the merge as head, forequarters, and forelimbs — their bodies joining along grown merge seams, Ratchetta's ridge fur locking the lines, every seam living tissue in continuity. Within the form they remain three minds in fast, constant negotiation. The separation is equally the design: the job done, consent lapses by its own terms, the seams unridged in reverse order — wings folding home, base uncoiling, cat dropping clear — and three small species stand in the dust, whole, themselves, and already arguing about the posture.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 job sites within the trio's range — crossings under repair, institute works, flood banks; rarity class very rare as an encounter, because it exists only mid-job. Method: wild encounter is really witness — meet the trio first, in their home range, and be present, usefully, when a job of the right size appears. Conditions: fair working weather preferred by Ratchetta, any weather accepted by Coilcoon, night jobs lit by Dingbat's echo and the site's lamps. Level band in words: high band while combined — the form's effective presence far exceeds any component's. First-meeting note: do not address Gestaltitan as a stranger; greet the three by name, in order, and state the job or hold your peace — a first meeting with the titan is a job interview conducted by a committee.
