### Termight — C3-1 · stage 1 of 2
- **Line / concept:** C3-1 Termight → Cathedrall — a pale termite worker; the line is a colony that builds cathedral mounds. Basis: termites (open as a designed animal; Pokémon has ants, not termites).
- **Types:** Terra / Chrome — working labels, as marked in the source
- **Category:** the Worker Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — a fingertip-scale crawler, easy to cup in one palm alongside nestmates
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Termight spend their whole lives building, carrying grains of earth one mouthful at a time toward a mound they will never see finished. A single Termight is shy and nearly colourless. Together, in their thousands, they raise chimneys, arches, and cool dark halls.
- **Temperament:** Diligent, shy, colony-devoted; unsettled alone, calm and purposeful in a procession.
- **Habitat:** Warm earth at the edges of Region 4 clearings, always in or near the colony mound; scouts range only a short way over open ground.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Feeds on fallen wood fibre, dry leaves, and the earth itself as it tunnels. Works in single-file lines nose-to-tail; when the line is broken, the workers circle patiently until the scent of the route is found again.
- **Evolution:** Colony-formation — an individual Termight does not transform alone; when the colony it belongs to completes its cathedral mound and the mound gains a queen-heart, the colony-form Cathedrall is what the line has become (see Cathedrall).
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — mound country on warm, dry clearing edges. Rarity: common wherever a mound stands, effectively absent away from one. Method + conditions: follow a worker procession at ground level on a warm, still day; they do not hide, they simply hurry. Level band (in words): earliest band — among the first small crawlers a Ranger meets in mound country. First-meeting note: the Ranger meets a line, not an individual — one Termight stops, tastes the air with its antennae, and the whole line politely reroutes around the Ranger's boot.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: faint dry ticking and mandible clicks, barely louder than a fingernail on paper. Pitch contour: flat and very high, with tiny rising ticks when the line changes direction. Duration class: very short bursts. Rhythm pattern: rapid triple-ticks in step with the procession. Variations — idle: sparse single clicks while carrying; call: a quickened tick-run that travels down the line like a passed message; hurt: one sharp snap and silence; faint: clicks slow, space out, and stop mid-pattern; affection: a soft, fast antennal drumming against a nestmate or a trusted Ranger's fingertip; evolution moment: the whole colony ticks at once, a dry patter like first rain on a roof, as the mound's heart wakes.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Six short legs in a low, even gait; soft segmented abdomen carried behind a small head; two short antennae that do all the "looking." Manipulators: mandibles only — it carries in its mouth, so feeding, building, and attack-delivery all read through the head. Mouth/jaw: small chewing mandibles, no bite menace. Locomotion: steady scuttle, single-file. Signature feature: the pale, almost translucent worker body — animate it as one bead in a moving line, never as a lone hero pose.
- **Palette seed:** pale bone-cream, warm earth tan, soft clay red, dusty amber
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: paired pinpoint dots in two neat rows, six per stride, with a faint central drag line from the low abdomen. Toe/claw count and arrangement: no toes — each leg leaves a single needle-point dot, three per side per step. Gait pattern: alternating tripod gait, prints evenly spaced and almost always in single-file processions overprinted by hundreds of nestmates. Print size class: minute — individual prints need a crouch and good light; the procession trail is what reads first. Distinctive marker: the trail always curves toward the mound, and fresh grains of excavated earth are scattered along it like spilled sugar.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: low, soft oval chain — three gently swelling segments in a row. Proportions in words: a small head, a slightly larger thorax, and a plump teardrop abdomen that makes up most of the read. Key visual masses: the pale abdomen is the single biggest mass; antennae are thin strokes in front. What reads at sprite size: the pale teardrop body and the two antenna strokes — one bead of a line. Identifying negative space: the small notch-gap under the raised abdomen between the back legs.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Frail alone and slow to hurt anything; its real profile is endurance and numbers — tireless work rate, negligible individual threat, surprisingly stubborn when defending the mound. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is a pale three-bead worker in a moving line; key features are the plump translucent abdomen, short antennae, and mouth-carried grain of earth. Must-never-change: never draw it alone as a monster — its identity is the procession and the mound; never give it a soldier's menace or weapons; the worker stays soft, pale, and unarmoured.
- **Flags:** none

### Cathedrall — C3-1 · final stage — colony-form species entry
- **Line / concept:** C3-1 Termight → Cathedrall — the colony-mound itself gaining a queen-heart; the final "stage" is the colony as a building. Basis: termite cathedral mounds with chimneys, arches, and one tall spire the region navigates by. Design note from the source: R3's colony-promotion idea is social; this one is architectural — the evolution is a building.
- **Types:** Terra / Chrome — working labels, as marked in the source
- **Category:** the Cathedral Mound Mochi
- **Size class:** Huge — a landmark, not a body: a mound tall enough to navigate by, read against the sky like a small hill with a spire
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** When a Termight colony's mound gains its queen-heart, the whole building wakes as one creature, Cathedrall. Its chimneys breathe cool air over the region on hot days. Travellers steer by its tallest spire, and Termight processions move through its arches like blood through a heart.
- **Temperament:** Patient, sheltering, and slow in every sense; protective of the colony within and oddly welcoming to travellers who rest in its shade without chipping at its walls.
- **Habitat:** It is its habitat — a fixed landmark in Region 4 mound country; a Cathedrall does not relocate.
- **Diet & behaviour:** The colony feeds it as it feeds the colony: workers bring wood fibre and earth, and the mound's slow inner life turns work into warmth, cool air, and new chambers. It "behaves" on a timescale of seasons — a new arch here, a sealed chimney there.
- **Evolution:** Colony-formation, completed — Cathedrall is the result, not a further stage; it does not evolve further. The trigger, in words: a mature Termight colony finishing its cathedral mound, and the mound gaining a queen-heart.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — mound country; a Cathedrall is found by seeing it on the horizon, not by searching grass. Rarity: very rare as a waking colony-form, though large un-woken mounds are uncommon. Method + conditions: approach on foot on a clear day when the spire is visible from far off; the waking is recognised by warmth at the chimneys and the colony's single shared ticking. Level band (in words): late band — a landmark encounter, met when a Ranger is already seasoned in the region. First-meeting note: the Ranger shelters from noon heat in an arch that is cooler than any shade should be, and only later understands the building was breathing.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: the colony heard as one body — deep, slow ticking and earthen settling sounds carried through the mound's chimneys, with a low hollow resonance like wind in a bottle. Pitch contour: low and falling over long phrases, the reverse of the worker's tiny ticks. Duration class: long, widely spaced phrases. Rhythm pattern: a slow heartbeat spacing — tick, tick… long pause — quickening only in alarm or celebration. Variations — idle: sparse deep ticks and soft chimney sighs; call: a resonant pulse that every Termight in the colony answers at once; hurt: a groaning settle and falling dust from the arches when the mound is damaged; faint: not applicable as a battle faint in the ordinary sense — a threatened colony-form goes quiet and still, chimneys cooling, until it is safe; affection: a warm draught from the chimneys and a sheltered arch offered to a resting Ranger; evolution moment: it is an evolution — see Termight's evolution-moment cry (the colony ticking as one as the heart wakes).
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** No limbs, no mouth, no locomotion — do not animate it walking or striking. Manipulators: the Termight workers are its hands; any "action" is performed by processions streaming from its gates. Mouth/jaw: none — the chimney mouths breathe, they do not bite. Locomotion: none; its animation is breathing (warm shimmer at chimneys), dust settling, processions entering and leaving, and shadow moving over arches. Signature feature: the single tall navigation spire. Move animation must be environmental — a move from Cathedrall arrives as earth, dust, and worker swarms delivered from the mound.
- **Palette seed:** sun-baked clay, warm ochre, pale bone-cream, deep shade umber, dusty amber at the chimney mouths
- **Footprint:** It does not walk, so it leaves no footprints — the "track" a Ranger reads instead is the mound's sign: fresh fan-shaped aprons of excavated earth at its base, procession trails of minute paired dots converging on its gates from every direction, shed worker husks in dry drifts against the lee wall, and chimney warmth felt a step before the mound is seen. Distinctive marker: all trails in the area bend toward it — in mound country, every small road leads to Cathedrall.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: architectural — an irregular mound mass crowned by one tall, clean spire, with smaller chimneys and arch openings breaking the base. Proportions in words: a broad, grounded base, a rising middle of stacked terraces, and the spire as a single vertical exclamation. Key visual masses: the mound body itself, then the spire — nothing else competes. What reads at sprite size: spire-over-mound, readable at map distance as a landmark icon. Identifying negative space: the dark arch openings at the base — the holes are part of the read, and the tallest arch is always left of the spire's fall line.
- **Stat profile (words only):** A wall in the truest sense — immense staying power and sheltering presence, no speed, no pursuit; offense, where it exists at all, is the colony acting through it. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is the mound-and-spire landmark; key features are the one tall navigation spire, breathing chimneys, arched gates with Termight processions, and the sense of a building that is alive. Must-never-change: never give it legs, a face on the mound, or a walking animation; never shrink it to creature scale; the queen-heart is felt (warmth, ticking, breath), not shown as a monster inside.
- **Flags:** none

### Silvlet — C3-2 · stage 1 of 2
- **Line / concept:** C3-2 Silvlet → Bookwyrm — a quicksilver sliver of a bug that lives in libraries. Basis: silverfish (open).
- **Types:** Chrome
- **Category:** the Quicksilver Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — a sliver about as long as a bookmark is wide, gone before a hand comes down
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Silvlet lives between the pages and behind the shelves of old libraries, eating paste, dust, and the glue of old spines. It moves like a dropped bead of mercury — there, then not there. Librarians pretend to chase it and quietly leave the damaged bindings where it can reach them.
- **Temperament:** Nervous, bright, and harmless; a startle-first creature that freezes in shadow and darts in light.
- **Habitat:** Region 4 institute libraries and archives — book stacks, binding shelves, and the warm dust under reading desks.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Paste, paper dust, shed skin, and the glue of old spines. Mostly nocturnal in habit even in lamplit stacks; it traces the edges of shelves in fast, wavering runs and vanishes down the gap behind a row of books.
- **Evolution:** In words: a Silvlet that has lived long in one archive, eaten deeply of its bindings, and learned its stacks by heart becomes Bookwyrm (see Bookwyrm). No level is recorded.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — the institute's library and archive stacks. Rarity: uncommon — plentiful, but so fast and shy that sightings are brief. Method + conditions: search the stacks after closing, lamplight low, moving slowly; look along shelf edges and behind loose bindings, not on open floor. Level band (in words): early band — a small indoor encounter for a Ranger patient enough to look. First-meeting note: something silver crosses the margin of the book the Ranger is reading, and is gone; only a nibbled corner of the page proves it was there.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: a near-silent papery rustle with a faint metallic tick, more felt in a quiet room than heard across one. Pitch contour: very high and level, a whisker of sound. Duration class: extremely short. Rhythm pattern: single ticks, or a stutter of three when it changes direction mid-dart. Variations — idle: almost nothing — an occasional rustle-tick while feeding; call: a slightly longer silvery rasp that brings other Silvlet out of the same shelf-gap; hurt: one bright tick and a dart; faint: the rustle slows to a stop under cover — it plays dead in shadow until left alone; affection: a soft repeated ticking against the edge of a book it has been fed from; evolution moment: the ticking stretches into Bookwyrm's longer page-whisper as the body lengthens.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Long, low, many-legged sliver body; no manipulators — it pushes and tastes with two long antennae and three fine tail filaments. Mouth/jaw: tiny chewing mouthparts under the head, always nibbling when still. Locomotion: the signature — a swimming, side-to-side darting run, fish-like on land, with instant stops. Signature feature: the liquid-metal sheen; animate the run as a wiggle-dart, never as a steady scuttle.
- **Palette seed:** liquid silver, pale mercury grey, library dust beige, ink-shadow blue-grey
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: a fine sinuous drag-line with hair-thin leg ticks feathering both sides, like a stitch unpicked across dust. Toe/claw count and arrangement: no toes — dozens of minute leg points too small to count on a casual look, recorded as a feathered fringe. Gait pattern: wavering S-curves that stop dead at shadow edges and resume beyond them. Print size class: minute — visible only in undisturbed library dust or on a sunlit desk. Distinctive marker: the trail begins and ends at books — a shelf edge, a spine gap, a nibbled page corner.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a thin tapered sliver — pointed at both ends, widest just behind the head. Proportions in words: almost all body is length; antennae ahead and tail filaments behind extend the line further. Key visual masses: none large — it is a line, not a lump. What reads at sprite size: the bright silver dash and its wiggle. Identifying negative space: the forked split of the three tail filaments at the rear — the only "notch" in an otherwise smooth sliver.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Extremely fast and extremely frail; a creature whose whole profile is not being there when anything lands. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is a living silver dash; key features are the mercury sheen, long antennae, and triple tail filaments. Must-never-change: never bulk it up or armour it; never slow its darting, swimming gait; it stays a sliver that reads as motion first and body second.
- **Flags:** none

### Bookwyrm — C3-2 · final stage
- **Line / concept:** C3-2 Silvlet → Bookwyrm — the archive's pest and mascot; it has read everything and will not discuss endings. Basis: silverfish, grown long on a life among books. Region fit from the source: Region 4's institute region needs an archive haunter; this is it.
- **Types:** Chrome
- **Category:** the Archive Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — long rather than large: a forearm-length of silver sliver draped over a stack, still thin enough to slip behind a shelf
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Bookwyrm is the institute archive's pest and mascot in one long silver body. It has eaten the paste of a thousand bindings and, librarians swear, read everything it digested. It will not discuss endings, and goes pointedly still if asked.
- **Temperament:** Dry, knowing, and unbothered; the calm of a creature that has lived in one quiet building longer than any of its keepers.
- **Habitat:** Deep archive stacks of the Region 4 institute — the oldest shelves, the restricted bindings, the warm top of the tallest bookcase.
- **Diet & behaviour:** The glue and paste of old spines, paper dust, and the occasional offered page-corner, which it accepts like a tribute. It drapes, reads the room, and relocates only when a shelf is moved — an event it treats as an earthquake and a personal insult.
- **Evolution:** Final stage — does not evolve further. It arrived here from Silvlet by a long archive life (see Silvlet).
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — the institute archive's oldest stacks. Rarity: rare — one established Bookwyrm per true archive is the usual story. Method + conditions: search after closing with a librarian's permission, in the restricted stacks, and wait rather than chase; it comes to inspect a reader who sits still long enough. Level band (in words): mid band — a quiet, late-library encounter. First-meeting note: it drapes itself over the spine of the very book the Ranger came for, regards them for a long moment, and slides aside — exactly one book's width.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: a long papery whisper-rustle, like a page turned very slowly, threaded with Silvlet's metallic tick grown lower and slower. Pitch contour: high but settled, falling gently at the end of each phrase, like a sigh over paper. Duration class: short phrases, longer than Silvlet's ticks. Rhythm pattern: unhurried single phrases with long reading pauses between. Variations — idle: slow page-whisper while draped and feeding; call: a drier, crisper rustle that settles every Silvlet in the stacks to stillness; hurt: a sharp page-snap sound and a silver uncoiling dart; faint: the whisper frays into separated ticks and stops, body flat in shadow; affection: a soft, continuous rustle while draping near a familiar librarian's or Ranger's reading place — its version of purring, in paper; evolution moment: not applicable — final stage (the moment is recorded under Silvlet).
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Silvlet's plan, lengthened: many short legs under a long, low, flexible body that drapes over edges rather than standing. Manipulators: none — antennae taste and tap; the tail filaments have grown into three fine, expressive streamers. Mouth/jaw: small nibbling mouthparts; feeding is a head-down graze along a spine. Locomotion: the same swimming wiggle-dart as Silvlet, slower and more deliberate, with long still drapes between moves. Signature feature: the drape — it hangs over a book's edge in a soft catenary curve, and move delivery comes from the uncoiling dart, not from any limb strike.
- **Palette seed:** aged silver, parchment cream, library dust beige, faded ink blue, shelf-shadow grey
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: Silvlet's feathered stitch-trail grown bolder — a clear sinuous drag with paired leg-tick fringes, crossing open dust in long S-curves. Toe/claw count and arrangement: no toes; the fringe of minute leg points is denser and more even than Silvlet's. Gait pattern: unhurried waves with long gaps where it draped or rested — the trail pauses at books. Print size class: small — readable in archive dust without magnification. Distinctive marker: the trail visits bindings in order, shelf by shelf, like a reading list — and stops, always, one book before the end of a row.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a long silver ribbon with a softly pointed head and a three-streamer tail. Proportions in words: length dominates completely; the body is a low, even ribbon with no large mass anywhere along it. Key visual masses: the draped middle curve is the read — a hanging arc between two shelf points. What reads at sprite size: the bright ribbon and its drape. Identifying negative space: the twin notches of the forked tail streamers, and the open curve under a drape where a book's edge shows through.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Still fast, still frail, but seasoned — quicker to vanish, harder to corner, and far more accurate in its darting strikes than Silvlet; a veteran of one quiet building. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is a long silver ribbon draped over books; key features are the mercury sheen aged to a softer shine, the triple tail streamers, and the drape pose. Must-never-change: never make it a literal dragon or give it wings, legs of length, or a book for a body; it stays a silverfish grown long and knowing — and it never, ever discusses endings.
- **Flags:** none

### Stinklet — C3-3 · stage 1 of 2
- **Line / concept:** C3-3 Stinklet → Perfumarch — a shield bug whose scent glands make, against all expectation, perfume. Basis: shield / stink bugs (open).
- **Types:** Flora
- **Category:** the Scent-Shield Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — a shield-shaped bug that sits comfortably on a thumbnail, broad for its length
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Stinklet carries the shield bug's famous scent glands, but the scent that comes out is lovely — green, sweet, and faintly floral. It releases a puff when startled, then seems faintly embarrassed about the mess. Other bugs follow it for the smell alone.
- **Temperament:** Mild, startle-prone, and quietly pleased with itself whenever its perfume lands well.
- **Habitat:** Region 4 hedgerows, institute gardens, and the planted borders around Rift settlements — anywhere leaves are thick and the air is still.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Sips plant juices from stems and soft fruit skins. Spends long still hours flattened on the underside of a leaf; its defence is the perfume puff — beautiful, distracting, and just strong enough at close range to make anything chasing it stop and reconsider.
- **Evolution:** In words: a Stinklet whose glands mature and whose scent turns from lucky accident to craft becomes Perfumarch (see Perfumarch). No level is recorded.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — institute gardens and hedgerow borders. Rarity: common in planted ground, uncommon in the wild Rift scrub. Method + conditions: search leaf undersides on a warm, still morning; follow the one unexpectedly pleasant smell in the hedge. Level band (in words): earliest band — a gentle garden encounter. First-meeting note: the Ranger's hand closes on leaves, a small sweet cloud blooms, and Stinklet is sitting in the middle of it, very still, hoping the perfume did the introductions.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: a soft leafy squeak with a faint fizzy edge, like a tiny cork easing out. Pitch contour: mid-high, popping up at the start of each note and settling. Duration class: very short. Rhythm pattern: single squeaks, or an apologetic double-squeak after a startle. Variations — idle: sparse soft squeaks while feeding; call: a slightly fizzier squeak that draws other Stinklet to the same leaf; hurt: a sharp squeak with the perfume puff released at the same instant; faint: squeaks thin out and the shield goes still and leaf-flat; affection: a mellow, bubbly little trill while perched on a familiar hand; evolution moment: the squeaks deepen and layer as the glands mature into Perfumarch's perfumed hum.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Six legs under a single broad shield shell; the body is the shield — there is no separate "back armour" piece. Manipulators: none; the piercing mouthparts are a fine rostrum folded under the head. Mouth/jaw: a sap-sipping beak, not a bite. Locomotion: slow, flat leaf-walking with short, neat steps; it flies only as a last resort, in a brief buzzy lift. Signature feature: the scent glands at the rear of the shield — any scent or Flora delivery animates from there, as a visible blooming puff, while the bug itself stays put.
- **Palette seed:** fresh leaf green, soft chartreuse, warm amber at the shield edge, pale cream belly
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: six tiny pinpricks in two staggered rows under a faint shield-shaped body-press where it rested. Toe/claw count and arrangement: one minute point per leg, three per side, the middle pair set widest. Gait pattern: slow, even, straight-line walking between leaves, prints widely spaced for so small a bug. Print size class: minute — found on dusty leaves and pale garden soil rather than open ground. Distinctive marker: a faint sweet-smelling damp spot at each rest point — the only print in the bucket a Ranger can follow by nose.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a clean geometric shield — broad rounded shoulders tapering to a soft point at the rear. Proportions in words: width is the story; the body is nearly as wide as it is long, low and flat. Key visual masses: the single shield mass, unbroken. What reads at sprite size: the shield shape itself, in green. Identifying negative space: the small triangular notch where the head tucks into the shield's front edge.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Slow and soft-hitting, with staying power out of proportion to its size — the shield shrugs off glancing blows, and the perfume buys it room. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is the living shield; key features are the unbroken shield shell, the folded sap-beak, and the perfume puff from the rear glands. Must-never-change: never make the scent a stink — the joke and the identity is that it is beautiful; never add horns, spikes, or menace to the shield; the puff is a bloom, not a gas attack.
- **Flags:** none

### Perfumarch — C3-3 · final stage
- **Line / concept:** C3-3 Stinklet → Perfumarch — the region's perfumer, processing in a cloud of its own best work. Basis: shield / stink bugs, grown grand. Temperament from the source: artist.
- **Types:** Flora
- **Category:** the Perfumer Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a grand shield bug, about a palm across, carried high and proudly for a crawler
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Perfumarch is the region's perfumer, and knows it. It processes through the gardens in a drifting cloud of its own finest scent, adjusting the blend leaf by leaf. Being near it is lovely for a moment, then a great deal — it has never once understood this.
- **Temperament:** Artist — proud, meticulous, and generous with its work whether the audience asked or not; slightly hard to be near, entirely without malice.
- **Habitat:** Region 4's finest planted ground — institute gardens, festival flower rows, and the hedges around perfumers' and florists' doors, which it visits like a critic.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Plant juices chosen like ingredients — it feeds selectively, stem by stem, and its scent changes with its diet across the season. It processes slowly from flower bed to flower bed, trailing perfume, pausing to release a considered puff the way a painter steps back from a canvas.
- **Evolution:** Final stage — does not evolve further. It arrived here from Stinklet when accident became craft (see Stinklet).
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — institute gardens and festival flower ground. Rarity: uncommon — unmistakable when present, but it keeps a perfumer's hours and a critic's circuit. Method + conditions: search by nose on a still, warm afternoon; the encounter begins several garden rows before the creature is seen. Level band (in words): mid band — a garden encounter with some presence behind it. First-meeting note: the Ranger smells it long before they see it, follows the scent like a ribbon, and finds Perfumarch mid-procession, turning slowly to present its best side and its best blend.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: Stinklet's leafy squeak matured into a layered, perfumed hum-squeak — soft, round, and self-satisfied, with a faint chime at the end of phrases. Pitch contour: mid range, rising with pride at phrase ends. Duration class: short phrases, longer and more composed than Stinklet's pops. Rhythm pattern: deliberate singles with artist's pauses; a pleased triple-note when a blend comes out right. Variations — idle: low composed hum-squeaks while processing; call: a carrying, chime-ended phrase that gathers Stinklet from the hedge like apprentices; hurt: a startled squeak and an uncontrolled, overwhelming perfume burst — its embarrassment is audible; faint: phrases trail off into the cloud as it goes leaf-flat and still; affection: a warm, bubbly trill with a deliberately gentle puff, its version of a gift; evolution moment: not applicable — final stage (the moment is recorded under Stinklet).
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** The shield shell is now a grand, high-domed canopy carried on six deliberate legs; the body walks *under* its own masterpiece. Manipulators: none; the rostrum still sips, now with a connoisseur's selectivity. Mouth/jaw: fine sap-beak, never a bite. Locomotion: a slow procession — short steps, long pauses, the cloud moving with it. Signature feature: the perfume cloud itself, produced from the rear glands in visible, layered blooms; animate scent as its primary "action" language — puffs, trails, and the grand burst — while strikes, if any, are small and secondary.
- **Palette seed:** deep leaf green, gold-amber shield trim, blush-rose at the glands, cream, a haze of lilac in its cloud
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: six firm pinpricks in two rows with a broad shield body-press at each pause, bolder than Stinklet's. Toe/claw count and arrangement: one point per leg, three per side, evenly and proudly spaced. Gait pattern: a procession in prints — clusters of steps, then a rest-press, then steps again. Print size class: small — readable on garden paths and pale soil. Distinctive marker: each rest-press carries a lingering sweet damp ring, so the trail can be followed blindfold, by scent alone.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a grand domed shield on legs — a small walking canopy. Proportions in words: the shield is wider and higher than the body beneath it; legs read as a fringe below the dome. Key visual masses: the dome alone; everything else is detail at its edge. What reads at sprite size: dome-plus-cloud — the perfume haze is part of the silhouette and should be drawn as a soft halo mass. ### Monkalm — C4-6 · final stage
- **Line / concept:** C4-6 Smoothsea → Monkalm — the dome plus the face: a bald sea-monk the size of weather, posing one low riddle, ferrying the calm. Basis: umibozu, re-toned.
- **Types:** Aqua / Umbral
- **Category:** the Riddle-Ferry Mochi
- **Size class:** Huge — the size of weather: a head and shoulders over the sea like a small black hill with a face, a back broad enough to ferry on
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Monkalm is the dome plus the face — a bald sea-monk the size of weather, which is worse and better. Panic brings waves; a calm answer to its one low riddle earns a ferry ride on its back. The riddle is never hard. That is the test: it is asking whether you can be calm, not whether you are clever.
- **Temperament:** Serene, grave, and quietly kind; a examiner of calm whose ferry is a reward for the steady-hearted.
- **Habitat:** Open Region 4 Rift sea on mirror-calm days — the same water as Smoothsea, fulfilled.
- **Diet & behaviour:** It does not eat. Its behaviour is the encounter: it rises on mirror-calm water, regards the vessel, poses its one low riddle in a voice like a hull-hum given words *only for the riddle* in the encounter's framing as the source gives it — one riddle, calmly answered or calmly failed — and ferries the calm across whatever water they need to cross. Those who panic get waves and a sinking-away giant, and a long swim of a lesson.
- **Evolution:** Final stage — does not evolve further. It arrived here from Smoothsea when the dome gained the face (see Smoothsea).
- **Discovery:** Region 4 — open sea, mirror-calm, on ferry routes between the Rift islands. Rarity: event-class — it rises when the water goes mirror-still, for a vessel ready to be calm. Method + conditions: be on calm water, keep the boat calm, and wait; shouting, rowing at it, or panic all end the meeting before it begins. Level band (in words): late band — the open sea's own examination, taken when it is offered. First-meeting note: the dome rises, and rises, and the face comes with it — worse, because it is looking at you; better, because it is looking *kindly* — and the whole sea waits while it asks its one low riddle.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: Smoothsea's hull-hum become a presence — a very low, round resonance with slow water-sounds at the shoulders, and the riddle delivered as the source frames it: one low riddle, a single calm question, not a conversation. Pitch contour: the hum is level and deep; the riddle is spoken at the bottom of the voice, evenly, with a long pause after it that is part of the asking. Duration class: hum phrases are long; the riddle is one unhurried sentence of a question. Rhythm pattern: hum… silence… riddle… a silence as long as it takes. Variations — idle: a distant hull-hum under mirror-calm water, felt rather than heard; call: the hum swells as the face rises — other Smoothsea hums still themselves, as if listening; hurt: it does not cry out; the hum breaks, waves come up with its displeasure at panic, and it sinks — the sea's noise is the whole report; faint: withdrawal, not defeat — a sinking fall of resonance and a long troubled settling of water; affection: after a calm answer, the hum warms and steadies under the ferry ride the whole way across — passengers describe it as being hummed *to*, very low, by the sea; evolution moment: not applicable — final stage (the face's coming is recorded under Smoothsea).
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** The dome fulfilled: a vast smooth head — bald as a monk's, in the source's image — with a calm, plain face, broad shoulders at the waterline, and a back that serves as the ferry deck. Manipulators: none in view; the ferrying is the back and the steadiness. Mouth/jaw: a small, still mouth in the huge face, animated sparingly — open for the riddle, closed for everything else; all other sound is whole-body resonance, cries in hum form, never chatter. Locomotion: rising, sinking, and a slow ferry-glide that barely ripples. Signature feature: scale-with-stillness — it moves like weather changing its mind, and any delivery it makes is a swell, a settling, or the gift of flat water.
- **Palette seed:** wet black, mirror silver, deep sea green, dawn pearl on the face, flat grey calm
- **Footprint:** No footprints; its trace is a NON-print sign — a ferry wake of impossible geometry: a straight, glassy calm lane across otherwise ordinary sea, the width of its back, persisting after it has gone, with the circular mirror-ring at the place where it rose. - **Silhouette:** Outline class: a black hill with a face — dome, shoulders, and waterline, doubled in reflection into a dark medallion on the sea. Proportions in words: the face is small in the dome the way a face is small in a mountain, and it is the whole read anyway. Key visual masses: the head-dome and the shoulder line at the water. What reads at sprite size: black dome, calm face, mirror doubling — instantly a presence, never a monster shape. Identifying negative space: the clean arc of sky between its shoulders and any land — it takes the horizon's place.
- **Stat profile (words only):** A weather-scale wall of calm — beyond ordinary contest; encounters with it are examinations, not battles, and its "strength" is the sea itself agreeing with it. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Silhouette is the dome plus the face on mirror water, back offered as a ferry. Key features are the bald monk-like head, the small kind face at vast scale, and the riddle-silence staging. Must-never-change: never stage it as a capsizing sea-horror — panic brings waves, the creature brings a question; never give it a hard riddle, a cruel price, or a second demand; the ferry is earned by calm, and given kindly.
