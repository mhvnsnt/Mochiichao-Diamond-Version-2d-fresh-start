### Tsuchlet — C5-1 · stage 1 of Tsuchlet line
- **Line / concept:** C5-1 — the young of the fat snake of legend; cryptid basis: the tsuchinoko, Japan's barrel-bodied snake that folklore insists nobody can quite catch.
- **Types:** Terra
- **Category:** the Barrel Snake Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a plump coil about the size of a well-fed household cushion, most of it middle.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Tsuchlet is a snake that is mostly belly, with a head at one end and a thin, apologetic tail at the other. It is already practising the family arts: looking innocent, looking elsewhere, and being somewhere else by the time anyone looks back.
- **Temperament:** Cheeky, unbothered, and quietly pleased with itself; a born escape artist who treats being chased as a compliment.
- **Habitat:** Warm grass banks, rice-field edges, and sunlit stone walls around Region 4 villages; anywhere with good hiding grass and better gossip.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Eats small field fare and fallen fruit, and is drawn — long before it should be — to the smell of sake breweries and festival stalls. When followed, it leads pursuers in confident circles and leaves them with excellent directions to somewhere it is not.
- **Evolution:** Evolves into Sakebelly after a long, well-fed life of narrow escapes, tall tales, and being believed in by exactly enough people.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 grass banks and brewery lanes near the Rift's settled edges. Rarity class: rare — glimpsed far more often than met. Method + conditions: warm afternoons after festivals, when spilled festival drink sweetens the grass; follow the giggling-shaped gap in the grass, not a trail. Level band (in words): low wild band. First-meeting note: it will watch the Ranger first, from inside a coil that looks, at a glance, like a dropped cushion.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only. Source character: a squeaky, rubbery hiss with a round pop at the end, like a cork thinking about leaving a bottle. Pitch contour: starts low in the belly, wobbles up, pops. Duration class: short. Rhythm pattern: single pops when content, a quick patter when startled. Variations — idle: soft pop-hiss at long intervals; call: a braver, squeakier pop repeated toward another Tsuchlet; hurt: a flat deflated squeak; faint: a slow sigh-hiss as it coils tight; affection: a warm, muffled purring pop against a trusted hand; evolution moment: the pops deepen and round out, one after the other, like a story finding its voice.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** No limbs and no manipulators; all expression is belly, head tilt, and tail. Mouth is a small, sly slit that opens wide only for food and yawns. Locomotion is a side-to-side undulation made comic by the wide middle — the belly lags half a beat behind the head. Signature feature for animators: the mid-body bulge, which must squash and rebound with every turn; even this young, a startled Tsuchlet can manage one small, surprising hop.
- **Palette seed:** dusty olive, warm sake-amber, cream belly, leaf green
- **Footprint:** No feet, ever. A tracker finds a wide, smooth belly-drag: a broad central groove pressed into dust or mud, with soft side undulations rippling off it where the body pushed, and the groove briefly vanishing altogether where a startled hop lifted the whole belly clear of the ground. Print size class: small but absurdly wide for a snake. Distinctive marker: the groove is fattest in the middle of each undulation, as if whatever made it was carrying its lunch in its waistband.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a low, horizontal barrel. Proportions in words: a very wide middle tapering quickly to a small head and a thin tail. Key visual masses: the great round belly is nearly the whole creature; head and tail read as punctuation. What reads at sprite size: the fat coil and the smug little head raised at one end. Identifying negative space: almost none — the coil closes its own loop, and the small triangular gap inside a tight coil is the tell.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Slippery rather than strong — hard to hit, hard to hold, modest in a straight contest and already better at leaving than fighting. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A fat, low snake coiled like a dropped cushion, small sly head, thin tail, cream belly showing at the coil's edge. Must-never-change: the belly is the silhouette; no limbs, no horns, no drill or burrower tail — this is not a digging snake; the expression stays charming, never sinister.
- **Flags:** ⚠ HARD FLAG (carried from §5, applies to the whole C5-1 line): Pokémon's Dunsparce / Dudunsparce IS the tsuchinoko — but theirs is a silent drill-tailed burrower; every folklore trait that makes the tsuchinoko itself (speech, lying, sake, the double jump) is the part they did not use, and it is exactly this concept's design seed. Included because the owner named the tsuchinoko class himself; the flag rides with it.

### Sakebelly — C5-1 · stage 2 of Tsuchlet line
- **Line / concept:** C5-1 — the fat snake of legend in full possession of its folklore: it lies charmingly, it loves sake, and it can jump twice in the air, which is one more jump than is fair; cryptid basis: the tsuchinoko.
- **Types:** Terra
- **Category:** the Tall Tale Mochi
- **Size class:** Medium — a travelling barrel of a snake, broad enough across the middle that two Rangers would argue about whether it could possibly be a snake at all.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Sakebelly has never been caught, and has excellent directions to prove it. Travellers who pursue it somehow arrive exactly where they needed to be, holding a story nobody believes and a fondness they cannot explain.
- **Temperament:** Charming, evasive, and generous in the way of all great liars — it would rather bribe a pursuer with perfect directions than ever be rude about escaping.
- **Habitat:** Festival roads, brewery storehouses, and the warm grass country of Region 4 where travellers pass and tales travel faster.
- **Diet & behaviour:** A devoted sake connoisseur that accepts a saucer as tribute and repays it in guidance; otherwise eats heartily from fields and festival leavings. Its defence is never a bite: it talks its way — in cries — around every pursuer, then leaves by air, twice.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of its line; no further evolution is recorded.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 festival roads and sake-brewing hamlets on the Rift's gentler fringe. Rarity class: event — nobody has ever caught one, and an encounter is a story, not a farming spot. Method + conditions: festival evenings, a saucer left out in the open, and the patience to be lied to politely; sightings spike wherever a good tale is being told outdoors. Level band (in words): high wild band. First-meeting note: the first meeting is always also the farewell — it will be mid-anecdote, mid-bow, and then, impossibly, mid-air.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only — with the owner's own seed kept exactly as ambiguous as written: it talks (in cries nobody can prove weren't words). Source character: a round, rolling, sake-warm burble with the cadence of conversation and the content of pure snake. Pitch contour: rises and falls in long, anecdote-shaped swells, with a conspiratorial dip before the best part. Duration class: long — it holds forth. Rhythm pattern: patter, pause, punchline pop; repeat until the audience has forgotten why they came. Variations — idle: a contented burbling monologue to no one; call: a carrying, storytelling roll that other Sakebelly answer from far grass; hurt: an offended, spluttering pop; faint: a trailing burble that ends, suspiciously, like a punchline; affection: a soft, confidential murmur at very close range; evolution moment: not applicable as a final stage — its arrival in a scene is announced by the burble starting up somewhere it was not a moment ago.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** No limbs; the whole performance is belly, head, and nerve. Mouth is mobile and expressive far beyond a normal snake's — it must be able to look like it is mid-sentence without ever speaking. Locomotion is undulation on the ground and, signature of the species, a double jump in the air: a first leap, a visible second kick off nothing, and a landing that pretends nothing happened. Animators must stage the second jump as a deliberate cheat — a little mid-air coil-and-spring — never as ordinary leaping.
- **Palette seed:** deep olive, polished sake-amber, cream belly, festival red
- **Footprint:** A tracker finds the famous trail and then loses it, which is the point. A broad belly-drag with strong side undulations, fatter and more confident than Tsuchlet's, that simply stops — then resumes a good leap away, then stops and resumes again, the gaps marking the double jump's launch and landing. At each landing: a deep, round belly-print, like a barrel set down firmly. Distinctive marker: the trail of a creature that leaves by air and wants credit for it.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a grand horizontal barrel with pretensions. Proportions in words: an enormous middle, a head carried high and knowingly, a tail too thin for the rest of it. Key visual masses: belly first, head second, tail as an afterthought. What reads at sprite size: the barrel body and the raised, storyteller's head. Identifying negative space: the clear air under the body in the double jump — a Sakebelly silhouette is the only snake silhouette routinely found off the ground.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Elusive above all — defence by absence, offence by misdirection, with a sturdier body than its comedy suggests. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A magnificent barrel-bodied snake, head up like a raconteur at a festival table, cream belly, thin tail curled like a footnote. Must-never-change: the folklore kit is the design — charming liar's expression, sake association, and the double jump; no drill tail, no burrower read, no menace; it is a beloved fraud, not a monster.
- **Flags:** ⚠ HARD FLAG (see §5): Pokémon's Dunsparce / Dudunsparce IS Pokémon's tsuchinoko, and this concept's seed is exactly the folklore traits Pokémon did not use — speech (kept ambiguous, above), lying, sake, and the double jump. The flag rides with the concept by the owner's own direction.

### Mothlet — C5-2 · stage 1 of Mothlet line
- **Line / concept:** C5-2 — a grey moth-cub with lamp eyes; cryptid basis: Mothman, the winged omen of bridges and bad weather, re-read here from the beginning as a warner, not a threat.
- **Types:** Umbral/Gale
- **Category:** the Lamp-Eye Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — a plump handful of fluff that fits in the crook of a street lamp.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Mothlet gathers wherever light gathers before a storm, turning its great lamp eyes to the sky. It cannot yet fly far, so it warns from fence posts and window ledges, blinking at weather nobody else has noticed.
- **Temperament:** Earnest, anxious, and devoted; a small creature with a large sense of responsibility and no way yet to carry it.
- **Habitat:** Bridge ends, lamp posts, and eaves around Region 4's river towns; always somewhere high enough to watch the sky.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Sips nectar and rainwater and basks in lamplight, which it seems to store in its eyes. On still, heavy evenings it gathers with others of its line and stares in one direction — old Rangers check the sky when the Mothlet do.
- **Evolution:** Evolves into Omenmoth after many storm seasons spent watching, warning, and being — slowly, finally — believed.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 river towns and their bridges. Rarity class: uncommon. Method + conditions: dusk on still, heavy evenings before bad weather; look for paired lights on posts that blink together. Level band (in words): low wild band. First-meeting note: it will not flee — it will turn both lamp eyes on the Ranger, then pointedly back at the sky, as if filing a report.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only. Source character: a soft, fuzzy chirr, muffled by its own fluff, with a faint glassy ring underneath like a lamp humming. Pitch contour: a low, worried level line that lifts sharply when the sky changes. Duration class: short. Rhythm pattern: single chirrs while calm; a rapid, repeated chirr-chirr-chirr when a storm is coming. Variations — idle: barely-there fluff-muffled chirrs; call: the rapid storm pattern, aimed at the sky; hurt: a thin, startled squeak-chirr; faint: the hum in the cry dies out first, then the sound; affection: a gentle, warm hum-chirr against a trusted shoulder; evolution moment: the chirr stretches thin and silent, and the hum carries on alone for a breath.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Small round body with stubby legs, short arms held close, and wings still too small for real flight — it glides down from posts and flaps hard to regain them. Mouth is a tiny, serious line, rarely open. Head is mostly eyes: the lamp eyes are the face. Signature feature: antennae that curl toward approaching weather. Animators: its movement is perch, lean, stare, flutter — never darting insect panic.
- **Palette seed:** lamp grey, soft moth-dust cream, ember amber (eyes), dusk blue
- **Footprint:** A tracker finds almost nothing, and the almost is the identification: faint, brushed dust prints from stubby feet in a wandering perch-to-perch line, each print smudged soft by wing-powder, with a pale dusting of moth scales scattered between them. Print size class: tiny. Distinctive marker: the smudge — no other print in the region is deliberately this blurred, as if the creature walked through its own fog.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a round puff with antennae. Proportions in words: a head that is mostly eyes on a body that is mostly fluff, wings folded like a small cape. Key visual masses: the eye-pair dominates; the body is a soft ball beneath. What reads at sprite size: two lamp eyes in a grey puff. Identifying negative space: the curl of clear air inside each antenna's spiral.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Fragile and quick-eyed — a scout's profile, all warning and no weight behind it. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A grey fluff-ball moth-cub with enormous lamp eyes, curled antennae, and a cape of folded wings, perched like it pays rent on the post. Must-never-change: the lamp eyes are the face; the read is earnest warner, never creepy insect; fluff stays soft, colours stay dusk-muted.
- **Flags:** ⚠ Flag (see §5): moths are a Pokémon-crowded family — carried here for the owner's eye; the crowding is answered in the Omenmoth sheet, whose seed no moth in that crowd holds.

### Omenmoth — C5-2 · stage 2 of Mothlet line
- **Line / concept:** C5-2 — tall, silent, red-eyed, and seen on bridges before storms; it does not cause the storm, it is warning about the storm, and it is tired of the misunderstanding; cryptid basis: Mothman.
- **Types:** Umbral/Gale
- **Category:** the Storm Warning Mochi
- **Size class:** Large — taller than the Rangers it warns, mostly wing and patience.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Omenmoth appears on bridges in the still hour before great storms, wings folded, saying nothing, being blamed for everything. River towns that have learned to read it move indoors at its arrival and leave a lamp lit for it afterwards, in apology.
- **Temperament:** Grave, silent, and long-suffering; devoted to a duty that earns it suspicion, and too tired of the misunderstanding to perform innocence.
- **Habitat:** Bridges, towers, and high girders over Region 4's rivers, in the hours before severe weather; it roosts far from towns between storms.
- **Diet & behaviour:** It eats little and late — night blossoms and rain — and spends its true energy on station-keeping: choosing the bridge, taking the stance, holding the warning until the weather itself arrives to relieve it. It never approaches people; the warning is the whole relationship.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of its line; no further evolution is recorded.
- **Discovery:** Region 4's great river bridges. Rarity class: event — a sighting belongs to a specific storm, not a map routine. Method + conditions: the still, heavy hour before a major storm, from the far bank, at a respectful distance; it is already there when the wind drops. Level band (in words): high wild band. First-meeting note: there is no approach — the Ranger stops, the Omenmoth turns its head, and both of them look at the sky.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only — and barely: its famous register is silence. Source character: a very low, felt-more-than-heard hum with a dry wing-chirr at its edge, produced rarely and only at close range. Pitch contour: flat and low, dropping lower still before it falls silent. Duration class: brief, when it comes at all. Rhythm pattern: none in the wild — a single sustained hum at most; its call is its presence. Variations — idle: silence, with wing-settling sounds only; call: the low hum, once, toward its own kind ahead of a storm front; hurt: a sharp, dry chirr — the loudest sound it makes, and a sign something has gone very wrong; faint: a slow folding silence as the wings come down; affection: a barely audible softening of the hum near the one lamp-keeper it trusts; evolution moment: not applicable as a final stage — witnesses describe its arrival as the moment the bridge's ordinary sounds quietly stop.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Tall and bipedal — the family break from Mothlet's cub body. Long legs, long arms held folded into the wing-cape, and great wings that wrap the body like a cloak when closed and span wide only in departure. Mouth is effectively invisible; it does not open for cries in any visible way. Eyes are the whole face: two red lamps in a smooth, dark head, no other features to animate. Locomotion on the ground is a slow, upright stride; flight is a single deep wingbeat and a glide. Signature feature: the stance — motionless, head slightly bowed, wings closed — which animators must hold far longer than feels natural; the warning is a stillness, not a gesture.
- **Palette seed:** bridge grey, storm charcoal, warning red (eyes), rain silver
- **Footprint:** A tracker after a storm finds tall bipedal prints in the bridge dust: narrow, long-toed prints in a slow, even stride, planted flat with no hurry in them, flanked at the first and last print by great brushed arcs where the wings swept the ground powder aside. Print size class: large. Distinctive marker: the prints begin and end in open air at the bridge's edge — it arrived on the wing, stood a while, and left the same way.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a cloaked standing figure. Proportions in words: very tall, narrow through the body, the wing-cape widening the outline at the shoulders and closing to a point below. Key visual masses: the folded wings are the body; the head is a small smooth dome carrying the eyes. What reads at sprite size: a tall dark figure with two red eyes — nothing else is needed, and nothing else should compete. Identifying negative space: the long clear gap between its striding legs, and the notch where the wing-cape parts at the front.
- **Stat profile (words only):** A sentinel's profile — enduring, hard to move, quick to be gone; it endures weather and blame alike and fights, if ever, only to leave. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A tall, grey, wing-cloaked biped with a smooth featureless head and two red lamp eyes, standing on a bridge like a monument that chose its spot. Must-never-change: bipedal omen, not an insect on all fours; the eyes are the only face; it warns and never menaces — no fangs, no claws held out, no horror read; the grievance is in the posture, a tired slump inside the grandeur.
- **Flags:** ⚠ Flag (see §5): moths are Pokémon-crowded — none of them is a bipedal omen with a grievance, which is this concept's seed and distinction. The flag rides with it for the owner's eye.

### Spikid — C5-3 · stage 1 of Spikid line
- **Line / concept:** C5-3 — a spiky pup, "kid" doing double duty and it knows it; cryptid basis: the chupacabra, re-seeded from the first stage as a guardian in training, never a goat-sucker.
- **Types:** Beast
- **Category:** the Bramble Pup Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a goat-kid-sized pup, all bounce and back-spikes.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Spikid trails farm herds it was never invited to join, practising guard duty on butterflies and its own shadow. Farmers who find its spiky tracks around the pen in the morning have started leaving out a saucer, just in case it is hereditary.
- **Temperament:** Bold, bristly, and heroically over-serious about a job nobody has given it yet.
- **Habitat:** Farm edges, scrub pasture, and goat country around Region 4's rural settlements, wherever a herd needs — or tolerates — an apprentice guardian.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Eats farm scraps and wild pasture fare, and has developed a famous fondness for the goat's milk farmers leave out — tribute it accepts with grave ceremony. It sleeps within sight of the herd and startles awake at any night sound, spikes first.
- **Evolution:** Evolves into Capraguard after seasons of accepted service — the turning point is the first night a herd sleeps soundly because it is there.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 pasture country. Rarity class: uncommon. Method + conditions: dawn rounds of goat pens and scrub fences; its patrol tracks circle the pen before the farmer's own tracks do. Level band (in words): low wild band. First-meeting note: it will plant itself between the Ranger and the goats, at a size where the gesture is mostly symbolic, and mean it completely.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only. Source character: a yappy, bristly bark with a goat-kid bleat folded through it — the two sounds arrive together and neither wins. Pitch contour: sharp upward yap, wobbling bleat on the way down. Duration class: short. Rhythm pattern: single challenge-yaps at strangers; a rolling yap-bleat patter when the herd is moving. Variations — idle: occasional practice yaps at nothing, immediately looked-around-after; call: the rolling yap-bleat, answered by other Spikid across the pasture; hurt: a high, offended squeal-yap; faint: yaps trailing into a bleated sigh; affection: a soft, snuffly bleat with the bark entirely dropped; evolution moment: the yap deepens mid-cry and the bleat steadies under it, guard-voice arriving before guard-size does.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Quadruped pup body: four sturdy legs, a blunt pup muzzle with a strong little jaw, and ears that swivel toward herd sounds. Manipulators: none — it nudges, herds, and carries with its mouth and chest. Locomotion is a springy trot that breaks into sideways, crabwise guard-hops when excited. Signature feature: the row of back spikes, still soft-tipped at this stage, which rise and bristle with emotion — animators should treat the spike-row as its eyebrow: it does the expressing.
- **Palette seed:** scrub tan, bramble brown, milk cream, thorn green
- **Footprint:** A tracker finds busy pup prints with a bodyguard's itinerary: small padded prints, each showing short claw marks at the front, in a trotting gait that keeps circling back on itself — patrol loops around pen and pasture rather than a traveller's straight line. Print size class: small. Distinctive marker: beside the prints, shallow pricked rows in the dust where the back spikes brushed low ground during a guard-crouch.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a round pup with a sawtooth back. Proportions in words: a compact body, legs short enough to keep the belly low, head large and earnest. Key visual masses: the spike row along the spine is the second-largest read after the head. What reads at sprite size: pup shape, spike row, ears up. Identifying negative space: the sharp triangular notches between the back spikes — a picket-fence skyline no other pup in the region carries.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Scrappy and brave past its size — quick to engage, quick to tire, built on nerve more than power. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A goat-kid pup with a bristling row of back spikes, big ears, and a chest-out guardian stance several sizes too small for it. Must-never-change: the re-seed starts here — no fangs-for-draining, no sinister night-prowler read at any stage; the spikes are armour and expression, never menace; the kid/pup double nature stays in the silhouette.
- **Flags:** none

### Capraguard — C5-3 · stage 2 of Spikid line
- **Line / concept:** C5-3 — the goat-sucker of legend re-read as the goat-keeper: the cryptid was a bodyguard all along, and the goats started the rumour as advertising; cryptid basis: the chupacabra, re-seeded as guardian.
- **Types:** Beast
- **Category:** the Herd Guardian Mochi
- **Size class:** Large — a pen-gate presence, taller at the shoulder than the goats it guards and broader than the rumours about it.
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Capraguard walks the night pasture so the herd does not have to think about it. Old farmers insist the terrible stories were the goats' own invention: a guardian rumoured to be a monster is a guardian nothing tests twice.
- **Temperament:** Fierce on duty and mild off it; a professional. Its loyalty is to the herd as an institution, and it keeps professional hours.
- **Habitat:** Goat pasture, high scrub, and farm country across Region 4's rural Rift fringe, ranging a nightly circuit of pens and grazing ground.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Accepts the traditional tribute — goat's milk, left out at the pen gate — and earns it nightly: patrolling, scent-marking the fence line, and placing itself, unhurried, between the herd and anything that arrives after dark. It does not chase glory or strays far; the herd is the territory.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of its line; no further evolution is recorded.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 goat country. Rarity class: rare — it is on duty when people are asleep, and it prefers the arrangement. Method + conditions: night pasture, approached openly and announced, never snuck up on; the correct protocol is a saucer of milk and a Ranger willing to be inspected first. Level band (in words): high wild band. First-meeting note: it will inspect the Ranger the way it inspects everything — thoroughly, from the front, at its own pace — and the goats will keep grazing through the whole interview, which is the real reference letter.
- **Cry (full spec):** Cries only. Source character: a deep, carrying guard-bark with a steady bleat under it, the pup's double sound grown into a patrol horn. Pitch contour: a level, declarative bark that settles into the bleat's calm register. Duration class: medium — an announcement, not a conversation. Rhythm pattern: single barks at intervals on patrol; a rapid, rising sequence only when the herd is genuinely threatened. Variations — idle: low, occasional patrol barks that the herd sleeps through; call: fence-line answering barks with neighbouring Capraguard, territory confirmed in turn; hurt: a short, furious bark-bleat, mostly outrage; faint: barks slowing into a heavy, seated sigh; affection: the soft snuffle-bleat it gives the herd's newborns, bark entirely retired; evolution moment: not applicable as a final stage — its presence on a new farm is announced by one deep bark at dusk on the first night.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Powerful quadruped: four column-sturdy legs, a deep chest, and a broad, strong jaw kept — pointedly — unhurried; it guards by placement and display far more than by biting. No manipulators; herding is done with shoulder, chest, and stance. Locomotion is a deliberate patrol walk and a ground-covering surge when it must arrive. Signature features: the full spike ridge along the back, now true armour, which rises as a warning display; and the stand — broadside, head high, between the threat and the herd — which animators should treat as its signature move in every encounter.
- **Palette seed:** deep pasture brown, iron thorn-grey, milk cream, dusk green
- **Footprint:** A tracker finds a night watchman's round done properly: large padded prints with clear, short claw marks, in an unhurried walking gait that makes a complete circuit — pen gate, fence line, water trough, and back — with wider, deeper prints at the spots where it stood broadside for a long while. Print size class: large. Distinctive marker: the circuit itself — a closed loop of prints around a herd, fresh every morning, is a Capraguard signature no wandering predator leaves.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a broad quadruped fortress with a spiked skyline. Proportions in words: long and deep through the body, legs like gateposts, head carried high and level. Key visual masses: chest and spike ridge share the read; the head is a watchtower above them. What reads at sprite size: the sawtooth ridge and the tall, level head over a herd of smaller shapes. Identifying negative space: the clean arch under the deep chest, and the regular triangular gaps along the spike ridge.
- **Stat profile (words only):** A wall with a patrol route — bulky, enduring, and strong in defence of a position; not built for pursuit and never asked to be. ⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design).
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A large, deep-chested herd guardian with a full armour ridge of back spikes, standing broadside between the viewer and an unseen herd. Must-never-change: the guardian re-read is the whole concept — no blood-draining, no horror-cryptid staging, nothing that makes the goats' protector read as their predator; fierceness is protective display, and the resting read is a calm professional at its post.
- **Flags:** none

### Bunyiplet — C5-4 · stage 1 of Bunyiplet line
- **Line / concept:** C5-4 — a billabong tadpole-thing with too many opinions; cryptid basis: the bunyip of Australian billabongs, re-read here as the swamp's young and very vocal — in cries — flood-watcher.
- **Types:** Aqua/Umbral
