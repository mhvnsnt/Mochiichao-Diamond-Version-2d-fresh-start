### Capup — C2-1 · stage 1 of Capup → Chillbara
- **Line / concept:** C2-1 — a capybara pup that is born unbothered; the real animal is the capybara (✓ verified open, no official Pokémon based on it).
- **Types:** Aqua/Flora
- **Category:** the Unbothered Pup Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — roughly a plump loaf a child could carry in both arms, low to the ground
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Born already calm, Capup dozes half-submerged at the water's edge while the bank bustles around it. Other small creatures instinctively settle against its warm side, and a sleeping Capup is rarely sleeping alone.
- **Temperament:** Serene, unbothered, socially magnetic in the quietest possible way.
- **Habitat:** Warm riverbanks, reed beds, and slow shallows in Region 4's low wetlands.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Grazes bank grasses and soft water plants in long, unhurried bouts; spends much of the day soaking, only its head and back above the surface. Never hurries away from company.
- **Evolution:** Evolves as its calm deepens — measured, in local saying, by how many other species are asleep on it.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 low wetlands and riverbank clearings; rarity class: common; method: walk along banks / shallows edge, daytime; conditions: warm, still water nearby; level band in words: early wild band; first-meeting note: usually found already asleep with something smaller asleep on top of it.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: soft, breathy pup squeaks and contented huffs, never startled-sounding. Pitch contour: low and flat, a gentle rise only on the call. Duration class: short. Rhythm pattern: single notes spaced wide, or a drowsy double-huff. Variations — idle: slow huff-snore; call: soft rising squeak; hurt: brief surprised chirp, immediately settling; faint: sighing exhale trailing off; affection: low purring chatter through the teeth; evolution moment: a long, placid yawn-cry that makes nearby listeners yawn too.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Four short, sturdy legs; broad blunt muzzle, no visible fangs; no tail to speak of; locomotion is a steady waddle on land and a slow paddle in water; head sits low and heavy — animators should sell weight and stillness, not bounce.
- **Palette seed:** warm tan, river-mud brown, reed green, cream belly
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: blunt four-toed forefeet and three-toed hindfeet, toes slightly splayed for soft ground. Toe/claw count and arrangement: short, hoof-like nails in a neat front arc, no deep claw gouges. Gait pattern: alternating walk, prints in two close parallel lines. Print size class: small. Distinctive marker: prints often end abruptly at the waterline where it slipped in to soak, and bank prints are frequently overwritten by the tiny prints of whatever was following it.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: low, rounded rectangle — a loaf with a blunt head. Proportions in words: long body, short legs barely lifting the belly, head almost as wide as it is long. Key visual masses: one continuous back line from nose to rump with no neck break. What reads at sprite size: the flat top line and half-lidded head. Identifying negative space: almost none — the silhouette is deliberately solid; only the small gaps under the belly between the short legs break it.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Slow, bulky for its stage, and exceptionally steady — a small wall whose defence is that nothing wants to disturb it — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A compact, loaf-bodied capybara pup with a broad blunt muzzle, half-lidded eyes, and short legs, reading instantly as calm. Must-never-change: the unbroken flat back that other creatures nap against, the blunt rectangular head, and the total absence of hurry or menace in the pose.
- **Flags:** none — capybara verified open in §5.

### Chillbara — C2-1 · stage 2 of Capup → Chillbara
- **Line / concept:** C2-1 — the waterbank's self-appointed grandfather, a warm island every small creature naps against; the real animal is the capybara (✓ verified open).
- **Types:** Aqua/Flora
- **Category:** the Warm Island Mochi
- **Size class:** Medium — a broad, low island of an animal, large enough for several small creatures to sleep across its back at once
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Chillbara's calm is contagious: things near it slow down and mean it. It takes up its place at the bank like a grandfather taking his chair, and by dusk its back is a dormitory of smaller species, all sleeping in a row.
- **Temperament:** Grandfatherly, permissive, immovable in the kindest sense; its signature calm settles everything around it.
- **Habitat:** Established riverbanks, warm pools, and reed clearings where many species come to rest.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Grazes coarse bank grasses for long stretches, soaks for hours with only eyes and ears above water, and tolerates — appears to welcome — being climbed on, nested on, and slept against.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of the line; does not evolve further.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 riverbank rest-clearings and warm pool margins; rarity class: uncommon; method: walk, daytime, often located by finding the cluster of sleeping species first; conditions: calm weather, established bank; level band in words: mid wild band; first-meeting note: approach slowly — not for its sake, but because everything asleep on it will wake if you rush.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: deep, slow huffs, soft tooth-chatter purrs, and a low contented grunt. Pitch contour: low, mostly level, sinking at the end of phrases. Duration class: short-to-medium, unhurried. Rhythm pattern: widely spaced single notes; a slow chatter when pleased. Variations — idle: deep breathing huffs bordering on snores; call: low grunt repeated patiently; hurt: one startled bark, then calm reasserting; faint: a long settling sigh as it lies down; affection: sustained soft tooth-purr; evolution moment: not applicable as final stage — its arrival cry on first meeting a Ranger is the same low, welcoming grunt.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Four column-short legs, massive blunt head, barrel torso with a long flat dorsal plane — the back must animate as a stable platform; locomotion is a deliberate plod and a smooth, barely rippling swim; jaw works side-to-side when grazing.
- **Palette seed:** deep warm brown, tan, moss green on the wet lower body, grey-brown muzzle
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: blunt four-toed foreprints and three-toed hindprints, deeper and wider than Capup's. Toe/claw count and arrangement: short hoof-like nails, splayed on mud. Gait pattern: slow alternating walk, short stride, prints often surrounded by a scatter of much smaller species' prints converging on the same spot. Print size class: medium. Distinctive marker: the resting hollow — a broad, smooth, body-shaped depression in bank grass beside the prints where it lay and others piled on.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a long, low, flat-topped island shape. Proportions in words: very long torso, short legs, huge blunt head carried level with the back. Key visual masses: the unbroken dorsal plateau is the whole read. What reads at sprite size: flat top, blunt front, sheer stillness. Identifying negative space: minimal — small notches under the belly only; the solid mass IS the identity.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Slow and very bulky, a true wall with calming presence; offence is an afterthought — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A big, flat-backed capybara rendered as a living piece of bank furniture — warm, broad, and sleepy-eyed, with room on its back for the region to nap. Must-never-change: the flat dormitory back, the grandfatherly blunt head, and calm as a physical, contagious quality rather than an expression.
- **Flags:** none — capybara verified open in §5.

### Punchrimp — C2-2 · stage 1 of Punchrimp → Rainbrawler
- **Line / concept:** C2-2 — a thumb-sized shrimp whose punch boils the water in front of it; the real animal is the mantis shrimp (✓ verified open — distinct from the pistol shrimp, see Flags).
- **Types:** Aqua/Beast
- **Category:** the Boiling Punch Mochi
- **Size class:** Tiny — thumb-sized in the source's own words, a curled spark of a shrimp
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Punchrimp lives in a burrow in the rock and settles every argument with one punch so fast the water in front of its fist briefly boils. It is very small, entirely aware of this, and completely untroubled by it.
- **Temperament:** Bold, quick-tempered, territorial about its burrow mouth.
- **Habitat:** Rocky shallows, reef rubble, and Rift-touched tidal pools in Region 4.
- **Diet & behaviour:** An ambush hunter of small shelled prey, smashing shells from its burrow entrance; spends the rest of its time tidying the burrow and challenging passers-by many times its size.
- **Evolution:** Evolves as its punch and its colours develop together — the brawler grows into its rainbow.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 tidal pools and rocky shallows; rarity class: uncommon; method: water-edge / shallow wading, daytime; conditions: clear, sunlit shallows where burrow mouths are visible; level band in words: early wild band; first-meeting note: often detected by a tiny, sharp *snap-boil* and a puff of disturbed sand before it is seen.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: not a voice so much as a punctuation — sharp clicks and snaps made with its striking limbs, plus a faint bubbling hiss when the punch boils water. Pitch contour: high, flat, percussive. Duration class: very short. Rhythm pattern: single warning clicks, then a rapid-fire burst when committed. Variations — idle: occasional single click; call: paired clicks, challenge rhythm; hurt: frantic clicking rattle; faint: clicks slowing and stopping; affection: soft, slow clicking against a trusted shell or stone; evolution moment: one outsized boom-click with a visible boil-burst.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Segmented curled body; the signature organ is the pair of folded raptorial striking limbs under the head — they must read as cocked hammers that unfold faster than the eye; stalked eyes that move independently; swimmerets under the tail fan; locomotion is burrow-crawling and short backward flick-swims.
- **Palette seed:** jewel green, orange, electric blue accents, sand-pale underside
- **Footprint:** Water-trace instead of a land print: a burrow signature — a neat round hole in sand or rubble ringed with tiny excavated pellets, plus, after a strike, a scorched-clean semicircle of disturbed sand and a brief trail of micro-bubbles. Gait equivalent: no trackway; it holds position at the burrow mouth. Distinctive marker: the perfectly kept burrow entrance with shell fragments of opened prey stacked beside it.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a curled comma with a cocked fist. Proportions in words: large head section, body curling tightly under, tail fan tucked. Key visual masses: the folded strikers under the front and the stalked eyes above it. What reads at sprite size: the curl, the eyes on stalks, the raised striker pair. Identifying negative space: the clear gap inside the body curl and the notch between the two folded strikers.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Tiny, fast, and disproportionately hard-hitting for its size, but frail if actually caught — a glass striker — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A thumb-sized mantis shrimp, curled and cocky, with stalked swivelling eyes and visibly folded hammer-limbs. Must-never-change: the punch mechanism reads as a mantis shrimp's raptorial strike (never a claw that shoots or a pistol snap — see Flags), the independent stalked eyes, and the jewel-bright hint of the rainbow to come.
- **Flags:** ⚠ Pokémon's Clauncher / Clawitzer is a *pistol* shrimp — a different animal with a different mechanism (§5.3). Punchrimp is a *mantis* shrimp: raptorial striking limbs, not a snapping claw. The distinction must survive every design and animation pass.

### Rainbrawler — C2-2 · stage 2 of Punchrimp → Rainbrawler
- **Line / concept:** C2-2 — wearing the full rainbow carapace, seeing colours nobody else in the region can; the real animal is the mantis shrimp (✓ verified open — distinct from the pistol shrimp, see Flags).
- **Types:** Aqua/Beast
- **Category:** the Rainbow Sight Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — still a shrimp, but a substantial one; a two-handed lift, all muscle and armour
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Rainbrawler sees colours nobody else in the region can, and describes them, patiently, to anyone who sits still. Between descriptions it remains a brawler: its punch still boils the water, and its burrow is still its castle.
- **Temperament:** Patient explainer and proud fighter in equal measure; gentle with listeners, absolute with challengers.
- **Habitat:** Reef edges, rock burrows, and clear Rift pools where the light is good.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Hunts shelled prey with shell-shattering strikes; otherwise holds court at its burrow, turning its stalked eyes on visitors and — in cries and gestures — holding forth about colours only it can see.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of the line; does not evolve further.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 reef burrows and clear Rift pools; rarity class: rare; method: water-edge / shallow wading, bright daytime; conditions: strong clear light — it comes out to look at the light; level band in words: mid-to-high wild band; first-meeting note: it will study a new Ranger for a long moment with both eye-stalks before deciding between a display and a punch.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: resonant clicks and snaps, deeper than Punchrimp's, with rolling bubbling flourishes during displays. Pitch contour: percussive base with a surprisingly melodic rise-and-fall in display sequences. Duration class: short notes in longer conversations. Rhythm pattern: deliberate, almost speech-like phrasing — bursts, pauses, bursts — cries only, never words. Variations — idle: slow contemplative clicks; call: full rolling display sequence; hurt: hard rapid snaps; faint: a final dignified single click; affection: soft paired clicks offered like a gift; evolution moment: not applicable as final stage.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Full segmented carapace as rainbow armour plates; raptorial strikers larger and more ornate, unfolding in a blur; stalked eyes are the most expressive feature — independent swivels, tilts, and slow blinks must carry its personality; tail fan strong for retreat flicks.
- **Palette seed:** full rainbow carapace — saturated red, orange, green, blue, and violet bands over a jewel base
- **Footprint:** Water-trace instead of a land print: a grander burrow signature — a wide, swept-clean arena in front of the burrow mouth, shell trophies arranged around it, and boil-scoured sand where strikes have landed. Distinctive marker: the arena is kept immaculate and rainbow shell fragments are sorted, not scattered.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: an armoured curl, taller and prouder than Punchrimp, strikers raised like a boxer's guard. Proportions in words: big front section, arched segmented back, eye-stalks held high. Key visual masses: the rainbow back plates and the raised guard. What reads at sprite size: rainbow banding, stalked eyes, boxing-guard front. Identifying negative space: the gap between the raised strikers and the arch of the back, and the inside of the tail curl.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Fast and very hard-hitting with strong senses; defence middling, endurance modest — it ends fights early — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A mantis shrimp as a rainbow-armoured prizefighter-philosopher: full spectrum carapace, high stalked eyes, strikers held in a guard. Must-never-change: the mantis (not pistol) strike anatomy, the full rainbow shell, and the patient, seeing-more-than-you expression in the eyes.
- **Flags:** ⚠ Same distinction as Punchrimp: Clauncher / Clawitzer is a *pistol* shrimp (§5.3). Rainbrawler is a *mantis* shrimp — verified distinct and open (C2-2). Never give it a shooting claw.

### Tunlet — C2-3 · stage 1 of Tunlet → Tardigreat
- **Line / concept:** C2-3 — a microscopic-class water bear rendered at Mochi scale whose answer to every extreme is to curl into its tun form and wait; the real animal is the tardigrade (✓ verified open).
- **Types:** Beast
- **Category:** the Little Water Bear Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a plump barrel-bodied bear-cub of a thing, low and round
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Tunlet is a water bear the size of a cub, with a barrel body and a permanently unbothered smile. Drought, frost, or the Rift's weird weather — its answer to all of it is the same: curl into its tun, and wait for a better century.
- **Temperament:** Cheerful, patient to an inhuman degree, impossible to panic.
- **Habitat:** Moss beds, damp bark, puddle edges — and, in tun form, absolutely anywhere in Region 4, for as long as it takes.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Sups at mosses and the juices of tiny plants with a slow, contented graze; at the first sign of true extremes it withdraws its legs, rounds itself into the tun, and sleeps through whatever comes.
- **Evolution:** The line's evolution is a rumour other species tell — Tunlet is said to become Tardigreat simply by still being there when the waiting ends.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 moss beds, damp forest floor, and puddle margins; rarity class: uncommon (common in tun form, if you know what you're looking at); method: walk, any weather — in extreme weather search for tuns, not bears; conditions: none, which is rather the point; level band in words: early wild band; first-meeting note: a first meeting may be with a tun — a dry little barrel that wakes, blinking, when the weather improves or a Ranger waits with it.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: tiny, round, contented squeaks — a bear-cub voice in miniature. Pitch contour: mid and gently domed, rising with pleasure, never spiking in fear. Duration class: short. Rhythm pattern: slow singles; a sleepy repeated peep as it settles into the tun. Variations — idle: soft wandering squeaks while grazing; call: a slightly louder, friendly peep; hurt: one squeezed squeak, then the tun response begins; faint: it does not so much faint as tun — a descending, satisfied sigh-peep as it curls; affection: warm, rapid little chuckle-peeps; evolution moment: the same cry, unchanged in character, emerging from a much larger barrel.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Plump barrel torso; four pairs of short stubby legs ending in tiny claws, all of which retract in the tun transformation; small round head with a blunt snout and no visible neck; locomotion is a slow, ambling, bear-like waddle; the tun form is a smooth, leg-less barrel — animators must make the curl-in read as cosy, not distressed.
- **Palette seed:** dusty rose, soft tan, moss green accents, cream face
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: tiny round pads in four paired rows — eight little prints per step cycle, each with minute claw ticks at the front. Toe/claw count and arrangement: four stubby toes' worth of claw marks clustered tight. Gait pattern: slow amble, prints in paired left-right bundles, trail often wandering in graze-curves through moss. Print size class: tiny. Distinctive marker: the trail simply stops where the tun formed, replaced by a single smooth barrel-roll impression in the moss.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a plump barrel on stubs, smiling. Proportions in words: body far wider than the legs are long; head a small rounding of the front, not a separate mass. Key visual masses: the barrel itself. What reads at sprite size: roundness, stub legs, contented face. Identifying negative space: tiny gaps between the paired stub legs under the belly; in tun form, none at all — a perfect sealed barrel.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Extremely hardy and enduring beyond all proportion to its size; slow, weak-hitting, and in no hurry to be anything else — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A plump, barrel-bodied water-bear cub with four pairs of stubby clawed legs and a serene smile, built to curl into a smooth tun. Must-never-change: the barrel proportions, the eight stub legs, the tun as a smiling act of patience — and never make it look like an insect.
- **Flags:** none — tardigrade verified open in §5.

### Tardigreat — C2-3 · stage 2 of Tunlet → Tardigreat
- **Line / concept:** C2-3 — not stronger than the pup; simply still there; the real animal is the tardigrade (✓ verified open).
- **Types:** Beast
- **Category:** the Still There Mochi
- **Size class:** Medium — the same barrel bear, grown great: a broad, low mass a Ranger could lean against
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Tardigreat is not stronger than Tunlet was. It is simply still there — after the drought, after the frost, after the Rift's worst weather, the same smile uncurls from the same tun, in a considerably larger barrel.
- **Temperament:** Monumentally patient, gently amused by everything, including history.
- **Habitat:** The oldest moss banks and stone hollows of Region 4; in tun form, wherever it happened to be when the century turned bad.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Grazes moss and damp greenery at a geological pace; weathers every extreme in tun form; other species shelter against it in storms the way they shelter against a hill.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of the line; does not evolve further. Its evolution remains, officially, a rumour other species tell.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 old-growth moss banks and weather-beaten hollows; rarity class: rare; method: walk — or find a tun and wait; conditions: post-extreme-weather moments are when it uncurls and can be met awake; level band in words: high wild band; first-meeting note: locals may insist the one you found has always been there, and decline to say since when.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: Tunlet's round squeak grown into a deep, slow, barrel-resonant hum-cry, still cheerful. Pitch contour: low and domed, unhurried. Duration class: medium — it takes its time. Rhythm pattern: single long notes, widely spaced; a slow settling sequence as the tun forms. Variations — idle: low grazing hums; call: a deep, carrying peep that sounds oddly small for its size, then doesn't; hurt: a grunt, followed by unhurried curling; faint: the tun sigh, enormous and content; affection: warm resonant chuckling through the whole barrel; evolution moment: not applicable as final stage.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** Same body plan at great scale: barrel torso, four pairs of thick stub legs with claws, blunt smiling head, full retraction into a vast smooth tun; locomotion is a slow, weighty amble — every step should land; in storms, animate it as landscape, not as a creature bracing.
- **Palette seed:** weathered rose-brown, stone tan, deep moss green, cream face
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: broad round pads in four paired rows, pressed deep. Toe/claw count and arrangement: stub claw ticks, widely set, at each pad's front. Gait pattern: slow amble, paired bundles, long wandering graze-trails that may be interrupted by a tun impression and resume — in some trails, on the far side of a visibly older patch of ground. Print size class: medium. Distinctive marker: trails that stop for a season and start again from the same barrel-roll hollow.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a great barrel — a hill with a smile. Proportions in words: immense width, legs almost lost under the mass, head a gentle front rounding. Key visual masses: one. What reads at sprite size: the barrel, the smile, the stubs. Identifying negative space: almost none; in tun form, a perfectly sealed, featureless barrel silhouette.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Among the hardiest profiles in the bucket in words — endurance and bulk first, speed last, offence modest; it wins by remaining — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** Tunlet's exact body plan, scaled to greatness: a vast, smiling barrel-bear whose power is persistence. Must-never-change: no added armour, weapons, or menace — its greatness is only size and survival; the tun must remain a smooth, cosy barrel at this scale too.
- **Flags:** none — tardigrade verified open in §5. Name note from §5.5: Tardigreat is a deliberate tardigrade portmanteau with no Pokémon name resemblance.

### Tapaye — C2-4 · stage 1 of Tapaye → Ayeerie
- **Line / concept:** C2-4 — a night forager that taps dead wood with its thin ball-jointed finger and listens with ears like two open doors; the real animal is the aye-aye (✓ verified open).
- **Types:** Umbral/Beast
- **Category:** the Listening Finger Mochi
- **Size class:** Small — a crouched, big-eared night pup, mostly ears and eyes
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Tapaye spends the night tapping dead wood with its thin, ball-jointed finger, listening through ears like two open doors for what moves inside. By day it sleeps curled in the dark, one ear still half-open.
- **Temperament:** Gentle, curious, utterly nocturnal; startles at daylight bustle, fearless in the dark.
- **Habitat:** Dead trees, hollow logs, and the deep shade of Region 4's night woods — the Dark Forest's counterpart country one region over.
- **Diet & behaviour:** Finds grubs and insects by tap-listening: tap, listen, hook them out with the same thin finger. Forages alone, in silence broken only by its own tapping.
- **Evolution:** The finger lengthens; the listening deepens.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 night woods and dead-tree groves; rarity class: uncommon; method: walk, night only; conditions: darkness and quiet — it avoids lantern-lit paths; level band in words: early-to-mid wild band; first-meeting note: you will hear it before you see it — a soft, patient *tap… tap…* from inside a dead tree.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: small, dry clicks of the tapping finger form the percussion; the voice itself is a soft, breathy squeak, rarely used. Pitch contour: mid-high and tentative, falling at the end. Duration class: very short. Rhythm pattern: voice comes in singles; the tapping carries the rhythm — slow, probing, irregular. Variations — idle: near-silence with occasional tap sequences; call: a hushed squeak repeated twice; hurt: sharp squeak and immediate stillness; faint: a tiny sigh as it curls tight; affection: soft squeak plus gentle tapping against the trusted person's hand or boot; evolution moment: the tapping slows, deepens, and the cry drops in pitch mid-sequence.
- **Anatomy notes (animation-variance):** The signature organ is the thin middle finger with a ball joint — it must articulate independently, tapping and hooking; enormous thin ears that swivel and tilt separately; large forward eyes for night vision; crouched quadruped posture with grasping feet; locomotion is a careful climb and a low scurry, never a daytime bound.
- **Palette seed:** charcoal brown, ash grey, pale amber eyes, pink-grey ear membranes
- **Footprint:** Print shape class: grasping hand-like prints — long finger marks radiating from a small palm pad, hind prints showing an opposable toe angled inward. Toe/claw count and arrangement: five long digits, the middle one conspicuously thinner and longer in the foreprint. Gait pattern: crouched walk, prints close and overlapping around tree bases rather than in travel lines. Print size class: small. Distinctive marker: foreprints circling dead trunks, paired with tiny tap-pits in the bark directly above them.
- **Silhouette:** Outline class: a crouch crowned by two huge ear-sails. Proportions in words: ears nearly as tall as the body is long, body low and hunched, tail a modest brush behind. Key visual masses: the ears dominate; the thin finger reads only in close view. What reads at sprite size: ear pair, big eyes, hunched back. Identifying negative space: the clear V between the two upright ears and the gap under the crouched belly.
- **Stat profile (words only):** Frail and quiet, with senses far beyond its size — finds what hides, avoids what fights — plus "⚑ numbers unassigned (owner/game-design)".
- **DESIGN BRIEF:** A gentle night pup defined by two enormous ears and one impossibly thin, ball-jointed tapping finger. Must-never-change: the ears-like-open-doors read, the specialised thin finger (never a claw, never a wand), and a kind — never sinister — expression despite the Umbral typing.
- **Flags:** none — aye-aye verified open in §5.

### Ayeerie — C2-4 · stage 2 of Tapaye → Ayeerie
- **Line / concept:** C2-4 — the Dark Forest's counterpart one region over: huge-eyed, gentle, and universally believed to be a ghost; the real animal is the aye-aye (✓ verified open).
- **Types:** Umbral/Beast
- **Category:** the Supposed Ghost Mochi
- **Size class:** Medium — a long, low night shape, taller at the ears than anything else in its woods
- **Bio (Mochiioteca):** Ayeerie is universally believed to be a ghost by species who haven't met one. Those who have describe a huge-eyed, gentle listener who taps the dead trees all night, keeping count of everything that stirs inside the wood.
- **Temperament:** Serene, solitary, and kind; completely indifferent to its ghostly reputation.
- **Habitat:** The deepest dead-tree stands and hollow groves of Region 4's night country.
- **Diet & behaviour:** A master tap-forager: the lengthened finger and deepened listening let it map a whole trunk's hidden life before it ever breaks the bark. Moves through the canopy and the dark floor with equal, silent care.
- **Evolution:** Final stage of the line; does not evolve further.
- **Discovery:** Region 4 deep night woods, dead-tree stands; rarity class: rare; method: walk, deep night only; conditions: moonless or dim nights favour it; lantern light drives it back; level band in words: mid-to-high wild band; first-meeting note: most first meetings begin as a ghost story — two huge eyes and two ear-sails in the dark, resolving, on patience, into a gentle animal tapping a tree.
- **Cry (full spec):** Source character: a soft, low, breathy cry — almost a sigh with pitch — over the constant dry percussion of tapping.
