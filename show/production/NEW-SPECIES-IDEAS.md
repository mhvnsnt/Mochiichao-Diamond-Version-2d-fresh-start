# NEW SPECIES IDEAS — regional brainstorm under the Flavor Law

**Status: FUTURE CONSIDERED IDEAS — brainstorm, NOT canon (2026-10-27).**
The canon roster is **47 species**; nothing in this file adds to it, renames it, or
fills any gap in it. These are candidates logged for the owner to lock from later,
built under `REGIONAL-FLAVOR-LAW.md`. Every name is an original coinage in the owner's
style (short, meaning-loaded portmanteau); inspirations are recorded per concept and no
source name — Pokémon, Digimon, Monster Rancher, folklore character, or other — is
carried over (construction-kit filter). Element labels use canon's show elements
(Aqua/Flame/Flora/Chrome/Umbral/Beast) first; other labels (Volt, Gale, Frost, Terra…)
are brainstorm working labels only.

**Counts:** 60 brainstormed concepts — Bucket A (R2): 10 evolution lines · Bucket B
(R3): 11 concepts · Bucket C (R4): 39 concepts. Plus 2 owner-directed anchors (§0),
which are **not** brainstorm and not counted — and Bucket D: 6 light R5 register
sketches (owner-decided flavour, 2026-10-27), deliberately light and likewise not
counted in the 60.

## Bucket A — Region 2: Digimon-drift evolution lines (10)

*Pokémon core, a little more Digimon: body plan holds for two stages, breaks once at
the final; gear is grown; one bond flavour per line at most. Stages: base → evolved →
final.*

**A1. The guard-dog line — Pupparmor → Shieldane → Bastihound** *(Beast/Chrome)*
A round-armoured puppy whose soft plates harden each stage; the final stands upright
as a tower-shield hound, one foreleg grown into a full shield it plants like a gate.
*Drift marker:* bipedal armoured final + grown shield. *Evolution note:* the break is
posture, not species — still unmistakably the same dog.

**A2. The lance-fish line — Flickfin → Pikeperil → Halberdrake** *(Aqua/Chrome)*
A minnow with a needle nose; the needle becomes a pike, then the whole fish becomes a
halberd-headed drake that duels by fencing. *Drift marker:* weapon grows from the
snout across all three stages — the line's one idea, weaponised. *Evolution note:*
final stage trades the fish silhouette for a drake's — the permitted single break.

**A3. The sprout-knight line — Seedquire → Budguard → Verdilance** *(Flora/Beast)*
A seedling squire carrying a twig; a bud-page with a leaf shield; a tall lance-bearing
knight whose armour is bark and whose lance is a grown sunflower stalk. *Drift marker:*
humanoid final, chivalric read. *Evolution note:* deliberately *not* a turtle — R1's
Flora chair (Sproutle's line) is a tortoise; this is the region-2 answer, a person.

**A4. The imp ladder — Ashimp → Embrute → Diablaze** *(Flame/Umbral)*
A sooty imp that carries one coal; a brute with a furnace belly; a horned, grinning
battler whose chest is an open furnace door. *Drift marker:* Digimon's imp→devil
escalation ladder, kept show-warm (mischief, never menace-theology). *Evolution note:*
each stage's fire moves from hand → belly → chest: the body becomes the furnace.

**A5. The reef line — Polypup → Reefender → Atollossus** *(Aqua/Chrome)*
A coral polyp pup that budges into a reef-backed defender; the final is a walking
atoll — a whole reef ecosystem on one creature, tube-sponges grown as water cannons,
small fish sheltering in its branches. *Drift marker:* grown artillery + habitat-body.
*Evolution note:* it doesn't outgrow the reef; the reef outgrows *it* — final stage is
a place as much as a creature.

**A6. The mole-miner line — Tunnlet → Excavarm → Magmole** *(Terra/Chrome)*
A tunnelling pup with soft mitts; an excavator-armed digger; a bipedal magma mole
whose drill gauntlets glow at the tips because it digs *toward* the volcano.
*Drift marker:* tool-arms as anatomy + upright final. *Evolution note:* written to
feel at home near R3's volcano country without belonging to it.

**A7. The paper-crane line — Foldchick → Papercrane → Thouswings** *(Gale/Flora)*
A chick folded like paper; a crane with crease-marked wings; a majestic final whose
thousand paper feathers detach as blade-feathers and return. *Drift marker:* elegant
weaponised plumage — Digimon's ornament-as-arsenal. *Evolution note:* the only A-line
whose final is more graceful, not more armoured; variety is the point.

**A8. The rune-wolf line — Snowpup → Frosfang → Fenrirune** *(Frost/Beast)*
A snow pup; a frost-fanged hunter; a great wolf with runes grown as ice along its
spine and an ice blade grown from one shoulder. *Drift marker:* mythic escalation +
grown greatsword. *Inspiration:* the Fenrir myth, name re-coined (Fenrir+rune), no
source design carried. *Evolution note:* the runes are a map of everywhere it has
wintered — the line keeps a diary on its back.

**A9. The vow line — Lonelily → Pairbloom → Twinvow** *(Flora/Umbral)*
A single shy lily bud. It cannot evolve alone: two Lonelily raised within sight of
each other bloom into one **Pairbloom**, and two Pairbloom in accord become
**Twinvow** — two flowers, one root, facing opposite directions so one always watches
the other's back. *Drift marker:* the bucket's bond mechanic — evolution as a
relationship event (Digimon's partner logic, Pokémon's gentleness). *Evolution note:*
the only line whose evolution is a *pair* of creatures becoming a pair-shaped one.

**A10. The sky-serpent line — Noodlet → Ribbongler → Azulong** *(Aqua/Gale)*
A noodle-thin ribbon eel; a longer, ribboned angler of shallow seas; a majestic
sky-serpent that leaves the water entirely and swims the wind over the coast.
*Drift marker:* the humble-to-majestic leap (Digimon's signature small-cute →
noble-final jump). *Inspiration:* the azure-dragon shape, re-coined (azure+*long*,
the Chinese dragon word) — a shape homage, no source name. *Evolution note:* stage 2
is the awkward century; stage 3 is why nobody teases it.

## Bucket B — Region 3: Monster Rancher-drift concepts (11)

*Mixture bodies, ranch jobs, one-word temperaments, proportion licence. Single-stage
is normal here. The region's year turns around the Volcano Oni's Festival (anchor,
§0) — several concepts carry festival jobs on purpose.*

**B1. Plowpus** *(Terra/Aqua)* — a platypus whose bill is a seed-drill and whose
claws are ploughshares; it tills the festival fields in spring and is paid in river
pebbles it doesn't need. *Job:* plough. *Temperament:* methodical. *Mixture:*
platypus body, farm-implement parts. ⚠ *Flag: shares the platypus with Psyduck —
seed is a digging farm animal, no duck, no headache-psychic read (see §5).*

**B2. Sentripea** *(Flora)* — a pea-pod on a stalk with one large eye at the top;
the ranch lookout, it rustles the pod to sound alarms (the peas are the rattle).
*Job:* lookout. *Temperament:* nosy. *Proportion licence:* mostly eye and stalk.
*(Drafting note: the one-eye-stalk is Rancher's Suezo signature — the seed here is a
plant with peas, not an eyeball monster; flagged in §5 for the owner's eye.)*

**B3. The Stormewe crossing — Cinderewe × Rainewe → Stormewe** *(Flame × Aqua →
Volt)* — two ewe species designed as a Rancher crossing pair: **Cinderewe**, whose
wool smoulders softly and is sheared for winter kindling, and **Rainewe**, whose wool
is a permanent personal raincloud. Their cross, **Stormewe**, wears a thunderhead:
wool that crackles, rains on one chosen flowerbed at a time, and is the festival's
official weather. *Mixture:* the bucket's worked example of combination flavour.
⚠ *Flag: sheep are Pokémon-occupied (Wooloo) — these are elemental weather-sheep,
not wool livestock (see §5).*

**B4. Stackid** *(Terra)* — a goat made of stacked flat stones, cairn-bodied; it
stands at trail turns as a living signpost and resents being decorated, except at
festival, when it allows ribbons. *Job:* signpost/guide. *Temperament:* stubborn
(literally and otherwise). ⚠ *Flag: goats are Pokémon-occupied (Skiddo/Gogoat) —
this body is a cairn, not a goat shape (see §5).*

**B5. The orchard line — Pipworm → Ciderpent** *(Flora/Beast)* — a pip-small worm
that lives in a fallen apple; it evolves by *moving house* into a cider barrel,
becoming a long, mellow serpent who is the orchard's quality inspector and chief
taster. *Job:* cellar-keeper. *Temperament:* gourmet. *Drift:* evolution as
promotion/relocation, pure Rancher. ⚠ *Flag: worms are Pokémon-adjacent (Wurmple is
a caterpillar) — this is an earthworm-class orchard worm (see §5).*

**B6. The colony line — Nudigger → Molerarch** *(Terra/Umbral)* — a naked-mole-rat
worker, pink, wrinkled, tireless; a colony's most loyal worker is *promoted* —
evolution as coronation — into **Molerarch**, the great queen whose burrow-city is a
minor wonder of the region. *Job:* the whole colony is the job. *Temperament:*
devoted. *Animal:* naked mole rat — verified unused by Pokémon (§5). *Drift:*
evolution as social promotion, the bucket's second evolution experiment.

**B7. Spindevil** *(Beast)* — a Tasmanian devil that expresses every emotion by
spinning: joy, rage, greetings, grief. Ranch hands read the spin-rate like a gauge.
*Job:* alarm clock (dawn spin). *Temperament:* all of them, rotationally.
*Animal:* Tasmanian devil — verified unused (§5). *Single-stage, deliberately:*
some creatures are finished as they are.

**B8. Faintail** *(Beast)* — an opossum that faints theatrically at surprises,
arguments, loud praise, and its own reflection; between faints it is the ranch's
softest napping pillow, and all baby creatures are parked on it. *Job:* pillow.
*Temperament:* dramatic. *Animal:* opossum — verified unused (§5).

**B9. Platemoll** *(Aqua/Chrome)* — a chiton with eight perfect plates that curls
into a flawless wheel; it volunteers as the mill's rolling pin on weekdays and a
cart wheel on market days, and uncurls only for lunch. *Job:* wheel. *Temperament:*
obliging. *Animal:* chiton — verified unused (§5). *Proportion licence:* it is
basically a wheel that eats algae.

**B10. Shagdox** *(Beast/Frost)* — a musk ox so shaggy that only its nose and the
tips of its horns exit the wool; birds nest in it without asking; at shearing time
it donates a haystack. *Job:* haystack. *Temperament:* unbothered. ⚠ *Flag:
buffalo are Pokémon-occupied (Bouffalant) — the musk ox is a distinct animal and
the seed is the shag, not the charge (see §5).*

**B11. Dustbunny** *(Umbral/Beast)* — not a rabbit: a colony of dust that has taken
a rabbit's *shape* the way dust takes a corner's shape. It cleans the ranch house
by absorbing dust into itself (it is always slightly hungry), hides under furniture
when thanked, and sneezes in tiny grey puffs. *Job:* cleaner. *Temperament:* shy.
*Mixture:* object-hybrid in Rancher's anything-can-be-a-monster spirit. ⚠ *Flag:
rabbits are heavily Pokémon-occupied — this is not a rabbit animal at all, only a
rabbit-shaped absence of one (see §5).*

## Bucket C — Region 4: the full mix + toy-battle register + expansion harvest (39)

*R4 restraint rule (from the Flavor Law): one tradition leads per concept, the others
flavour. The Rift is the in-fiction excuse — nowhere else could this bucket live.*

### C1. Toy-battle / mecha register (7)

**C1-1. Marblit → Popriffin** *(Gale/Beast — Bakugan register)* — a chick that sleeps,
travels, and hides as a perfect carved ball; startled or called, it *springs* open
into a griffin, wings first, with an audible pop the region uses as a doorbell.
*Evolution note:* the ball never goes away — the adult Popriffin still folds back to
a ball to sleep, and old ones are perfectly round. *Inspiration:* the pop-open battle
toy + the griffin of folklore; no donor name or shape carried.

**C1-2. Whirligig → Cyclossus** *(Chrome/Beast — Beyblade register)* — a top-bodied
beetle that battles by spinning: endurance, balance, and nerve instead of blows. At
full spin its shell blurs away and the small bright creature-core inside shows its
face. The final, **Cyclossus**, is a walking colossus that still settles disputes by
spin-duel, and has never once been seen to stop on its own. *Evolution note:* the
core face is the same at both stages — the body around it is the toy; the face is
the creature.

**C1-3. Packpup → Rigrex** *(Beast/Terra — Transformers register)* — a pup that
carries things on its back by instinct; the final **Rigrex** is a great saurus with
two complete ways of being: beast mode, and **carrier mode**, where it settles into
a walking cargo frame that smaller species ride like a bus with opinions.
*Evolution note:* both modes are the creature — nobody built the frame; it grew
saddle-shaped. This is the register's alt-mode, kept biological per the Secret-law
care in the Flavor Law.

**C1-4. Pilotto & Framoth — a pair-entry** *(Gale/Flora — Gundam register)* —
**Pilotto**, a palm-sized otter-like rider, bonds for life with **Framoth**, a gentle
giant moth whose wing-cape folds into a nest-hollow cockpit. Framoth's wing-scales
shed as living scale-mites that fly in formation as a swarm shield — the pair's
remote children. *Design note:* the pair is the species-entry; neither is complete
alone, and the bond is symbiosis, not machinery. ⚠ *Flag: moths are Pokémon-crowded
(Volcarona, Frosmoth…) — the design is the pair-bond and the frame, not the moth
(see §5).*

**C1-5. Shacklet → Restreint** *(Umbral/Chrome — Evangelion register)* — a large,
gentle beast **born inside** a shell of grown restraint-armour: bands and plates it
grew itself, which keep its enormous strength company so it doesn't have to think
about it. **Restreint** calms instantly at its keeper's voice; separated and
frightened, it doesn't rage — it *searches*, which at its size is enough of a
problem. *Tone law:* the bio-mecha drama is trust and panic, never horror, never
gore. *Evolution note:* the armour is never removed in either stage; learning to
unclench one plate at a time *is* its character arc.

**C1-6. Masklet → Sigilord** *(Beast/Chrome — Bionicle register)* — a tribal
creature that grows a carved shell-mask for each role it takes in life — tide-mask,
ember-mask, road-mask — and sheds the old one onto the tribe's mask-tree. A
**Sigilord** is an elder wearing the mask of masks, a layered shell of every role
it has ever held. *Design note:* masks are grown chitin with original carvings —
power here is social and ritual, never technological. (No donor mask shapes or
names carried.)

**C1-7. The Gestalt three — Ratchetta + Coilcoon + Dingbat → Gestaltitan**
*(Chrome/Volt — combining register)* — three small species who are, separately, a
ratchet-furred cat, a spring-tailed raccoon, and a bell-bodied bat — and who can,
by agreement, **combine** into the towering **Gestaltitan** for exactly one job.
Afterwards they separate and argue about whose fault the posture was. *Design
note:* combining is the R4 signature move; separation and the argument are part of
the species, not a footnote.

### C2. Animals Pokémon has not used — verified (12)

*Each animal below was checked against the full official roster before being claimed
unused; method and sources in §5. "Verified open" = no official Pokémon is based on
the animal as of Gen 9.*

**C2-1. Capup → Chillbara** *(Aqua/Flora — capybara ✓ open)* — a capybara pup that
is born unbothered; the final **Chillbara** is the waterbank's self-appointed
grandfather, a warm island every small creature naps against. Its signature calm is
contagious at the level of game feel: things near it slow down and mean it.
*Evolution note:* growth is measured in how many other species are asleep on it.

**C2-2. Punchrimp → Rainbrawler** *(Aqua/Beast — mantis shrimp ✓ open)* — a thumb-
sized shrimp whose punch boils the water in front of it; the final **Rainbrawler**
wears the full rainbow carapace and sees colours nobody else in the region can —
it describes them, patiently, to anyone who sits still. ⚠ *Flag: Pokémon's
Clauncher is a* pistol *shrimp — a different animal with a different mechanism
(see §5).*

**C2-3. Tunlet → Tardigreat** *(Beast — tardigrade ✓ open)* — a microscopic-class
water bear, rendered at Mochi scale: a plump barrel-bodied bear-cub of a thing
whose answer to every extreme — drought, frost, the Rift's weird weather — is to
curl into its **tun** form and wait, smiling, for a better century. The final
**Tardigreat** is not stronger than the pup. It is simply still there.
*Evolution note:* the only line whose evolution is a rumour other species tell.

**C2-4. Tapaye → Ayeerie** *(Umbral/Beast — aye-aye ✓ open)* — a night forager that
taps dead wood with its thin ball-jointed finger and listens with ears like two
open doors; the final **Ayeerie** is the Dark Forest's counterpart one region over:
huge-eyed, gentle, and universally believed to be a ghost by species who haven't
met one. *Evolution note:* the finger lengthens; the listening deepens.

**C2-5. Fossle → Slashfoss** *(Beast — fossa ✓ open)* — Madagascar's cat-that-isn't:
a low, long, tree-running predator pup; the final **Slashfoss** moves through
canopy like weather and lands without a sound anyone can point to. *Note:* the
fossa's cat-like read is a different family from canon's Kittember line (a domestic
fire cat) — flagged in §5 so the owner can judge the distance himself.

**C2-6. Okapup → Rainokapi** *(Flora — okapi ✓ open)* — the forest giraffe: a shy
pup with rain-striped legs; the final **Rainokapi** is said to appear only in the
hour after rain, when its stripes are still wet and visible; in dry weather it is
a rumour with hooves. *Evolution note:* the stripes arrive with the first storm it
survives.

**C2-7. Casslet → Helmbeak** *(Beast/Gale — cassowary ✓ open)* — a cassowary chick
with a nub of a casque; the final **Helmbeak** wears the full helmet crest and one
raptor claw, and the whole region agrees on the etiquette: you don't approach it
front-on, you don't need to, it is busy. *Temperament:* respected, unbothered,
absolute. (A deliberate mirror to Chillbara's calm — his is social, Helmbeak's is
armed.)

**C2-8. Kakapoof → Barrelboom** *(Flora/Gale — kakapo ✓ open)* — a round, flightless
night parrot that smells of honey and old books; the final **Barrelboom** is a
walking barrel of a bird whose booming call is felt in the ribs of everyone in the
valley — the region's natural festival drum. (Pairs with R3's festival register
without living there.) *Evolution note:* it never flies. It has never once minded.

**C2-9. Wombit → Burrowbrick** *(Terra — wombat ✓ open)* — a wombat pup that digs
like a rumour spreads; the final **Burrowbrick** is famous for the wombat's real
party trick, perfect cube droppings — which it builds with: little brick walls,
burrow doors, one-upmanship. *Evolution note:* the region's only architect whose
bricks are also a punchline; the design plays it completely straight, which is why
it works.

**C2-10. Narwhit → Tuskicorn** *(Aqua/Frost — narwhal ✓ open)* — a small whale
calf with a bud of a tusk; the final **Tuskicorn** carries the full spiral — which
in this world is a *sense organ*: it tastes the water's memories, and it navigates
by stories other whales told the sea. *Evolution note:* the unicorn read is earned
by the folklore, not the silhouette — it's a whale first, a wonder second.

**C2-11. Vitrel → Clearibbit** *(Aqua/Flora — glass frog ✓ open)* — a tadpole-clear
froglet; the final **Clearibbit** is a glass frog whose transparent belly shows its
heart, beating, for anyone trusted enough to be shown. It hides by becoming the
leaf's window. ⚠ *Flag: frogs are Pokémon-crowded (Poliwag, Toxicroak, Bellibolt…)
— the seed is transparency-as-trust, a mechanism none of them use (see §5).*

**C2-12. Velvlet → Slimelasso** *(Flora — velvet worm ✓ open)* — a soft, many-legged
velvet worm that hunts by firing twin strands of quick-setting slime — a living
lasso; the final **Slimelasso** is the region's gentlest predator and its best
rescuer: the same slime that catches dinner catches falling climbers. *Evolution
note:* an ancient body plan (older than insects, older than fish stories) that
never needed improving — R4's quietest flex.

### C3. Insects & small crawlers beyond the standard set (4)

**C3-1. Termight → Cathedrall** *(Terra/Chrome working labels — termites ✓ open as
a designed animal; Pokémon has ants, not termites)* — a pale termite worker; the
line is a colony that builds **cathedral mounds** with chimneys, arches, and one
tall spire the region navigates by. The final "stage" is the colony-mound itself
gaining a queen-heart, **Cathedrall**. *Design note:* R3's colony-promotion idea
(B6) is social; this one is architectural — the evolution is a building.

**C3-2. Silvlet → Bookwyrm** *(Chrome — silverfish ✓ open)* — a quicksilver sliver
of a bug that lives in libraries and eats paste, dust, and the glue of old spines;
the final **Bookwyrm** is the archive's pest and mascot — it has read everything
and will not discuss endings. *Region fit:* R4's institute region needs an archive
haunter; this is it.

**C3-3. Stinklet → Perfumarch** *(Flora — shield/stink bugs ✓ open)* — a shield bug
whose scent glands make, against all expectation, **perfume**. The final
**Perfumarch** is the region's perfumer and knows it: it processes in a cloud of
its own best work, very proud, slightly hard to be near. *Temperament:* artist.

**C3-4. Bomblet → Chemcannon** *(Flame/Chrome — bombardier beetle ✓ open as a
mechanism)* — a small beetle carrying two harmless sips in separate tanks; mixed,
they pop — boiling, loud, harmless at range. The final **Chemcannon** is the
festival's noisemaker and the region's tiniest artillery, hired for weddings.
⚠ *Flag: beetles are Pokémon-crowded — the seed is the two-chamber chemistry,
which no Pokémon beetle uses (see §5).*

### C4. Yokai, oni, spirits & demons (7)

*Pokémon's yokai chairs are occupied (kitsune, kappa, baku, yuki-onna, tengu,
nekomata, kamaitachi — treated as taken, §5). This bucket goes to the ones it
hasn't taken.*

**C4-1. Hopbrella** *(Gale/Umbral — karakasa-obake)* — a one-eyed, one-legged
umbrella spirit that hops after rain, sticking out a very long tongue at people
who forgot theirs. It doesn't curse anyone. It *lends itself*, pointedly, and
expects to be returned. *Single-stage:* some yokai arrive finished.

**C4-2. Boltcloth → Wraproll** *(Gale — ittan-momen)* — a roll of living cotton
cloth that flies at dusk; folklore says it smothers — this one **wraps**: lost
travellers wake up tucked into a warm white bolt with one corner folded like a
pillow. The final **Wraproll** is ten metres of flying hospitality. *Evolution
note:* it evolves by being thanked enough times. (Nobody has counted. It's a lot.)

**C4-3. Boneling → Skelloss** *(Umbral — gashadokuro, re-toned)* — a small bone-
sprite that collects lost buttons and teeth; the final **Skelloss** is a gentle
giant assembled from a forgotten meadow's worth of bones, ringing faintly like
distant bells, who wants exactly one thing: for somebody to remember the story
of the place it came from. *Tone law:* melancholy, never horror.

**C4-4. Nuelet → Chimerune** *(Umbral/Beast — nue)* — a patchwork pup: monkey
face, tanuki body, tiger legs, a snake for a tail, and a night-bird's cry. The
final **Chimerune** appears before storms of *feeling* — arguments, festivals,
reunions — and its cry tells Rangers which kind is coming. ⚠ *Flag: Pokémon's
Silvally is an artificial chimera — this is a natural folklore one, and the
mechanics are opposite (born, not built) (see §5).*

**C4-5. Amabit → Prophescale** *(Aqua — amabie)* — a shining-scaled sea sprite
with three tail-fins and kind eyes; the final **Prophescale** surfaces once, to
deliver one true and useful sentence, and leaves before anyone can ask a second
question. Rangers keep a book of its sentences. Most pages are weather.

**C4-6. Smoothsea → Monkalm** *(Aqua/Umbral — umibozu, re-toned)* — a smooth black
dome in a flat-calm sea: a bald sea-monk the size of weather. It rises when the
water goes mirror-still. Panic brings waves; a calm answer to its one low riddle
earns a ferry ride on its back. *Evolution note:* Smoothsea is the dome; Monkalm
is the dome *plus the face*, which is worse and better.

**C4-7. Barrelimp → Festivoni** *(Flame — oni)* — a thumb-high imp that lives in
sake barrels; the final **Festivoni** is a big red oni who brews thunder-sake,
arm-wrestles storms for practice, and judges the region's festivals on volume.
⚠ *Double flag (see §5): Pokémon's Ogerpon occupies the oni chair (a masked ogre
child with type-changing masks — this is a grown festival brewer, a different
design entirely); and R3 already holds the owner-directed Volcano Oni anchor —
Festivoni is a* different *oni for a different region, a drinker where the anchor
is a volcano. If the owner wants only one oni in the world, this is the concept
to cut first — it's flagged, not hidden.*

### C5. Cryptids (4)

**C5-1. Tsuchlet → Sakebelly** *(Terra — tsuchinoko)* — the fat snake of legend: it
talks (in cries nobody can prove weren't words), it lies charmingly, it loves
sake, and it can jump *twice* in the air, which is one more jump than is fair.
Nobody has ever caught one; it bribes pursuers with excellent directions.
⚠ *Flagged hard (see §5): Pokémon's Dunsparce/Dudunsparce IS the tsuchinoko —
but theirs is a silent drill-tailed burrower; every folklore trait that makes the
tsuchinoko itself (speech, lying, sake, the double jump) is the part they didn't
use, and it is exactly this concept's design seed. Included because the owner
named the tsuchinoko class himself; the flag rides with it.*

**C5-2. Mothlet → Omenmoth** *(Umbral/Gale — Mothman)* — a grey moth-cub with lamp
eyes; the final **Omenmoth** is tall, silent, red-eyed, and seen on bridges before
storms. It doesn't cause the storm. It is *warning* about the storm, and it is
tired of the misunderstanding. ⚠ *Flag: moths are Pokémon-crowded — none of them
is a bipedal omen with a grievance (see §5).*
