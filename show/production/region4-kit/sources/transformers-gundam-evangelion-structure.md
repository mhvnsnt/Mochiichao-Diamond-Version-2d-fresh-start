## TRANSFORMERS — body machinery
- The mecha are not piloted: they are ALIVE. A "self-configuring
  modular robotic lifeform" carries its identity in a SPARK (a soul-
  core); the body is rearranged at will between two (or more) complete
  forms — person-form and vehicle/beast-form. Transformation is a
  species trait, not a device.
- Society is split into two ancient FACTIONS defined by philosophy
  (protection vs. domination), and the war is a civil war exported to
  other worlds — every local conflict is a front of the same war.
- Disguise is structural: the second form is how the giants live among
  smaller peoples unnoticed — form choice is social strategy.

## GUNDAM — war-machine politics
- The "real robot" turn: mobile suits are WEAPONS — mass-produced,
  maintained by crews, limited by fuel/ammunition/terrain, piloted by
  soldiers inside a cockpit in the torso. War is logistics and politics
  first; the duel is the visible tip.
- Two state powers (a federation and a breakaway principality) fight a
  total war with atrocities on the record; pilots are teenagers because
  the war consumes everyone. Aces become propaganda assets and are
  caged, used, or discarded by their own side.
- NEWTYPE machinery: prolonged life in the new environment is evolving
  a minority of humans — expanded awareness, near-telepathic empathy,
  the ability to feel an enemy pilot's intent. The new sense is the
  war's hope and its next weapon; institutions study and exploit it.
- Cockpit intimacy: the machine only fully answers one pilot; pilot
  and suit are written as a relationship — trust, grief when a suit is
  lost, the suit "remembering" its pilot (later systems literally
  retain pilot imprints).

## EVANGELION — bio-mecha horror + institute secrecy
- The mecha are ORGANIC: giant cloned beings wearing armour that is
  really RESTRAINT — the machine's true nature is a living thing the
  institute holds down, and "piloting" is a psychic synchronisation
  between the pilot's nervous system and the being (via a capsule
  cockpit in the spine, immersed in a breathable liquid). Sync ratio
  is the measurable skill; when sync breaks, the being can go BERSERK
  — it fights on its own, savagely, and the institute's control is
  revealed as a lease, not ownership.
- The pilots must be CHILDREN of a specific profile; the selection
  institute's criteria are classified, and the reason (the beings
  carry souls tied to the pilots) is the story's buried core.
- THE INSTITUTE IS A FRONT: a public defence agency layered over
  shell companies and a predecessor body, executing a hidden agenda
  (an engineered end-of-world/transcendence project) directed from a
  secret council's scriptural timetable. Staff loyalty is compartmental
  — most employees serve the cover story in good faith. Revelations
  arrive as personnel learn which floor of the building they were
  really on.
- The enemy's force field can only be cancelled by the same field —
  the beings are the only weapons against the threat because they ARE
  the threat's kind. Defence and apocalypse share one biology.
