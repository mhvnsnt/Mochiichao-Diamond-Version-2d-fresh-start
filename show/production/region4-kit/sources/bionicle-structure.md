## World machinery
- THE WORLD IS A BODY: the whole setting is the inside of a planet-sized
  robot — the "Great Spirit" — whose people live within him, tending
  cities that are his organs/systems. The inhabitants do NOT know this
  for most of the saga; they worship the Great Spirit as a god. When he
  is struck into a coma, the world itself sickens; the final quest is
  to wake him. (This is the single closest published rhyme to
  Mochiichao's THE SECRET: a machine-world whose people must not know.)
- THREE CASTES, one ladder: villagers (the prime populace) → heroes
  (elemental guardians sworn to protect the villagers) → elders (former
  heroes, now storytellers and law-keepers; the elders are the only
  ones who remember the old world accurately — knowledge is rationed
  by caste and by story).
- MASKS ARE THE POWER INTERFACE: every person wears a mask; masks
  grant one defined power each (shield, speed, levitation, water-
  breathing…). A hero without a mask is weakened; mask-quests (finding
  the full set, one per power) structure whole arcs. Masks are earned,
  traded, lost, and forged — an entire economy of faces.
- SIX ELEMENTAL TRIBES: fire, water, air, earth, ice, stone — each
  with its own region of the island, temperament, and dialect of
  custom; secondary elements (light, gravity, lightning) are rare and
  arrive late, as escalation.
- ONE SUBSTANCE ECONOMY: everything — land, bodies, tools — is one
  fundamental material in different states (solid, liquid, *energised*
  — the energised state destroys or TRANSFORMS whatever touches it;
  transformation is dangerous and uncontrolled).
- MAINTENANCE SWARMS: a mechanical cleaning crew exists whose sealed
  purpose is to strip the world's surface bare when the Spirit wakes —
  the world's own upkeep is an apocalypse if triggered early.
- Moral code is three words long, taught to children: a unity virtue,
  a duty virtue, a destiny virtue. The saga's politics all reduce to
  who interprets the three words.
