## Reading the map
- **R2** is built mainly by Digimon (games + anime shapes) on a
  Pokémon-bank fabric, with his own shows supplying the writing craft
  and the transplant pool supplying faces (Pantera).
- **R3** is built mainly by Monster Rancher machinery on Pokémon-bank
  fabric, crowned by an owner-directed transplant (the Susano Oni)
  and the Mochichao Festival — the one region whose centrepiece is a
  *celebration*, so its donor set skews festival/crowd/festival-sport.
- **R4** is the fusion region by law: the three game traditions plus
  the six-part mecha register in R4-FLAVOR-LAW.md, on Pokémon-bank
  fabric, with the Institute written from the Evangelion floor-plan
  and the Rift's physics from Bakugan's two-cores shape.
- **R5 stays owner-TBD** in every row by design: today's laws imply
  the fusion continues (the Grand Tournament and The Front are
  already in canon), but no donor assignments are made for R5 until
  the owner speaks — Season 1 is R1 and Season 2 may still be R1.
