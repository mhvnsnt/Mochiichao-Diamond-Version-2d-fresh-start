# #325 — RECORD & PORTRAIT FIDELITY → the device shows *your* one, truthfully, at the tier you earned

**The gap this fills.** The Mochiioteca's art is
species art. But a kept individual is not the
species: it has a variation (#322), it may carry a
ruled deviation (#318), and a portrait sitting
already owes it the truth — #196's canon anchors
carry #80's schema ruling verbatim: a registered
deviation's consistency is "MUST REPRODUCE in every
generation of this individual." What no package has
done is generalise that order from the portrait
studio to **every surface that renders an
individual**. This spec is the generalisation.

**A citation correction, made where it lands.**
The portrait rule lives in **#196** (Portrait &
Photo Studio), not #195 (#195 is seasonal world
events). #196 is also where the seated rule's own
citation of #80 was verified in this build.
Registered in STAGING.

## Native spec — `mochiichao/record-fidelity/v1` (fragment beside this doc)

- **The fidelity law.** Once an individual is
  **kept** (and, for a met-but-not-kept individual,
  once its record exists in the Ranger's history),
  every rendering of *that individual* renders
  **that individual**:
  - its #322 variation snapshot (markings as placed,
    proportions as derived) — rendered **from the
    record**, never re-derived at draw time against
    a retuned table;
  - its #318 ruled deviation, where one exists —
    reproduced exactly, including **absence-class**
    deviations: the maskless Verdilance's record art
    shows **no shield**, at every surface, forever.
    A renderer that "completes" him with the species
    standard part has committed the precise error
    the register exists to prevent.
- **The surfaces bound (closed list at founding):**
  the Mochiioteca individual page (the kept tier of
  #207's derivation), the party/summary views,
  portraits and the photo album (#196/#60 — a
  portrait is a shutter record of a subject, and the
  subject is the individual), encounter frames for
  recognised individuals (#319), and Garden/follower
  presentation (#58/#65 seats read the individual's
  own read, not the species sprite, where the
  individual is the subject).
- **The tiers, against #207's axes.** Fidelity is
  earned by discovery tier, and the tiers are
  #207's own states doing the gating:
  - **unseen** — nothing renders but the species
    silhouette (below);
  - **seen** — species art + the name: a *species*
    truth. An individual met in the wild is rendered
    in the encounter by its own variation (#322 is
    encounter presentation), but the *record page*
    at seen tier still shows the species — the
    Ranger has seen the kind, and glimpsed one of
    them;
  - **kept** — the individual page opens, and from
    that moment the fidelity law binds in full: this
    page, and every later surface, shows **this
    one**.
- **Silhouettes stay species-level. (A law, with
  its reason stated.)** The silhouette axis (#197's
  lock, #207's presentation) renders the **species**
  — never an individual. A silhouette of the
  maskless Verdilance would leak the deviation
  before any Ranger has earned the meeting, and
  would make the silhouette a per-individual asset
  factory besides. Individual truth appears **only**
  at the tier that earned it: the silhouette is the
  species' shadow; the kept page is the individual's
  face.
- **The wild side of the same law.** A recognised
  individual met again wild (#319) renders as
  itself in the encounter — variation, deviation and
  all — because the meeting's subject is the
  individual. An *unrecognised* wild individual
  renders by its variation snapshot alone (it is
  visibly itself; the Ranger simply doesn't know
  its name yet).

Law seated beside #207 (its tiers do the gating)
and #196 (its home rule, generalised — the

#58/#65 presentation seats. Writes: nothing — this
is a rendering law over records that exist.
