# R4 FOLKLORE METHOD — folklore-to-creature process (List 7, #63)

The method by which Region 4's spirit/folklore slice (and any
later folklore line) goes from a tradition to a Mochiichao.
Region 4's register is canon: mix + originals — yokai, oni,
spirits, demons, cryptids, folklore broadly, all original work
with inspirations. Folklore creatures are public tradition —
centuries of shared telling, no owner; what is *not* public is
any specific modern artist's or franchise's rendition of them.
The method takes the tradition, never a rendition.

## The process (five steps, in order)

1. **Tradition research.** The creature as the folk tradition
   itself carries it: its behaviour, its haunts, its signs, the
   story-shape it appears in (omen, trickster, guardian, warning).
   Sources are the tradition's own record — folklore collections,
   encyclopaedia of the tradition — not a game/anime bestiary.
   Output: a one-paragraph *tradition brief* in our own words.
2. **Trait extraction.** From the brief, extract the *load-bearing
   traits*: the one behaviour and the one shape-idea the tradition
   never drops (a walking hut; a snake-fat omen body; a chimera
   assembled from named animals). Traits are ideas, not drawings.
   If a trait only exists in one modern rendition, it is not a
   tradition trait — drop it.
3. **The Mochiichao turn.** The traits are rebuilt as a Mochiichao
   species: a habitat in our world, a place in an ecology (the
   habitat-first creature standard), a cry, a fight-read. This is
   where the design becomes ours — the tradition supplies the
   seed, the world supplies the creature. Design influences per
   the regional dial; render in the one Mochiichao style.
4. **Naming in house style.** Names follow the canon naming
   pattern (the move-rename law's species twin: punchy, meaning
   preserved, no existing franchise creature names).
5. **Sheet + gate.** Full species data first (data-first law),
   then design under the design gate — silhouette, fight-read,
   style — with the tradition brief filed in the sheet so review
   can check *inspiration vs copying* at a glance.

## The queued lines, read through the method (queue = source)

- **Nuelet → Chimerune** (C4 spirit) — the nue: a chimera of
  named parts with an uncanny cry. Load-bearing traits: assembled
  body, a cry that is itself the omen. (The cry spec does real
  work on this line — trait and apparatus meet.)
- **Tsuchlet → Sakebelly** (C5+C6) — the tsuchinoko: a fat,
  snake-like body, elusive, never quite caught. Traits: barrel
  body, elusiveness — discovery data (rare encounter) is the
  design, as much as the shape.
- **Hutlet → Wanderhut** (C5+C6) — the walking-hut motif of
  Slavic tradition. Trait: the house that walks. Our turn must
  make it a creature of *our* forests — it is not a hut with a
  witch; no character comes with the motif.
- **Barrelimp → Festivoni** (C4) — the oni register: festival-
  brute energy. (The transplant programme's Volcano Oni anchor is
  a *different* pipeline — an owner casting, not a folklore-method
  line; the two must not be merged in the sheets.)
- **Bunyiplet → Bunyawn, Pooklet → Pooknight** (C5+C6) —
  billabong ambusher and shapeshifter traditions respectively;
  traits: the water-haunt, the borrowed shape.

Each already has its data sheet (queue: all HAS DATA). The method
above is the review lens for their designs when the queue reaches
them — it adds no data and changes no names.

## Hard lines

- No copied folklore-character likenesses: no rendition's face,
  costume, colour scheme, or signature prop arrangement comes
  over. If a design reads as *a specific known character*, it
  fails regardless of quality — the same filter as the
  construction-kit rule, applied to tradition.
- Sacred/religious registers (wave-2 plans name ontology/religion
  registers): tradition briefs for those come from the tradition's
  own record with extra care, and any line the owner flags is his
  call — the method's job is to keep the lane honest, not brave.

## Coverage

Method: standard adaptation practice, stated in house terms.
Contents: the five queued line-groups above, from the queue;
wave-2 folklore is planned-not-sheeted and untouched here.
