# PILOT ADAPTATIONS — six finished templates, Mochiichao-written

*House format throughout: scene blocks (VISUAL / ACTION /
DIALOGUE / SUBTITLES / SFX / MUSIC), per the cape-jones shooting-
script standard the EP02–EP05 drafts already follow. Each pilot
names its target episode, its slot in that episode's beat sheet,
the template(s) it builds from, and its subtitle accounting.
Creatures: pure cries only. Humans speak. The Forest is never
entered. Nothing here spends a locked EP01 element.*

---

## P1 — "THE SHELTER" → EP02, new scene between Beat 4 (The Choosing) and Beat 5 (greetings)
**Templates:** S7 (Shelter Offer, Pokémon EP001) + G3 (silence).
**Subtitle accounting:** NONE — EP02's two subtitles are locked
to cuts 16/26 and its third slot is held unspent by the draft's
own law. This scene is the proof the law can hold: the bond
reads in staging alone.
**Canon check:** post-Switch staging — camera at Mochii height;
Mochiko framed as Mochii's person.

**VISUAL:** EXT. GARDEN POND — LATE AFTERNOON. The light going
the colour of shallow water. A weather front the episode has
not mentioned arrives as three fat drops on the Pond, then a
proper sprinkle. The residents take their covers without fuss —
this is weather they know.
**ACTION:** MOCHII does not know it. It startles at the first
drop on its head, bounces in a full circle looking for the
attack, and lands facing MOCHIKO with its whole body doing the
question. Mochiko's hands come up — the keeper's instinct to
gather it in — and she stops them herself, mid-air. Instead she
sits down in the wet grass, opens the capsule backpack, and
holds its flap up like a roof. She says nothing. The rain
speaks. Mochii looks at the flap, at the sky, at her. It climbs
her knee, then her shoulder — its choice, at its speed — and
tucks itself under the flap's edge with only its face out.
Hold. The sprinkle passes in under a minute, the way garden
weather does.
**DIALOGUE:** None.
**SUBTITLES:** None.
**SFX:** rain on leaves, rain on canvas flap, one small
settling cry (mochii-cry.wav, contented variant — performance
TBD), distant gate bell NOT rung.
**MUSIC:** Cue out entirely for the climb; title-track figure
returns, solo, on the hold.

## P2 — "THE ESCORT PACKS THREE TIMES" → EP03, opens Beat 2 (The Outing)
**Templates:** E11 (Escalation Ladder, counted) + E4 (scheme
mechanism) + G4 (prop rules — the net-hat accrues its rule).
**Subtitle accounting:** NONE (EP03's two stay where the draft
put them).
**Canon check:** Gabu featured; staged from Mochii's
perspective per EP03's locked POV.

**VISUAL:** EXT. GARDEN GATE — MORNING. A line on the grass,
kit-layout style — except the style is Gabu's, which is to say
it is a pile with ambitions.
**ACTION:** GABU packs for the first trip beyond the Gate.
PACKING ONE: the net-hat, worn at a heroic angle, and nothing
else — he is the escort; the hat is the office. He presents
himself to DOROWANI, who looks at the hat, then at the road,
then pointedly at the sun. PACKING TWO: Gabu returns with the
hat PLUS a bundle — rope, a flask, three stones he has
selected personally for throwing-at-things purposes. Dorowani
opens the Manual to the day's page and holds it out. Gabu
reads the page the way he reads everything: briefly, and with
his whole chest. PACKING THREE: Gabu arrives towing the
bundle, the rope, the flask, the stones, a second hat (in case
the first hat is lost to heroism), and a leaf under which all
of it is meant to travel. The leaf collapses. Everything
spills across the Gate line — half in the Garden, half beyond
it, which is where MOCHIKO finds it, and him sitting in the
middle of it, guarding both halves at once. She does not laugh
first. She kneels and starts handing him things in the order
they will actually be needed, and he takes them in that order,
and the pile becomes a kit: hat, flask, rope. The stones stay
in the Garden. He looks at the stones a long moment — then
puts ONE in the kit, on top, like a flag.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "Escort's privilege. One." / GABU:
(gabu-cry.wav, formal — he salutes the stones).
**SUBTITLES:** None.
**SFX:** canvas, rope, stones (three distinct clacks — the
third is the flag), leaf-collapse whoomph.
**MUSIC:** A small packing rhythm built from the SFX; it
resolves into the outing cue as the kit closes.
**Payoff note (for the episode's later beats):** the ONE stone
is the flaw-as-weapon — it is thrown, once, at the exact
moment the ring game's rule needs a marker beyond the Gate
line. The hat's new rule (G4 ledger): *the escort's hat
carries one passenger* — owed to a later episode.

## P3 — "THE ESCORT REPORTS" → EP03, Tag/Button (after the return)
**Templates:** E7 (Interview Segment) + R1 (Late-Night Desk).
**Subtitle accounting:** spends EP03's THIRD and final
subtitle (the draft carries two of a max three; this is the
one the ration was saving).
**Canon check:** Dorowani translates by reaction, never by
subtitle — except the segment's one, which is the punchline
and the feeling at once.

**VISUAL:** EXT. GARDEN POND — DUSK. The flat stone at the
Pond's edge, dressed by nobody: it is simply where Dorowani
sits with the Manual closed beside it, which makes it a desk.
GABU takes the guest side with ceremony, net-hat straight.
**ACTION:** Dorowani opens the Manual to a blank page — the
interview record. QUESTION ONE, delivered as a low, formal cry
and a fin-streak inclined toward the road: *the journey out.*
Gabu reports at length. It is a cry with chapters. Dorowani
nods in the wrong places with total gravity. QUESTION TWO —
the fin inclines toward the Garden: *the journey home.* Gabu's
report is shorter and entirely different in shape; at one
point he stands, removes the hat, and holds it against his
chest, reporting on the stone. Dorowani writes nothing; the
Manual's page stays blank; the interview is the record.
QUESTION THREE — Dorowani looks up from the blank page and
asks it with its whole face, and Gabu answers before the
question is finished, both of them already knowing.
**DIALOGUE:** None (cries only — dorowani-cry.wav formal
register; gabu-cry.wav, chapters).
**SUBTITLES:** ONE — over Gabu's answer to question three:
*"…The road is longer when you're the escort."*
**SFX:** Pond evening, a pen that is never used set down
once, hat against chest (soft canvas).
**MUSIC:** One sting of the "desk theme" — three notes of the
title track played like a signature — then out.

## P4 — "COMMENTARY" → EP04, inside Beat 3 (Preparation)
**Templates:** E12 (Broadcast Frame) + S5 (Training Montage)
+ G10 (cry-motif as rhythm track).
**Subtitle accounting:** NONE.
**Canon check:** EP04's bout custom — stakes are honour and a
board mark, never harm; the frame serves the custom, not a
scoreboard.

**VISUAL:** EXT. GARDEN POND (RING) — LATE AFTERNOON. The ring
measured to Hex's stride. Two warm-up areas, staged as a
broadcast would stage them: HEX and DOROWANI frame-left in
hard, clean light; MOCHIKO and MOCHII frame-right in Pond
light, wet at the edges.
**ACTION:** The warm-ups intercut as EVENTS, and the Garden
supplies the commentary the only way it can: the residents
watch in a row, and their attention is the broadcast. EVENT
ONE — FORM: Dorowani runs its drill; every turn finishes in a
picture (PRESENT — Tension), fin-streaks sweeping white; the
watching row holds its breath on 2s/3s; Kittember's tail-tip
flame leans toward the grace like a plant toward sun. EVENT
TWO — THE SPLASH: Mochii's warm-up is water play, scored by
nobody, and its cry-motif — bounce, bounce, splash — becomes
the sequence's rhythm track; Hex watches it the way a
metronome watches weather. EVENT THREE — THE MEASURE: Hex
re-measures the ring, finds it true, and — for the first time
in the episode — smiles at something that is not theirs:
Mochii has fallen asleep mid-bounce, upright, in the shallows,
entirely ready by its own philosophy. Dorowani, without
looking away from its drill, extends one fin and shades it.
The commentary row says nothing. The frame holds.
**DIALOGUE:** HEX: "That is not a warm-up." / AEON (mild,
from the commentary row): "No. It's a philosophy. You'll
meet it in the ring."
**SUBTITLES:** None.
**SFX:** fin-streak air, water play, one measured stride
repeated, a small snore.
**MUSIC:** The cry-motif rhythm (G10) under Event Two only;
Events One and Three play dry.

## P5 — "FIRST LIGHT ROUNDS" → EP05, the dawn sequence (Beat 2 hinge)
**Templates:** S2 (Quiet Two-Hander) + G15 (Gag Withdrawal)
+ EP039's hero-as-disturbance in miniature.
**Subtitle accounting:** NONE — EP05's subtitles are spent on
Wispkin and the lantern line; the dawn must earn its feeling
without one. This is the pilot that proves it can.
**Canon check:** Sproutle featured at dawn (rotation); the
Treeline seen only from the Garden side; the Forest never
entered; Wispkin on shift in background, per draft.

**VISUAL:** EXT. GARDEN GREEN / POND EDGE — DAWN, after the
third night. Frost on the grass in a pattern the episode has
been teaching us: not a blanket — a PATH. The chalk line at
the Pond, a hand's width of cold beyond it. SPROUTLE is awake
before anyone, standing at the frost path's start the way you
stand at the edge of cold water.
**ACTION:** Mochiko comes out with the rounds basket and her
sleepless face. She means to begin the rounds. Sproutle steps
— deliberately, a whole ceremony of a step — ONTO the frost
path, and looks back at her. Not an invitation she can refuse:
an instruction. She follows at Sproutle's pace, which is the
garden's slowest honest speed, and the round becomes a guided
action (S2's law: the creature SHOWS). The frost path runs
past the Pond's cold spot and does not stop there — it bends,
thin as a hair, toward the Treeline side, and thins out
before it arrives. Sproutle stops where it stops. It does not
look at the Forest. It looks at Mochiko until she sees what
it is showing her: the path is a ROUND. Somebody walks it
nightly. It has a shape, and the shape has a keeper's logic to
it. Mochiko kneels and — badly, then adequately — chalks a
small mark of her own beside the path: *noted. Not entered.
Noted.* Behind them, unnoticed, WISPKIN completes its own
round and pauses where the mark is, a moment longer than its
pace allows. (G15: no gag has occurred since the scene began.
Nobody remarks on that. The audience feels it.)
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO (quiet, to Sproutle): "…You walk the
edges first. I should have been walking the edges first."
**SUBTITLES:** None.
**SFX:** frost underfoot (two weights, two sounds), chalk,
the Garden's morning starting up around them like an
orchestra remembering itself.
**MUSIC:** None until the chalk mark; then one figure, low,
which the night score will answer in Beat 3.

## P6 — "THE KEEPER DOES THE ROUNDS" → EP05, hinge scene into Beat 3 (Investigation)
**Templates:** E14 (Forced-Evolution Bond Crisis, re-skinned:
care-loop gaming → rhythm corruption) + S2's closing law.
**Subtitle accounting:** NONE (see P5).
**Canon check:** no evolution mechanics imported — the
corruption is RHYTHM, the Garden's own system; Rule 12 is
staged as its shadow side and NOT quoted.

**VISUAL:** INT./EXT. GARDEN — THE ROUNDS, THREE QUICK PASSES
over one day. The keeper device in Mochiko's hand: light and
icons only (device stays abstract per bible §9.5).
**ACTION:** PASS ONE — Mochiko, who has not slept, does the
morning rounds TWICE. Once for the day, once "for the night
coming." The device chimes its approval both times. The
residents accept the second feeding the way you accept
weather. PASS TWO — she walks WISPKIN's cold path in daylight,
checking it, tidying it — moving the chalk line a hand's width
OUT, "to give it room." She annotates the board. She is, in
every visible way, an excellent keeper working very hard.
PASS THREE — DUSK: the Garden's rhythm audibly wrong in small
ways. Gabu is standing guard at the wrong post, guarding the
gate that is already guarded. Kittember's flame leans toward
nothing. The Pond's cold spot, given room, has taken it. And
Wispkin — whose round Mochiko walked, whose chalk line Mochiko
moved — begins its shift at the line SHE drew, stops, and
floats back to the TRUE line, which only it can see, and
stands there not beginning. The round does not start while a
stranger's mark says it should start elsewhere. Mochiko
watches her own good intentions hold the night shut. She goes
out with the chalk rubber herself — no basket, no device —
and rubs her line out, kneeling in the cold grass, while
Wispkin watches keeper to keeper. The round begins. Only then
does she ask — plainly, as one professional to another, the
question Beat 3 exists to ask.
**DIALOGUE:** MOCHIKO: "I did your job at it all day. I'm
sorry. — Walk me through your round. Properly. I'll keep
your pace."
**SUBTITLES:** None in this scene (Beat 3's first subtitle —
*"…Three nights. I counted."* — lands minutes later, and
this scene is why it will land at full weight).
**SFX:** the device chime, twice too often; chalk; chalk
rubbed OUT (the same sound, reversed in feeling); the
night's first cricket, on time again.
**MUSIC:** The care-loop figure, played correctly in Pass
One, correctly-and-again in Pass Two (it curdles), absent in
Pass Three until the round begins — then the night score,
right and unhurried.
