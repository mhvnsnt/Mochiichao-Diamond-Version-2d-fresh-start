# SOURCE CATALOGUE — Mochiichao Show Adaptation-Source Library

*Built 2026-10-27 under the owner's standing method: source material
is CONSTRUCTION KIT — structures, beat shapes, gag mechanisms,
pipelines and reference slots are adapted and re-skinned into
Mochiichao canon. Source characters, character names, and
logos/branding are NEVER carried over. Iconic lines are flagged,
never lifted (see DO-NOT-ADAPT.md). House law: gag mechanisms,
not quips.*

*Ratings: ★★★ = build episodes from it now · ★★ = strong second
wave · ★ = study/reference only · GAP = nothing actually there.*

### B1. 11-minute gag engines — SpongeBob SquarePants + Regular Show
- **Sources:** Encyclopedia SpongeBobia transcript pages + Regular
  Show Wiki transcript pages (Fandom-hosted, publicly posted).
  Sampled: SpongeBob "Pizza Delivery" (S1), "The Fry Cook Games"
  (S2); Regular Show "Just Set Up the Chairs" (S1), "High Score" (S2).
  Notes: sources/gag-engines/ (19,733 bytes total).
- **What it holds:** complete 11-minute episode architecture for the
  two dominant gag-engine shapes — mundane-task escalation (7
  counted escalation steps in BOTH sampled Regular Show episodes and
  in "Pizza Delivery") and the competition/game episode (broadcast
  frame, event ladder, tie contrivance, sentimental stop).
- **Adaptation value: ★★★** — escalation COUNTS are the transferable
  gold: a Mochiichao chore episode can be built to a 5–7 step ladder
  with a flaw-as-weapon reversal at the top. The competition grammar
  (deadpan broadcast over catastrophe, events scored by a house rule)
  feeds E8 directly. Regular Show's respect-as-currency tracking is
  a ready-made stakes system for Hex episodes.

### B2. Pokémon anime (the base show of the style stack)
- **Sources:** Pokémon Wiki (Fandom) per-episode `Transcript/
  International` pages — full public transcripts, verified dense
  for the original series (80 Indigo League transcript pages in
  the index category; later series present, thinner). Bulbapedia
  episode pages used as the cross-check layer (plot, major events,
  trivia, dub edits — Bulbapedia does NOT host transcripts).
  Deep samples: EP001 (bond/training premiere), EP058 (gym battle,
  part 1 of 2), EP039 (quiet/character, fake-release). Later eras
  (SM/JN/HZ) blurb-checked only — era gap is the honest shortfall.
  Notes: sources/pokemon-anime/ (22,507 bytes).
- **What it holds:** the partner-bond grammar the whole stack
  rests on — the offer-of-shelter turn, the rule-of-three starter
  ceremony, battle-as-three-lessons staging, gag-withdrawal as an
  emotional signal, and the house reference pattern (ONE reference
  per episode, in a throwaway slot, never load-bearing).
- **Adaptation value: ★★★** — Mochiichao's choosing/bond beats are
  already Pokémon-shaped; this cluster supplies the verified
  mechanics (and the do-not-adapt fences) for writing them at
  full strength.
