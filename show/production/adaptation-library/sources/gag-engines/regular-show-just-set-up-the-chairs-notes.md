# Regular Show — "Just Set Up the Chairs" — Structure Notes
Cluster: 11-minute gag-engine cartoons | Type: (a) mundane-task-escalates
Season 1 (paired premiere block). Source: transcript wiki, see SOURCE.md.
STRUCTURE ONLY — no transcript reproduced.

## Cold open
- No teaser: a boss's clipboard roll-call assigns every worker a party
  job in one scene. Status hierarchy is the joke engine — the heroes draw
  the worst job, a side character floats away on balloons, unremarked.

## Premise
Two slackers must set up 50 chairs for a kids' birthday party to prove
they are not slackers. Proving it becomes the catastrophe.

## Escalation — 7 steps after setup
1. Count failure: chairs run out at 38 of 50. A missing-object problem
   (13 chairs) replaces the boring task.
2. Locked-room breach: a door they force open holds not chairs but a
   vault of abandoned arcade machines (temptation replaces task).
3. Distraction montage: a head-to-head streak across joke mini-games
   (mundane-activity games: lemonade stand, hat selling, staring
   contest). The skilled-at-nothing hero wins ten straight.
4. Forbidden object: one machine is sheeted and signed out-of-order;
   an internal note explicitly warns against connecting two wires.
   They connect the wires.
5. Boss entity released: a pixel devil-head exits the game into the
   real park and starts destroying the party infrastructure; every
   parallel subplot collides with it (party bus, bouncy castle, the
   hired entertainment is incinerated).
6. Counter-weapon from Act 2's jokes: the abandoned mini-games are
   combined into one composite fighter, crewed cooperatively — each
   player assigned a body part to control.
7. Task reasserts mid-battle: the missing chairs are spotted inside a
   shed under fire; one hero risks himself for the chairs while the
   other's established flaw (button-mashing) becomes the winning
   weapon and defeats the boss.

## Act shape (~11 min)
- Act 1 (~0-3m): assignments, proving-it pact, counting to 38, parallel
  cutaways (boss in traffic hell; co-workers collect the seedy
  "special entertainment").
- Act 2 (~3-6.5m): arcade discovery, mini-game montage, forbidden
  machine, release.
- Act 3 (~6.5-11m): destruction tour, composite-fighter battle,
  chairs recovered, boss arrives to survey a burning success.

## Gag mechanisms (abstract)
- Celebratory chant loop: a two-person "OOOOOHHH" + a sung taunt about
  never doing the task again — used at the pact, and repeated verbatim
  after total disaster, unchanged by consequences (the loop is the
  character).
- Cutaway comeuppance: cutaways show everyone who mocked the heroes
  suffering their own assignment worse.
- Counting as suspense: chairs counted aloud; the count stopping is
  how the audience learns something is wrong.
- Explicit-warning rule: the more sacred/legalistic the warning note,
  the faster it is discarded on screen.
- Flaw-as-weapon reversal: incompetence established in Act 2 (button
  mashing, chair-robot horseplay) is the exact skill that wins Act 3.
- Ironic-success ending: the literal task IS completed — chairs are
  set up, children seated — inside a ring of fire. Success and
  disaster are framed in the same shot.

## Ending tag
Boss assigns clean-up starting with sweeping up the (incinerated)
entertainment; heroes immediately agree to take a break first.
Status quo restored by attitude, not by repair.

## Reference / parody slots (shape only)
- Arcade iconography as reward: defeated boss collapses into a
  classic fruit bonus worth 100 points, which the composite fighter
  eats. Lands because game logic, not cartoon logic, closes the fight.
- Kaiju/boss-battle grammar staged with office-party props and a
  golf cart as the mobile command platform.

## Do-not-adapt flags (identification only)
- The celebratory chant and the chair-specific sung taunt as worded.
- Boss-entity and composite-fighter names/designs (destroyer head,
  lemon-chef hybrid) and the mini-game titles listed in the vault.
- The boss's gumball-drop boasting line as worded by the side duo.
- Character names/designs and the park workplace setup as cast here.
