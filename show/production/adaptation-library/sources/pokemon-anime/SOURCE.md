# SOURCE.md — Pokémon anime: adaptation-source library
Cluster: Pokémon anime (base show of the Mochiichao style stack).
Method: browser search + page fetch only. **No full transcripts saved** — structure
notes only, per-episode files below. Short excerpts were read on-page; nothing was
copied into the notes beyond paraphrase and beat descriptions.

## Transcript availability verdict
**YES — full public transcripts exist and are openly posted.**
The Pokémon Wiki (Fandom) maintains per-episode "Transcript/International" pages
(English table first, other languages in their own tables). Its Indigo League
transcript category lists **80 transcript pages** (TB001–TB082 with gaps), including
all three episodes sampled here — each was opened and read end-to-end during this
pass. Transcripts for later series exist on the same wiki (Black & White examples
surfaced in search: BW022, BW033, BW049, BW056, BW080, BW081).
Bulbapedia does **not** host full transcripts; it hosts the detailed plot breakdown,
major events, debuts, trivia, errors, and dub edits used here for cross-checking.
Caveat on era spread: the *complete, verified* transcript archive is densest for the
original series, so the three deep samples are all Indigo League (1997–98) but of
three different types. Later-era continuity was spot-checked via Bulbapedia blurbs
(search-snippet level): SM075 "Some Kind of Laziness!" (2018, guardian-creature
training that teaches a new move and strengthens the bond), HZ020 "Kabu's Battle
Training!" (2023, Horizons — gym-leader training contest + multi-battle, lesson about
a trainee yielding for the wrong reasons), HZ062 "A New Song for Fuecoco" (2024 —
training/rematch built around a shared song), JN030 (2020 — partner jealousy /
quiet character premise). The bond-training and quiet-partner templates persist;
their full transcripts were not deep-read in this pass.
