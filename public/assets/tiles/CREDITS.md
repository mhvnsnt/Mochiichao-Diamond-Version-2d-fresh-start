# Environment tiles — credit

Ground tiles in `town/` are from Kenney Tiny Town (kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town),
16x16, Creative Commons CC0 (public domain, no attribution required).
Credit is given here anyway: Kenney (kenney.nl).

Only 4 tiles are shipped in this first step, each verified by viewing the
actual tile image (not guessed from a sheet index):
- tile_0000.png — grass
- tile_0025.png — plain dirt/sand ground
- tile_0043.png — light grass variant (used for tall grass)
- tile_0002.png — grass with flowers (used for flower tiles)

No Pokemon tileset art is shipped here. Pokemon decomp tilesets were used
only for structure (16x16 tiles, metatiles, layers), never for art.

- tile_0126.png — blue brick wall (step 2, used for BUILDING)

- voda-water.png — water tile, sheet grid (0,0)
- voda-grass.png — grass tile, sheet grid (4,1)

Tiles" (cc0waveanimtiles16x16.png, CC0) — it is a wave overlay with a
magenta placeholder area, not a ready water tile, so it was not mapped.
