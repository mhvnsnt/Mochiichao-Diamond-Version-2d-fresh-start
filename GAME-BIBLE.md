# PART I — THE OWNER'S DOCTRINES (2026-10-29)

## 1. EXISTENTIAL CHARGE & THE NUMINOUS (owner-ruled 2026-10-28; placed first by owner order, 2026-10-29)

Places carry a quality of their own, at the level of
existence, before any Ranger crosses them and after they
leave: a place's **existential charge**. **Numinous** —
numinosity — is how that charge *feels* from inside it:
presence, awe, and dread in the ground itself; the old
vocabulary's *genius loci*, a place's own spirit. Both words
are **production-layer only**: they appear in no UI string,
no sign, no Mochiioteca entry, no in-world speech. In-world,
people say what people have always said — a place is
**blessed**, or **haunted**, or **cursed**, or **lucky** —
and that is all anyone says. *This is a big thing about our
show and our game* (the owner's words, 2026-10-29).

- **States × strength:** BLESSED (a presence that shelters)
  / LUCKY (fortune leans in; no watcher implied) / HAUNTED
  (the past pressing in — what happened there has not
  finished happening) / CURSED (fortune leans away, and the
  lean feels *meant*) / UNMARKED (the default; most ground)
  × **faint / settled / deep / peak**.
- **Base charge is canon data** in the place's own record:
  set by the owner or by story, **never rewritten by any
  system**. **Current charge is derived, never stored** —
  the base as drifted by long-arc story events and
  accumulated in-place deeds, capped: drift deepens or
  softens strength, **never flips a state** on player
  action alone, and never founds a settled or deep charge
  on unmarked ground. A flip is a story fact, and story
  facts are the owner's. Unruled places build UNMARKED.
- **Felt, never itemised.** No meter, no tally, no UI
  label, no readout of any charge, ever. Charge effects
  are **fortune + presence only**: leans inside the
  already-derived effective encounter table (never a new
  row, rate, or species), finds and coincidence texture,
  phenomena (never creatures, encounters, or capture
  targets) — never damage, never a blocked path, never a
  battle or capture resolution. Effects are seeded (place +
  world beat + derivation version), reproducible, never
  pure RNG.
- **The acceptance test is the owner's, from firsthand
  play, and it is binding: the player FEELS the magic
  coming out of the setting.** A charge that reads
  correctly in the data and is not felt in the hands has
  failed, however clean its numbers are.
- **The Forest.** The Dark Forest is the most numinous
  ground in the world: a **dual read** — the deepest dread
  and the game's highest luck coexisting — at peak. Base
  charge ONLY: **no drift**; deeds there feed the DREAD
  tallies, never charge — in the Forest the player does
  not move what the place is. Its blessings arrive through
  the DREAD mediation: bent kindness, at first
  indistinguishable from a haunting, never explained by
  anyone, and **never making the Forest safe**. **The
  depths spawn table stays EMPTY (0 rows), and charge
  never touches it.** Charge never explains the Forest,
  the Secret, or what stopped moving.
- **Legendary sites (the peak class).** Maximum charge
  **bound to a one-time event** — a singular encounter,
  item, or rite; the Spear Pillar / Bell Tower / Mt. Pyre
  pattern on the Pokémon base. One time, ever: afterwards
  the charge remains and the event never repeats, never
  re-arms. An event, never a spawn-table entry; nothing
  there is farmable. **The class ships with zero
  instances** — no legendary site exists in canon until
  the owner rules one, and the Dark Forest is
  charge-extreme, NOT a legendary site, unless he says so.
- **Machinery + open items:** PLACE-CHARGE.md +
  `fates-placecharge-fragment.json` (the moral-choices
  package — not yet landed in this repo; workspace seat
  `~/workspace/mochiichao-show/moral-choices-system/`;
  landing owed). Owner slots 41–48 — assignments (41–42),
  drift caps/windows (43), story-event flips/foundings
  (44), the world-memory seam (45), the fortune menu +
  Forest luck ceiling (46), phenomena seats (47),
  legendary sites + the peak band's name (48) — are
  referenced, never filled by a build lane.

## 2. ONE COIN (owner ruling, 2026-10-29)

Pokémon keeps its game and its show as two different coins
— separate continuities that nod at each other. **Ours is
the same coin.** One world, one universe, one canon,
expressed through the game and through the show, built hand
in hand. A ruling made in one lane is a ruling in the other,
in the same terms (Show Bible §5, law 10); a place, a
person, a doctrine means the same thing on both sides.
Where the two sides express a thing differently, the
difference is the medium, never the meaning. That is why
this book stacks the Show Bible inside itself (Part II)
instead of merely citing it.

## 3. PRESENCE-FIRST (owner doctrine, 2026-10-28; set at bible level, 2026-10-29)

**Presence is created FIRST — in lighting, colour, and
scenery — and identity is revealed only when it is earned
and meant to be seen.** Every reveal in the game works this
way: Lavender Town's unidentified ghost is the small
version; the pattern is the whole game's. Presence-first is
the reveal grammar of existential charge (§1), of
foreshadowing (§5), and of the Syndicate's presentation
(the Persona UI dialect is a presence before it is a
person). If a scene names or explains a thing before the
player has *felt* it in the light and the ground, the scene
is built wrong.

## 4. THE LIGHTING LAWS (owner rulings, 2026-10-29)

- **The world clock owns the time-of-day tint.** One
  producer of phase and tint; no system computes its own.
  (The legacy producers in the canvas and tilesets collapse
  into the clock — the audit lane owns the collapse.)
- **Tint is OUTDOORS-ONLY.** The global day/night grade
  applies when the player is outside. It never washes a
  whole interior.
- **Indoors, daylight enters only through windows** — as
  real light rays: shafts and window-shaped floor patches
  whose angle, length, and colour derive from the clock's
  phase (dawn long and warm from the east; noon short and
  bright; dusk longest and red from the west; night faint
  moonlight only where a window faces it, otherwise none).
  The construction is the handheld craft (Nintendo DS /
  Switch / Switch 2 class, built to a mobile budget): a
  floor patch shaped like the window, two or three
  translucent shaft cards, dust motes in the beam. No
  volumetrics. Windows are **map data** — apertures
  declared when an interior is built, in the same change as
  the window art.
- **Every interior declares its lighting class: WINDOWED /
  LOCAL / MIXED / DARK.** WINDOWED: daylight by apertures
  only. LOCAL: windowless — authored local sources only
  (lamps and their kin are takeover-class sources under the
  LIGHTING-SYSTEM). MIXED: both, by the same rules each.
  DARK: caves and depths — no daylight and no tint at all;
  dark caves run the **Pokémon Flash pattern** (a carried
  light verb opens dark ground; the verb's name and how it
  is earned are **owner slots — never invented**).
  Acceptance test of record: the Cave of Echoes basement
  shows zero daylight objects at every phase.
- **Protected fills hold.** Environment colour rides rims,
  atmosphere, and shadow; it never re-hues a character's
  fills (LIGHTING-SYSTEM LS-1). The one exception is a
  different machine: the colorway shift (Show Bible §5A;
  COLORWAY-LIBRARY) — do not confuse the two.
- **The dramatic grade composes on top** of everything in
  this section (§5), and its two location laws stand: the
  Garden Gate never shifts — safety is its job — and the
  Dark Forest's keys are "Refusal" and "No Key": there, the
  light is withheld.

## 5. THE EXTENDED-GRADE FOREST & FORESHADOWING (owner rulings, 2026-10-29)

- **One specific forest carries the dramatic (JoJo) grade
  as its standing state.** It is **not** the Dark Forest
  (the forest formerly called Binary Woods — one place, two
  names; its Refusal law is untouched). It is a different
  forest, built around **after the third city**, tied into
  the story being built; its name and story tie are owner
  slots unless canon already names them.
- **Behaviour:** on entry the grade shifts dramatically
  over **about one second**; it **holds the whole time the
  player is inside — overworld and battles fought there**;
  on exit, once the player is out, the world returns to
  normal.
- **Layer order inside the forest:** the clock still runs —
  day and night still change the *lighting* — but the grade
  **controls the palette**; clock-driven light plays out
  within the grade, never over it.
- **Battle variance, JoJo-style:** an ordinary battle in
  the forest **inherits** the forest's grade; a significant
  battle — boss / leader / threshold class — may **re-key**
  to a whole different grade. Which battles are significant
  is a data table, adjustable by the owner without touching
  code.
- **Foreshadowing — existence, never access.** Presence
  signs for sealed and mythical beings are **allowed and
  wanted**: a sealed shimmer, a silhouette crossing a sky,
  a place that feels sacred or wrong — Pokémon's legendary
  grammar (Ho-Oh crosses the sky in the first episode,
  generations before anyone can reach him). The hard fence:
  a hint points at **existence, never at access** — no
  spawn row, no marked reachable location, no triggered
  encounter, no change to obtainability. The seal
  (Goddgachuu's, landed PR #96) stands exactly as it is.
  Signs are rare, brief, and never on demand. (This ruling
  reverses an earlier lane fence against any pre-built
  "sealed shimmer"; the reversal is the owner's, recorded
  here and in the dramatic-grade package.)

## 6. THE 2.5D STANDARD — HEARTGOLD / BLACK 2, WHOLE GAME (owner ruling, 2026-10-29)

The whole game — not only the opening stretch — is built to
the **HeartGold/SoulSilver + Black 2/White 2** presentation
standard: the 2.5D feel those games have (a turn Ruby and
Sapphire began): layered maps with real depth staging,
roofs and canopy overhead, shadow and light overlays,
animated tiles and living water, taller and varied
buildings, camera ease and intention, light-aware colour.
The owner's grade of the current build, in his terms:
**form FRLG, colour Yellow** — flat, single-layer, in his
word "almost 1D". Flat is the failure this section exists
to end. Areas are **rebuilt** to the standard in story
sequence (the SEQUENCE-PLAN rollout), never recoloured
into it. Construction follows the kit law (WORKING-METHOD;
Show Bible §5, law 4): Pokémon's systems, construction,
and presentation grammar are the base we build on; the art
that ships is Mochiichao's own.

## 7. NO BARE INTERIORS (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 — a hard acceptance rule)

An interior the player enters is a **real room**, or it is
not done. Every interior carries its **functional set** and
its dressing: furniture, props, signage, its declared
lighting class (§4) with its windows and lamps as built
data, and the traces of the people who use it. The named
failure, from the owner's own play: **the Mochi Center with
no counter, no PC, and no stairs.** An interior missing its
functional set fails its sequence's acceptance outright —
the same standing as a missing door.

## 8. THE SOUL STACK (owner rulings, 2026-10-29 evening)

### 8.1 PRESENTATION RESTRAINT (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 ~20:29 CDT)

Back-end changes do NOT all get shown on the front end. How much data
reaches the screen, and in what form, is copied from how the teardown
games and their actual code/files do it — Pokémon, Red Dead, Persona,
the industry-standard donors — wired that way first, and only then
built on top of. No hand-tooled "AI slop" data displays the player
didn't need. When a system needs a player-facing surface, the surface's
grammar (what's shown, when, how much) comes from the donor game's
presentation, not from invention.

Worked example: honor — RDR2 never shows a number; our honor wiring
likewise never surfaces band names in UI; the player feels it through
prices, greetings, and wanted heat.

### 8.2 THE FAN-LOVED BUILD LIST (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 ~20:31 CDT)

Build from as much of the actual code of the beloved games as possible,
and let the build list be the things fans love and keep coming back
for — all the way from the most popular amazing things to the most
mundane retention details (his worked example: the level-up jingle and
the press-A payoff ritual). Documented fan rankings set the popular end
(Battle Frontier, secret bases, Vs Seeker, following Pokémon —
completeness audit); mundane rituals are first-class build items,
never "polish" to be cut. When a gap could be filled by invention or
by a fan-loved mechanism found in held code, the held code wins.

### 8.3 THE SOUL IS SOUND (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 ~20:37 CDT)

Playing our game, he felt it "missing the soul" — no cries, no music,
none of the sound life. The doctrine:

1. **Sound effects:** the parameters (frequencies, envelopes) are taken
   EXACTLY from the donor games' code — in his words, "to the exact
   frequency and code" — and rendered as our own renditions. The
   recordings never come over; the measured grammar does.
2. **Music:** never use their music. Reverse-engineer HOW their music
   is built (orchestration, structure, loops, placement) and compose
   original Mochiichao songs in that method, placed the way they place
   them.
3. **The pocket-pet line is a soul source:** Digimon, Tamagotchi,
   Pokémon — creature cries and voices included.
4. **The target, in his words:** "that nostalgia, that subconscious
   shit burned into your subconscious from your childhood — that IS
   the soul of this shit."

Reference sound era: the GB / GBC / GBA / GBA-SP generation's
frequencies and button feel. In his words, the DS and Switch "don't
have the sounds like the frequencies" the older handhelds had. (The
technical root: GBA audio still ran the original Game Boy's
square-wave/noise channels; the DS moved to sampled sound — the
signature he hears is real.) Of the DS pair, **HeartGold is his
personal immersion peak** — "more HeartGold for me than BW2" — even
though BW2 remains the visual standard. Donor scope for sound-method
research includes Buu's Fury and other Nintendo/Switch games, graded
honestly (Buu's Fury holds no code in our library — design/docs
grade). This ruling binds the show side equally: sound design is soul
work, not finish work.

### 8.4 SOUL AND DEPTH — BOTH (owner thesis, 2026-10-29 ~20:45 CDT)

The old handheld games had all the soul and not enough depth; modern
games have the depth and are missing the soul (his verdict:
Scarlet/Violet have zero soul). Mochiichao is the change: **BOTH.**

Depth comes from the modern open-world stack (the essence law's kin
list); soul comes from the handheld stack — sound burned deeper than
visuals (square-wave-era frequencies, cries, jingles, the payoff
rituals), small-screen leanness that made the player's imagination do
depth-work, and presentation restraint. Every lane builds against both
halves: a system with depth but no soul is half-done, and soul
treatment on a shallow system is equally half-done.

His soul canon, for donor weighting:

- **Full soul:** HeartGold / SoulSilver.
- **Soul:** FireRed/LeafGreen, Yellow, Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, Pokémon
  Mystery Dungeon, Pokémon Snap, Pokémon XD / Colosseum.
- **Partial:** Pokkén.
- **Zero soul:** Scarlet/Violet — the cautionary case: all that depth,
  and nothing burned in.

### 8.5 THE LIVING WORLD — THE CASTELIA VERDICT (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 ~20:52 CDT)

A place full of people and nothing happening is a failure, however
memorable its camera. The named cautionary case is Castelia City
(BW/BW2), in his description: a city "circular like semi-circular,"
the camera panning around the curve, "three different main streets,"
"a little dock down at the end of the right side of the town," and the
exit at "the north side of the city." His verdict: he hated it, and it
is burned into his memory anyway — a whole city full of people where
you can't really battle, that "just feels like a city full of dead
fucking soulless NPCs that forces you into this weird camera angle."
A set-piece can be memorable and dead at once.

Our world takes the set-piece grammar (curve, pan, streets, dock,
north exit) and refuses the death: every town and city carries
authored life with stakes — Red Dead / GTA world-encounter grammar
(ambient events, stranger side-stories, witnessed deeds that feed
honor) inside the Pokémon world of training, battling, and raising;
buildings entered must matter (this extends the no-bare-interiors rule
to streets). Big showcase arrivals are staged with the JoJo treatment —
place-entry earns its grade (lighting + perspective) per the moment
grammar, so the spectacle is alive, not a forced camera over a dead
set.

### 8.6 THE THIRTEEN-YEAR LAW (owner ruling, 2026-10-29 ~20:56 CDT)

The target is a Pokémon-style game a player can play for **thirteen
years**, like GTA V, and still be discovering things. His evidence, in
his words: players "just discovered a glitch in fucking Grand Theft
Auto last week where you fucking go pull up your goddamn phone and
you fucking can beat a mission online by pulling up your phone and it
stops your goddamn wanted level time for like half a second" — found
13+ years after the game dropped.

The failure to defeat is Pokémon's wall — Champion beaten, dex full,
"ain't shit left to do in the game" — while players keep returning to
the old games anyway, for years, on the pure pull of catching,
battling, teaching moves, and discovering new ones. So the post-game
is not a mode or a checklist: systems must interact deeply enough
(honor × ecology × charge × clock × world encounters × bond) that
discovery never fully closes, secrets are layered under secrets, and
the world keeps changing on its own. A feature that is fully
exhausted the week it is finished fails this law; the STAYING-POWER
test is its local form. Build for the player who is still there in
year thirteen.

## 1. THE SHOW IN ONE LINE
A new keeper arrives at a garden of creatures in a hyper-future
Japan that runs on care — and beyond the gate, in full daylight,
there is a forest the light doesn't reach.

**Series one-line memo (season shape):** Mochiko learns the
Garden, one creature and one rule at a time, while the Dark Forest
keeps not being looked at.

## 2. THE STACK (what this show is made of — Jamar, 2026-10-27)
- **Pokémon (base):** keeper-and-creatures bond, creature
  showcase, the care loop, journey out from a home base.
- **Digimon:** partners with inner lives and histories; growth is
  mutual — the keeper grows as the creatures do (Seki's law).
- **JoJo's Bizarre Adventure:** art-forward presentation —
  dramatic posing, held tension, menace played straight,
  sound-effect typography as graphics.
- **Persona 5:** hyper-futuristic Japan graphic pop — one
  dominant colour per sequence, stylish title cards, UI as
  performance (identity kit: bible/IDENTITY-KIT.md).
- **Japanese lore & culture:** the Garden's bones — a wisp who
  keeps the night, a forest that behaves like the old stories say
  forests behave. Researched, specific, respectful.
- **Tamagotchi / digicore hyper-pop:** the keeper tech — little
  devices, check-ins, feeding rounds, pixel-cute interfaces. Cute
  is a design system.

## 3. THE WORLD
- **THE GARDEN** — home base. Gate, pond, green, keeper board,
  berry store. Runs on the Manual and its Rules. Warm brand-blue
  world (identity kit palette).
- **THE GATE** — the threshold. Everything crosses here:
  arrivals, introductions, names on the board.
- **THE DARK FOREST** — past where the path gives up. A horror of
  **anticipation in daylight**: the sun is up; the forest floor is
  in night anyway. No birdsong from inside it. Dread by stillness,
  never by gore. What stopped moving in there in EP01 is an OPEN

  quietly.
- **THE WIDER WORLD (hyper-future Japan)** — exists beyond the
  Garden (the game's world: towns, dojo, arena). Enters the show
  as the keeper tech, visitors, and journeys out. Detail level:
  bible grows it per season grid, Pokémon-style.

## 5. THE LAWS (locked 2026-10-27)
1. **Voice law:** creatures = pure cries and sounds only. No
   words. No name-saying. Emotion is performance + sound.
2. **Subtitle law:** subtitles are silent-film intertitles —
   they appear ONLY at certain important moments to carry a
   creature's meaning, then vanish. Rationed hard.
3. **Rule 12 (the thesis, Jamar's exact wording):**
   *"Everything in the garden belongs to somebody... And
   everybody belongs to the Garden."* — warm over the Garden,
   a warning over the Dark Forest. The show's promise and its
   threat are the same sentence.
4. **Construction-kit law:** WORKING-METHOD.md governs all
   borrowed material (no donor characters, names, logos).
5. **Writing law:** WRITING-STANDARD.md — Jamar's house system:
   rules first, canon-sourced beats, open items never silently
   filled, gags are mechanisms, silence is a tool, line economy.
6. **Animation law:** FIRST-START.md §3 — only frame-verified
   real performance counts as animation. No slideshows, ever.
7. **LIKENESS LAW (Jamar, 2026-10-27 — learned from the avatar):**
   every character is built from the character's ACTUAL image
   already in this repo — the way the owner's avatar was built
   from the picture he sent, candidates generated off that exact
   image. NEVER invent a likeness from a text description.
   Generative work takes the repo art file as its image source
   and must preserve the likeness and the art style of that
   source. **Likeness gate:** before any generated character
   asset is used or shipped, frames are checked against the
   character's model sheet — same character, same style, or it
   is rejected and does not enter the repo. (Rejected under this
   law, 2026-10-27: the glossy-3D gen-pilot2 batch — Mochii/Gabu/
   Kittember/Sproutle/Wispkin clips that drifted off-model. They
   were never pushed to this repo.)

8. **BATTLE DOCTRINE — AUTONOMOUS-ACTION MODEL (Jamar,
   2026-10-27):** in the SHOW, Mochi fight with their own
   agency. A battle is a continuous living fight — creatures
   do not stand idle waiting to be told each move. Rangers
   sometimes command and direct, but the fight runs between
   commands and without them: a battle is alive between its
   decisive beats. (The GAME lane runs the mirror doctrine —
   command-driven battles where between-command action is
   non-scoring presentation: docs/arena-battle-presentation-
   spec.md §9.) **Term — CLOSED (owner lock, 2026-10-27):**
   the canon name for the human who commands in battle is
   RANGERS. Game data's display wording was "trainers";
   "keeper" is the Garden role only. Structural ids
   (role:"trainer", trainer_*) are unchanged — this lock
   is display/canon text (§9, item 7, closed).

9. **THE SECRET (Jamar, 2026-10-27 — a production-layer truth,
   never an in-world fact):** the world is a computer that grew
   a wilderness. NO inhabitant knows this. Professor Aeon knows
   secretly: he may slip and say "the Mainframe," then correct
   himself to "the mainland" — **"the mainland" is the cover
   word**, and the slip-and-correction is the only shape in
   which the truth may surface in a script. Luigi has a hint,
   not knowledge: he may circle it, never state it. Villain
   hacker-speak is double-layered dramatic irony — villains may
   speak the Secret's vocabulary openly, because in-world
   listeners hear only mundane tech talk, and no ordinary
   character may ever decode the second layer. Production
   documents (this Bible) may hold the truth; in-world
   artifacts — place names, signs, dialogue — must not betray
   it. That is why the region reads IRIDESCENCE on every map
   and sign, whatever it is called in this room.

10. **TWO-LANE CONSISTENCY LAW (Jamar, 2026-10-27):** every
   canon change applies in BOTH lanes — the game data and the
   show data. Names, settings, doctrines: a ruling made in one
   lane is a ruling in the other, applied everywhere it
   appears, in the same terms. (Display names change; internal
   ids/keys stay stable, so wiring cannot break.) Neither lane
   may quietly keep a name or rule the other lane has
   superseded.

## 6. THE EPISODE ENGINE
Pokémon showcase × 6-beat garden formula (mystery-heart):
1. Arrival/errand at the Gate 2. The oddity 3. Investigation
(care-loop tools: feeding, checking, the Manual) 4. False scare
5. Reveal — the creature was never the problem 6. Resolution:
a name on the board, a rule learned + tag (gag mechanism).
**Slot shape (pilot format):** cold open at the Gate → title card
(Persona pop, identity-kit layout) → A-part → eyecatch (keeper
device check-in) → B-part → tag → STINGER (the Forest, when the
season calls for it).

## THE ESSENCE (owner law, 2026-10-27)

Mochiichao — the game and the show — is at its heart and soul an OPEN-WORLD RPG universe, deeper and more open than a Pokemon game, even though it is built on the Pokemon base. Its heart, soul, and mechanics are kin to Fallout, Skyrim, Persona 5, Grand Theft Auto, Red Dead Redemption, Pokemon, Dragon Ball Z: Buu's Fury, and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. It is one whole world and universe that gets created and can be explored through the show, or the game, or both. The Pokemon-base rule says how systems work; this law says what kind of thing we are making. When a scope or design choice makes the world smaller, more corridor-like, or shallower than that statement, the choice is wrong by default and the open-world reading wins.
